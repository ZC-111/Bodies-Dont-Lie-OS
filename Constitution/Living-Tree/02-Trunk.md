## Purpose

The trunk is the stable core of operational standards — referenced often, changed rarely. It connects roots (principles) to branches (domains).

## What Lives Here

- BDL-000 Constitution
- BDL-001 Wayfinding Kernel
- BDL-002 Living Tree
- Future BDL standards (003, 004…)

## Change Frequency

Very rare to occasional. Each standard earns its place through demonstrated need.

## Test

Is this a cross-domain standard that every project references? If yes, it belongs in the trunk.
