## Preamble

Bodies Don't Lie OS is an open framework for discovering, organizing, and applying knowledge about resilient systems.

We believe that every complex system—whether biological, ecological, engineered, social, or digital—produces observable evidence about its condition.

Our responsibility is not to force reality to fit our assumptions, but to continually refine our understanding so that our actions align more closely with reality.

We commit to curiosity before certainty, evidence before opinion, stewardship before ownership, and learning before conclusions.

---

## Core Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **System** | A collection of interacting components whose relationships influence the behavior of the whole. |
| **Health** | The capacity of a system to maintain function through adaptation. |
| **Evidence** | Observable information that increases or decreases confidence in a model. |
| **Wayfinding** | A disciplined process of discovering, testing, and refining understanding through observation, evidence, and iteration. |
| **Resilience** | The ability of a system to absorb disturbance, adapt, and continue functioning. |

---

## Article I — Purpose

The purpose of Bodies Don't Lie OS is:

1. To help people observe more clearly.
2. To improve decision making through evidence.
3. To cultivate resilient systems.
4. To preserve knowledge for future generations.
5. To build tools that amplify human understanding rather than replace it.

---

## Article II — Scope

Bodies Don't Lie OS applies to any complex system, including but not limited to:

Human Health · Medicine · Nutrition · Psychology · Ecology · Architecture · Engineering · Cities · AI · Education · Organizations · Communities

No single discipline owns truth. Every discipline contributes to understanding.

## Article V — The Wayfinding Cycle

Every project follows the same cycle. No stage may be skipped intentionally.

```
Observe → Measure → Understand → Model → Predict → Build → Validate → Learn ↺
```

Full specification: [BDL-001 Wayfinding Kernel](BDL-001-Wayfinding-Kernel.md)

---

## Article VI — Evidence

Claims should be proportional to the available evidence.

When uncertainty exists: document it. Do not hide it. New evidence is welcomed. Changing one's mind in response to evidence is considered a strength.

---

## Article VII — Contributors

Every contributor is expected to:

- Observe honestly
- Credit others
- Document assumptions
- Share improvements
- Remain teachable
- Protect future contributors by leaving clear documentation

---

## Article VIII — Technology

Technology exists to express the principles. Technology is not the principles.

Programming languages change. AI models change. Frameworks change. The Constitution remains.

---

## Article IX — Stewardship

Bodies Don't Lie OS is intended to grow beyond any individual. The goal is not simply to build software. The goal is to cultivate a living body of knowledge that future generations can improve.

---

## Non-Goals

Bodies Don't Lie OS does **not** exist to:

- Replace medical professionals, scientific inquiry, engineering judgment, or human expertise
- Accumulate information for its own sake
- Optimize for document count or repository size

It exists to improve observation, organization of knowledge, evidence-based reasoning, and collaborative learning.

---

## Closing Statement

We recognize that understanding is never complete. Therefore, this Constitution is not a declaration of certainty. It is a commitment to continual observation, disciplined learning, and responsible stewardship.

> **Seek reality with humility. Build with evidence. Leave the system healthier than you found it.**

> *Greater clarity through simplicity. The best part is less… or no part.*
