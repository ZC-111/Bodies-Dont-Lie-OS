# 01 — Observe

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Stage | 1 of 8 |
| Standard | [BDL-001](../BDL-001-Wayfinding-Kernel.md) |
| Question | What is the system showing us? |

## Purpose

Notice what is present before interpreting it. Observation is raw contact with reality — signals, patterns, anomalies, and context.

## Inputs

- A system under study (biological, ecological, social, digital, or other)
- Open attention — no hypothesis required yet

## Outputs

- Observation log: what was seen, when, under what conditions
- Unresolved questions worth measuring next

## Test

Can you describe what you observed without explaining why? If every statement includes "because," you haven't finished observing.
