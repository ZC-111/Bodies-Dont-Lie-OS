# 07 — Validate

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Stage | 7 of 8 |
| Standard | [BDL-001](../BDL-001-Wayfinding-Kernel.md) |
| Question | Did reality match expectation? |

## Purpose

Compare outcomes to predictions. Validation is honest contact with results — confirming, refuting, or refining.

## Inputs

- Build from [06-Build.md](06-Build.md)
- Prediction criteria from [05-Predict.md](05-Predict.md)
- New observations and measurements

## Outputs

- Result: confirmed, refuted, or inconclusive
- Evidence documented with confidence level
- Discrepancies explained (model error, measurement error, or unknown factor)

## Test

Did you compare outcome to prediction before interpreting? Celebrate refutation — it means the cycle worked.
