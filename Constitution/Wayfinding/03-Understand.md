# 03 — Understand

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Stage | 3 of 8 |
| Standard | [BDL-001](../BDL-001-Wayfinding-Kernel.md) |
| Question | Why is this happening? |

## Purpose

Explain the mechanisms behind observed and measured patterns. Understanding connects evidence to meaning.

## Inputs

- Measurements from [02-Measure.md](02-Measure.md)
- Existing knowledge from [Knowledge/](../../Knowledge/README.md) and [Research/](../../Research/README.md)

## Outputs

- Explanation of mechanisms (how components interact)
- Confidence level: observation, derived, interpreted, or speculative
- Gaps in understanding clearly marked

## Test

Can you explain the mechanism without jumping to a solution? If the explanation ends with "so we should…", separate understanding from intervention.
