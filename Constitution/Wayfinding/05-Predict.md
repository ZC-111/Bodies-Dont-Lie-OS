# 05 — Predict

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Stage | 5 of 8 |
| Standard | [BDL-001](../BDL-001-Wayfinding-Kernel.md) |
| Question | What should happen next? |

## Purpose

Derive testable expectations from the model. Prediction is where understanding becomes accountable to reality.

## Inputs

- Model from [04-Model.md](04-Model.md)
- Specific conditions or intervention to test

## Outputs

- Clear prediction: "If X, then Y under conditions Z"
- Criteria for confirmation and falsification
- Timeline or measurement plan for validation

## Test

Is the prediction specific enough to be wrong? A prediction that cannot fail is not a prediction.
