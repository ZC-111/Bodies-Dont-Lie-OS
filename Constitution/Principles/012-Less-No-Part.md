# 012 — Less, or No Part

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Design Principle |
| Article | IV |

## Principle

Greater clarity through simplicity. The best part is less… or no part.

## Meaning

The ideal document, workflow, or component may be one that doesn't need to exist at all. Before adding, ask whether the need can be met by linking existing modules. Before keeping, ask whether removal would actually reduce capability.

## Test

Does this need to exist? Could linking to something else suffice?
