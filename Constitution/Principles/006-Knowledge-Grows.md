# 006 — Knowledge Grows

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Knowledge grows through testing, refinement, and sharing.

## Meaning

Knowledge is not static. Every claim is provisional until tested. Refinement is not failure — it is the mechanism of growth. Sharing accelerates the cycle for everyone.
