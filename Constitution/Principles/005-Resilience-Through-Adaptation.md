# 005 — Resilience Through Adaptation

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Resilience is demonstrated through adaptation.

## Meaning

A resilient system is not one that never fails — it is one that absorbs disturbance, adapts, and continues functioning. Health is measured by capacity to recover and learn, not by absence of stress.

## Test

How does this system respond when conditions change?
