# 001 — Reality Leaves Evidence

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Reality leaves evidence.

## Meaning

Every complex system produces observable signals about its condition. These signals exist whether or not we notice them. Our job is to look, not to assume.

## Test

Can you point to observable evidence for this claim? If not, it is speculation — label it as such.
