# 004 — Understanding Precedes Intervention

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Understanding precedes intervention.

## Meaning

Acting before understanding creates noise, harm, and false confidence. The Wayfinding Cycle exists to enforce this order. No stage may be skipped intentionally.

## Test

Can you explain the mechanism before proposing the action?
