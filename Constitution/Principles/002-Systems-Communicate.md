# 002 — Systems Communicate

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Healthy systems continuously communicate their condition through observable signals.

## Meaning

A system in distress does not stay silent. Symptoms, metrics, behaviors, and patterns are all forms of communication. Learning to read these signals is the first skill of wayfinding.

## Test

What signals is this system producing right now? Are we listening?
