# 011 — Human-First

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Design Principle |
| Article | IV |

## Principle

Build tools that amplify human understanding rather than replace it.

## Meaning

Technology serves people. AI agents inherit principles — they do not override them. Every workflow should make a human more capable, not more dependent.

## Test

Does this make a person more capable, or more dependent?
