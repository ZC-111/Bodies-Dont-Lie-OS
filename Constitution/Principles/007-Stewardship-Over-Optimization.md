# 007 — Stewardship Over Optimization

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Long-term stewardship is more valuable than short-term optimization.

## Meaning

Optimizing for today at the expense of tomorrow creates fragile systems. Stewardship means leaving the system healthier than you found it — for the next contributor, the next generation, the next wayfinder.

## Test

Will this still make sense in ten years? Does it protect future contributors?
