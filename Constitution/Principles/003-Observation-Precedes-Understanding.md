# 003 — Observation Precedes Understanding

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | Foundational Principle |
| Article | III |

## Principle

Observation precedes understanding.

## Meaning

We cannot understand what we have not observed. Models, theories, and interventions must be grounded in direct observation — not preference, tradition, or authority alone.

## Test

Has this been observed before it was explained?
