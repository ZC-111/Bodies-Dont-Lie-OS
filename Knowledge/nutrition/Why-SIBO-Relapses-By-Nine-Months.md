## Answer

SIBO is relapse-prone when care stops at killing organisms. In the Clearfield / Arlen Hill webinar synthesis, roughly **half of patients (~44%) relapse by nine months** when secretions, motility, and nutrient deficits are left unfixed — with many real-world returns by 3–6 months.

Overgrowth returns because the **stage** stays favorable: low HCl/enzymes/bile, impaired MMC (including all-day grazing), sympathetic tone, and micronutrient collateral damage. Antimicrobial courses can clear symptoms temporarily without changing those drivers, so the revolving door continues.

This leaf states the **why of relapse timing**, not a personal prognosis and not a dosing protocol.
