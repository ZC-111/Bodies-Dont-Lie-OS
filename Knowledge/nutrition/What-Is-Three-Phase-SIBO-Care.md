# What Is Three-Phase SIBO Care?

## Answer

**Three-phase SIBO care** (Hill / Clearfield synthesis) is a staged model built around relapse prevention — not kill-only cycles:

1. **Phase 1 — Antimicrobial (~30 days):** Suppress / eradicate overgrowth; when *H. pylori* is also suspected, treat in the same sweep (speaker claim).
2. **Phase 2 — Secretions + motility (often through the ~9-month relapse window):** Restore HCl / enzymes / bile and support MMC (including meal spacing / stop grazing).
3. **Phase 3 — Nutrient rebuild (≥6 months):** Replete micronutrient collateral damage (e.g. B12, Fe, fat-soluble vitamins).

The teachable point: ask what the **Phase 2 and Phase 3** plan is, not only which antimicrobial comes next. Product names and exact doses in the lecture are `product_pitch` until slide-verified — this leaf carries the **structure**, not a consumer protocol.

Acupuncture adjuncts (middle-jiao / stress-gut patterns) may support **how hard to push** during Phase 2–3; they do not replace biomed red-flag workup.
