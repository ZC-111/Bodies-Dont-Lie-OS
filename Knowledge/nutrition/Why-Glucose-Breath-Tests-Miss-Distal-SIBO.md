# Why Do Glucose Breath Tests Miss Distal SIBO?

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

## Answer

Breath tests are imperfect decision tools. In the Hill / Clearfield synthesis, **glucose breath testing is cited near ~54% sensitivity** and **lactulose near ~42%** — useful when positive, weak as a veto when the clinical picture is classic.

**Anatomy explains many false negatives:** the small bowel is ~19 feet long; glucose is absorbed proximally and mainly samples the first few feet; aspirates likewise sample a short segment. Distal overgrowth can therefore be clinically present while a negative test looks “clear.”

Teaching implication: weigh symptoms and selective labs with the result; do not treat a negative breath test as proof of wellness, and do not self-start antimicrobials from this leaf alone. Alarm features still require escalation.
