# Why Is FODMAP Not A SIBO Diet?

## Answer

**Low-FODMAP is an IBS symptom tool, not the default SIBO diet** in the Hill / Clearfield teaching stance. Long restriction can strip oligosaccharides, polyphenols, and other supports while leaving the real relapse drivers — secretions, motility, and nutrient rebuild — unaddressed.

If FODMAP is used for IBS overlap, the same synthesis says **reassess by ~6 weeks if there is no benefit** and avoid chronic ultra-restriction as “SIBO maintenance.” Relapse prevention still tracks [three-phase care](What-Is-Three-Phase-SIBO-Care.md), not forever food fear.

Frame this as **one clinician’s stance**, not a universal nutrition law. It is not permission to abandon clinician-guided short bridges, and it is not a personalized meal plan.
