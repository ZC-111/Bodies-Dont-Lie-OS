## Location

On the extensor aspect of the thumb, at the junction of lines drawn along the radial border of the nail and the base of the nail, approximately 0.1 cun from the corner of the nail.

## Needling

Perpendicular or oblique insertion directed proximally 0.1 to 0.2 cun, or prick to bleed.

## Actions

- Revives consciousness
- Clears heat and benefits the throat

## Indications

Loss of consciousness from windstroke, cold/hot inversion; sore throat, throat painful obstruction, mumps, nosebleed, dry lips with desire to drink, febrile disease with cold shivering; agitation with cough and dyspnoea, mania, childhood fright wind, vomiting; pain and contraction of the wrist and thumb, heat of the palms.
