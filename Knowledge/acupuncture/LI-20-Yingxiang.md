## Location

In the naso-labial groove, at the level of the midpoint of the lateral border of the ala nasi.

## Needling

Transverse insertion medio-superiorly 0.3 to 0.5 cun, or join to Bitong (M-HN-14) at the highest point of the naso-labial groove. Note: according to some texts, contraindicated to moxibustion.

## Actions

- Opens the nasal passages
- Expels wind and clears heat

## Indications

Nasal congestion, nasal congestion and discharge, rhinitis, profuse nasal discharge, loss of sense of smell, nasal polyps, nasal sores, sneezing, nosebleed; deviation of the mouth, swelling and itching of the face, pain and swelling of the lip, heat and redness of the eyes, dyspnoea, round worms in the bile duct (biliary ascariasis).
