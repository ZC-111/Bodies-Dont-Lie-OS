# LI-11 Quchi (曲池) — Pool at the Crook

## Location

At the elbow, midway between Chize LU-5 and the lateral epicondyle of the humerus, at the lateral end of the transverse cubital crease (elbow flexed).

## Needling

Perpendicular insertion 1 to 1.5 cun, or joined by through-needling to Shaohai HE-3.

## Actions

- Clears heat
- Cools the blood, eliminates wind, drains damp and alleviates itching
- Regulates qi and blood
- Activates the channel and alleviates pain

## Indications

High fever that does not recede, injury by cold with residual fever, thirst, malaria; throat painful obstruction, loss of voice, toothache, redness and pain of the eyes, lacrimation, pain in front of the ear; agitation and oppression of the chest, manic disorders, poor memory, tongue thrusting, dizziness, hypertension, goitre, scrofula; erysipelas (cinnabar toxin), urticaria, wind rash, dry/scaly/itchy skin, shingles, clove sores; distention and pain of the abdomen, vomiting and diarrhoea, dysenteric disorder, amenorrhoea; numbness of the upper arm, wind painful obstruction, hemiplegia, clonic spasm, contraction and pain of the elbow and shoulder, atrophy disorder of the lower limbs.
