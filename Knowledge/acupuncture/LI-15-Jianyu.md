# LI-15 Jianyu (肩髃) — Shoulder Bone

## Location

In the depression anterior and inferior to the acromion, at the origin of the deltoid muscle (more palpable when the arm is abducted).

## Needling

i. Arm abducted, perpendicular insertion towards the centre of the axilla 1 to 1.5 cun; ii. transverse-oblique insertion distally towards the elbow 1.5 to 2 cun.

## Actions

- Dispels wind-damp, alleviates pain and benefits the shoulder joint
- Eliminates wind and regulates qi and blood
- Regulates qi and dissipates phlegm nodules

## Indications

Shoulder pain, weakness of the shoulder, wind-damp of the shoulder, heat sensation and redness and swelling of the shoulder, inability to raise the arm to the head, contraction and numbness of the arm, hemiplegia, wind paralysis, windstroke, wind atrophy disorder, inability to turn the head, painful obstruction of the fingers; wind-heat urticaria; scrofula, goitre, seminal emission due to taxation, hypertension.
