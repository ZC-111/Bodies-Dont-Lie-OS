# LI-6 Pianli (偏歷) — Veering Passage

## Location

3 cun proximal to Yangxi LI-5, on the line connecting Yangxi LI-5 with Quchi LI-11.

## Needling

Transverse-oblique insertion 0.5 to 1 cun.

## Actions

- Expels wind and clears heat
- Opens and regulates the water passages

## Indications

Tinnitus, deafness, toothache, tooth decay, cold teeth, redness and pain of the eyes, dimness of vision, rhinitis, nosebleed, dry throat, throat painful obstruction, deviation of the mouth, swelling of the cheek; difficult urination, oedema, ascites, obstruction of the diaphragm, borborygmus with oedema; malaria, manic raving; pain of the wrist, elbow and upper arm.

## Sources
