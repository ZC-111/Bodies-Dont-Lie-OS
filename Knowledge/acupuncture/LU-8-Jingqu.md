## Location

Above the wrist, 1 cun proximal to Taiyuan LU-9, on the line connecting Taiyuan LU-9 with Kongzui LU-6, in the depression at the base of the styloid process of the radius and on the radial side of the radial artery.

## Needling

Oblique proximal or perpendicular insertion 0.3 to 0.5 cun, avoiding the radial artery.

## Actions

- Descends Lung qi and alleviates cough and wheezing

## Indications

Cough, asthma, wheezing, dyspnoea, distention and pain of the chest and upper back, sore throat, throat painful obstruction, febrile disease with absence of sweating, febrile disease with breathlessness, heat in the palms; Heart pain with vomiting, wrist pain, malaria, much yawning, pain in the soles of the feet.

## Commentary

The jing-river point of the Lung; primarily indicated for excess-type cough, dyspnoea and wheezing with fullness and pain of the chest and upper back. Infrequently used in modern practice.
