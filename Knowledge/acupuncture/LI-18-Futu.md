# LI-18 Futu (扶突) — Support the Prominence

## Location

On the lateral side of the neck, level with the tip of the laryngeal prominence, between the sternal and clavicular heads of the sternocleidomastoid muscle.

## Needling

i. Perpendicular insertion 0.3 to 0.5 cun; ii. oblique insertion 0.5 to 0.8 cun. **Caution: deeper needling may puncture the carotid artery or jugular vein.**

## Actions

- Benefits the throat and voice
- Alleviates cough and wheezing

## Indications

Swelling and pain of the throat, sudden loss of voice, rattling sound in the throat, goitre, scrofula, difficulty in swallowing; cough, coughing with much spittle, wheezing, asthma.
