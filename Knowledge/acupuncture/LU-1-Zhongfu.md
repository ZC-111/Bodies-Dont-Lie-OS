# LU-1 Zhongfu (中府) — Middle Palace

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

| Chinese | 中府 |
| Point class | Front-Mu point of the Lung; Meeting point of the Lung and Spleen channels |
| Evidence Level | derived (cross-source confirmed: Deadman + Bencaodian) |
| Last Verified | 2026-09-04 |

## Location

On the lateral aspect of the chest, in the first intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the midline, 1 cun inferior to Yunmen LU-2.

## Actions

- Regulates and descends qi
- Unbinds the chest

## Indications

Fullness of the chest and lateral costal region extending to the back, inability to turn over when lying down, cough, shortness of breath.
