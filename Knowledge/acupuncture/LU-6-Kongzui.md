# LU-6 Kongzui (孔最) — Maximum Opening

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Domain | Acupuncture · Lung channel |
| Code | LU-6 |
| Pinyin | kǒng zuì |
| Chinese | 孔最 |
| Point class | Xi-Cleft point of the Lung channel |
| Evidence Level | derived (cross-source confirmed: Deadman + Bencaodian) |
| Last Verified | 2026-09-04 |

## Location

On the flexor aspect of the forearm, 7 cun proximal to Taiyuan LU-9, on the line connecting Taiyuan LU-9 with Chize LU-5.

## Needling

Perpendicular or oblique insertion 0.5 to 1.5 cun.

## Actions

- Disseminates and descends Lung qi
- Clears heat and moistens the Lung
- Clears heat and stops bleeding
- Moderates acute conditions

## Indications

Cough, asthma, wheezing, dyspnoea, distention and pain of the chest and upper back, sore throat, throat painful obstruction, febrile disease with absence of sweating, febrile disease with breathlessness, heat in the palms. Heart pain with vomiting, wrist pain, malaria.
