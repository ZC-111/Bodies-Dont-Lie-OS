## Location

On the radial side of the forearm, 2 cun distal to Quchi LI-11, on the line connecting Quchi LI-11 with Yangxi LI-5.

## Needling

Perpendicular insertion 0.5 to 1.5 cun.

## Actions

- Regulates qi and blood, activates the channel and alleviates pain
- Harmonises the intestines and Stomach

## Indications

Pain and immobility of the arm and shoulder, windstroke, paralysis of the arm, numbness of the arm, atrophy disorder, hemiplegia, contraction and inflexibility of the elbow, lumbar pain with inability to lie down; abdominal pain, vomiting and diarrhoea, periodic sensation of cold in the intestines, sudden turmoil disorder; toothache with swelling of the cheek, deviation of the mouth, loss of voice, scrofula.
