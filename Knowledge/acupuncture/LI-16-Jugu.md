## Location

On the upper aspect of the shoulder, in the depression medial to the acromion process and between the lateral extremity of the clavicle and the scapular spine.

## Needling

Perpendicular or oblique insertion 0.5 to 1 cun. **Caution: deep medial insertion carries a risk of pneumothorax, particularly in thin patients.**

## Actions

- Activates the channel, alleviates pain and benefits the shoulder joint
- Regulates qi and blood and dissipates phlegm nodules

## Indications

Pain of the shoulder and back, blood stasis in the shoulder, difficulty in moving or raising the arm, pain of the upper arm; fright epilepsy, vomiting of copious quantities of blood, blood stasis in the chest, scrofula, goitre.
