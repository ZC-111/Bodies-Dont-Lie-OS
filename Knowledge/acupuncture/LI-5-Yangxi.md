## Location

On the radial side of the wrist, in the centre of the hollow formed by the tendons of extensor pollicis longus and brevis (anatomical snuffbox).

## Needling

Perpendicular insertion 0.5 to 1 cun.

## Actions

- Clears heat and alleviates pain
- Clears yangming fire
- Calms the spirit
- Benefits the wrist joint

## Indications

Rhinitis, nosebleed, tinnitus, deafness, ear pain, redness, swelling and pain of the eyes, superficial visual obstruction, toothache, headache (frontal, chronic), throat painful obstruction; mania-depression, febrile disease with agitation of the Heart, manic raving, seeing ghosts, fright; cold cough, vomiting of foam, urticaria, malaria, fever with absence of sweating; weakness and pain of the wrist, contraction of the five fingers, heat in the palms, difficulty raising the elbow.
