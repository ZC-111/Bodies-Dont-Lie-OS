## What happened

Graduated the Lung channel (LU-1…LU-11) into `Knowledge/` leaves. A first pass confirmed 5 points from extract fields alone. Deeper inspection then revealed the extract's clinical fields were partly **contaminated**, which forced a correction: fix the extractor, re-validate against the Deadman source directly, and re-author the leaves. Final: 9 of 11 confirmed, 2 deferred.

## What we learned

1. **Extraction is not confirmation — and "non-empty" is not "correct."** The v0.2 extract reported high field-fill counts, but per-point checking found empty, OCR-garbled, and *cross-contaminated* fields. The first-pass leaves for LU-1 and LU-6 published the **wrong** clinical text (LU-6 and LU-9 had been given LU-8 Jingqu's indications). Only reading the gold-standard source caught it.
2. **The bug: landmark references were mistaken for shared clinical blocks.** `propagate_cluster_fields` treated any point code appearing in another point's LOCATION prose (e.g. "7 cun proximal to Taiyuan LU-9") as a cluster member and copied clinical sections across them. Fixed: clinical sections are never propagated; only a missing location may be shared, and it is flagged. This removed contaminated text from ~50 points book-wide.
3. **For health-domain claims, verify against the source, not a derived index.** Graduated leaves now cite the Deadman page each field was read from. The extractor remains a useful index (codes, names, i18n, cross-source, pages); it is not trusted for per-point clinical text on scrambled pages.
4. **Validation gates must test the right property.** The v0.1 gate checked "field non-empty"; it should have checked "field is the point's own, source-verified text." Gate strengthened accordingly.

## Reflection questions

- *Is this still true?* No — two published leaves were wrong and have been corrected. Recorded openly rather than quietly overwritten.
- *Can this be expressed more simply?* The leaves link to the dataset and cite source pages instead of copying the corpus.

## Loops back to

- **Build:** improve per-point section attribution for scrambled multi-column pages (would recover LU-4 indications, LU-8 actions, and speed future channels).
- **Observe:** the same contamination pattern affected ~50 points across other channels; audit each channel against the source before graduating — do not trust extract fields alone.
