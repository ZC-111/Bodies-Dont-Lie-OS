## What happened

Ran the Lung channel (LU-1…LU-11) through a [Validate gate](../Research/acupuncture/lung-channel-validation-v0.1.md): present in both sources, clean location/actions/indications, no contamination. 5 of 11 passed and became Knowledge leaves; 6 were held back.

## What we learned

1. **Extraction is not the same as confirmation.** The v0.2 extract reports 396 points with high field-fill counts, but per-point inspection found empty, OCR-garbled, and *contaminated* fields. The Validate stage earns its place — graduating on extract-count alone would have published wrong clinical text (e.g. LU-9 carrying LU-6's actions).
2. **Cluster-propagation over-reaches.** `propagate_cluster_fields` fills gaps by copying shared block text to every point named in a block. It also overwrites/duplicates onto points that already have their own text, producing silent contamination (LU-9, LU-8). Copied text should be flagged, and a point's own section should never be replaced by a neighbour's.
3. **Evidence proportionality works in practice.** Marking leaves `derived (cross-source confirmed)` and linking every claim to the source extract + validation note keeps the confidence visible, per Constitution Article VI.

## Reflection questions

- *Is this duplication?* No — leaves link to the source extract rather than copying the dataset; each holds one point.
- *Can we remove it?* The validation note is the evidence for the leaves; keep until the extractor is corrected and re-validated.
