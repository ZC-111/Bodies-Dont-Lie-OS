# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-03-18

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Microbiome ≠ only the colon** — oral, respiratory, and vaginal communities matter; microbial genes outnumber human genes by orders of magnitude (~3.3M vs ~23K).

2. **Balance is the goal** — digestion + homeostasis via SCFAs, immune modulation, bile conversion, and gut–brain/skin links.

3. **Butyrate is the barrier SCFA** — strengthens tight junctions; propionate/acetate cover lipid/energy roles.

4. **Four R protocol:** Remove (flour/sugar/dairy/gluten/corn) → Repair (DGL + L-glutamine) → Restore (pre/pro/postbiotics) → Replace (enzymes + salt for HCl support).

5. **Biomarker desk card:** calprotectin (IBD vs IBS), zonulin (permeability), gliadin IgA, eosinophil activation protein, *H. pylori* resistance genes, parasites.

6. **Probiotic timing is clinical:** *H. pylori* — 2h from antibiotics, continue 4–8 weeks; SIBO — after overgrowth is reduced.

7. ***Akkermansia* falls with age** — don’t assume it rises; loss ties to obesity risk; extremes both problematic.

8. ***Saccharomyces boulardii* is the diarrhea workhorse** — antibiotic-associated, traveler’s, *C. diff*, NEC framing; toxin inactivation + SI→colon colonization.

9. **Hormones talk to bugs** — cortisol drives dysbiosis; allopregnanolone→GABA pathway explains benzo-like calming without defaulting to benzos; pregnancy TH2/progesterone raises *Listeria* vulnerability.

10. **Test smart:** Diagnostic Solutions **GI-MAP** via **Rupa Health** aggregator for practical ordering/results workflow.

## Pre / Pro / Postbiotic Comparison

| Dimension | Prebiotics | Probiotics | Postbiotics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | Non-digestible fibers (food for microbes) | Live beneficial organisms | Functional microbial end-products |
| **Examples** | Inulin, dietary fiber | *Lactobacillus*, *Bifidobacterium*, *S. boulardii* | Anti-inflammatory / antioxidant metabolites (Pelton framing) |
| **Best use** | Feed diversity; may ↓ cortisol | Condition-specific restoration | Benefits when live organisms alone aren’t enough |
| **Timing caveat** | With food pattern change | *H. pylori*/SIBO timing rules | Often stacked with pre/pro |
| **Failure mode** | Feeding the wrong overgrowth if SIBO untreated | Wrong strain / wrong timing | Treating product hype without diet |

**Consensus from session:** Diet and barrier first; use live organisms intentionally; postbiotics are the “payload.”
