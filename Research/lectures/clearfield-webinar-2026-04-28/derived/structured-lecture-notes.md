## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Follistatin Spectrum (16:36–20:40)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 7)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Muscle preservation is not only protein + creatine + hormones. **Follistatin removes the myostatin “brake”** — same pathway across a potency spectrum from oral nutraceutical to gene therapy.

### Key Concepts
- **Myostatin** = negative regulator of skeletal muscle growth
- **Follistatin** binds myostatin → prevents receptor activation → anti-catabolic / growth-permissive signaling
- Continuum of potency:
  1. **Nutritional modulation** (mild)
  2. **Research peptides** (research-only; limited medical legitimacy)
  3. **Gene therapy** (potent, durable, disease-focused)

### Fortetropin (oral egg-yolk–derived compound)
- Promoted previously by Dr. Chris Meletis
- RCTs in older adults (~66yo): ~**20 g/day × 21 days** → **~18% ↑ muscle protein synthesis** in resistance-trained young men; lean mass **+1.7 kg vs +0.6 kg placebo**
- Immobilization / post-leg-surgery: blunted rise in circulating myostatin (muscle preservation)
- Better framed as **anti-catabolic**, not classic anabolic bodybuilding agent
- Safety: no meaningful FSH suppression despite ovarian follicular-fluid origin history; strong safety signal in mixed cohorts
- Clinical use case: sarcopenia, recovery, aging patients — slow muscle loss, modest gains

### Follistatin gene therapy (pathologic states)
- Intramuscular injection (e.g., quadriceps) studied in muscular dystrophy
- Reported: improved 6-minute walk (up to **+125 m**), hypertrophy on biopsy, reduced fibrosis, effects lasting months to >1 year
- Small cohorts; no serious AEs reported in those series
- **Not supplementation** — gene-level muscle physiology edit; different risk tolerance

### Cross-link to Andrews
- Andrews: BioViva / XPRIZE team includes **follistatin gene therapy** among multi-gene approaches

### Clinical Pearl
> Same pathway, different clinical intent: Fortetropin = scoop (gentle); gene therapy = injection (disease-modifying). Choose by patient risk, disease severity, and regulatory reality.

## Segment 3 — Bill Andrews: Aging as Disease & the Betty White Test (34:37–~50:00)

**Speaker:** Bill Andrews, PhD — Sierra Sciences

### Is Aging a Disease?
- FDA/regulatory view: disease = deviation from “normal.” Aging is species-normal → hard to label a disease; also argued to be **unmeasurable** until reversed
- Andrews’ counter: limited cell-division capacity is **not universal across species**
  - **Limited divisions (telomerase gene off):** humans, other primates, dogs, cats, horses, sheep, pigs, deer
  - **No such limit (telomerase active):** lobsters, tortoises, humpback whales, clams, some fish/birds
- Repair model: damaged cells replaced by division → each division spends a “ride ticket” (telomere)

### Measurement Problem
- Biomarkers (grip strength, epigenetic clocks, etc.) **correlate** with aging but reversing a biomarker ≠ reversing aging
- **Betty White test:** if an 85-year-old looks/feels/behaves 25 again, measurement debates end — success is unmistakable
- Strategy for regulators: test investigational therapy on an **aging-related disease** (e.g., dementia) while monitoring phenotypic reversal; once reversal is obvious, aging can be treated as a disease socially/clinically faster than FDA redefinition

### How Not to Age (today’s focus)
- Prior talk (~3 months earlier) covered what/why/how of aging; today’s emphasis = **how not** (interventions)
- Rare marketing caveat: Andrews usually avoids product marketing; today discusses Touchstone Essentials because he believes the stack supports telomere health

## Segment 4 — Telomere Biology: Limit, Enhancers, Epigenetics (~45:00–1:10)

### 4A. The 125-Year Limit
- Telomeres = chromosome tips; amusement-park tickets analogy: born with ~**100 tickets**; lose ~1 every **~2.5 years** on average (faster prenatally)
- Last ticket → cells can’t repair wear → death
- Basal endothelial turnover in vessels drives a fairly standard telomere-loss rate → theoretical max lifespan **~125 years**
- Independent demography/statistics studies converge on same ~125-year ceiling
- Jeanne Calment (122) story challenged as inheritance-tax identity substitution (Andrews’ view) — nobody reliably past ~120 without solving telomere biology
- Poor lifestyle + genetics accelerate shortening → almost nobody approaches the theoretical max

### 4B. Telomeres as Enhancer Sequences
- Beyond lifespan limit: telomeres explain **synchronized aging phenotypes**
- Literature (PubMed refs on slides): telomeres show hallmarks of **enhancer sequences**
- Gene regulation model: promoter (“dimmer”) + distant enhancer → DNA looping → ON/OFF
- Long telomeres fold and regulate gene batteries; **short telomeres lose enhancer reach** → epigenetic drift of aging
- Andrews: aging = age-associated **epigenetic change**; he showed old-vs-young expression profiles (1990s) and later reversed profiles in culture by lengthening/shortening telomeres via telomerase gene therapy

### ★ Absolute Constraint
> No matter what else you fix (mitochondria, oxidative stress, etc.), **aging is not cured unless telomere shortening is solved.**

### 4C. Telomerase Discovery Context
- Why children have longer telomeres than parents: reproductive/primordial germ cells express **telomerase**
- Telomerase binds TTAGG-repeat tips and lengthens them
- **Tug-of-war:** shorteners (everywhere) vs lengthener (telomerase; normally germline-restricted)
- Evolutionary aside: aging/cancer trade-offs; women live longer than men in ~95% of mammals (parental-care hypothesis)

### 5B. Telovital — only nutraceutical claimed to induce telomerase
- High-throughput screen: **~1M molecules**; **~600K plant extract fractions** (fractionated by size/charge/solubility — whole extracts too noisy)
- **~50 hits** induced telomerase in human cells; Touchstone blended **top 5** for synergy → **Telovital**
- Does **not** fully win the tug-of-war (unlike gene therapy) but **slows shortening**
- Critically short telomeres are **preferentially lengthened** (“ball of yarn” model — tip buried when long; accessible when short)
- Most marketed “telomere lengtheners,” diets, lifestyles, light chambers: **no telomerase induction** in Andrews’ assays
- Diet/lifestyle claims (incl. Ornish / PREDIMED interpretations in Q&A): often misuse **average** telomere assays
- Most potent screened fraction among five: **turmeric fraction missing curcumin** (~27 telomerase mRNAs/cell in assay); still recommend curcumin elsewhere (e.g., Wellspring)
- Gene therapy: lengthens telomeres by delivering active gene (expensive, risky, in trials) — only non-nutraceutical that works in his framing

### 5C. Why fake “lengthening” data happens
- Blood telomere **averages** mislead: mixed cell populations
- **Toxic** agents preferentially kill short-telomere cells → average rises (“fire the dumbest employees → company IQ rises”)
- **Immunogenic** agents recruit longer-telomere marrow cells into blood → average rises (“hire geniuses”)
- Both still force compensatory divisions → **body-wide shortening** despite prettier blood averages
- **Requirement:** show **telomerase expression/activity**, not only average length
- Look for Sierra Sciences / Bill Andrews seal of approval; only products with his discovered inducers have passed (acknowledged conflict: royalty on Telovital funds research)

### 5D. Practical testing recommendations
- **ALCAT** (alcat.com): non-IgE food/chemical reactivity via WBC morphology — inflammation triggers; eliminate offenders 6–12 months
- **Life Length** (lifelength.com): full telomere **distribution** + % critically short (preferred over average-only vendors)
- Epigenetic/multi-biomarker aging clocks: Ryan Smith’s approach cited as strong **correlation** tool (not proof of cure)
- Inflammation = #1 aging accelerator in Andrews’ clinical advice frame

### 5E. Disease breadth & policy
- Telomere length linked to nearly every disease class **except** alcoholism and sickle cell (per Andrews)
- Examples: HIV/AIDS (CD4 replacement exhausts telomeres), alcoholic cirrhosis (hepatocyte replacement), muscular dystrophy (accelerated myocyte turnover)
- Cancer myth: telomerase does **not** cause cancer; Andrews argues it **decreases** cancer risk / aids defense (see 2017 Tokyo talk)
- **Best Choice Medicine** (Andrews + Elizabeth Parrish / BioViva): reform FDA so patients with life-limiting disease may choose investigational options; Coalition for Radical Life Extension march planned ~early 2027 (Lincoln Memorial)
- Motto: **“Cure aging or die trying.”**
