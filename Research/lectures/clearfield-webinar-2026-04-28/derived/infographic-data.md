## 🎯 Infographic 1: "The Ride-Ticket Model"

**Title:** Why Cells Run Out of Repair Capacity

```
Birth ≈ 100 telomere "tickets"
        │
        ▼  ~1 ticket / 2.5 years (basal adult rate)
        │
Last ticket → cells can't replace wear → organism fails
```

| Claim | Number |
|---|---|
| Theoretical human max (telomere math) | **~125 years** |
| Independent demography convergence | **~125 years** |
| What accelerates loss | Inflammation, toxins, excess cell turnover, genetics |

**Footer:** Perfect lifestyle still faces basal shortening — tickets are spent whenever cells divide.

## ⚖️ Infographic 3: "The Telomere Tug-of-War"

**Title:** Shorteners vs Telomerase

```
SHORTENERS (left)              LENGTHENER (right)
  Sugar / inflammation            Telomerase enzyme
  Heavy metals / toxins          (normally germline only)
  Parasites / infections         Nutraceutical induction (modest)
  Immunogenic / toxic hits       Gene therapy (potent)
  Excess cell turnover
```

**Strategy ladder:**
1. Remove shortener players (terrain, diet, detox, infection)
2. Add telomerase induction (Telovital-class)
3. Gene therapy when disease severity justifies risk/cost

## 🔬 Infographic 4: "Why Average Telomere Tests Lie"

**Title:** Longer Average ≠ Lengthened Telomeres

| Trick | What happens | Company IQ analogy |
|---|---|---|
| Toxic hit | Kills short-telomere blood cells | Fire lowest IQ → average IQ ↑ |
| Immunogenic hit | Recruits long-telomere marrow cells | Hire geniuses → average IQ ↑ |
| True lengthening | Telomerase extends chromosome tips | Everyone actually gets smarter |

**Rule:** No telomerase expression data → no lengthening claim. Prefer full **distribution** + % critically short (Life Length).
