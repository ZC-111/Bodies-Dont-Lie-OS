# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-07-29

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Sexual health is a whole-health vital sign** — desire, arousal, orgasm, and pain-free intimacy reflect biochemistry, meds, mood, sleep, and relationships — not “partner duty.”

2. **Ask like you ask about smoking.** Normalize sexual ROS with “I ask all my patients…”; brief 5-question screens beat awkward silence.

3. **ED → snore screen.** Sleep apnea is a top reversible male sexual dysfunction driver; prefer real-world home sleep studies when possible.

4. **SSRIs steal libido — counsel first.** Statins, beta-blockers, and H2 blockers are also under-warned offenders; bupropion is often the mood med that spares desire.

5. **Vaginal estradiol is foundational.** Nightly × 2 weeks → 2×/week lifelong for GSM/recurrent UTI; black-box removal discussed; possible CV mortality benefit via infection/inflammation pathway.

6. **Molecule matters.** WHI-era risk after 60 used oral CEE + MPA. Transdermal estradiol ± real progesterone is not that regimen — and literature often conflates progestin with progesterone.

7. **Female testosterone is physiologic.** 2.5–5 mg/day targeting ~40–70 supports vitality, clarity, muscle, and vessels — not male-range bodybuilding levels.

8. **TPO antibodies are actionable.** Vitamin D to ~80–100, plant sterols, LDN, iodine (if low), selenium, and black cumin seed — Clearfield’s ladder — beat “nothing you can do.”

9. **Cancer and disability don’t erase sexuality.** Vaginal estrogen during cancer care, body-image counseling, and HRT for tissue/vitality in paraplegia are whole-person care — not optional luxuries.

10. **Patch rash hack:** Flonase on skin 30 minutes before estradiol patch often stops adhesive dermatitis (patient-taught pearl).

## Snapshot: Sexual Health Intervention Layers

| Layer | Tools | When it shines |
|---|---|---|
| **Lifestyle** | Strength training, sleep, quit smoking, alcohol moderation, stress boundaries | First-line for nearly everyone |
| **Relationship / psych** | Couples counseling, sex therapy, scheduled connection time | Empty nest, mismatch, trauma, cancer body image |
| **Local genital care** | Vaginal estradiol, lubricants | GSM, dyspareunia, rUTI |
| **Systemic hormones** | Transdermal E2 ± progesterone, female T, DHEA | Peri/postmenopause, vitality, MSK/brain symptoms |
| **Performance aids** | PDE5i, peptides (fertility-sparing contexts) | After CV screen; don’t skip partner tissue health |
| **Metabolic / thyroid** | Vit D, IR/leptin workup, Hashimoto stack | Hidden drivers of low desire & energy |

**Consensus from the hour:** Hormones help, but sexuality is not a prescription reflex — screen, destigmatize, and treat the whole stack.
