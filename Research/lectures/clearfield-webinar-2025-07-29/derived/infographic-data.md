## 💊 Infographic 2: "Meds That Quiet Desire (Under-Counseled)"

**Title:** The Prescription Stack Nobody Warns About

| Class | Examples / notes |
|---|---|
| SSRIs / SNRIs | Major libido hit — counsel *before* start |
| Statins | Extremely common; ask about sexual side effects |
| Beta-blockers | Engorgement / BP effects (propranolol vignette) |
| Diuretics / opioids | Vascular + CNS dampening |
| Antiepileptics | Synaptic / language / desire effects |
| H2 blockers | Mechanism unclear; still flagged |
| Chemo agents | Neuropathy + systemic toxicity |
| Antipsychotics / benzos | Sedation + desire loss |

**Callout:** Consider **bupropion** when an antidepressant is needed and libido matters.

## ⚖️ Infographic 5: "HRT Risk — Read the Fine Print"

**Title:** Why Trial Headlines Mislead Integrative Prescribers

```
WHI-era / age>60 risk signal
  └── Often oral CEE + MPA (progestin)

NOT THE SAME AS
  └── Transdermal estradiol ± micronized progesterone
  └── Vaginal estradiol alone
```

| Form | Relative CV/VTE framing here |
|---|---|
| Oral CEE + MPA | Highest concern in cited literature |
| Transdermal estradiol | Preferred systemic route |
| Vaginal estradiol | Local; systemic CV mortality benefit discussed |

**Rule:** Progestin ≠ progesterone. Always check the molecule.

## 🦋 Infographic 7: "TPO Antibodies — Clearfield Ladder"

**Title:** Don’t Say “Nothing You Can Do”

| Step | Intervention | Claimed antibody effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Optimize free T3 (~3.4–4.2) & TSH (~0.8–2.0) | Foundation |
| 2 | Vitamin D → serum ~80–100 | Antibodies often fall |
| 3 | Plant sterols (e.g. Moducare BID) | ~50% ↓ / year |
| 4 | LDN 0–3 mg | Improves function; watch thyroid dose |
| 5 | Iodine if deficient (~60–65% low) | Replace thoughtfully |
| 6 | Selenium 200 mcg BID | ~40% ↓ |
| 7 | Black cumin seed oil | +15–20% ↓ |

**Footer:** Check antibodies once on everyone — ~1/3 new patients positive in Clearfield’s locale.

## 🧂 Infographic 8: "Salt Tip — Celtic vs Himalayan"

**Title:** Functional Kitchen Minerals

| | Celtic | Himalayan |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Brittany salt flats (ocean) | Ancient rock deposits (Pakistan) |
| Texture | Moist, gray | Dry, pink, crystalline |
| Edge | Higher Mg / K / Ca | Lower ocean-pollutant concern |
| Watch-out | Microplastics reports | Source purity varies |

**Takeaway:** Third-party tested + moderation. Icelandic salt cited as low-contamination alternative.
