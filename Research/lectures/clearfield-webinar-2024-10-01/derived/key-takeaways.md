# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-10-01

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Menopause removed fertility to save lives — not hormones.** Restore physiologic bioidentical estradiol/progesterone; don’t invent non-human or non-physiologic regimens (Premarin/MPA trauma ≠ estradiol/progesterone).

2. **Target estradiol ~100 pg/mL (at least >50).** Ultra-low “safe” doses can be pro-inflammatory and fail to maintain vagina, brain, vessels, immune tone, and growth-factor repair.

3. **Stop saying “estrogen dominance.”** Name the molecule (E1/E2/E3), the source (ovary vs adipose aromatase), and whether progesterone is missing.

4. **BiEst is not physiologic HRT.** Estriol’s beta bias downregulates alpha pathways needed for CV, bone, and innate immunity — any hormones > none, but ovaries didn’t make BiEst.

5. **Nightly progesterone + tiny E2 is illogical.** Progesterone downregulates estrogen receptors; continuous use risks tachyphylaxis and blunts restorative growth-factor signaling.

6. **If you grow it, you must shed it.** Physiologic E2 grows endometrium — use ~200 mg progesterone × ~14 days/cycle (individualize) or consider elective endometrial ablation when childbearing is done.

7. **Prefer transdermal E2; avoid pellets and E2 troches.** Measure levels (AM trough / day-after patch). Compounded cream usually BID. Oral E2 ≈ higher clot risk and more estrone.

8. **Vaginal bladder care: estradiol > estriol** (alpha-rich bladder); estriol still OK for atrophy; vaginal DHEA is a useful adjunct.

9. **GLP-1s work better with adequate estradiol.** Optimize hormones before expecting max peptide weight-loss effect; natural GLP-1 support = fiber + exercise + E2.

10. **No arbitrary 10-year HRT stop.** Continuously renewing cells still need hormones, micronutrients, sleep, movement, and toxin reduction — same logic as not “aging out” of thyroid hormone.
