## 🎯 Infographic 1: "The Estradiol Target Zone"

**Title:** Ballpark Medicine — Aim Physiologic, Not Tiny

| Serum E2 | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **< 50 pg/mL** | Subtherapeutic — weak tissue support; more inflammatory tone | Raise dose / check absorption |
| **~50–80** | Better than nothing; may still undershoot systemic goals | Titrate upward if tolerated |
| **~100** | ★ Gersh ballpark target | Maintain; individualize |
| **~150–180** | Often acceptable if patient thriving | OK if asymptomatic / labs fit |
| **600–800** | Wiley-range extremes Gersh has measured | Not routine; high-risk experiment |

**Footer:** Luteal-phase E2 can naturally exceed 200. Vagina often fails below 50 systemic.

## 🧬 Infographic 2: "Know Your E — The Estrogen Family"

**Title:** Stop Saying “Estrogen.” Name the Molecule.

```
E2 ESTRADIOL  → Balanced α + β + GPER → Master HRT substrate
E1 ESTRONE    → α-biased → Inflammatory “ON” when overproduced in fat
E3 ESTRIOL    → ~80% β → Pregnancy immune modulator (not default HRT)
E4 ESTETROL   → Weak; may spare breast-cancer α isoform → future niche
```

**Callout:** “Estrogen dominance” is not a diagnosis — specify subtype, source, and progesterone status.

## ⚖️ Infographic 3: "Alpha vs Beta — Why BiEst Misses"

**Title:** Don’t Micromanage Receptors Nature Already Balanced

| Domain | Receptor lean (as taught) | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Arteries, bone, innate immunity | More **α** | Needed — not “evil” |
| Cortex, GI, B cells | More **β** | Also needed |
| Hypothalamus appetite/sleep sensors | More **α** | Metabolic control |
| Master clock | More **β** | Circadian blend |
| Pregnancy (high E3) | β↑ → α↓ | Fine for fetus; wrong for menopause HRT |

**Footer:** BiEst ≠ pregnancy ≠ ovaries. Replicate reproductive-age estradiol, not a concocted mix.

## 🔄 Infographic 4: "If You Grow It, You Must Shed It"

**Title:** Physiologic Dose Comes as a Package Deal

```
OPTIMAL E2
   │
   ├── Brain / vessels / muscle / skin / mito / immune  ✅ growth & repair
   │
   └── Endometrium  ✅ also grows
            │
            ├── Continuous tiny E2 + nightly P4  → brakes on receptors, suboptimal benefit
            ├── Escalating daily P4              → hormonal havoc, breakthrough bleeding
            └── Cyclic P4 ~14 days @ ~200 mg     → shed + reboot receptors
                     or elective ablation        → little/no bleeding, keep physiologic E2
```

**Title:** Mimic Nature’s Delivery When You Can

| — | **Troches** for E2 (swallowed ≈ oral) |
| — | **Oral E2** routine (~2× clot risk; → E1) |

## 📉 Infographic 6: "The Estrone Spiral of Untreated Menopause"

**Title:** Why “Doing Nothing” Is Not Neutral

```
↓ Estradiol
   → Visceral fat + dysbiosis + leaky gut
      → Systemic inflammation
         → ↑ Aromatase in adipose / breast fat
            → ↑ Estrone (E1)
               → Innate α activation → MORE inflammation
                  → Metabolic disease + ER+ tumor fuel risk
```

**Break the spiral:** Physiologic E2 + lifestyle + gut/liver support (± FMD after prep).

## ⏱️ Infographic 7: "Rhythm Beats Static"

**Title:** Why Cyclic Progesterone Still Matters After “Periods Are Optional”

1. **Receptor reboot** — continuous P4 → tachyphylaxis of estrogen receptors  
2. **Bone signal** — rhythmic P4 associated with better bone outcomes  
3. **Heart signal** — cited study: 14-day cyclic P4 → ↓ myocardial infarction  
4. **Tumor-suppressor timing** — rising E2/P4 pulses up/down-regulate receptor networks  
5. **No data** for arbitrary “one day off per week”

**Simple start:** E2 steady (or mild advanced cycling) + P4 **2 weeks on / 2 weeks off**.

## 🚨 Infographic 8: "Clinical Guardrails"

**Title:** Five Non-Negotiables from the Night

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Measure levels | AM trough / day-after patch — don’t chase post-application spikes |
| Start smart | Recent menopause: 0.1 patch / 2 pumps gel / Divigel 1.25; older/inflamed: lower + lifestyle first |
| Don’t add T blindly | Early menopause facial hair may be LH↑ + SHBG↓ — check total T + SHBG |
| No 10-year cliff | Cells keep renewing; hormones aren’t a countdown timer |
| Uterus present | E2 needs a progesterone plan (or ablation strategy) — not “estrogen only” |
