## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Psilocybin Pharmacology & Clinical Framing (12:52–17:34)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 2)

### Core Framing
> Counsel patients with pharmacology literacy — not advocacy. Outside regulated settings, variability remains high.

### Mechanism
- **Psilocybin** = prodrug → **psilocin** (5-HT2A agonist; tryptamine related to serotonin)
- Effects: cortical glutamate release ↑, neural entropy ↑, **default-mode network (DMN) disruption**, global connectivity ↑
- Neuroplasticity window lasts **days to weeks** post-dose (dendritic spine growth longer-lasting than ketamine)
- Preclinical: ↑ BDNF, synaptogenesis, dendritic spines → psychological flexibility

### Evidence Highlights (as presented)
| Indication | Signal cited |
|---|---|
| MDD (Johns Hopkins 2020 RCT) | Rapid GRID-HAM-D reduction within ~1 week; benefit at 4 weeks |
| Treatment-resistant depression | COMPASS Pathways Phase 3 program; effect size comparable/superior to escitalopram on some measures |
| Cancer-related distress (Hopkins / NYU) | Reduced distress; effects ~6 months in subsets |
| Addiction | Macrodose(s) + psychotherapy as catalyst |

### Safety & Legal Snapshot
- Screened populations: physiologically relatively safe; adverse effects mostly transient anxiety / BP elevation / psychological destabilization
- **Contraindications:** bipolar, psychotic disorders, unstable CVD, active serotonergic polypharmacy (SSRI risk framing)
- **Access:** Oregon regulated service program; Colorado natural-medicine program; Australia psychiatrist prescribing for TRD since 2023; Canada Section 56 exemptions
- US federal status remains **Schedule I** (since 1970)

### Clinical Pearl
Psilocybin = high-intensity, low-frequency **network disruptor / emotional-processing catalyst**, not a conventional daily antidepressant.

## Segment 4 — PCOS Diagnosis, Rotterdam Criteria & Conventional Pitfalls (40:20–1:09:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield, Victor Klausner

### Rotterdam Criteria (as taught; “reformulated 2023” framing)
Need **≥2 of 3**:
1. **Ovulatory dysfunction** — oligo-/anovulation, irregular or absent cycles
2. **Androgen excess** — hirsutism, acne (face/neck/shoulders), sometimes BP rise, edema, neuropathy sensations
3. **Polycystic ovaries** — strict follicle count framing (~**12** follicles / “string of pearls”; ultrasound)

### Androgen Workup
- Total + free testosterone, SHBG, DHEA-S (not only DHEA); DHT serum often poorly correlating
- **Free Androgen Index (FAI):** total T ÷ (0.288 × SHBG)
  - Females normal ~0.9–6.0; **optimal ~2–4%**
  - Males reference ranges discussed separately (~30–130; optimal ~50–115)

### Downstream Cascade Taught
Androgen excess → aromatization → estrogen dominance → insulin resistance, dysbiosis, cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, IL-17, IFN-γ), visceral fat, fertility loss, paradoxical bone thinning at extremes, mood/cognitive issues (executive dysfunction framed as GH-related in hormone teaching).

### Conventional Path (Serena age ~19)
- Endocrine: “atypical” PCOS with elevated T
- **OCPs** to suppress ovarian androgens + **spironolactone** (androgen-receptor blockade)
- Klausner pearl: she was **lean / non-classic phenotype** — no obvious IR or truncal obesity; easy to miss with textbook PCOS mental model

### Spironolactone Practical
- Start ~25 mg; often **50 mg** enough; can titrate toward 200 mg
- Blocks androgen side effects while preserving useful androgen biology in other contexts
- Improvement often in **2–4 weeks** (Clearfield) vs months (some chat)

### Why Clearfield Dislikes Long-Term OCPs for PCOS Root Work
- Mask ovulatory dysfunction / kick the can
- Nutrient depletion (B vitamins / methylation), dysbiosis
- Can **worsen insulin resistance** and central adiposity
- VTE / lipids / BP / migraine / smoker / age >35 / clot history cautions
- Ethinyl estradiol = potent hepatic first-pass; synthetic progestins vary androgenic ↔ anti-androgenic
- Bioidentical hormones **not reliable contraception** — still need a plan if pregnancy prevention required

### HOMA-IR Formula (Clearfield)
\[
\text{HOMA-IR} = \frac{\text{fasting glucose} \times \text{fasting insulin}}{405}
\]
- Normal **< 2.9**; optimal **< 1.9** — track every visit

### Integrative Insulin / Androgen Tools Cited
| Tool | Notes |
|---|---|
| Berberine | ~400–500 mg × 2, twice daily → ~1% A1c drop in ~6 months (metformin-comparable claim); improves lipids |
| Berberine + **trans**-resveratrol 100 mg | Weight-regain prevention post–GLP-1 / weight-loss programs; take with yogurt; prefer **trans** (~95%) over cis |
| Cinnamon (Ceylon preferred; store-bought used) | Insulin sensitizing |
| Chromium picolinate | Chat / adjunct |
| Metformin | Standard of care but many PCOS patients refuse (GI / lactic acidosis / mito framing from panel) |

## Segment 5 — Evolution to Thyroiditis + Lab Hierarchy (1:09:00–1:22:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield, panel chat

### Clinical Timeline (ages 21–24 → present at 25)
- Fatigue, brain fog, unrefreshing sleep
- Cyclical **ocular migraines** (worse follicular; better luteal)
- Hair thinning, brittle nails → classic thyroid dysfunction signals
- Stopped OCPs Nov 2025 for side effects → **immediate amenorrhea** (HPO axis rebound shutoff)

### Exam Clues (Klausner)
- Asymmetric small breasts / poorly formed left nipple (androgen interference with breast development)
- Mild virilization; facial hair often plucked; hair on small of back / legs
- Residual spironolactone may suppress acne/facial hair appearance

### IFM-Style Hierarchy (Clearfield)
If forced to order: **adrenals → thyroid → sex hormones** (in practice often concurrent).

### Full Thyroid + Nutrient Panel Emphasized
TSH, free T3, free T4, reverse T3, TPO Ab, anti-thyroglobulin Ab, spot urine iodine (± 24h if abnormal), B12, folate, vitamin D, **ferritin**.

### Spot Urine Iodine Interpretation Pearl
- Rough normal band cited ~**100–199**
- Low (<100): consider iodine even with autoimmunity (careful protocol)
- Very high on supplementation (thousands) ≠ automatic overdose — usually means patient is **taking iodine**; confirm with 24h if extreme

### Reverse T3 as Stress Proxy
- rT3 **> ~15** often symptomatic
- Pattern: AM fatigue → mid-morning crash → afternoon nap need → evening second wind → insomnia + salt/sugar craving

### PCOS–Hashimoto Overlap Stats (as presented)
- General population dual rates vary by region/iodine; women with PCOS **~3–4×** more likely to have Hashimoto’s (**OR ~3.27**)
- Shared drivers: chronic inflammation, insulin resistance, gut permeability, immune dysregulation
- Up to ~**25%** of females under 25 estimated to have some PCOS-spectrum symptoms (speaker claim)

## Segment 6 — Why T4→T3 Fails & Seven Root Causes of Thyroiditis (1:22:00–1:49:00)

**Speaker:** Bill Clearfield

### Conventional Model Critiqued
“Failure to convert T4→T3” (± nutrient malabsorption) → TSH (± reflex T4) → **levothyroxine only**; “diet doesn’t matter.”

### Drivers of Poor Conversion / Interference
- Advanced age; **raw goitrogens** (cruciferous, spinach, strawberries, pears, some nuts) — cook rather than eliminate; limit ~3–6 servings/week cooked
- Thyroid meds **alone**, few sips water; **1 hour** empty stomach before/after; no coffee/tea/juice; consistent clock time (nighttime void dosing trick)
- T3 (liothyronine) half-life ~**3–4 h** → dose ≥ q6h (BID–TID)
- OCPs, chemo/radiation, toxins, extreme exercise, inflammation, low iron / GH / testosterone
- Drug interferers: **amiodarone**, beta blockers, fentanyl, Dilantin, prednisone, lithium, **synthetic** progestins

### Seven Root Causes (Clearfield checklist)
1. Immune dysregulation  
2. GI tract / permeability  
3. Heavy metals / toxins / receptor blockers  
4. Infections — **EBV #1**, **H. pylori #2** (~15% thyroid Ab improvement after Rx cited), hepatitis, herpes, Lyme, Yersinia  
5. HPA axis / cortisol  
6. Iodine–halide story (I / F / Br)  
7. Medications + **molecular mimicry** (gluten / casein ↔ thyroid; IL-6/15/17/22/23 cytokine homework)

### Integrative Diagnostics Beyond TSH
- GI-MAP (± Genova); SIBO if stuck
- Micronutrients / Dutch OAT; hair metals screen (Reno uranium endemic note)
- CRP, full hormone panel, spot or 24h urinary iodines/halides

### Serena Labs Highlighted
| Marker | Value / note | Target framing |
|---|---|---|
| A1c | 5.4 | Not IR phenotype |
| Insulin | 4.6 | Low-normal IR picture |
| Vitamin D | 56 | Midpoint ~65; goal often 50–80 |
| **Ferritin** | **9** | Goal **90–110**; iron deficiency alone → fatigue, brain fog, hair loss, failed T4→T3 |
| Total T | **98** | Sweet spot ~**30–40** |
| DHEA-S | 265 | Adequate ~200–250 |
| TSH | 3.1 | “Normal” lab; integrative goal **0.8–2.0** |
| Free T3 / T4 | Low-mid / low-normal | Goal fT3 ~3.4–4.2 |
| TPO / anti-TG | ~4–5× upper limit (Quest vs LabCorp scales differ!) | Jump on **any** Ab positivity early |

### Antibody Scale Caution
LabCorp vs Quest cutoffs differ (e.g., TPO normal ~34 vs ~9). Chart which lab — switching labs can fake “improvement.”

### Five Phases of Hashimoto’s (Clearfield)
1. Genetic predisposition + triggers (EBV, gluten); normal labs → **unexplained anxiety** first symptom  
2. Immune infiltration; **Abs+**, thyroid function “normal” (TSH 2–3) — most miss this  
3. Overt hypo — high TSH, low/normal fT3/fT4  
4. Broader autoimmunity (GI, dry eyes / Sjögren-type)  
5. Burnout — non-functional thyroid (lymphocytic scarring irreversible)

**Teaching push:** Phase 2 is where integrative care can still reverse trajectory.

## Segment 7 — Integrative Protocol, Nutrients, LDN & PCOS Stack (1:49:00–2:38:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield, Melvin Nario, Jeanette Serna (Speaker 3), panel

### Protocol Order (practical)
Gut → nutrients → adrenals → detox → infections → halides (often overlapping; symptom-prioritize). IFM sometimes adrenals-first.

### Eliminate Triggers
Gluten, dairy, soy, corn, sugar — primary gut-permeability / molecular-mimicry drivers.

### 4R Gut Healing
Remove (caffeine/alcohol/bad actors) → Replace / Repair (whole foods, omega-3 oily fish, aloe, turmeric if tolerated, evening primrose, **L-glutamine**) → Reinoculate (kombucha, plain yogurt; kimchi optional) → enzymes/bile salts.  
Product callout: Ortho Molecular **Glutamine Shield** (L-glutamine + DGL + aloe).  
Budwig snack: cottage cheese + olive/coconut oil ± chia.

### Nutrient Repletion Targets
- **Ferritin 90–110** (consider IV iron if severely symptomatic at 9)
- Selenium + iodine for thyroid molecule & Ab reduction
- RBC zinc & magnesium (not serum)
- Methylated B12/folate if MCV >97–98 (big empty cells); MCV <80 → iron
- Vitamin D **50–80**; rule of thumb ~**+8 ng/mL per 1,000 IU D3**
- MCH 27–33; outside range often iron story

### Immune Modulation Stack
1. **First-line plant sterolins** — Moducare / “Maia care” branding; CholestOff same sterol concept; **Wobenzym N** (~1/3 cost) — Ab drops up to ~90% claimed (case: TPO 4226 → 101)
2. **Low-dose naltrexone (LDN)** — second-line immune modulator  
   - Blocks opioid receptors ~6–8 h → rebound β-endorphin (natural anti-inflammatory)  
   - Side effects: insomnia ~10%, transient nausea, vivid dreams/nightmares ~20%  
   - Nario: LDN at night, thyroid AM; daytime LDN if vivid dreams  
   - Separate thyroid from all other meds/supplements at dosing time
3. Thymosin alpha-1 peptide (immune + hair note)
4. Liposomal glutathione (detox; Parkinson tremor/rigidity short-lived 48–72 h; IV preferred in practice talk)

### PCOS Androgen / Metabolic Adjuncts
- **Spearmint tea** — favorite 5α-reductase tool; ~**20% total T drop in <1 month**; need **~100 mg spearmint** → **double tea bag** 4–5 days/week
- Saw palmetto half male dose (~240–260 mg QD–BID) — 5ARI ± weak aromatase effect
- NAC — e.g., 900 mg tabs → 1800 mg; or IV 1200–1800 mg over ~30 min 1–2×/week × 4–6 weeks
- Alpha-lipoic acid with berberine/cinnamon stacks; neuropathy support
- **Myo-/chiro-inositol 40:1** — restore cycles in functional anovulation/oligo
- Progesterone options debated: continuous low-dose oral (~50 mg, calming/myelinating, BBB) vs cyclic 200 mg × 10 days; topical weaker for anxiety CNS effect (Serna)

### Lithium Orotate Caution
Chat suggestion 10–20 mg for mood — Clearfield: even orotate can impair T4→T3; Serna notes environmental lithium / RO water depletion map.

### 90-Day Roadmap (backbone)
| Window | Focus |
|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Adrenal/thyroid eval; begin E/P balancing; start myo-inositol for anovulation |
| Days 31–60 | Nutrient repletion (B12, folate, D, ferritin) |
| Days 61–90 | Strict gut healing / 4R elimination |
| ~Day 100 | Reassess and iterate |

### Subjective Success Markers
Brain fog lifts; hair/nails/eyebrow edges thicken; menses return **without** OCPs.

### Objective Targets Recap
- TSH 0.8–2.0  
- Abs: LabCorp <20 / Quest <9 framing  
- Ferritin 90–110  
- FAI 2–4; total T <40 (≈30 sweet spot)  
- If Abs stay high → hunt infections (EBV: Teitelbaum-style **celecoxib 200 mg + acyclovir 400 mg BID × 28 days** cited; H. pylori can reverse some thyroiditis)

### Advanced Testing Menu
24h urinary halides; functional stool (dysbiosis, enzymes, calprotectin); SpectraCell micronutrients via **Rupa Health**; OAT (hard to interpret).
