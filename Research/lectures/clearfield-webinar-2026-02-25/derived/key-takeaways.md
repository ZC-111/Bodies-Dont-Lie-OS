# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-02-25

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Psilocybin literacy ≠ advocacy** — counsel on 5-HT2A / DMN network effects, TRD/MDD signals, and hard contraindications (psychosis/bipolar, unstable CVD, serotonergic polypharmacy); regulated-state programs only.

2. **Lean PCOS is still PCOS** — Serena lacked textbook IR/obesity; hyperandrogenism + cycle dysfunction + later Hashimoto overlap still fit.

3. **Rotterdam = ≥2 of 3** — ovulatory dysfunction, androgen excess, PCO morphology (~12-follicle / string-of-pearls framing).

4. **FAI and HOMA-IR are clinic-fast ratios** — FAI = T ÷ (0.288 × SHBG), optimal ~2–4%; HOMA = (glucose × insulin)/405, optimal <1.9.

5. **OCPs + spironolactone treat cosmetics, not roots** — OCPs can worsen IR/central fat and deplete B vitamins; spiro ~50 mg often enough for receptor blockade.

6. **Ferritin 9 is an emergency for thyroid biology** — goal 90–110; iron deficiency alone drives fatigue, hair loss, brain fog, and failed T4→T3.

7. **TSH 3.1 with high Abs is not “reassurance”** — integrative TSH goal 0.8–2.0; chart Quest vs LabCorp Ab scales; jump on Phase 2 Hashimoto’s before scarring.

8. **Seven root causes beat levothyroxine-only** — gut, nutrients, metals/toxins, infections (EBV, H. pylori), HPA, halides, meds/mimicry; cook goitrogens; thyroid med alone on empty stomach.

9. **Immune stack: plant sterolins first, LDN second** — separate LDN from thyroid dosing; spearmint double-bag tea can drop total T ~20% in under a month.

10. **Fix trauma/HPA or hormones won’t stick** — PTSD/panic hijack the axis; 40:1 myo/chiro-inositol and thoughtful progesterone help restart cycles without reflex OCPs.
