## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Scars can run the show.** Wrist surgical scars and post-tonsillectomy fossae may act as neural-therapy interference fields — test with lidocaine; ≥50% relief in under a minute implicates the scar.

2. **Continuous migraine + Mirena + missing progesterone** is a hormone-terrain problem until proven otherwise. Progesterone (oral/topical) and considering IUD removal ranked high for Emily.

3. **Hyperinsulinemia is not “treat with mealtime insulin first.”** Fasting insulin ~25, CGM spikes to ~280, and reactive hypos → keto/low-GL diet and IR work before exogenous insulin.

4. **Trust CGM over a reassuring lab A1c.** Lab A1c 5.7 vs CGM-projected ~6.3 — serum can average away the danger.

5. **Ferritin 25 and vitamin D 25** are still low-terrain markers even when “better than before.” Recheck iron stores; don’t declare victory early.

6. **High ANA + negative hsCRP** does not equal “nothing autoimmune.” Family lupus/MS/Hashimoto’s + Raynaud’s = watch and treat terrain, not wait for a textbook label.

7. **High pregnenolone (437) without progesterone on the panel** suggests a conversion / progestin-block story — order progesterone and clear the IUD for interpretable cycles.

8. **Mainstream endo CE still mocks reverse T3 and iodine.** Integrative practices that use rT3 as a cortisol proxy and iodine challenge testing are culturally invisible in pharma-vendor conferences.

9. **Gut transit is migraine medicine.** Vegetable avoidance → Mg/C deficiency + slow bowels → “autotoxicity” framing; probiotics, vitamin C, magnesium, and daily elimination are foundational.

10. **Sequence before you escalate.** Neural therapy + hormones + diet/gut first; GlycoCheck/fucoidan, SVF, and therapeutic plasma exchange are later/resource-dependent layers.
