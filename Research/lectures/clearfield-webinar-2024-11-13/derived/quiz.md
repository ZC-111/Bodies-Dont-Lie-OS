## Section A — Early Detection & Risk Framework (6 questions)

### Q1. What clinical window does Mitchell emphasize as the highest-yield target for cognitive intervention?
- A) Only after Alzheimer’s diagnosis
- B) Only after MCI diagnosis
- C) **The preclinical stage before MCI/AD diagnoses** ✓
- D) Only symptomatic late dementia

> *AD ~10% and MCI ~12–18% of people >65 — decline starts earlier. Focus on mitochondrial dysfunction as an early signal.*

### Q2. Which blood biomarker panel did Mitchell highlight as an FDA breakthrough device with up to ~91% accuracy for Alzheimer’s diagnosis?
- A) ApoE genotyping alone
- B) **PrecivityAD2 (Aβ42/40 + p-tau217)** ✓
- C) Homocysteine only
- D) Standard lipid panel

### Q3. Which mitochondrial stress marker available via commercial labs (e.g., LabCorp) did Mitchell cite as elevated in long COVID, diabetes, metabolic syndrome, and cognitive decline?
**Answer:** **GDF-15** (a mitokine).

### Q4. Between MitoScreen (finger-prick blood card) and MitoSWAB (buccal swab), which does Mitchell prefer for mitochondrial function testing, and why?
**Answer:** **MitoScreen** — more reliable sample integrity. Buccal cells for MitoSWAB are fragile and must be shipped quickly, making results “iffier.”

### Q5. Which newly emphasized Lancet Commission dementia risk factor accounts for roughly 7% of risk attribution and is often under-addressed clinically?
- A) Obesity
- B) **Hearing impairment** ✓
- C) Smoking
- D) Hypertension

### Q6. True or False: Mitchell argues amyloid pathology is always the primary root cause of Alzheimer’s rather than a stress response.
**Answer:** **False.** She presents the hypothesis that amyloid/tau may be immune stress responses (red herrings), with mitochondrial dysfunction closer to a root driver.

## Section B — Omega-3s, B Vitamins & Metabolic Therapies (8 questions)

### Q7. Per the meta-analysis Mitchell cites, which combination of conditions best predicts cognitive benefit from omega-3 intervention?
- A) 4 weeks + 100 mg DHA only
- B) **≥12 months + ≥500 mg omega-3s with EPA up to ~420 mg** ✓
- C) Any dose of ALA plant oil for 6 weeks
- D) Omega-6–dominant oils for 12 months

### Q8. Why does Mitchell prefer phospholipid-form EPA/DHA (or krill) over standard triglyceride fish-oil capsules for brain delivery?
**Answer:** Her mouse work showed phospholipid forms enter the adult brain far better than TG forms. Commercial fish-oil extraction strips phospholipids; pairing TG fish oil 1:1 with any phospholipid (lecithin, phosphatidylserine) can rescue uptake.

### Q9. What approximate EPA:DHA split did Mitchell suggest as a practical ~1 g omega-3 product for cognition/mood?
- A) 900 mg DHA + 100 mg EPA
- B) **~600 mg EPA + ~400 mg DHA** ✓
- C) ALA only
- D) 100% C15 fatty acid

### Q10. Which B-vitamin polymorphism did Mitchell call out as reducing folate availability by >20% and correlating with depression/cognitive decline?
- A) ApoE4
- B) **MTHFR** ✓
- C) CYP2D6
- D) COMT Val/Val only

### Q11. What magnitude of weight loss in Mitchell’s Noom-related study was enough to improve cognition and EEG error detection?
- A) 20%
- B) 15%
- C) **5%** ✓
- D) No effect until BMI < 22

### Q12. What key nutrient depletion should be monitored when using metformin for metabolic/cognitive support?
**Answer:** **Vitamin B12** (and broader B-vitamin status).

### Q13. Rank Mitchell’s preference among MCT chain lengths for cognitive/mood benefit:
- A) C12 > C8 > C10
- B) **C10 > C8 > C12** ✓
- C) C8 only; C10 harmful
- D) All equivalent

### Q14. What daily MCT dose range did Mitchell recommend, and what practical caveat applies?
**Answer:** **15–20 g/day**, split across the day to reduce GI upset. Prefer MCTs over exogenous ketone salts/esters for tolerability.

## Section C — Menopause, Pollution & Multimodal Care (4 questions)

### Q15. Why might severe hot flashes/night sweats signal future dementia risk, according to Mitchell’s metabolic framing?
**Answer:** Estrogen loss impairs brain glucose metabolism and pushes β-fatty-acid oxidation; if fuel is insufficient, the brain may catabolize white matter — linking vasomotor symptoms to later cognitive vulnerability.

### Q16. For women who decline HRT, which non-hormonal option did Mitchell call her strongest clinical pick for severe vasomotor symptoms?
- A) Testosterone monotherapy
- B) **Equol 10 mg for at least 8 weeks** ✓
- C) High-dose soy protein alone without equol
- D) Aricept

> *Equol: high ER-β potency and brain bioavailability. PhytoSERM (genistein + daidzein + equol) also promising but not yet commercial.*

### Q17. What timing window for estrogen-only HRT did Mitchell cite (via Lisa Moscone’s work) as more favorable for cognitive/risk benefit?
**Answer:** Within approximately the **first 5 years** after menopausal transition — earlier initiation outperforms late start.

### Q18. The FINGER study suggests roughly what fraction of dementia risk is modifiable through multimodal lifestyle (diet, exercise, community)?
- A) 5%
- B) 15%
- C) **~40%** ✓
- D) 90%

## Section D — Clinical Integration (2 questions)

### Q19. A 58-year-old with post-COVID brain fog, fatigue, and poor concentration asks for an evidence-aligned OTC starting stack based on this lecture. What do you recommend and how do you track?
**Answer (per Mitchell):**
1. Prioritize **NAC** (best OTC signal she cited for post-COVID fog); consider Mg, vitamin D, curcumin, quercetin, low-carb as adjuncts
2. Optimize omega-3s (phospholipid/krill or TG + phospholipid; EPA-forward ~1 g)
3. Screen mito stress (GDF-15 / MitoScreen), B vitamins/polymorphisms, metabolic status
4. Track symptoms with the **Fatigue and Altered Cognition Scale** over time
5. Address hearing, pollution exposure, sleep/exercise — multimodal, not single-pill

### Q20. A postmenopausal woman with severe hot flashes declines systemic estrogen. She also lives in a wildfire-smoke region and wants a mito-centered plan. Outline your approach using pearls from this webinar.
**Answer:**
1. **VMS:** Trial equol 10 mg × ≥8 weeks; consider DIY PhytoSERM components (genistein/daidzein/equol); address mood/fatigue symptomatically
2. **Brain fuel:** CGM education, aim for metabolic optimization; consider C10-enriched MCT 15–20 g/day if glucose utilization poor
3. **Omega-3 + phospholipids**; methylated B vitamins if MTHFR/MTR/CBS risk
4. **Pollution:** mitigate exposure; consider mito-targeted antioxidants (mitoquinol evidence in diesel/particulate models; resveratrol also cited)
5. **Lifestyle:** FINGER-style diet/exercise/community; hearing screen
6. Optional advanced testing: PrecivityAD2 / mito function assays if clinically appropriate
7. Avoid relying on Aricept alone if cognition worsens — always multimodal
