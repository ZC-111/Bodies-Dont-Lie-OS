## 🐟 Infographic 4: "Omega-3 Rules That Actually Move Cognition"

**Title:** Dose · Duration · Form · EPA Bias

| Rule | Target |
|---|---|
| Duration | ≥ **12 months** |
| Dose | ≥ **500 mg** omega-3s (up to **~1 g** practical) |
| EPA | Up to ~**420 mg** in positive meta-analysis arms |
| Form | **Phospholipid** (krill) ≫ TG fish oil alone |
| Hack | TG fish oil + **1:1 phospholipid** (lecithin / PS) |
| Example split | ~**600 EPA / 400 DHA** |

**Footer:** EPA resolves inflammation (mood). DHA builds membranes. Skip rancid oil — add E + D; consider B2.

## ⚡ Infographic 5: "MCT Chain-Length Ladder"

**Title:** Not All Medium-Chain Triglycerides Are Equal

```
C12 (lauric / coconut-dominant)
  └── Poor ketone yield · little anti-inflammatory brain effect

C8 (caprylic)
  └── Popular “Bulletproof-style” · decent ketones

C10 ★
  └── Best cognitive + mood signal in Mitchell’s aged-rat work
```

**Dose:** 15–20 g/day, split · easier than ketone salts/esters  
**Why MCTs:** alternate brain fuel + mito efficiency when glucose uptake fails

## 👩 Infographic 6: "Hot Flashes Are a Brain-Fuel Warning"

**Title:** Vasomotor Symptoms ↔ Dementia Risk

```
Estrogen ↓
  → Brain glucose metabolism ↓
  → β-fatty-acid oxidation ↑
  → If still fuel-starved → white-matter catabolism (animal data)
```

**Action ladder:**
1. HRT early (estrogen-only within ~**first 5 years** — Moscone framing)

## 🧩 Infographic 8: "Mitchell’s Efficiency Stack"

**Title:** Make Mitochondria Efficient — Don’t Floor the Accelerator

| Priority | Intervention | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mitoquinol (MitoQ)** | Enters mito/brain better than CoQ10 |
| 2 | **High-EPA oil + phospholipids** | Inflammation + membrane delivery |
| 3 | **NAC (± glycine)** | Precursor → GSH in tissues; “younger” mito markers in elders |
| Partner | **CoQ10 still useful** | Covers cytosol/lysosomes where MitoQ isn’t concentrated |
| Lifestyle | CR / IF / ketones / exercise | Efficiency · biogenesis · FINGER ~**40%** modifiable risk |

**Mindset:** Excess nutrients (obesity) make mitochondria haywire. Efficiency > max ATP.
