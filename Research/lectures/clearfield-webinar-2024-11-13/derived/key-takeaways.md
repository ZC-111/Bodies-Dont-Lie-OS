# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-11-13

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Intervene before MCI/AD labels.** Mitochondrial dysfunction can appear while amyloid biomarkers are still silent — treat the preclinical window.

2. **Add hearing and air pollution to every dementia-risk review.** Lancet Commission attributes ~7% of risk to hearing loss; PM2.5 tracks with amyloid burden.

3. **Phospholipid omega-3s beat standard fish oil for brain delivery.** Use krill or pair TG fish oil 1:1 with lecithin/PS; bias EPA (~600/400 EPA:DHA toward 1 g).

4. **EPA is the mood/inflammation workhorse; DHA is the membrane builder.** Depression often behaves like inflammatory “sickness behavior.”

5. **Test B-vitamin genetics (MTHFR, MTR, CBS)** — null cognition trials often ignore polymorphisms that change folate/homocysteine handling.

6. **Only 5% weight loss can improve cognition** (Noom/EEG signal). Use CGMs + early metformin (watch B12); GLP-1s have cognitive signals but malnutrition/muscle risks and no lifespan data yet.

7. **Prefer C10-enriched MCTs (15–20 g/day) over strict keto** when compliance is the bottleneck — C12 coconut oil is a weak cognitive tool.

8. **Severe hot flashes/night sweats are brain-fuel red flags.** Early estrogen (first ~5 years) or equol 10 mg (≥8 weeks) / PhytoSERM components if HRT declined.

9. **Post-COVID fog: lead with NAC**, track with the Fatigue and Altered Cognition Scale; mito injury after viral illness is not quickly self-resolving (−28% memory/attention; ~6 IQ points in large data).

10. **Mitochondrial goal = efficiency, not max ATP.** Mitoquinol + high-EPA phospholipids + NAC (± glycine); keep CoQ10 as a partner for non-mito compartments.
