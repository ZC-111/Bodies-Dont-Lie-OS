### ★ Core Thesis
> Calories-in/calories-out fails once muscle becomes **insulin resistant** — unused calories divert to **abdominal fat**, which then behaves like an endocrine organ.

### Four Stages (Strand framework)
| Stage | Clinical signals |
|---|---|
| **1** | Carbohydrate cravings; nighttime hunger; early BP creep (>120/80) |
| **2** | HDL falling (women prefer ≥50); **TG/HDL** rising; waist expanding (women ~32–34"; men ~36–39" at iliac crest) |
| **3** | Full metabolic syndrome / prediabetes — ≥3 of: central obesity, TG >150, low HDL, BP ↑, fasting glucose ↑ |
| **4** | Progression to **type 2 diabetes** (fasting glucose ≥126 or A1c diagnostic range) |

### TG/HDL Ratio Staging (Hollis summary after audio drop)
- Ratio **≥2** ≈ stage 2  
- Ratio **≥3** ≈ stage 3  
- Higher ratios → deeper IR → slower response to drops

### Clinical Pearl
> Stage 1 responders may lose weight in days; stages 3–4 may need **2–3+ weeks** of strict low-glycemic adherence before fat loss “unlocks.” Don’t quit early.
