# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-09-24

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **~50% of common drugs deplete nutrients.** Treat DIND as a third checklist item beside allergies and drug–drug interactions — especially in polypharmacy.

2. **Statins block CoQ10 synthesis** via HMG-CoA reductase. Use **crystal-free** CoQ10; grit between fingers = crystalline waste.

3. **Metformin → B12 (± folate).** Prefer **methylfolate / folinic**, not folic acid; ensure B6 + B12 for methylation.

4. **Acetaminophen depletes glutathione** — the mechanism behind overdose hepatotoxicity; think NAC/GSH support in chronic users.

5. **Tobacco / secondhand smoke are nutrient thieves.** Priority: **C, B6, Mg, Se, Zn, GSH/NAC**. Higher B6 ↔ less lung cancer; higher Mg ↔ less stroke in large cohorts Bailey cited.

6. **Functional testing beats isolated serum levels.** Normal B12 + ↑ MMA = still deficient. Bailey’s workhorse: **Genova metabolomics**.

7. **Thyroid labs 4–6 h post-dose** to capture free T3; co-order **vitamin D** (nuclear transport of thyroid hormone).

8. **Screen nitric oxide** with tongue strips — by age 60, output may be ~**15%** of youth. Bailey saw ~**15 mmHg** SBP drop when repleted.

9. **Protect the oral nitrate pathway:** minimize antiseptic mouthwash and aggressive tongue scraping; tongue-crypt anaerobes convert NO3 → NO2.

10. **Bailey’s lipid triad (non-statin lean):** fiber 20–30 g/day + **gamma-delta tocotrienols** + fish oil; panel also exploring NO for resistant lipids / plaque.
