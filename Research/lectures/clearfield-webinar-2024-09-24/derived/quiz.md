## Section B — Testing, CoQ10 & Thyroid Pearls (6 questions)

### Q9. What “poor man’s” CoQ10 quality check does Bailey recommend?
**Answer:** Cut open a liquid softgel, rub between fingers — **grittiness = crystals** (poor bioavailability). Prefer **crystal-free** CoQ10.

### Q10. What functional B12 pattern did Bailey describe?
- A) Low serum B12 with low MMA
- B) **Normal serum B12 with elevated methylmalonic acid = insufficient functional B12** ✓
- C) High serum B12 always means toxicity
- D) MMA is irrelevant if CBC is normal

### Q11. Which lab panel does Bailey prefer for nutrition assessment?
- A) Standard Chem-14 only
- B) Hair minerals exclusively
- C) **Genova metabolomics / Metabolomix-style functional testing** ✓
- D) Random urinary dipstick

### Q12. When should thyroid patients take their dose relative to lab draw, per Bailey?
**Answer:** **4–6 hours before** the blood draw — to capture free T3 while it is still measurable (T3 half-life ~4–6 h).

### Q13. Why check vitamin D with thyroid labs?
- A) Vitamin D lowers TSH directly
- B) **Vitamin D is needed to transport thyroid hormone from the cell to the nucleus** ✓
- C) Vitamin D replaces T4
- D) It only matters in Hashimoto’s

### Q14. Harlan’s pearl on cardiac MitoQ (via Mark Houston) is:
**Answer:** Cardiac-targeted **MitoQ** concentrates in heart muscle and may **not** cover systemic needs — pair with a separate **ubiquinol** for whole-body CoQ10.

## Section C — Nitric Oxide & Smoking Product (6 questions)

### Q15. At age ~60, endogenous nitric oxide production is roughly what percent of youthful output (per Bailey)?
- A) 50%
- B) 30%
- C) **15%** ✓
- D) 5%

### Q16. How does Bailey typically screen patients for nitric oxide status in clinic?
**Answer:** **Berkeley Life tongue test strips** (~10 seconds); if depleted, prescribe Berkeley Life (or equivalent) nitrate capsules and give an educational handout.

### Q17. True or False: Most commercial “nitric oxide” supplements contain nitric oxide gas in the capsule.
**Answer:** **False.** They supply **precursors** (nitrates → nitrites → NO). Quinn emphasized many products have **no NO** itself.

### Q18. What oral habits can impair dietary nitrate → nitrite conversion?
- A) Chewing gum only
- B) **Frequent mouthwash use and vigorous tongue scraping that destroy tongue-crypt anaerobes** ✓
- C) Drinking coffee
- D) Flossing

### Q19. Bailey’s three preferred non-statin tools for cholesterol/TG issues are:
**Answer:** (1) **High fiber 20–30 g/day**, (2) **gamma-delta tocotrienols** (block LDL oxidation), (3) **fish oil** for triglycerides.

### Q20. What is the clinical intent of Bailey’s patented smoking product?
**Answer:** Replenish smoke-depleted nutrients to **reduce morbidity/mortality**, improve biomarkers, often **decrease cigarettes smoked** and change smoke taste in ~2 weeks — **not** a guaranteed cessation drug; separate formulas for smokers, secondhand exposure, pregnant smokers, and recent quitters.
