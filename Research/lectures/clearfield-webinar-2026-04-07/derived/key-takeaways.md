# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-04-07

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **PT-141 is a central MC4 / dopamine arousal drug**, not a peripheral vasodilator — use it when desire/motivation fails, and rethink “ED” that is really reward-circuit failure.

2. **Teach the four sisters:** E1 ↔ E2 interconvert; **E3 is one-way downstream** and a practical local choice for vaginal atrophy.

3. **ERα vs ERβ matter clinically** — α leans proliferative (breast/uterus) and clotting; β leans immune/CNS/epithelial modulation.

4. **Berkson’s advocacy line:** large analyses + WHI re-reads frame estrogen as often **breast-protective**; fear culture from the 2002 press release still drives bad counseling.

5. **FSH is not irrelevant to HRT/breast biology** — mid-50s+ FSH may amplify Ki-67/HER2 signals in Berkson’s teaching; Clearfield/Berkson both pushing FSH literacy.

6. **Tano’s epidemiologic hook:** pesticide geography ≈ obesity/diabetes/CVD/cancer geography; CDC biomonitoring proves universal chemical body burden.

7. **Glyphosate/GMO era (~1994–96)** is his inflection point for obesity curves and youth estrogenic disease (early breast ca, teen PCOS, precocious puberty).

8. **2-OH vs 16α-OH estrone** remains a useful clinic fork — but **DIM/I3C can paradoxically worsen the 2:16 ratio**; always retest.

9. **Hot flashes ≠ automatic estrogen prescription** — progesterone (and detox) often suffice; if estrogen is needed, Tano’s rescue is low-dose **BiEst ~20/80**.

10. **Individualized medicine is the meta-lesson** (Nario): receptor-cover camp, hormone-protective camp, and xenoestrogen-minimal camp can all be “right” for different patients — outcomes over ideology.
