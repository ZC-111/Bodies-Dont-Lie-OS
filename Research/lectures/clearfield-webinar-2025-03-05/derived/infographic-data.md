## 🥗 Infographic 1: "Culinary Medicine vs Culinary Translation"

**Title:** From Prescription to Plate

| Concept | What it is | Who it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **Culinary medicine** | Evidence-based blend of culinary arts + nutrition science + medical education | Clinicians & teams (ideally teaching-kitchen training) |
| **Culinary translation** | Turns diet advice into affordable, cultural, delicious meals | Patients who hear “eat greens” and freeze |

**Footer:** Knowing the science ≠ getting dinner on the table. Translation closes the gap.

## 🌾 Infographic 3: "Whole Grain Anatomy"

**Title:** Don’t Break the Symphony

| Part | Role | Lost when refined? |
|---|---|---|
| **Bran** | Fiber + many nutrients | ✅ Mostly removed |
| **Germ** | Life potential (germinate); PUFAs / phytochemicals | ✅ Mostly removed |
| **Endosperm** | Starch energy; fewer nutrients | Remains (white rice / white flour) |

**Stat callout:** Refining can strip up to **~95%** of bran/germ nutrients before partial “enrichment” (fiber not restored).

**Hack:** Soak overnight + 1 Tbsp acid (yogurt/kefir/vinegar/lemon) → better digestion, minerals, cook time.

## 🫘 Infographic 5: "Soy — Whole Food vs Fragment"

**Title:** Breast Cancer Soy Clarity

| Form | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Tempeh, miso** | ★ Preferred whole soy |
| **Tofu** | Food form (fiber removed) — still better than isolates |
| **Soy protein isolate / capsules** | ⚠ Fragmented — where harm signals clustered |
| **GMO soy** | Polt avoids — unknowns + glyphosate/microbiome concern |

**Clinical note:** Large women’s health data: soy did not block tamoxifen; may support aromatase-inhibitor effect. Phytoestrogens ≠ xenoestrogens.

## 📉 Infographic 6: "Lower the Glycemic Load — Food Moves"

**Title:** It’s Not “Sugar Feeds Cancer” — It’s Context

```
KEEP WHOLE          →  brown rice grain > rice flour
PAIR SMART          →  carb + protein + fat + fiber
RESISTANT STARCH    →  cook potatoes, then cool
AL DENTE            →  pasta less mushy = lower GL
SPROUT              →  sprouted grain/bean breads (not flour)
BLEND, DON’T STRIP  →  keep fiber (smoothie > extracted juice)
```

**Insulin reminder:** Excess insulin = growth signal; cancer cells gladly take spare glucose.

## 🧼 Infographic 7: "Produce Wash Ladder (When Organic Isn’t Affordable)"

**Title:** Dirty Dozen Damage Control

| Rank / Method | Recipe | Evidence cue |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Baking soda** | 1 tsp / ~2 cups (500 mL) water; soak ~15 min; rub | Studies favor over ACV |
| **2. ACV** | ~¼ cup (60 mL) / 1 L water; soak + rub | Antimicrobial; cheap ACV OK |
| **3. White vinegar** | Straight vinegar; 15–20 min; rinse | Strongest in Crouch collaborators’ testing |
| **Backup** | Running water + heat; peel/trim skins | Better than nothing |

**Rule:** Pick **one** method. Washing ≠ removing soil-absorbed toxins (arsenic).
