## Segment 1 — Hormone Open: Oral vs Cream Progesterone & Continuous Bleeding Protocols (00:18–08:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield; Carol (Speaker 1); Melvin Nario (Speaker 2)

### Oral vs topical progesterone
- An integrative OBGYN influencer (Felice Gersh orbit / Irvine) has argued oral progesterone is weak / fattening and that cream is preferred.
- **Carol’s counter:** oral often works *better* when skin delivery fails — low thyroid → poor peripheral circulation / cool skin; high adrenaline → vasoconstriction → cream absorption falls.
- High-dose compounded creams (e.g. 100–200 mg/g) can work; OTC creams are often too weak.
- Commercial oral products (US) frequently use **peanut oil**; NZ / Australia / Canada / UK formulations use safflower/sunflower oils without dyes.
- Regulatory asymmetry: pregnenolone / DHEA easy OTC in US; DHEA is controlled in Canada.

### Continuous postmenopausal bleeding (Wiley-style cycling)
- Felice Gersh–endorsed Wiley-type continuous estrogen with late progesterone → heavy endometrium buildup in some patients.
- Case cited (Amy Horniman, public): high progesterone (up to ~600 mg) still inadequate → ablation → later Stage 1 cancer → hysterectomy.
- Nario cultural note: some Middle Eastern / polygamy-context patients value continued bleeding as “youth” signal — clinically risky framing for US practice liability.

### Practice outcomes anecdote
- Clearfield: near-zero MI/stent events over years in integrative practice vs “3 MIs/week” in prior insurance practice — wants university stats collaborator (Quinn joke).

## Segment 3 — Piper Dobner Intro: Microbiome as Immune Educator (17:08–30:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner, ND

### Bio / framing
- Path: childhood eczema → nutrition → chemistry major → ND school; microbiology tutor introduced fecal transplant → ecosystem/terrain medicine focus.
- Clinic: **Biome Basics** (`biome-basics.com`); recurrent/resistant *C. difficile*; FMT donor work.

### What “microbiome” means clinically
- Not bacteria-only: bacteria, viruses, fungi/yeast, protozoa, parasites/helminths.
- Multiple niches: gut (primary), oral (gateway), skin, soil/environment.
- **Core thesis:** gut microbes are the great educator of the immune system via small-molecule metabolites (“microbial poop”).

### Clinical examples
- **TMAO:** microbial TMA → hepatic TMAO; high carnitine/choline diets; add alliums (garlic/onion) — carnivore steak + garlic may blunt TMAO production.
- **Preservatives = micro-antibiotics** in food.
- **SSRIs/SNRIs** (e.g. Prozac) have antimicrobial literature → chronic use depletes commensals.
- Species *names* may match ~10% across people; **functional/metabolite output** converges in health → prioritize function over taxonomy.

## Segment 4 — Testing Limits, Dysbiosis vs SIBO, Helminths (30:03–51:16)

**Speakers:** Piper Dobner; Melvin Nario

### Testing realism
- Preferred functional stool markers (digestive enzymes, inflammation, fiber markers): Doctor’s Data, Genova, Vibrant (to an extent).
- Whole-genome consumer kits (Nirvana Biome / Cosmos ID backend; Jonah AI layer): rich taxonomy, weak “what are they doing?” answer.
- NIST conversation: metabolomic primers not yet standardized enough for reliable clinical metabolomics.
- Microbiome field ~20 years old vs brain research >100 years → still infancy.
- Nario: no universal “healthy microbiome” definition; single stress meal changes results; PCR detects dead DNA → false-positive pathogen traps.
- Dobner practice: OAT before stool on patients; most stool testing is for **donors**; stool rarely changes treatment plan — used for diversity tracking / research.

### Don’t treat asymptomatic “pathogens”
- Enteropathogenic *E. coli* on donor: toss that week’s stool, wait ~2 weeks, retest clear — **no antimicrobial** (pass-through ≤~14 days).
- Colleague with asymptomatic similar *E. coli*: do not Biocidin/abx — diarrhea can be clearance.

### Diversity & autoimmunity
- Mid-2010s literature: MS, Crohn’s, UC, autoimmunity → consistently **low microbial diversity**.
- Goal: ↑ diversity / restore competition, not endless kill protocols.
- SIBO/SIFO reframed as **severe dysbiosis** allowing overgrowth — restore commensals rather than fixating on the label.

### Helminthic therapy & Blastocystis
- **Blastocystis hominis:** subtypes 4 & 7 may be anti-inflammatory; don’t auto-eradicate.
- Case: years of failed antiparasitics for Blasto → history revealed mold illness; stop killing Blasto → binders / bile flow → symptoms improved over ~9 months.
- **Necator americanus** (human hookworm): studied for drug-free UC remission (study designs imperfect); ↑ T regulatory cells.
- Immune “teeter-totter”: Th1 vs Th2; Tregs restore pliability; steroids freeze the board; worm “spit” ≈ ~200 unique peptides communicating with immunity.
- Ecosystem peptides vs single commercial peptide products.

### Ferments & soil analogy
- Stanford ferment vs fiber study (~early 2020s, ~10 weeks): fermented foods ↑ diversity + ↓ inflammation more than fiber push alone.
- Regenerative farms pour **sauerkraut juice** ~2 years to degrade glyphosate residues / restore soil diversity.
- Low-and-slow for post-Rifaximin SIBO refugees: start with ⅛ tsp sauerkraut juice only.

## Segment 5 — Clinical Q&A: Oral Care, Oxalates, Mold, Histamine (51:16–1:25:43)

**Speakers:** Piper Dobner; Bill Clearfield; Melvin Nario; panel

### Oral microbiome
- Prefer **nano-hydroxyapatite** toothpaste (Boka texture favored; FYGG/Fig too sweet via xylitol for her).
- Do **not** rinse immediately — leave mineral on teeth 10–15 min before water.
- Mouthwash (even alcohol-free) still suspect for oral diversity / Alzheimer’s signal (Perlmutter IHS talk); prefer brush + tongue scrape + water.
- Oil pulling endorsed as adjunct.

### High urine oxalates
- Restore oxalate-consuming capacity in the biome (*Oxalobacter* and others via horizontal gene transfer — don’t fixate on one named bug).
- Restrictive low-oxalate diets → food fear / orthorexia; diversify diet + address glyphosate/pesticides.
- Nario: oxalate panic often masks underlying inflammation (DM, lipids, hsCRP); oxalates can be protective chemistry — fix terrain.

### FMT timing insight (gnotobiotic mice)
- Germ-free mice: mucus architecture “swaps” (thick SI / shedding LI).
- After FMT exposure (~6–7 weeks): architecture normalizes — chronic FMT is **not overnight** (unlike *C. diff* often 24–48h clear; virulent strains may need redose).

### Mold — Crohn’s case (signature teaching story)
- 15yo male Crohn’s × 3 yrs; failed Remicade / Humira / Stelara; still bleeding + constipated; home mold agar plates positive everywhere; single-mom family cannot move.
- Protocol emphasis: **move bowels first** (beets as bile movers; vitamin C to bowel tolerance) — binders worsen constipation.
- At ~8 months: add helminthic therapy to create microbial competition vs mycotoxins while still in mold home.
- ~15 months: clinical Crohn’s remission on vitamin C + helminths (no binder); age 19 now — gym, girlfriend, social recovery; still in moldy home.
- Jill Crista products / “Gentle Bind” (okra + lemon balm + other) if binder needed after motility restored.
- Lemon balm caveat (Clearfield): can lower thyroid.

### MCAS / low DAO histamine
- Ultra-low-dose helminthic therapy to support diversity + Treg balance (source of worms not discussed on recording).
- Bifidobacterium often less histamine-producing; Lactobacillus can increase histamine.
- Bifido depletion noted in severe COVID / long COVID literature.

### Probiotics
- Rotate products — don’t stay on one strain forever.
- Transparent: works with SFI Health; likes **LactoPrime Plus** (no prebiotic fiber; ~11–12 species) vs inulin-containing siblings that bloat.
- Probiotics = “tourists” on the microbial island — transient; benefit via metabolite/gene exchange, not guaranteed colonization on retest.
- Yogurt / Therabiotic-style blends mimic dairy ferments when food intolerance blocks sauerkraut/kimchi.

## Segment 6 — Neurodegeneration, Sleep, Constipation Foundations (1:25:43–1:59:15)

**Speakers:** Piper Dobner; Bill Clearfield

### Parkinson’s / Alzheimer’s microbiome links
- Parkinson’s often preceded by ~**20 years of constipation**.
- Centrifuge observation: constipated stool → huge fiber pellet, tiny microbial pellet (1–2 mL vs ~5 mL on Bristol 4) → microbes trapped in fiber matrix → ↓ diversity signal → ↑ inflammation / neuroinflammation / ↓ brain oxygenation.
- Lifetime antibiotic burden ↑ Alzheimer’s risk; chronic sleep deprivation ↑ dementia risk.
- High-risk open-heart / bypass-type oxygenation insults also discussed as dementia risk factor.
- Alcohol: kills microbiome + “type 3 diabetes” metabolic Alzheimer pathway.

### Sleep = “brain squeeze”
- Deep sleep → brain physically shrinks (rodent MRI metaphor) — squeezes metabolic “dirty water” like a sponge.

### Constipation hierarchy
- Before FMT or colonics: ask **water intake** — Mayo/Cleveland-level patients often never asked.
- Slip-and-slide without water analogy; chronic colonics without hydration → dependency.
- Case: refused to try water → discharged from FMT candidacy (ownership required).

### Vitamin C dosing for motility / mold support
- Unbuffered ascorbic acid to **bowel tolerance**: start ~6 g/day, ↑ 1 g/day until diarrhea or ~25 g — depletion gauge; then may add high-dose vitamin A (not if pregnant / trying) — FOXP3 / Treg support is vitamin A–dependent.
- Panel note: ascorbate often better GI tolerance; start ~4 g.
- Mg citrate useful but if >~600 mg/day and still stuck, stop escalating osmotic load (~300–400 mg often enough) — add other supports.

### “Normal” stool test + symptomatic patient
- Prefer diversity scores (Nirvana) + OAT (KBMO / metabolic insight panels) + metals / foundations.
- Often emotional/trauma layer when labs blank; rare true isolated L-glutamine deficiency case after extensive psych work.
