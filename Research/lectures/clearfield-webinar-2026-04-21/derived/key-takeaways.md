# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-04-21

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Restore diversity; stop antimicrobial whack-a-mole.** Autoimmunity literature consistently maps to low microbial diversity — add competition (ferments, commensals, helminths, FMT) rather than chasing every PCR flag.

2. **Function > Latin names.** Species lists may overlap ~10% between healthy people; metabolite signaling converges. Don’t panic over a missing Akkermansia if the ecosystem is working.

3. **Asymptomatic “pathogens” often need observation, not Biocidin.** Pass-through organisms (e.g. EPEC) can clear in ~≤14 days; diarrhea may be the clearance mechanism.

4. **SIBO/SIFO as severe dysbiosis.** Overgrowth fills a vacuum — rebuild the community instead of living inside the diagnostic label.

5. ***Necator americanus* as Treg / peptide therapy.** Human hookworm secretions (~200 peptides) can rebalance the Th1/Th2 teeter-totter; used carefully in autoimmunity, mold-associated Crohn’s, and ultra-low-dose histamine/MCAS contexts.

6. **Mold patients: motility before binders.** Constipation traps mycotoxins; beets + vitamin C to bowel tolerance first. Binders on a stuck gut make things worse. Jill Crista gentle binders only after movement.

7. **Fermented foods beat fiber-only pushes** (Stanford ~10-week signal). Low-and-slow sauerkraut juice → yogurt → rotating probiotics if ferments explode sensitive guts.

8. **Ask about water before FMT or colonics.** Foundational hydration is routinely skipped even after tertiary GI workups; no ownership of water → poor FMT candidacy.

9. **Male estradiol: don’t crush to zero.** Use aromatase inhibitors for gynecomastia, emotional lability, or E2-driven sexual dysfunction — not influencer “estrogen doesn’t matter” extremes in young men.

10. **Oral progesterone still has a role** when cream fails (low thyroid / high adrenaline impair dermal delivery). Continuous Wiley-style bleeding protocols can drive dangerous endometrial buildup.

## Comparison: Kill Protocols vs Restoration Lens

| Dimension | Kill / suppress | Restore / ecosystem |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary move** | Abx, herbals, antiparasitics | Ferments, diversity, FMT, helminths |
| **Lab trigger** | Any “pathogen” on PCR | Symptoms + terrain + diversity trend |
| **SIBO framing** | Eradicate overgrowth | Rebuild missing competition |
| **Mold + constipation** | Bind hard immediately | Move bowels → then gentle bind |
| **Histamine/MCAS** | Avoid all ferments forever | Bifido-leaning support; low-dose helminths in select cases |
| **Success metric** | Negative stool flag | ↑ diversity, clinical function, less fear of food |

**Consensus from Dobner:** After repeated kill cycles fail, change the philosophy — rebuild the community.
