# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-02-05

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Uveal melanoma ≠ cutaneous melanoma.** Different genetics (GNAQ/GNA11 vs BRAF/NRAS), low mutational burden, poor response to cutaneous-style targeted/IO drugs.

2. **~50% of primary UM metastasizes — usually to liver — with 80–90% dying within ~1 year** once metastatic. Primary local control does not equal systemic cure.

3. **Risk stratify molecularly:** monosomy 3 + BAP1 loss = high metastatic risk; DecisionDx-UM (Castle, 15-gene) is the practical US assay discussed; PRAME upshifts class.

4. **Tebentafusp (Kimmtrak) is the first FDA-approved metastatic UM systemic win** (gp100–CD3 bispecific) but is **HLA-restricted** — presenter cited only ~9% of patients in the responsive/eligible pool.

5. **CRISPR kinome dropout screens can surface druggable axes:** LKB1–SIK2 loss → ↑ SLC8A1 → Ca²⁺ addiction + mitochondrial ROS → proliferation.

6. **Preclinical combo signal:** SLC8A1 inhibitor (KB-R7943) + MitoQ outperformed either alone for apoptosis and xenograft shrinkage — interesting for integrative oncology hypothesizing, **not** ready as protocol.

7. **Globe-preserving care is the modern primary-UM preference** (plaque/proton RT, local resection) over routine enucleation when feasible.

8. **Do not manage UM by a single blood marker.** CEA is nonspecific; RGCC CTCs are adjunctive — **clinical picture trumps CTC counts**.

9. **Repurposing + nanodelivery** (e.g. Ca-channel blockers, cyclodextrin nanoparticles, PDT with ICG/methylene blue) are attractive integrative ideas because they’re faster than de novo drugs — still need mechanism and safety data.

10. **Rare cancers are collectively common (3.5–7% / 300–500M people).** Orphan incentives, consortia, and mission capital are how therapies get built when markets are tiny.

| **Ca²⁺ angle** | Addiction phenotype; channel ≠ proven | Try nifedipine/verapamil hypotheses |
