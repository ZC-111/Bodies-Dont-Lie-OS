## 🎯 Infographic 1: "UM Is Not Skin Melanoma"

**Title:** Same Cell Family, Different Disease

| Feature | Cutaneous Melanoma | Uveal Melanoma |
|---|---|---|
| Classic drivers | BRAF / NRAS | **GNAQ / GNA11** |
| Mutational burden | Often high | **Very low** |
| UV as main cause | Strong | **Weak / not primary** |
| Therapy progress | Rapid IO/targeted gains | **Poor cross-response** |
| Lethal pattern | Variable | **Liver mets → months** |

**Footer:** Do not copy-paste cutaneous melanoma protocols onto ocular disease.

## 📊 Infographic 2: "UM by the Numbers"

**Title:** Rare Incidence, High Stakes

```
3–5% of all melanomas
5–7 cases / million / year
~90% arise in choroid
Mean diagnosis ~60 (peak ~65)
~50% develop metastasis (esp. liver)
80–90% die within ~1 year once metastatic
Tebentafusp helps — but ~9% HLA-matched pool (as cited)
```

**Callout:** Affects the **last third** of a typical lifespan — quality of remaining years matters.

## 💊 Infographic 4: "Therapy Reality Check"

**Title:** Primary Control ≠ Systemic Cure

```
PRIMARY UM
  ├── Radiotherapy (plaque / proton)
  ├── Local resection
  └── Enucleation (declining preference)
           │
           ▼
METASTATIC UM
  ├── No universal standard of care
  ├── Many trials (targeted / IO / vaccines)
  └── Tebentafusp (Kimmtrak, 2021)
        • gp100 × CD3 bispecific
        • OS benefit (wanes by 3y follow-up)
        • HLA-restricted → most patients ineligible
```

## 🔬 Infographic 5: "The LKB1 → Calcium → ROS Cascade"

**Title:** CRISPR Hit → Preclinical Combo Idea

```
LKB1 loss
   │
   ├── SIK2 dysregulation
   │
   └── ↑ SLC8A1
          │
          ├── Ca²⁺ addiction
          └── ↑ mitochondrial ROS
                 │
                 └── ↑ proliferation / survival

PRECLINICAL COUNTER:
  KB-R7943 (SLC8A1) + MitoQ (mito ROS)
  → apoptosis ↑ , xenograft size ↓ (combo > alone)
```

**Footer:** LKB1 is a **tumor suppressor** — loss worsens the phenotype. Still far from clinic.
