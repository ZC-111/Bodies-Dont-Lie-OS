### Q13. Speaker 5’s core argument for offering bioidentical E2/E3 was:
- A) Xenoestrogens are harmless
- B) **Receptors prefer bioidenticals; without them, cells “sponge” environmental estrogens** ✓
- C) Only estrone from adipose is needed for brain health
- D) Progesterone alone occupies all estrogen receptors

### Q14. Speaker 5’s practical cream dosing pearl for many women was approximately:
**Answer:** Cream at **0.625 mg estradiol per gram**; **two clicks ≈ 0.3 mg E2**, often enough; higher doses (~1–1.5 mg) warrant cycling with progesterone and a hormone-free week.

### Q15. Why did Speaker 5 argue adipose-derived estrogen is not equivalent to replacement estradiol?
**Answer:** Adipose conversion is mostly **estrone**, whereas **estradiol** is the key ligand for many neuronal/brain receptor roles (framed as neurotransmitter-like activity with dedicated brain production sites).
