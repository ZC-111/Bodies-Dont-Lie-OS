# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-09-09

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Detect → remove → repair** is Campbell's entire toxin playbook. Wrong diagnosis or wrong treatment keeps patients chronically ill.

2. **Serum IgG + IgE to 14 mycotoxins** (retest ≥6 months) beats urine mycotoxin tests, OATs fungal markers, ERMI, and petri-dish kits for clinical decision-making.

3. **Itraconazole 100 mg BID with food** kills the spore source of mycotoxins; fluconazole does not cover multicellular molds. ~10% need brand Sporanox.

4. **Remove ongoing exposure or treatment fails.** HVAC, basements, bathrooms, and construction moisture matter even in deserts (Phoenix, Dubai, Vegas).

5. **Binders lack human evidence** for indoor mycotoxins, bind minerals/vitamins, and mainly address aflatoxin (not the indoor toxigenic mold set).

6. **Avoid glutathione/NAC when gliotoxin/brain-MS pictures are in play** — GSH can increase gliotoxin cytotoxicity.

7. **“Chronic Lyme” is often not Lyme** — Johns Hopkins series: 84% of Lyme referrals had no active disease; prolonged antibiotics for PTLDS fail five trials.

8. **Chronic sinusitis is frequently fungal** (Ponikau/Mayo: 96% mold) — Amphotericin nasal + systemic itraconazole; cribriform plate is a brain shortcut.

9. **Zearalenone wrecks hormones** — low T in men, E/P imbalance and early menopause in women, premature puberty in girls.

10. **Check ferritin/iron panel before megadose vitamin C** — Fenton chemistry turns C into an oxidant when iron is high; transferrin saturation separates inflammation from overload.
