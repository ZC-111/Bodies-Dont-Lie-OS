## 🧪 Infographic 4: "Mebendazole Mechanism Map"

**Title:** Microtubules + Hedgehog/WNT + VEGF

```
MEBENDAZOLE
  │
  ├── β-tubulin bind → mitotic arrest (watch Ki-67)
  │
  ├── Hedgehog (SMO/Gli1) + WNT/β-catenin ↓
  │         → CSC death or differentiation
  │
  ├── Mitochondrial intrinsic apoptosis (p53-independent)
  │
  └── VEGF ↓ → less kinked neovessels / less Treg drive
```

**Synergy chips:** Thymosin α1 · retinoic acid / HDAC-i · PDT/SDT · nattokinase · losartan
