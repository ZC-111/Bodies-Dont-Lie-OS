# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-06-24

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Shrink ≠ eradicate.** After chemo/RT, residual disease is often **CSC-rich + fibrotic** — denser on imaging, hypoxic, immune-cold, and drug-impermeable.

2. **Ivermectin is multi-modal:** WNT/β-catenin CSC hit, mitochondrial ROS apoptosis (p53-bypass), importin α/β block of STAT3/NF-κB, and TGF-β antifibrotic action.

3. **Mebendazole is multi-modal:** β-tubulin mitotic arrest, Hedgehog + WNT CSC suppression, mitochondrial apoptosis, VEGF normalization, and antifibrotic overlap.

4. **Monitor the right things:** CD44/ALDH1 (and related stemness markers), Ki-67, TGF-β/fibrosis markers, **and tumor density** — not diameter alone.

5. **Dose ranges cited:** ivermectin ~12–26 mg/day or 200–400 µg/kg; mebendazole 100 mg BID–TID for months — always within FDA safety ceilings; load via cyclodextrin/platelet nanoparticles when possible.

6. **Don’t pick a tribe.** Keep conventional tools as **smart, low-dose, photo-activated, pharmacogenetic** weapons; add CSC/fibrosis remodeling instead of ditching chemo wholesale.

7. **Prevention ≠ treatment.** Prefer optimization, polyphenols, methylene blue (~10 mg with breaks), metformin-class tools, and hormone balance for prevention — reserve ivermectin for treatment contexts.

8. **Brain tumors need different picks:** avoid relying on ivermectin CNS penetration; mebendazole/albendazole discussed as better BBB candidates.

9. **Aspirin tip:** still a CV workhorse; adjunct signal in inflammatory depression (esp. + NAC); oncology and OB prevention signals — watch folate/C/iron depletion.

10. **Detox/chelation hierarchy for burdened patients:** cyclodextrin ± EDTA (fat-soluble + metals), binders/liver support; **apheresis** only when still inflamed/toxic after basics.

## Comparison Table: Ivermectin vs Mebendazole (Lecture Framing)

| Dimension | Ivermectin | Mebendazole |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary CSC hit** | WNT/β-catenin; mTOR via P21 path | Hedgehog (SMO/Gli1) + WNT/β-catenin |
| **Mitosis** | Indirect via signaling/apoptosis | Direct **β-tubulin** mitotic arrest |
| **Mitochondria** | ROS → cytochrome c apoptosis | Intrinsic mitochondrial disruption |
| **Fibrosis** | Strong TGF-β / myofibroblast block | Overlapping TGF-β / matrix softening |
| **Angiogenesis** | Via NF-κB/STAT3/VEGF programs ↓ | Direct **VEGF** suppression (Avastin-like) |
| **BBB / brain** | Poor penetrator; neurotoxicity concern | Better CNS candidate at higher doses |
| **Monitor** | CD44, ALDH1, density, TGF-β | Ki-67, stemness markers, density, VEGF axis |
| **Stack mates** | Ozone, PDT/MB, nattokinase, losartan | Retinoic acid/HDAC-i, thymosin α1, PDT/SDT |

**Consensus:** Synergistic pair for CAF + CSC — combine or cycle based on polypharmacy; personalize with RGCC-style sensitivity.
