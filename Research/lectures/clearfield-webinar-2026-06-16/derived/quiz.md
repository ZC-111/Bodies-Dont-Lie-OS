## Section B — C60 Structure, Process & Mechanism (8 questions)

### Q6. Carbon 60 was discovered in ____ and awarded a Nobel Prize in ____.
- A) 1978 / 1985
- B) **1985 / 1996** ✓
- C) 1996 / 2012
- D) 2012 / 2016

### Q7. Why does Greska argue C60 is preferable to C70 as an electron donor?
**Answer:** C60 is a true sphere (soccer-ball geometry) and can donate electrons without collapsing; C70 is elongated (rugby-ball), less structurally stable when giving up electrons.

### Q8. Approximate particle size claimed for Greska’s C60 vs many solvent-grade products:
- A) 1200 nm vs 20 nm
- B) **20 nm vs ~1200 nm** ✓
- C) Both ~100 nm
- D) Micron-sized only

### Q9. Purple tint in competitor C60 oils is attributed to:
- A) Higher C70 content
- B) Iron contamination
- C) **Residual solvents swelling the lattice / changing light refraction** ✓
- D) Intentional food coloring

### Q10. True or False: On this lecture’s framing, “purity” as C60:C70 ratio equals contaminant-free.
**Answer:** **False.** Ratio purity ≠ absence of metals/solvents; clean product should lack toluene residues and elemental contaminants (trace O₂ only claimed).

### Q11. Primary mitochondrial framing for C60 benefit:
- A) Creates new mitochondria (biogenesis)
- B) **Neutralizes free radicals generated during ATP production inside mitochondria** ✓
- C) Replaces Complex IV
- D) Directly synthesizes ATP molecules

### Q12. Compared with typical dietary antioxidants, Greska’s size claim for C60 is roughly:
- A) 2× smaller
- B) 200× smaller
- C) **~200,000× smaller** ✓
- D) Larger but fatter soluble

### Q13. Nario’s contrast between vitamin C and C60 as antioxidants:
**Answer:** Vitamin C donates an electron then may become unstable/pro-oxidant (recycled via glutathione; high-dose C + iron risk). C60 acts as a stable **“free-radical sponge”** that donates without collapsing — SOD-like metaphor.

## Section C — Clinical Use Patterns & Caveats (7 questions)

### Q14. Recommended default timing for oral C60 in this talk:

- B) **Morning (and possibly late afternoon ~6 pm if more severe)** ✓
- C) With every meal including midnight snack

### Q15. Preferred approach for eye-related use as stated:
- A) Direct intraocular drops of oil
- B) **Oral systemic ± topical on eyelids (mist preferred); not into the eye (not sterile-certified)** ✓
- C) Intravenous only
- D) DMSO + intraocular injection

### Q16. Approximate clearance / washout framing when stopping C60:
**Answer:** Most eliminated around **~10 days**; many feel return of fatigue/sluggishness by ~**2 weeks** off.

### Q17. Frequency vs megadose preference:
- A) Drink half-bottle daily is optimal for everyone
- B) **More frequent dosing (up to ~3×/day gut feel) preferred over simply taking huge single doses** ✓
- C) Weekly pulse only
- D) Must combine with methylene blue

### Q18. DMSO co-administration finding reported:
**Answer:** Tried; **no meaningful absorption benefit**; unnecessary given particle size; DMSO may ferry contaminants — not recommended.

### Q19. Best evidence characterization for most human disease claims in this webinar:
- A) Large multi-center RCTs
- B) **Predominantly anecdote + some animal/in vitro literature; one small human biome swab study mentioned** ✓
- C) FDA-approved indications
- D) Cochrane meta-analyses only

### Q20. Short answer: List Nario’s five regenerative cofactor categories from Tip of the Week.
**Answer:** **Vitamin C, collagen peptides, zinc, magnesium, amino acids** (glycine/proline ± arginine, glutamine, HMB).
