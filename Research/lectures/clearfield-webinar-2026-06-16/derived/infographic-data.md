## Infographic 4: "Clean vs Solvent C60"

**Title:** Why Color & Size Matter

| | Clean process (claimed) | Solvent / industrial grade |
|---|---|---|
| Particle | ~20 nm spheres | ~1200 nm crystals / aggregates |
| Color | Deep black | Purple tint (lattice swell) |
| Mix time | ~2 minutes | Up to ~2 weeks |
| Contaminant risk | Trace O₂ | Toluene / paraffin residues |
| Bioavailability | High (mitochondrial niche) | Lower; tissue trapping concerns |

**Callout:** “Purity” as C60:C70 ratio ≠ solvent-free.
