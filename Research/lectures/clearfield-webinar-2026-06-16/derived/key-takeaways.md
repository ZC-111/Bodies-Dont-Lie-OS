# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-06-16

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Regenerative injectables are signals, not substrate.** If PRP/peptides underperform, audit vitamin C, collagen peptides, zinc, magnesium, and amino acids before stacking another peptide.

2. **Pair vitamin C with collagen** whenever the goal is collagen deposition — hydroxylase cofactor + glycine/proline/hydroxyproline delivery.

3. **Zinc 15–30 mg elemental with food** supports fibroblast proliferation and matrix remodeling; often omitted in “glow stack” protocols.

4. **C60’s proposed edge is geometry + size:** spherical fullerene can donate electrons without collapsing; ~20 nm claims vs micron-scale solvent crystals change bioavailability stories.

5. **Solvent history matters.** Purple tint and toluene/paraffin residues were framed as the main reason many commercial C60 products disappoint or accumulate toxicity — ask vendors for contaminant panels, not just C60:C70 “purity.”

6. **Redox literacy:** Vitamin C/E can become pro-oxidant after donating (high-dose C + iron; β-carotene in smokers). C60 was taught as a stable “free-radical sponge”; methylene blue overlaps as redox/ETC carrier but is a different clinical tool.

7. **Use patterns:** daily oral (AM), optional second dose early evening, topical “flood” for local complaints; eyelid application — not intraocular drops (not sterile-certified).

8. **Frequency over megadose.** Informal customer testing favored standard concentration; half-bottle dosing mainly wastes money; clearance framing ~10 days, symptomatic washout ~2 weeks.

9. **Evidence humility:** Most human disease claims here are anecdote; animal fullerene literature and one small biome study were cited. Do not translate expo pain demos into labeled indications.

10. **Shungite / historical carbon lore ≠ product QA.** Interesting cultural parallel (Karelia), but clinical decisions still need particle size, solvent residuals, and transparent third-party testing.
