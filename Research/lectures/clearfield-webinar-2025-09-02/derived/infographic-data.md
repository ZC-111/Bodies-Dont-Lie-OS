## Infographic 4: "Food Pyramid — Highest Sources"

**Title:** Spermidine-Rich Foods (Lecture Inverse Pyramid)

| Rank | Foods |
|---|---|
| ★ Top | Wheat germ (some mushrooms may compete) |
| High | Aged cheese, peas, nuts, apples/pears, broccoli |
| Practical | Natto (Japan) — daily eaters may not need a capsule |

**Callout:** Healthy diet ≈ **6–10 mg/day**. Doubling via food alone adds calories → can ↑mTOR and blunt benefit — hence physiologic supplementation.

## Infographic 5: "Dose Ladder — Humans"

**Title:** Clinical Dosing Window (Extract Trials)

| Dose | Role |
|---|---|
| **1–2 mg/day** | Early dementia / cognitive trials — benefits start |
| **3 mg/day** | Cost-accessible sachet maintenance |
| **6 mg/day** | ★ Ideal / EFSA food-supplement max framing |
| **~20–25 mg/day** | Speculative Blue Zone–style *total* intake (diet + supp) |
| **≫ megadose** | ❌ Preclinical oxidative stress risk — not the goal |

**Rule:** Sustained physiologic autophagy > acute megadoses. Prefer **natural wheat-germ extract** over synthetic.
