## Section B — Spermidine Biochemistry & Autophagy (8 questions)

### Q5. Spermidine is best classified as a:
- A) Steroid hormone
- B) **Polyamine with three amino groups** ✓
- C) Long-chain omega-3 fatty acid
- D) Peptide hormone

### Q6. Roughly what fraction of daily spermidine needs comes from diet/supplements vs endogenous synthesis vs gut microbes (Cano’s thirds model)?
**Answer:** About **one-third each** from (1) diet/supplements, (2) cellular synthesis from arginine/ornithine, and (3) gut bacterial production.

### Q7. What is the single most important takeaway mechanism Cano emphasized?
- A) Direct LDL receptor upregulation
- B) **Induction of autophagy (including mitophagy)** ✓
- C) Irreversible mTOR knockout
- D) ACE2 inhibition

### Q8. How does spermidine primarily trigger autophagy in the pathway she highlighted?
- A) Blocking lysosomal fusion
- B) **Hypusination / modification of translation factor eIF5A → TFEB-driven autophagy programs** ✓
- C) Binding ApoB particles in plasma
- D) Inhibiting vitamin D synthesis

### Q9. Which statement about fasting is correct per the Graz / *Nature Cell Biology* framing presented?
- A) Fasting benefits are independent of spermidine
- B) **Caloric restriction raises spermidine, which is required for fasting-mediated autophagy / longevity benefits in the models shown** ✓
- C) Fasting depletes all polyamines to zero
- D) Only intravenous spermidine works during fasting

### Q10. Which longevity agents were listed as also converging on autophagy activation?
**Answer:** **Rapamycin** (↓mTOR), **metformin** (↑AMPK), **resveratrol** (sirtuins) — alongside spermidine’s eIF5A / related paths.

### Q11. True or False: Higher dietary spermidine intake has been linked in epidemiology to lower all-cause mortality / longer average lifespan (~5 years in cited framing).
**Answer:** **True** (as correlation in the lecture — not proven causation from supplementation RCTs).

### Q12. What is mitophagy, and how does spermidine relate vs urolithin A?
**Answer:** Mitophagy = selective mitochondrial autophagy. Spermidine activates **general autophagy + mitophagy + proteostasis**; urolithin A is framed as more **mitophagy-niche**. Cano: establish autophagy “clean background” before stacking other agents.
