# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-12-17

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Estradiol is the gold standard for vasomotor symptoms** — and for optimizing bone, brain, autonomic, gut, and metabolic health. Symptom-suppression-only doses are not enough.

2. **VMS last 7–10 years on average** and can persist into the 70s–80s. Word-recall/memory problems are often the #1 complaint; never dismiss night sweats as trivial.

3. **Mechanism = KNDy / neurokinin B pathway** in the hypothalamic thermoregulatory center. High FSH + low estradiol (SWAN) tracks with worse VMS burden.

4. **Fezolinetant (Veozah) is last-line for Gersh** — ~$600/month, delayed onset, FDA liver-injury warning from systemic NK-receptor blockade. Prefer SSRI/SNRI → gabapentin before NK antagonists when hormones can’t be used.

5. **Phytoestrogen stack:** minimally processed soy (isoflavones), flax (lignans), legumes; Siberian rhubarb stilbene supplement; ashwagandha for VMS + sleep/mood — adjuncts, not full HRT replacements.

6. **Vaginal micronized progesterone 200 mg (up to 400)** beats oral first-pass (80–90% metabolized). Topical progesterone is not reliably systemic; blood monitoring, not saliva.

7. **Start many recently menopausal women on 0.1 estradiol patch** (~100 pg/mL common); upper buttock placement; avoid spray. Titrate to tolerance/optimization, not minimal flash control.

8. **Reframe “estrogen dominance” as estrone dominance** from inflamed/adipose aromatase. Giving estradiol + progesterone + anti-inflammatory lifestyle helps reverse the lock-in.

9. **Oophorectomy = testosterone emergency** (~25% of T lost; no brain sensors / no adrenal compensation). Natural aging T decline is not an emergency; avoid reflexive pellets.

10. **Nandrolone literacy:** U.S.-labeled for CKD anemia; therapeutic 50–100 mg IM q3–4 wk themes (bone/sarcopenia/joints); gym doses are another planet — counsel before Reddit does.
