## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Rigidity = opposing muscle fails to relax** — co-contraction loads the joint, truncates ROM, and produces pain when patients force motion.

2. **Muscle spindles run a ~30 ms loop** (~30,000 spindles at 50–100 Hz). When gamma drive is unchecked, voluntary motor control (~100–200 ms) loses — a working model for aging and post-TBI stiffness.

3. **Skin microbiome quorum sensing can drive local tone, swelling, and pain.** Jamming QS with GRAS topicals (CLA, glucono-/glucurono-lactones) produced minute-scale clinical changes in his cases.

4. **Substance P from stressed tenocytes** can be retrogradely transported and keep interneurons firing for hours — a bridge from local tendon injury to sustained spinal hyperexcitability.

5. **Extended wrist rotation (70–90°)** is a practical rigidity vital sign for TBI / early Parkinsonism screening in clinic.

6. **Early Parkinsonian rigidity may respond dramatically to topical CLA**; multi-year disease with painful peri-muscular nodules needs QS pain control before mechanical release — and may not fully reverse.

7. **Ivermectin is a theoretical macrolactone QS disruptor** in this talk — he has **not** used it; he stays on food-safe lactones while protocols mature.

8. **Prefer 9,11-CLA** (natural isomer) over mixed industrial 9,11/10,12 blends when chasing cognitive / neuro tone effects; oral CLA may also blunt GLP-1–related sarcopenia.

9. **L-dopa may worsen Malassezia pathogenicity** — a speculative explanation for long-term L-dopa fade; skin–muscle lymphatics are under-studied.

10. **HPU tip:** excess pyrroles drain zinc and B6 → mood/hormone/fatigue clusters; test light-protected urinary kryptopyrrole; replete P5P, zinc, magnesium, antioxidants.
