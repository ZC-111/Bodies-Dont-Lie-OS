## 🧬 Infographic 2: "Immunosenescence Checklist"

**Title:** What Ages in the Immune System

| Change | Clinical Meaning |
|---|---|
| Thymic involution | ↓ naïve T cells; starts in teens |
| Memory T-cell bias | Weaker response to new antigens |
| B-cell decline | ↓ antibody diversity |
| NK / DC impairment | Weaker first-line defense |
| M1 > M2 macrophages | More inflammatory tone |
| Inflammaging (IL-6, IL-8…) | Chronic low-grade fire |
| Viral “energy tax” (EBV/CMV) | Fatigue + autoimmunity risk |

**Callout:** Not just “old age” — mold, Lyme, toxin, and virus load age immunity early.
