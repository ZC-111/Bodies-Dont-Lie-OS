# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-04-15

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Healthspan beats lifespan** — support the immune system as a hub influencing many hallmarks of aging instead of stacking one supplement per hallmark.

2. **Immunosenescence starts early** — thymic involution in the teens; mold, viruses, toxins, and Lyme can age immunity in younger patients too.

3. **iAge / CXCL9 matter** — Furman’s protein-based immune clock (and CXCL9) may be more actionable than methylation clocks for tracking inflammaging interventions.

4. **IDP ≠ purified lactoferrin** — a ~50-protein NZ whey suite (~40% lactoferrin + lactoperoxidase) outperformed ultra-pure LF because synergy matters.

5. **Immunel (colostrum extract) fills growth-factor / NK gaps** — proline-rich peptides, IGF-1, oligosaccharides, β-glucans; boosts NK and macrophage activity.

6. **Carnosic acid targets CXCL9 + NRF2** — rare natural CXCL9 lever; builds endogenous antioxidants without drowning signaling in exogenous AOX.

7. **Kawakawa completes gut–inflammation coverage** — NZ Māori *Piper excelsum* for barrier, COX-2/NRF2 overlap, and cytokine calm.

8. **Dosing:** 1–2 caps/day maintenance; 3–4 for complex chronic multi-infection patients.

9. **Safety gate:** avoid full formula in pregnancy/young kids (rosemary); main drug red flag = **warfarin**; expect **no Herxheimer** die-off.

10. **Grocery pearl:** choose purple/red grapes for anthocyanins (+ resveratrol/quercetin) over green.

## Stack vs Usual Piecemeal Care

| Dimension | Usual “whack-a-mole” stack | Four-pillar formula framing |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Hit each hallmark separately | Upstream immunosenescence + gut |
| **Antimicrobial** | Separate antimicrobials / LF | IDP selective kill + biofilm support |
| **NK / growth factors** | Colostrum or peptides alone | Standardized Immunel |
| **CXCL9 / NRF2** | Often missed | Carnosic acid (+ Kawakawa NRF2) |
| **Gut barrier** | Pro/pre/postbiotics pile | Shared IDP + Kawakawa + Immunel |
| **Die-off risk** | High with aggressive kills / sulforaphane | Modulatory — Herx uncommon |
| **Caveats** | Polypharmacy confusion | Pregnancy/peds/warfarin limits |
