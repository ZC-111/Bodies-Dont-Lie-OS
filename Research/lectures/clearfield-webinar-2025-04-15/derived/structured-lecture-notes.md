## Segment 4 — Lisa Tamati Intro & Thesis: Immune System as Aging Hub (17:24–37:44)

**Speaker:** Lisa Tamati — ultra-marathon background (70,000+ km), Māori Sports Woman of the Year, author (*Running Hot*, *Running to Extremes*), host of *Pushing the Limits*

### ★ Core Thesis
> **Healthspan > lifespan.** The immune system sits at the core of the hallmarks of aging; supporting immunosenescence may be more efficient than a “whack-a-mole” supplement stack for each hallmark separately.

### Immunosenescence Defined
Gradual age-related deterioration of immunity → ↓ vaccine efficacy, ↑ infection risk, autoimmune dysregulation, cancer surveillance failure.

**Key anatomic/functional changes:**
1. **Thymic involution** — fewer naïve T cells; thymus largely fatty nub by late 50s (starts involuting by teens)
2. **Altered T-cell function** — memory bias, ↓ TCR diversity, impaired signaling
3. **B-cell decline** — ↓ antibody production/diversity
4. **Innate impairment** — ↓ NK and dendritic cell function; M1 > M2 macrophage skew

### Hallmarks Link
Immune cells interact with genomic instability, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, dysbiosis, inflammaging (IL-6, IL-8, etc.), telomere shortening.

### Who Gets Immunosenescence?
- Not only elderly — anyone ~40+ plus younger patients with repeated viral load, toxins, mold, surgery, Lyme, etc.
- Chronic viral suppression (CMV, EBV, herpes, post-COVID reactivation) drains mitochondrial ATP → fatigue + autoimmunity risk

### iAge / 1000 Immunomes Project
- **Dr. David Furman** (Buck Institute / Stanford 1000 Immunomes) — longitudinal multi-omics aging immunology
- **iAge (IH) clock** — protein-based immune/inflammation aging marker; clinically more actionable than methylation clocks per Lisa
- Standout chemokine: **CXCL9** (cardiac remodeling, senescence, chronic inflammation)
- NASA angle: space accelerates aging ~30×; astronauts return with ↑ CVD risk, viral reactivation, neurodegeneration

## Segment 5 — Formulation Pillar 1: Immune Defense Protein (IDP) (40:01–49:55)

**Speaker:** Lisa Tamati

### What It Is
- Bioactive **whey protein fraction** from pasture-fed New Zealand cows
- **~50 synergistic bioactive proteins** (not purified lactoferrin alone)
- ~**40% lactoferrin** + lactoperoxidase + other actives
- Insight from Dr. Rod Claycomb / Contech–Quantec: purifying lactoferrin **reduced** activity — synergy of the natural suite matters (~15 years research)

### Mechanisms
| Pathway | Effect |
|---|---|
| Selective antimicrobial | Kills pathogens; spares Bifido / Lactobacillus |
| Iron sequestration | Delivers iron where needed; denies iron to microbes/viruses |
| Secretory IgA / mucosal immunity | IgA binds pathogens; TGF-β aids IgA class switching |
| NF-κB inhibition | ↓ TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β → less chronic inflammation |
| Biofilm disruption | Improves clearance of chronic infections |
| Type I interferon | Antiviral; blocks viral entry/replication |
| TLR4 / gut–immune axis | ↑ TEER (tight junctions); modulates IL-10 |

### Clinical Framing
Useful adjunct for recurrent URI/GI infections, low sIgA, leaky gut, EBV/CMV reactivation, chronic inflammation, autoimmunity, compromised immunity.

## Segment 6 — Formulation Pillar 2: Immunel / Immune Now (MNL) (49:55–55:28+)

**Speaker:** Lisa Tamati

### Why Colostrum Extract (Not Crude Colostrum)
- Colostrum has 12,000+ studies but poor standardization (day of collection, bioactive dose, fat/lactose load)
- Bovine calves lack placental Ig transfer → bovine colostrum immunoglobulin-rich
- **Immunel (Immune Now / MNL)** = standardized colostrum extract with proline-rich peptides, nucleotides, growth factors (incl. **IGF-1**), milk oligosaccharides, beta-glucans

### Immune Effects
- ↑ **Natural killer cell** cytotoxicity (virally infected + tumor cells)
- ↑ Macrophage phagocytosis via TLR2/TLR4
- Cytokine balance: ↑ IL-10; modulates IL-6 / TNF-α
- Adaptive support: promotes Th1 / IFN-γ for intracellular pathogens
- Stem-cell / tissue repair signaling via IGF-1, TGF-β, EGF
- Helps “older” thymus function via proline-rich peptides

### Safety Note (IDP + MNL alone)
Described as generally safe even in pregnancy (nature’s reproductive milks); full 4-ingredient formula has caveats (see Q&A).

## Segment 7 — Pillars 3–4: Carnosic Acid & Kawakawa + Who Benefits (55:28–1:08:26)

**Speaker:** Lisa Tamati

### Carnosic Acid (Rosemary Extract)
- Target: lower **CXCL9** (hard to find natural CXCL9-lowering agents)
- ↑ **NRF2** endogenous antioxidant capacity (vs flooding exogenous antioxidants)
- Mild COX-2 modulation + ↓ NF-κB (not full NSAID blockade)
- Neuroprotective signal (future webinar planned)
- Also ↑ macrophage/NK activity; ↓ TNF-α / IL-6

### Kawakawa (*Piper excelsum*)
- Māori **taonga** plant — traditional gut, immune, pain use
- NZ-only; pharmaceutical-grade extract in formula
- Actives include myristicin, dihydromethysticin-class compounds + flavonoids (transcript: “meristem and Diane gambin”)
- ↓ TNF-α / IL-6; chemokine CCL2 modulation
- Gut soothing, barrier protection, microbial balance; overlaps COX-2 + NRF2 with carnosic acid via slightly different routes

### Who Benefits (Combined Stack)
Gut dysbiosis, food sensitivities, leaky gut, low sIgA, epithelial barriers (GI / lung / BBB), immunocompromise, autoimmunity, low lymphocytes, inflammaging/longevity, frequent infections, antimicrobial/anti-inflammatory support.

### Case Anecdote
Mother’s lymphocyte count chronically maxed ~0.6 (as low as 0.2) → rose to **1.6** after formulation; Lisa’s own low lymphocytes normalized.
