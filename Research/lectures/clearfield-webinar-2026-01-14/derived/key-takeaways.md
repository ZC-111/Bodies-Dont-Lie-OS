# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-01-14

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Humanin ≠ MOTS-c ≠ inject-and-forget.** Mitochondrial 24-aa peptide: biomarker/mechanism of aging resilience — support with training, fasting/CR, sleep, mito nutrients; not an FDA therapy.

2. **Talk to your ≥60 patients about cannabis** — use is rising fast; many initiate after 60 and rate it helpful. Guide product type and dose; don’t ignore self-experimenters.

3. **Quality of life can improve even when pain scores barely move.** “Gave me my life back” is a recurring qualitative theme — track function/mood, not VAS alone.

4. **Expect deprescribing.** Older-adult cohorts show large **drug-substitution** effects (opioids, benzos, Beers-list agents) after adding cannabis — monitor BP, pain, and psych meds actively.

5. **BPSD is the desperate unmet need.** ~80% develop behavioral symptoms; antipsychotics carry black-box mortality and weak long-term behavior gains — non-pharm first, then consider cannabinoids as enhancer.

6. **Anti-amyloid antibodies are a poor value frame here** (as presented): tiny clinical deltas, ARIA risk, $25–35K/year — behavioral and functional outcomes matter more to caregivers.

7. **THC doses for dementia are often tiny.** Agitation protocols cluster ~**6–15 mg/day**; cognitive/neuroprotection signals appear at **~0.3–1 mg/day**. Start lower than cannabis culture expects.

8. **Night-prime then titrate.** Subtherapeutic QHS THC for several nights builds AE tolerance faster than therapeutic tolerance — then add daytime doses.

9. **CBG complements THC via α2 agonism** (clonidine-like). Paradoxical sleep/agitation? Flip CBG↔CBD. Falls are not the dominant safety signal at studied doses.

10. **Start low, go slow, don’t fear the high end.** Interindividual variability dwarfs age/weight; most do well at 1–5 mg THC/dose, some need ~20 mg/dose — titrate to response, protect ECS from chronic overuse downregulation.
