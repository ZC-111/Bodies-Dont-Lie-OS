## Infographic 6: "Dementia THC Dose Ladder"

**Title:** Start Low — Go Slow — Go Far If Needed

```
Neuroprotection / mild MCI hint:   0.5 mg THC once daily
                                   (e.g., cannabis tea cup)

Conservative night prime:          ½ gummy QHS × 3–7 nights

Agitation Stage 1–2 (TID gummies): ~6–15 mg THC / day total

High responders:                   some need 20–25 mg / day
                                   (per-dose ceiling often 1–5 mg;
                                    outliers thrive at ~20 mg/dose)
```

**Rule:** Adverse-effect tolerance builds faster than therapeutic tolerance — night priming cuts daytime side effects.

## Infographic 7: "Full vs Broad vs Isolate"

**Title:** Read the Label Before You Counsel

| Label | What’s inside | Clinical note (as presented) |
|---|---|---|
| Full spectrum | Cannabinoids **+** residual THC | Synergy / trace actives theorized |
| Broad spectrum | Everything **minus** THC | Used when THC must be zero |
| Isolate | Single molecule (e.g., CBD only) | High-dose CBD for antipsychotic-like effects |

**Hemp loophole (2018):** <0.3% THC **by weight** → heavy gummies can deliver clinical mg while staying “hemp.”

**Regulatory cliff:** ≤0.4 mg THC **per package** would erase most full-spectrum products.

## Infographic 8: "CBG Night / CBD Day Flip"

**Title:** When Agitation or Sleep Goes Paradoxical

```
Too much THC → ↑ agitation (most common AE)
     │
     ├─ Lower dose / night-prime restart
     │
CBG (α2 agonist) → often helps sleep / sympathetic tone
     │
     └─ Paradoxical CBG? → swap to CBD daytime formula
```

**Falls myth:** 1.5 mg THC gait study (2009) — no clinically significant change; falls not a literature signal in dementia cannabis trials per Sulak.
