## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Regressive Vicariation & Homotoxicology (10:07–13:14)

**Speaker:** Dr. Nario (Speaker 2)

### Key Concepts
- **Regressive vicariation** = disease moving from deeper phases back toward natural elimination (excretion, controlled inflammation)
- **Progressive vicariation** = disease moving deeper (acute → chronic → degeneration)
- During anti-homotoxic therapies, stored toxins are mobilized → temporary symptom worsening

### Clinical Pearl
> True regressive vicariation should be **benign, transient, self-limited, and short-lived**. If symptoms become severe, prolonged, progressive, or involve new organ dysfunction — do NOT label it "detox." Further evaluation is required.

### Hering's Law
Healing progresses:
1. Inside → outside
2. Top → bottom
3. Reverse chronological order of original symptom appearance

### Practical Protocol Mentioned
- Liver support: glutathione, NAC
- Lymphatic: exercise, rebounding, vibration therapy
- Gut: probiotics, fiber

## Segment 4 — Stefan Hartman: Eliminating Heart Disease — A Reformed Lipid Denialist (16:19–58:00)

**Speaker:** Stefan Hartman, DO — Iron Primocane Family Medicine, Melbourne FL

### ★ Core Thesis
> **ApoB below 65 prevents plaque formation. LDL below 60 prevents plaque. LDL below 40 reverses plaque.** This is anti-aging medicine.

### 4A. The Estrogen–ApoB Connection

**Key Mechanism:**
- Optimally dosed estradiol ↑ LDL receptors on liver → ↓ ApoB
- Estradiol suppresses PCSK9 → more LDL receptor recycling
- Postmenopausal estrogen decline → ↑ PCSK9 activity → ↓ LDL receptors → ↑ ApoB

**Case Studies — Mother vs. Daughter Pairs:**

| Marker | Mother (age 60, postmenopausal) | Daughter (age 25) |
|---|---|---|
| LDL particle count | ~3,000 | ~800 |
| LDL-C | 258 mg/dL | 100 mg/dL |
| Estradiol | 9.4 pg/mL | 184 pg/mL |
| ApoB | Very elevated | 75 mg/dL |

**Clinical Implication:** If you practice HRT medicine and dose estradiol to FSH 5–24 (Clearfield protocol), follow lipids — you may see ApoB reduction as a bonus.

### 4B. Why Functional Medicine Is at Risk for Lipid Denialism

- Post-COVID distrust of pharma → "threw out the baby with the bathwater"
- Keto/carnivore influencers promote high-LDL-is-safe narrative
- Keto CTA study (Norwitz et al.) — **retracted**; actually showed 2× plaque growth in 1 year
- Dr. Klitz (carnivore influencer): CAC score 700→800, now has 3 coronary blockages

**Case examples from practice:**
- 60yo male: 7-day water fast didn't improve LDL particle count of 3,100
- 60yo male: primary carnivore → ApoB 123–96, HbA1c diabetic range, persistent hyperglycemia
- Carnivore patient: cholesterol 381, LDL-P 3,275 (practice record)

### 4C. ApoB — The Superior Biomarker

**Why ApoB over LDL-C:**
- Unaffected by fasting status (patients who "fast" with honey/oil in coffee)
- Not tricked by high triglycerides (chronic alcoholic case: TG 803, LDL-C looked at goal 68, ApoB still elevated)
- Stronger predictor of fatal MI/stroke than LDL-C (175,000-person study)
- European + Canadian guidelines now favor ApoB
- Cost: ~$5–6 per test

### 4D. Statins — Repurposed for Functional Medicine

**Evidence base:** Cholesterol Treatment Trialists' Collaboration — 170,000 participants, 26 RCTs
- **22% reduction** in major vascular events per 38 mg/dL LDL reduction
- Significant reductions in MI, stroke, revascularization
- Est. 100 million cases prevented/year; 20–35% morbidity reduction

**Functional medicine repurposing of Rosuvastatin:**
1. Suppresses hepatic cholesterol synthesis
2. Lowers CRP and IL-6
3. Reduces oxidative stress
4. Stabilizes endothelial function / ↑ nitric oxide
5. Balances immune responses
6. Used in long-COVID clinics, chronic Lyme/CIRS

**Cost:** Rosuvastatin 5mg = **$0.42/month** ($13.90 per 1,000 tablets from McKesson)  
vs. Mark Houston's supplement protocol = **$740/month**

### 4E. Ezetimibe — Brain & Cardiac Protection

**Mechanisms:**
- Inhibits cholesterol reabsorption in the gut
- Disrupts 14-3-3/hexokinase interaction → ↓ protein aggregation (Alzheimer's)
- Activates autophagy pathways
- Improves cellular resilience

**Evidence:** Large observational cohort (~1 million older adults) → **7-fold reduction** in cognitive decline risk

**Practical:** Dirt cheap, almost zero side effects — even lipid denialists (e.g., Nick Norwitz) are now taking it

### 4F. PCSK9 Inhibitors (Repatha)

- Injectable, twice monthly
- Dramatically ↓ LDL and ApoB
- FOURIER + ODYSSEY trials: significant morbidity reduction
- Loss-of-function PCSK9 mutants (e.g., Andrew DeCairo, LDL of 9) live healthy with minimal cardiovascular risk

### 4G. Imaging Modalities

| Modality | Detects | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **CAC score** | Calcified plaque only | Misses soft plaque (more dangerous) |
| **CT angiogram** ★ | Soft + calcified plaque | More radiation; Hartman's preferred |
| **CIMT** | Carotid intima-media thickness | Useful screen |
| **Carotid US** | Stenosis | Often insurance-covered |

### 4H. Plaque Reversal Evidence

- **REVERSAL, ASTEROID, SATURN trials**: Plaque regression with optimal lipid lowering
- ApoB < 65 → consistently shows plaque regression, stabilization, thicker fibrous caps
- Mendelian randomization studies + familial hypercholesterolemia cases provide causal proof

### 4I. Nattokinase Warning ⚠️

Hartman shares case of patient with carotid disease who tried nattokinase → suspected plaque destabilization → stroke (survived via emergency revascularization). Evidence is weak; may carry risk of plaque rupture.

## Segment 5 — The Niacin Debate (1:02:00–1:15:00)

**Speakers:** Steven McConnell (Speaker 7), Stefan Hartman, Dr. Collins, Victor (Speaker 9)

### Steven McConnell — Pro-Niacin Position

**Clinical experience:** ~5,000 patients started on niacin; 90% retention rate

**Key claims:**
- Niacin is the **only monotherapy** that moves every lipoprotein in the right direction
- Lowers LP(a) by up to 75% (his wife's case: 40% → 63% → 75% over 11 years — fatigues ApoA gene expression)
- Coronary Drug Project: **90% relative risk reduction**, NNT < 4
- Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, anti-fibrotic
- Raises growth hormone → normalizes deiodinase (T4→T3 conversion)
- ↓ Oxidized phospholipids (only drug that does this)
- Published data on reversing kidney disease with niacin
- ↑ GFR, ↓ PTH, ↓ FGF-23
- FDA: #1 of 150 approved drugs for fatty liver (2025 data)
- Shrinking glioblastomas (Alberta, Canada research)

**Dosing protocol (from Dr. Castelli):**
1. Start at 50mg per meal (very low)
2. Titrate slowly over weeks
3. Take with food
4. Educate patient on flushing management
5. Target: 3g/day for full effect
6. Side effects (flushing, burning) resolve within 2 weeks–1 month

**Critique of AIM-HIGH / HPS2-THRIVE:**
- Used niacin improperly; Cleveland Clinic didn't even use niacin in their trial
- 4-methylnicotinamide (4py) blamed on niacin is actually from junk food metabolism

### Practical Takeaway for Statin-Intolerant or Uninsured Patients

## Segment 7 — Practical Applications & Patient Cases (1:15:00–1:40:00)

### Familial Hypercholesterolemia in Children
- Conventional pediatric guidelines now recommend lipid panels as early as age 8
- Hartman: test ApoB in kids as early as age 9; treat if elevated
- Case: 15yo obese male, father died of MI at 40 → ApoB 130, TG 200 → candidate for lipid therapy
- McConnell: 7yo girl with Coke-bottle glasses (retinal damage), 3yo boy post-stroke — FH family on Medicaid

### Statin Side Effects — Vitamin D Solution
- **Vitamin D target ~40 ng/mL** → 93% of statin myalgia resolved
- Mechanism: statins deplete cholesterol precursors → disrupts vitamin D and CoQ10 synthesis
- CoQ10 less effective than vitamin D for myalgia per McConnell's experience

### Desmosterol and Brain Health
- If desmosterol driven too low by statins → cognitive impairment (especially in ApoE4 carriers)
- ApoB cannot cross blood-brain barrier → brain depends on local cholesterol synthesis
- Reversible if statin removed within ~5 years; may be permanent after
- Boston Heart Diagnostics: only lab still carrying desmosterol assay

### Stefan's Summary Protocol
> 1. Get ApoB below 60 (prevention) or below 40 (established CVD)
> 2. First-line: Rosuvastatin 5mg + Ezetimibe 10mg (~$1/month combined)
> 3. If statin-intolerant: Ezetimibe alone + niacin + lifestyle
> 4. Follow ApoB serially — don't rely on CAC score
> 5. CT angiogram preferred for plaque assessment
> 6. Optimize estradiol (women) — may independently lower ApoB
