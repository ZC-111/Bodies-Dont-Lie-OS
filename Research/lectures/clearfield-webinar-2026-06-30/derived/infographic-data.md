## ⚡ Infographic 5: "Niacin — The Misunderstood Miracle"

**Title:** 5 Things You Didn't Know About Niacin

1. **Only monotherapy** that moves every lipoprotein favorably
2. **Lowers LP(a) up to 75%** (fatigues ApoA gene expression over years)
3. **$5/month** — accessible for uninsured patients
4. **Raises growth hormone** → normalizes T4→T3 conversion
5. **#1 of 150 FDA drugs** for fatty liver (2025)

**Dosing ladder:**
```
Week 1–2:  50mg × 3 meals = 150mg/day
Week 3–4:  100mg × 3 meals = 300mg/day
Month 2:   250mg × 3 meals = 750mg/day
Month 3+:  Titrate to 1g × 3 = 3g/day
```
**Rule:** Always with food. Flushing resolves in 2–4 weeks.
