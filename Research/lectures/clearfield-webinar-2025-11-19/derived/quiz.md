## Section A — GMOs & Food System Tip (3 questions)

### Q1. According to Nario’s tip, what is the primary design goal of most U.S. GMO commodity crops?
- A) Higher micronutrient density for human mitochondria
- B) **Cheaper, faster, more chemically efficient large-scale farming** ✓
- C) Improved soil microbiome diversity
- D) Lower total herbicide use across acreage

> *Convenience for agribusiness/chemical systems — not nutritional upgrade.*

### Q2. True or False: GMO concerns about sugar beets also apply equally to beetroot nitric-oxide supplements.
**Answer:** **False.** Sugar beets are a volume GMO sucrose crop; beetroot supplements come from red beet varieties grown for nutrient density (nitrates, antioxidants, polyphenols) and are typically non-GMO.

### Q3. If a processed-food label lists only “sugar,” what does Nario say it usually is in the U.S.?
**Answer:** Often **GMO beet sugar**, unless the label specifically says **cane**.

## Section B — Stress–Mineral Physiology & HTMA (8 questions)

### Q4. How does Hansen primarily use HTMA in coaching?

### Q5. In the proposed sympathetic stress pattern on HTMA, cells tend to:
- A) Dump sodium and retain potassium
- B) **Retain sodium, push out potassium, use up magnesium, mobilize calcium into tissue (“calcium shell”)** ✓
- C) Raise magnesium while lowering calcium
- D) Only alter zinc without touching Na/K/Ca/Mg

### Q6. What does a “calcium shell” clinically signal for Hansen?
**Answer:** CNS “numbing” / push-through stress physiology (athletes, postpartum, night shift). Theorized links to bone loss risk and soft-tissue calcification. It does **not** mean patients should avoid dietary calcium.

### Q7. On the lab she uses, calcium above approximately what level is considered an extreme calcium shell?
- A) 80
- B) 120
- C) **180** ✓
- D) 400

> *Case: night-shift client Ca 321.*

### Q8. Which four minerals does Hansen prioritize first when reading HTMA ratios?
**Answer:** **Sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium.**

### Q9. What daily potassium intake target does she commonly coach toward?
- A) 1,500 mg
- B) 2,300 mg
- C) **About 4,000 mg (slightly above the ~3,400 mg AI)** ✓
- D) 8,000 mg

### Q10. Approximately how much potassium can ~¼ teaspoon of cream of tartar add when mixed into a drink?
- A) 50 mg
- B) 150 mg
- C) **~500 mg** ✓
- D) 2,000 mg

### Q11. Why does Accutane history matter in her mineral/vitamin A discussion?
**Answer:** Multiple Accutane courses can **deplete hepatic vitamin A**, which then cascades into copper bioavailability and iron-recycling support needs (acne clients).
