# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-11-19

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Stress drains minerals.** Sympathetic tone retains Na, dumps K, burns Mg, and mobilizes Ca into a tissue “shell” — HTMA can make unrecognized stress visible.

2. **Calcium shell ≠ calcium restriction.** High hair calcium signals CNS-numbing physiology (athletes, postpartum, night shift). Keep dietary calcium; fix the stress driver.

3. **HTMA is a coaching lens, not a verdict.** ~$50 (ARL), useful for ratios/progress; pair with blood micronutrients, urine iodine, and Fe/ferritin/ceruloplasmin when Cu–Fe questions matter.

4. **One mineral rule: raise potassium.** Aim ~4,000 mg/day; ¼ tsp cream of tartar ≈ 500 mg K. Magnesium is the close second (~300 enzymatic roles).

5. **Replete and de-stress together.** Minerals without boundary/HPA work (and vice versa) underperform — STAIN lifestyle factors can override fancy protocols.

6. **Beliefs are biology.** Oxytocin, HeartMath-style coherence, and subconscious clearing change the cellular environment; hormones often regulate **last**.

7. **Copper excess can feel like anxiety.** ↑ Norepinephrine → sympathetic irritability; respect Cu:Zn, especially with copper-containing products.

8. **Depression stack order:** adrenals → thyroid → testosterone → estrogen. Cycle-worsened mood → check progesterone:estrogen balance.

9. **GMO tip:** commodity traits serve chemical farming, not patient mitochondria. “Sugar” often = GMO beet; beetroot supplements are a different plant story.

10. **Hair-test caveats:** dye wait 3–4 months; root-proximal sample; regional metal/mineral patterns exist; hair misses many mercury forms.
