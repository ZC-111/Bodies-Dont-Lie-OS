## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: GMOs in the U.S. Food System (19:05–23:24)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 4)

### ★ Core Thesis
> U.S. GMOs exist mainly for large-scale farming convenience and chemical systems — not to improve mitochondria, soil, or nutrient density.

### Crops Covered
| Crop | Pearl |
|---|---|
| **Soy** | Nearly ubiquitous; herbicide-tolerant; in protein bars, oils, restaurant food |
| **Corn** | Field corn → syrup, starch, oil; mono-cropping + glyphosate-resistant weeds |

### Clinical Pearl — Sugar Beets ≠ Beetroot
- **Sugar beets:** volume crop, GMO, sprayed, refined to sucrose
- **Beetroot / NO supplements:** red beet varieties, typically non-GMO, retain nitrates, antioxidants, betalains, polyphenols

### Take-Home
- Choose **non-GMO / organic** to reduce herbicide load and support soil-respecting agriculture
- Risk lands on consumers, clinicians, and environment; benefits accrue to agribusiness

## Segment 3 — Kelly Hansen: Stress, Minerals, HTMA & Somatic Healing (26:33–59:32)

**Speaker:** Kelly Hansen, RD — ~5 years clinical experience (bio incorrectly said “two decades”)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Chronic stress depletes minerals and drives anxiety, fatigue, brain fog, and mood swings. Replenish minerals **and** remove the stress driver — HTMA is a coaching/education tool, not a standalone diagnostic.

### 3A. Background & STAIN Framework
- Athletic overtraining history → amenorrhea, cystic acne post–birth control; suboptimal D, iodine, omega-3, antioxidants; estrogen dominance on DUTCH; “calcium shell” on HTMA
- RAGBRAI overtraining → severe dysmenorrhea that resolved when returning to yoga/weights + lower stress
- **STAIN** (IFN Academy): lifestyle factors that can override functional interventions
- Client mix: mainly women ~20–45 (+ peri/menopause); disordered eating, PCOS, endometriosis, weight management

### 3B. Stress Drivers of HPA Axis Dysfunction
- Over-exercise without recovery
- Postpartum depletion (can last years)
- Long-term hormonal birth control
- Lack of boundaries / perfectionism / chronic body shame
- Blood-sugar roller coaster → anxiety + stress hormones
- Scarcity / fear-based thinking (even in patients who deny “stress”)

### 3C. HTMA — How She Uses It
- **Not diagnostic** — progress monitor + “wake-up call” that stress is unrecognized
- Accessible cost (~**$50** Analytical Research Labs; washes hair)
- Pulls: oxidation rate (slow / mixed / fast) → macronutrient hints; stress ratios; high calcium
- Also uses Vibrant America micronutrient blood panel; **urine iodine**; blood for iron / ceruloplasmin / ferritin when needed

### 3D. Proposed Stress–Mineral Mechanism
```
Sympathetic activation
  → Cells retain Na, push out K (toward rest/digest recovery)
  → Mg used to calm nervous system
  → Ca mobilized (bone → tissue) = "calcium shell" (numbs CNS)
  → Possible copper status shifts
```
- **Calcium shell:** common in “push through” athletes, postpartum, night-shift workers; symbolic of CNS numbing; theorized link to osteoporosis risk / soft-tissue calcification — **does not** mean avoid dietary calcium
- First four minerals to prioritize: **Na, K, Ca, Mg**

### 3E. Copper–Iron–Vitamin A Axis
- Copper + vitamin A needed for iron recycling
- High estrogen can influence Fe/Cu status
- “Bio-unavailable” copper: support vitamin A (± vitamin C) → bind ceruloplasmin
- Low copper → possible anemia contributor; high serum copper associated with PCOS
- Accutane (multiple rounds) → depletes hepatic vitamin A → cascade into Cu/Fe work

### 3F. Sodium & Potassium Targets
- Encourage adequate Na + **K aiming ~4,000 mg/day** (AI recently ~3,400; she pushes a bit higher)
- Practical K boosters:
  - Cream of tartar ~¼ tsp → ~**500 mg** K (in electrolyte drink/juice)
  - Coconut water, potatoes, dairy
- Low-carb diets often underdeliver potassium
- Electrolyte drinks + animal foods / dairy as mineral supports

### 3G. Foundations Beyond Supplements

## Segment 4 — Q&A: Minerals, Mood, Adrenals, Hair Testing (59:32–1:26:00)

**Speakers:** Kelly Hansen, Melvin Nario, Bill Clearfield, audience

### Testing Modality
- HTMA preferred for **stress/nervous-system pattern** + cost; blood micronutrients still valuable; urine for iodine
- Copper/zinc: prioritize Na/K/Ca/Mg first; **blood** essential for accurate Zn/Cu
- Dietary liver trial for Cu + vitamin A in select clients (mood improvement noted personally)

### Copper Toxicity Pearl (Nario)
- Excess copper ↑ norepinephrine → sympathetic drive → anxiety, irritability, mood shifts
- Watch Cu:Zn when using copper-containing products (e.g., Immunogenics colloidal silver + copper — minute µg doses; still pair with Zn in cold season)

### Lithium (Nario clinical observation)
- Low hair lithium common in cancer patients with comorbid anxiety/depression; considering formal study
- Jonathan Wright note (chat): low-dose lithium orotate **5–25 mg/day** — mood balance, hangover prevention, neuronal support; levels not required at that dose
- Kelly: not a primary HTMA focus yet; open to learning

### One Mineral Rule (Hansen)
> **Increase potassium intake** (magnesium a close second; Nario’s favorite for ~300 enzymatic processes)

### Copper & Alzheimer’s Mechanism Hint
- Oxidative stress → impaired ceruloplasmin → unbound high copper → further oxidative stress + mitochondrial ETC injury (esp. neurons)

### Depression / Thyroid / Adrenal Cascade
- Audience case: 16yo post-COVID PTSD context, cortisol ↑, pituitary assist?, thyroid chaos, cycle-worsened anxiety/depression
- Panel consensus: **adrenals first**, then thyroid, then testosterone, then estrogen (Clearfield “four hormones of depression”)
- Mid-cycle + late-luteal worsening → relative **progesterone deficiency** vs estrogen — check ratios
- Homeopathy (Gerber): Staphysagria (old abuse), Ignatia (acute grief), Arnica (old injuries/head trauma), Thuja (vaccine damage); many more for depression; also rule out occult infection

### Hair Testing Logistics
- Analytical Research Labs ~$50; Doctor’s Data ~$79 (minerals + toxic metals)
- Regional patterns: Clearfield — high **uranium** (downwind nuclear testing history); Orlando — high **copper** / melasma; Brevard County iodine deficiency publication (Quinn)
- Caveats: hair dye (wait 3–4 months); avoid sweaty/greasy hair; sample **~1 inch closest to root**; methyl-mercury may appear in hair but other Hg forms missed — hair not ideal sole mercury toxicity screen

### Strength Training
- Hansen emphasizes women’s strength programs (app/community); personal cycles respond better to strength than hard cardio
- Hartman/panel: overdone hard cardio / ultra-endurance often looks metabolically costly
