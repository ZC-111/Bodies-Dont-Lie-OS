## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Three Jobs of a Biomarker"

**Title:** Why Test — Not Guess — in Longevity Medicine

```
1. DETECT  → Find abnormalities & deficiencies early
2. DEFINE  → Set OPTIMAL ranges (≠ lab “normal”)
3. PROVE   → Measure whether the intervention worked
```

**Footer:** Wellness programs without biomarkers are mostly hope. Testing enables personalization, precision, and prioritization.

## 📉 Infographic 2: "hsCRP — Lower Is Better"

**Title:** The Rare Marker Without a U-Curve

| hsCRP Level | Longevity Framing (Shea) |
|---|---|
| **> 3 mg/L** | Common even in “healthy” adults (~20–30%); higher risk |
| **~1 mg/L** | Conventional CVD risk threshold zone |
| **< 0.5 mg/L** | Better all-cause mortality than ~1 mg/L |
| **→ 0** | ★ Clinical aspiration — drive inflammation down |

**Callout:** Use **hsCRP** for chronic risk; standard CRP for acute spikes only.

**Title:** Sweet Spot — Not Maximum

```
        DANGER / OVERSHOOT
              │  > 100 μM
              │  (2PY/4PY, vascular inflammation risk)
    ──────────┼──────────
    IDEAL     │  50–75 μM  ★

    ──────────┼──────────
```

**Footer:** Retest 3–4 weeks after starting precursors. Elite athletes can still be severely deficient.

## 💊 Infographic 4: "How to Raise NAD — What Actually Works"

**Title:** Precursors Beat Infusion Theater

| Method | Intracellular NAD | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Oral **NMN** | Strong | ★ Preferred (Shea) |
| Oral **NR** | Strong | Excellent alternative |
| Oral **niacin** | Can overshoot (~150 μM) | Monitor carefully |
| **IV NAD** | Minimal | Extracellular / t½ ~6 h |
| IM/SQ NAD | Partial | Rarely optimizes alone |

**Dosing ladder (NMN):**
```
Start:  1000 mg/day  → ~75% hit optimal
Need more: ~1500 mg  → ~25% of patients
Need less: <1000 mg  → ~5% (avoid overshoot)
```

## 🧬 Infographic 5: "Klotho — The Dynamic Longevity Protein"

**Title:** Named for the Goddess Who Spins Lifespan

```
SOURCES:  Mostly kidney (+ some brain)

LOWERS:   Oxidative stress
          Inflammation
          Psychological stress / depression

BEHAVIOR: Moves fast — like CRP
          COVID case: ~5+ → 2.8 → recovered >6

GOAL:     Avoid deep lows (<<5); extreme lows ↔
          cancer, long COVID, CKD, Alzheimer’s signals
```

**Action today:** Fix health drivers first. Gene therapy (e.g., BioViva) exists; no cheap validated pill yet. Bacterial Klotho proteins — doubtful (glycosylation).

## 🔥 Infographic 6: "Oxidative Stress — The Overlooked Epidemic"

**Title:** Americans vs Europeans

```
USA:        ~70%+ high oxidative stress
Europe:     ~10% high oxidative stress

Likely drivers: lifestyle + food system

Why it matters:
  • All-cause mortality
  • Cancer outcomes (e.g., cervical cancer survival)
  • Feeds senescence & NAD decline
```

**Callout:** Everyone talks inflammation. Far fewer measure oxidative stress — Shea says fix both.

## 🗺️ Infographic 7: "Personalization Heat Map Logic"

**Title:** Fix the Top Three Killers First

```
RED    = Most deficient / urgent
ORANGE = Significant issue
YELLOW = Suboptimal
WHITE  = Optimal
```

**Cases that prove the point:**

**Footer:** Appearance of health ≠ absence of sub-health. Test → prioritize → retest.
