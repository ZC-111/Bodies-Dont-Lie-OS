# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-11-20

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Biomarkers have three jobs:** find deficiencies, define *optimal* (not just normal), and prove whether interventions work — especially in longevity programs where outcomes are otherwise fuzzy.

2. **Drive hsCRP toward zero.** <0.5 mg/L associates with better all-cause mortality than levels around ~1 mg/L. Inflammation is one of the rare markers that is not U-shaped.

3. **Most longevity labs are U-shaped.** HbA1c/glycation too low *or* too high raises mortality — aim for the middle sweet spot, not “as low as possible.”

4. **Vitamin D target >50 ng/mL** in Shea’s framing (30 is not optimal). Cheap intervention; majority of Americans still deficient.

5. **Klotho behaves like CRP** — dynamic with illness (COVID case: ~5+ → 2.8 → recovered >6). No validated single pill yet; best lever is fixing inflammation/oxidative stress/overall health.

6. **NAD is a force-multiplier.** Higher NAD → ~80% response to anti-inflammation protocols vs ~30% when NAD is low. Optimize NAD so *other* treatments work.

7. **NAD target window: 40–100 μM (ideally 50–75).** Above ~100 μM risks nicotinamide metabolites (2PY/4PY) and vascular inflammation. Test — don’t guess.

8. **Prefer oral NMN/NR over IV NAD.** IV NAD does not raise intracellular NAD (t½ ~6 h); any benefit is extracellular/temporary. Pair IV with oral precursors if used at all.

9. **NMN ≈ NR for raising NAD;** Shea slightly prefers NMN (one enzymatic step closer; NR kinase dependency). Start ~1000 mg NMN; ~75% hit target; titrate with labs at 3–4 weeks.

10. **Looks healthy ≠ metabolically optimized.** Elite athletes can have NAD <20 μM. Personalization heat maps prioritize the top 3 “killers” per patient.

## Comparison: Raising NAD — Routes & Precursors

| Approach | Intracellular NAD | Overshoot risk | Practical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oral NMN** | Strong | ~5% at 1000 mg exceed 100 μM | Preferred; start 1000 mg, retest 3–4 wk |
| **Oral NR** | Strong (comparable) | Similar class risk | Needs NR kinase step |
| **Oral niacin** | Can go very high (~150 μM) | High | Monitor NAD; 2PY/4PY CV concern |
| **IV NAD** | Minimal intracellular | Cost/invasiveness | Extracellular only; add oral precursors |
| **IM/SQ NAD** | Partial | Dose/frequency limited | Rarely optimizes alone |

**Consensus from session:** Oral precursors + serial NAD testing beat infusion theater for intracellular optimization.
