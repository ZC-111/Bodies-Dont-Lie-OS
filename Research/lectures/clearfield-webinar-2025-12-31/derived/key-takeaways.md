# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-12-31

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Bioregulators are short (≈2–4 AA) epigenetic normalizers**, not forcing hormones — microgram dosing, tissue-specific gene expression, patients already self-sourcing.

2. **Prefer ascorbate over ascorbic acid** for high-dose oral tolerance and IV use; watch **sodium load** with IV ascorbate in HF/renal patients.

3. **VCICI-style oral “adequate” ≈ 4 g/day** (split AM/PM), **+1 g per chronic stressor**; plasma ascorbate >50 µmol/L / leukocyte >15 µg as adequacy anchors.

4. **Vitamin C is an endocrine cofactor** — documented talk claims include ↑ testosterone (~2 g), ↑ progesterone (~750 mg × 3 mo → ~77%), T4→T3 / ↓ TPO, improved insulin sensitivity, cortisol–DHEA rebalancing.

5. **Vitamin C can raise estradiol** with OCPs/BHRT → monitor for **estrogen dominance**.

6. **5α-reductase inhibitors can impair the progesterone → allopregnanolone → GABA pathway** — screen mood/sleep risk before finasteride/dutasteride, especially young men on 1 mg.

7. **Microbiome care = ecosystem diversity**, not Akkermansia-or-bust; biofilms, redundancy, and metabolites matter more than one hero bug.

8. **Chronic dysbiosis often needs restoration, not endless Rifaximin/antibiotic cycles**; antifungals/antivirals/antipsychotics also tax diversity.

9. **FMT is ecosystem transplant / genetic backup**, not a guaranteed panacea; capsule C. diff dosing often 30-capsule bolus (± multi-day for virulent disease); bloating ≠ automatic SIBO.

10. **Rotate probiotics** — benefit may be horizontal gene transfer / metabolite fill-in even when species don’t “stick” on retesting; keep pre/post tests on the **same lab**.

## Comparison Snapshot: Suppress vs Restore

| Dimension | Antimicrobial-first | Restoration-first (Dobner lean) |
|---|---|---|
| **Acute C. diff / sepsis** | Often appropriate | Adjunct / emerging primary FMT data |
| **SIBO / IBS / IBD cycles** | Risk of diversity collapse | Ferments, diet, diversity rebuild |
| **Mental model** | Clear forest → plant | Diversify around existing community |
| **Patient history clue** | Lifelong antibiotic stacks | Lower diversity + metabolic unhealth |
| **Success metric** | Pathogen negative | Shannon/beta diversity + function |
