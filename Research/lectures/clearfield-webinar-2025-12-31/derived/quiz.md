## Section A — Bioregulators (4 questions)

### Q1. Bioregulators, as defined in Nario’s tip, are typically:
- A) 20–40 amino acid receptor agonists
- B) **Short peptides of about 2–4 amino acids that restore tissue-specific gene expression** ✓
- C) Full-length recombinant hormones
- D) Synthetic steroids that override nuclear receptors

### Q2. Which statement best matches the Epitalon/epithalamin fibroblast finding cited (Khavinson 2003)?
- A) Telomeres shortened with malignant transformation
- B) **Telomerase activated, telomeres lengthened, cells exceeded the Hayflick limit without malignant transformation** ✓
- C) Only melatonin receptors were upregulated
- D) Thymic involution accelerated

### Q3. Thymalin is framed clinically as addressing:
- A) Pure immunostimulation like a vaccine adjuvant
- B) **Immunosenescence — restoring T-cell differentiation / CD4:CD8 balance** ✓
- C) Acute anaphylaxis only
- D) Direct cortisol replacement

### Q4. True or False: Bioregulators are FDA-approved and interchangeable with high-dose forcing peptides.
**Answer:** **False.** Presented as **not FDA-approved**, low microgram dosing, epigenetic normalization tools — not hormone/receptor override agents.

## Section B — Vitamin C Form, Dose & Endocrine Effects (8 questions)

### Q5. For oral tolerance and IV use, Clearfield prefers:
- A) Ascorbic acid for both oral high-dose and IV
- B) **Ascorbate (buffered) for sensitive GI tracts and as the IV standard; caution sodium load** ✓
- C) Only liposomal ascorbic acid
- D) Ascorbic acid IV because it is less acidic

### Q6. The VCICI-style “adequate” oral starting target emphasized in the talk is approximately:
- A) 90 mg/day (RDA)
- B) 500 mg/day
- C) **4,000 mg/day orally (+ ~1,000 mg per additional chronic stressor)** ✓
- D) 50 g/day orally

### Q7. Which dose was cited for increasing testosterone in infertile/older men (>41)?
- A) 200 mg/day
- B) **2,000 mg/day** ✓
- C) 20 g IV only
- D) 75 mg/day

### Q8. Vitamin C with BHRT or OCPs may:
- A) Always lower estradiol
- B) **Increase serum estradiol and potentially worsen estrogen dominance (↑ E2:P)** ✓
- C) Irreversibly destroy progesterone receptors
- D) Have no effect on sex steroids

### Q9. What progesterone response was cited with 750 mg ascorbate for 3 months in luteal-phase defect context?
**Answer:** Approximately a **77% increase** in progesterone levels.

### Q10. Clearfield’s quick HOMA-IR calculation and goals were:
- A) Insulin × glucose / 22.5; goal <5
- B) **(Fasting insulin × fasting glucose) / 405; goal <1.9 (normal <2.9)** ✓
- C) A1c alone; goal <5.0
- D) Triglyceride/HDL ratio only

### Q11. Why is finasteride/dutasteride a concern in the GABA/neurosteroid discussion?
**Answer:** 5α-reductase also converts progesterone → dihydroprogesterone → **allopregnanolone** (GABA tone). Blocking it may contribute to psychiatric adverse effects; special caution in young men on 1 mg and those with mood/sleep risk factors.

### Q12. Practical split for 4 g/day oral vitamin C:
**Answer:** **2 g after morning meal + 2 g after evening meal.**

## Section C — Microbiome Ecosystem & FMT (8 questions)

### Q13. Dobner’s preferred primary readout on stool testing for restoration is:
- A) Presence/absence of Akkermansia alone
- B) **Diversity metrics (e.g., Shannon / beta diversity) and ecosystem function** ✓
- C) Only *C. difficile* toxin
- D) Blood CRP

### Q14. In the primary C. diff study discussed, FMT by enema vs vancomycin was described as:
- A) FMT clearly superior with 96% cure
- B) Vancomycin 95%, FMT 20%
- C) **Non-inferior (~70% each), with fewer relapses in the FMT arm — despite enema being a weaker delivery route** ✓
- D) Enema FMT unsafe and abandoned

### Q15. Which FMT delivery pair was cited as highly efficacious (~96%) historically?
- A) Nasogastric only vs topical cream
- B) **Colonoscopic delivery and frozen oral capsules** ✓
- C) Skin patch vs sublingual spray
- D) IV FMT vs intramuscular

### Q16. For standard C. diff capsule dosing in her practice, day-1 bolus is typically:
- A) 1 capsule with food
- B) **30 capsules on an empty stomach after a prior fasting day** ✓
- C) 10 capsules weekly for a year only
- D) Continuous NG drip for 30 days

### Q17. What is Dobner’s main critique of “clear the forest, then plant” antibiotic pretreatment before FMT in non–C. diff settings?
**Answer:** She questions whether antimicrobial wipeout is necessary before diversification; chronic patients are often already depleted, and further suppression can worsen the ecosystem problem restoration is meant to fix.

### Q18. The biggest patient misconception about FMT, per Dobner:
- A) That capsules can cause choking
- B) **That FMT is a panacea / guaranteed overnight cure for all conditions** ✓
- C) That donors need screening
- D) That bloating can occur

### Q19. Common early adverse effect of capsule FMT and first-line mitigation:
**Answer:** **Bloating** (often mislabeled as SIBO) — mitigate with **bitter herbs/tinctures** for ~2–4 weeks; theoretical anaphylaxis/hyper-reactivity is on consent but frank anaphylaxis rare in her experience.

### Q20. Why might a patient feel better on a probiotic without the named species appearing on follow-up stool testing?
**Answer:** **Horizontal gene transfer / metabolite capacity** may transfer into the resident biofilm community without durable colonization by the labeled probiotic species — one reason she recommends **rotating** probiotics.
