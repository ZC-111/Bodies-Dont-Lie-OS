## 🧬 Infographic 1: "Bioregulators ≠ Forcing Peptides"

**Title:** Epigenetic Post-it Notes for Aging Tissues

| Feature | Classic forcing peptide / hormone | Bioregulator (2–4 AA) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary action | Push receptors / replace hormones | Restore tissue-specific gene expression |
| Dose scale | Often mg | Often **µg** |
| Mental model | Override | Remind / normalize |
| FDA status (as framed) | Variable | **Not FDA-approved** |
| Clinical conversation | Protocol-driven | Office education (patients already self-sourcing) |

**Named map:** Epitalon (pineal/telomere) · Thymalin (thymus/T-cells) · Pinealon · Vasogen · cartilage & liver agents

**Footer:** Aging = loss of signaling fidelity — not only “damage.”

## 🍊 Infographic 2: "Ascorbic Acid vs Ascorbate"

**Title:** Same Endpoint, Different Patient Experience

```
ASCORBIC ACID                    ASCORBATE (BUFFERED)
pH ~2–3                          pH ~6–9
Cheap / abundant                 Better GI tolerance
>2 g → heartburn / irritation    Preferred high-dose oral
Not preferred for IV             IV standard (adds Na+)
                                 Caution: HF / renal / Na-sensitive
         \                         /
          \                       /
           → Both become ASCORBATE in blood ←
```

**Callout:** Clearfield clinic stopped carrying ascorbic-acid tablets after audit — half the SKUs were the wrong form.

## 💊 Infographic 3: "Vitamin C Dose Ladder (as taught)"

**Title:** From RDA to Conference “Adequate”

| Level | Dose | Context |
|---|---|---|
| RDA | 75–90 mg/day | Deficiency prevention only |
| Insulin-sensitivity signal | **>200 mg** | Antioxidant / sensitivity |
| Thyroid Ab study dose | **500 mg × 3 months** | ↓ TPO (cited) |
| Testosterone support | **2,000 mg/day** | Older/infertile men |
| Conference oral adequate | **4,000 mg/day** | Split AM/PM with meals |
| Per chronic stressor | **+1,000 mg** | e.g., Hashimoto’s → 5 g |
| IV pharmacologic | **20–50 g/day** | Cancer / chronic protocols |

**Lab targets:** Plasma ascorbate **>50 µmol/L** · Leukocyte ascorbate **>15 µg**

## 🧪 Infographic 7: "FMT Delivery & C. Diff Dosing"

**Title:** How You Deliver Matters

| Route | Relative note from talk |
|---|---|
| Colonoscopy | Gold standard |
| Frozen oral capsules | ≈ colonoscopy efficacy historically (~96%) |
| Enema | Weaker — yet primary-therapy study still **non-inferior** to vancomycin (~70%) with **fewer relapses** |

**Capsule C. diff playbook:**
```
Day 0: Fast
Day 1: 30 capsules empty stomach
Virulent / failed pharma / multi-recurrence:
  Day 1: 30  →  Days 2–4: 10 + 10 + 10
```

**Chronic disease:** Bolus + weekly/daily × 4–8+ weeks (indication-dependent)
