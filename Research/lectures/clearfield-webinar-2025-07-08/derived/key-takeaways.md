# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-07-08

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **PBM ≠ “any red light.”** Photobiomodulation usually means red/NIR; deep and brain targets often need invisible NIR (~800–1070 nm), not a bright visible red glow.

2. **Mechanism stack:** cytochrome c oxidase → ↑ ATP; NO displacement → flow/O₂; hormetic ROS → resilience; downstream ↑ BDNF and anti-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL-10).

3. **Dose is hormetic.** Once–twice daily near the device’s sweet spot beats marathon sessions; past the sweet spot → headache, sleepiness, diminished benefit.

4. **Hydrate before every session.** IR interacts with body water; dehydration blunts benefit and raises headache/fatigue risk.

5. **40 Hz** is the pulse with the clearest cognitive / working-memory signal; **10 Hz** often feels calmer; constant wave still covers most published evidence.

6. **tPBM evidence spans** stroke aphasia (small n), long COVID fog, depression, TBI/concussion, autism behaviors, MCI/dementia, and sleep efficiency — mostly early/small studies but clinically encouraging.

7. **Safety gates:** active cancer, non-titanium cranial metal, implanted stimulators (esp. pulsed). Epilepsy is **not** an automatic contraindication for non-ocular head PBM.

8. **Hashimoto’s signal (Clearfield cite):** 830 nm, ~10 sessions / 5 weeks — 47% reduced or stopped levothyroxine in one 2013 laser study; TPO Abs and echogenicity improved.

9. **Tribulus for PCOS:** 250–750 mg/day standardized extract; cited trials at **750 mg × 2–3 months** restored ovulation via LH/FSH modulation **without significant T rise** — pair with inositol + berberine.

10. **Buy with specs.** Demand irradiance / testing data; generic Amazon “red light” panels are often underpowered. Neuronic framing: ~$1,795 home / ~$3,400 clinical 1070 nm helmet.

## Comparison Table: Wavelength Intent

| Band | Approx. range (talk framing) | Best suited for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visible red** | ~600s nm | Skin, superficial | What users “see” |
| **Near-infrared** | ~750–1100+ nm | Deeper tissue / brain | Often invisible; tPBM sweet zone |
| **Far-infrared** | ~3000 nm | Systemic warmth | Water heating — not cytochrome-c PBM |

**Consensus:** Match wavelength and form factor (helmet/wrap vs flat panel) to the anatomic target; closeness and bare skin matter as much as brand hype.
