## ⚡ Infographic 3: "How Red/NIR Light Works"

**Title:** From Photon to Cellular Energy

```
Red / NIR photons
  │
  ├── Cytochrome c oxidase absorbs light
  │         → ↑ ATP production
  │
  ├── Displaces nitric oxide from blocked sites
  │         → ↑ oxygen delivery + blood flow
  │
  ├── Controlled ROS “workout” signal
  │         → cellular resilience / recovery programs
  │
  └── Downstream
            → ↑ BDNF, ↑ IL-10, neuroprotection
            → possible BBB + glymphatic support
```

**Footer:** Hormesis rule — sweet spot dosing; more is not always better.

## 📶 Infographic 5: "Pulse Rate Cheat Sheet"

**Title:** Constant vs Pulsed Light (Emerging Guidance)

| Setting | Practical takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Constant** | Most published protocols; safe default |
| **40 Hz** | Working memory / brain brightening / amyloid microglial interest |
| **10 Hz** | Often “calmer”; may penetrate deeper than constant |
| **14 Hz** | Provider anecdote for long COVID / ANS dysregulation |

**Callout:** Light itself drives much of the benefit; frequency is refinement, not magic alone (panel discussion).

## ⚠️ Infographic 6: "Safety Gate Checklist"

**Title:** Before You Put Light on a Head

```
STOP / CAUTION
  ├── Active cancer (angiogenesis concern)
  ├── Non-titanium metal in head (shrapnel, DBS)
  ├── Implanted stimulators + pulsed light (caution)
  └── Hyperthyroid (manufacturer precaution)

OK / MYTH BUST
  ├── Epilepsy ≠ automatic contraindication (non-ocular head PBM)
  ├── Titanium skull hardware generally acceptable
  └── Hydrate first to cut headache/fatigue risk
```

## 💊 Infographic 7: "PCOS Stack — Tribulus in Context"

**Title:** Supporting Ovulation Without Spiking T

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| **Inositol** | Insulin sensitivity + ovulation support |
| **Berberine** | “Natural metformin” — glucose/lipids |
| **Tribulus 250–750 mg/day** | LH modulation; restore ovulation (750 mg × 2–3 mo in cited studies) |

**Myths cleared:** Does not significantly raise testosterone in women at studied doses.

**Avoid:** Pregnancy; hormone-sensitive cancers. Watch for mild GI upset.
