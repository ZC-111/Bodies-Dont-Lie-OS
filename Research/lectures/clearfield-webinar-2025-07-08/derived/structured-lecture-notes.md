## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: PCOS Supplements / Tribulus terrestris (12:32–17:00)

**Speaker:** Dr. Melvin Nario (Speaker 4)

### ★ Core Thesis
> **Tribulus terrestris** (puncture vine / caltrop) can support ovulation and LH/FSH balance in PCOS **without meaningfully raising testosterone** in women.

### Key Concepts
- PCOS affects up to **~10%** of reproductive-age women
- Conventional first-line: metformin, oral contraceptives
- Common integrative tools already in use: **inositol** (insulin sensitivity / ovulation), **berberine** (“natural metformin” for glucose/lipids)
- Traditional reputation of Tribulus = male libido / “testosterone support” → concern in hyperandrogenic PCOS

### Evidence Cited (Nario tip)
| Study type | Dose / duration | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| RCT (logical endocrinology) | 750 mg/day standardized extract × 3 months | ↓ LH; improved ovulation |
| Prospective cohort (*Phytomedicine*) | Same dose × 2–3 months | Restored ovulation in anovulatory PCOS |

### Mechanisms (clinical framing)
- Modulates **LH** and helps **normalize LH:FSH ratio** (often disrupted in PCOS)
- Libido effects more likely dopamine / circulation than hormonal spikes; human data do **not** show significant T elevation (even in men)

### Practical Protocol
- Typical dose: **250–750 mg/day** standardized extract
- Side effects: mild GI (nausea, cramping); avoid in pregnancy and hormone-sensitive cancers
- Pair with inositol + berberine as part of a fertility protocol; personalize with labs

### Clearfield Add-On
- Tribulus is **not** a strong T-booster; can ↓ SHBG → relative free-T increase when SHBG is high (prior group lecture on non-hormonal hormone modulators)

## Segment 3 — Jordan Hamlin: Photobiomodulation Foundations (18:30–38:00)

**Speaker:** Jordan Hamlin (Speaker 5) — Neurofeedback BC, HeartMath coach; Neuronic consultant

### Definitions
- **Phototherapy / LLLT:** broad use of light frequencies for wellness (includes UV/blue)
- **Photobiomodulation (PBM):** typically **red + near-infrared (NIR)** specifically
- Popularity of PBM searches rose sharply after ~2021

### Light Hygiene Context
- Indoor living → less solar infrared (~50% of sunlight is IR)
- Excess evening blue light → sleep disruption; morning blue can be alerting
- Green light: easiest for human eye; migraine green lights; forest bathing calming
- SAD full-spectrum lights; visual entrainment devices (pulse for cognition / hypnagogic states)

### Clinical Application Domains
- Dentistry (curing, tissue regen, post-op healing)
- Eyes (emerging; dry eye primary; glaucoma promising but not definitive)
- Injury recovery (LED pads vs clinician lasers)
- Gut (emerging: ↓ inflammation, biomarker rebalancing — mechanism incomplete)
- Wellness: facial masks, hair-growth caps, infrared saunas

### Safety
- Generally well tolerated; LEDs produce less heat than lasers
- Studied in children as young as ~5; newborns historically get UV/blue for jaundice

### Penetration & Device Parameters
| Factor | Clinical point |
|---|---|
| Wavelength | Longer (red→NIR) = deeper penetration |
| Tissue composition | Skull/hair vs abdomen changes delivery |
| Coherence | Lasers high coherence → shorter sessions; LEDs need longer |
| Pulsing | More relevant for brain than body |
| Irradiance | Many consumer devices too weak for therapeutic effect |
| Distance | Beyond ~1 inch loses significant dose; clothing blocks light |
| Form factor | Panels for large areas; wraps/helmets for curved targets |

### Mechanisms of Action
1. **TRP channel activation** (esp. TRPV1) — possible pain-modulation pathway
2. **Mitochondrial / cytochrome c oxidase** absorbs red–NIR photons → ↑ ATP
3. ATP / NO displacement → improved O₂ delivery + blood flow
4. Controlled ROS signaling → hormetic resilience (gym metaphor)
5. Outcomes: neuroprotection, ↑ **BDNF**, ↑ IL-10 (anti-inflammatory), BBB integrity support, possible glymphatic support (esp. night IR — early data)

### Dosing Philosophy
> Optimal dose is a **hormetic sweet spot** — typically once or twice daily; more is not better. Exact minutes depend on device irradiance.

| **Sleep (no diagnosis)** | Sleep deprivation ↑ CSF amyloid-β; PBM may inhibit APP activation, ↓ IL-1β, ↑ IL-10; 6-day protocol: sleep efficiency + REM improved by day 5 |

### Case Highlight — Gray Maynard (UFC)
- Pre: attention/focus, sleep, impulsivity/compulsive thinking
- 3-month tPBM: EEG power improvements (delta/theta); could read 10 min and retain; return to computer work for new business

### EEG & Connectivity
- Consistent ↑ **alpha and beta power** after tPBM (even palm light → systemic EEG change)
- Greatest change under light site, but whole-head downstream effects
- ↑ functional connectivity (often multiple networks); working-memory tasks show PFC connectivity + cognitive efficiency
- **Peak alpha frequency:** healthy adults ~9–11 Hz; slower (≤8 Hz, especially ~7 Hz) associated with cognitive impairment; observational reports of peak alpha speeding toward healthy range with tPBM

### Hydration Pearl
> Hydrate before PBM. IR interacts with body water; dehydration raises risk of headache/fatigue and blunts benefit.

## Segment 5 — Device Specs, Pulse Rates, Contraindications & Future Directions (1:00–1:12)

**Speaker:** Jordan Hamlin

### Wavelength Guidance
- Superficial (skin/beauty): visible red
- Deeper / brain: NIR (~800–1000+ nm)
- ~750 nm and above largely invisible to the eye
- Far IR (~3000 nm): heats via water molecules — **not** cytochrome-c / ATP PBM

### Pulse Rates Discussed
| Pulse | Suggested use (emerging / anecdotal) |
|---|---|
| **Constant** | Most published research baseline |
| **40 Hz** | Working memory, “brain brightening,” amyloid microglial targeting (preclinical framing) |
| **10 Hz** | Stronger biologic impact than constant in some data; deeper penetration; calmer subjectively |
| **14 Hz** | Provider reports for long COVID / dysregulated ANS |
| **1024 Hz** | Anecdote for meditation — no cited evidence |

### Contraindications / Cautions
- **Active cancer** (angiogenesis concern); history → case-by-case
- **Metal implants in head** (shrapnel, DBS) — heating risk; **titanium** generally acceptable
- Hyperthyroid — precautionary (theory / manufacturer labeling)
- Implanted stimulators — caution with pulsed light
- **Epilepsy is NOT a contraindication** for non-ocular head PBM; emerging data may reduce seizure frequency / protect neurons

### Future Directions
- Combinations: TMS, ultrasound, RF (cancer research), IV/intracatheter light (historical Russian work)
- Pain / opioid-sparing potential (local inflammation + central pain processing)
