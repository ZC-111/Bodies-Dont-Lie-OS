# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-06-17

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **CoQ10 depletion is a four-drug problem.** Statins, beta blockers, oral hypoglycemics, and SSRIs all drain cellular energy — and cardiac patients often stack the first two on day one of “prevention.”

2. **Drug benefit can mask nutrient bankruptcy.** Post-MI/procedure meds look great for ~3–5 years; years 6–10 worsen when CoQ10/B-vitamins/carnitine/PQQ depletion catches up. Replete on purpose.

3. **Hormones need fuel.** Mitochondrial support does not replace HRT — it gives hormones the cofactors (B vitamins, Mg, iodine, cellular energy) to work.

4. **Dose CoQ10 by risk, not folklore.** Age >50 base ~100 mg; statin often +200–300 mg; add for diabetes/HTN. Personal high-oxidative-load doses land ~500–600 mg/day.

5. **PQQ rebuilds; CoQ10 fuels.** Biogenetic agent + energy nutrient is the “new engine + gasoline” stack — fermented soy is a traditional PQQ food source.

6. **Vitamin K2 is the calcium traffic cop.** 10:1 D:K2 (e.g., 5,000 IU → 500 mcg K2). Without K2, calcium wanders into intimal plaque; K2 ≠ CoQ10 and K2 ≠ K1 clotting story.

7. **Exercise beats any single pill for mito capacity** — especially intermittent/racket patterns that force reset. Best single pill remains CoQ10.

8. **Grape seed extract is a cheap CV tool** — ~100–300 mg/day, meta-analytic BP drop (~5/2 mmHg), NO support, ACE-inhibitor–like effect, no grapefruit-style CYP hit.

9. **Long COVID is mitochondrial failure until reframed.** Full-dose Cell Energy logic: CoQ10 + NADH + NAC + ALA + methylated Bs.

10. **Sleep apnea is not “altitude training.”** Fix it — memory, HTN, AFib, stroke risk. Elevated Hct with negative JAK2 should trigger apnea workup.
