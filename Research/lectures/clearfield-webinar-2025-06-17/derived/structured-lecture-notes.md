**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield, Dr. Melvin Nario (Speaker 3), Derek de Silva (Speaker 1)

### Tip of the Week — Grape Seed Extract (GSE) for CV Health (Dr. Nario)
- **Primary surprise:** Strongest consistent benefit beyond lipids is **blood pressure**
- **2016 meta-analysis** (*Phytotherapy Research*, 16 RCTs): GSE ↓ SBP ~**5 mmHg**, ↓ DBP ~**2 mmHg**; greatest effect in prehypertensive/hypertensive patients
- **Effective dose:** ~**100–300 mg/day**
- **2009 RCT** (*Metabolism*, metabolic syndrome): 150 or 300 mg GSE daily → BP drop + improved **flow-mediated dilatation** within ~4 weeks (dose-dependent)
- **Mechanisms:** ↑ nitric oxide production/duration; ↓ oxidized LDL; **ACE inhibition** (natural ACE-inhibitor analog)
- Practical frame: affordable alternative to beet / arginine / citrulline NO boosters (many patients convert poorly due to low eNOS)

### Safety Pearl
> **Grape seed ≠ grapefruit.** No CYP450 interaction with GSE (de Silva clarification). Grapefruit CYP concerns do not apply.

### OPCs / Antioxidant Tier
- **Oligomeric proanthocyanidins (OPCs):** ~20–50× more potent than vitamin C or E for free-radical neutralization
- Top tier: **grape seed extract** and **Pycnogenol**; resveratrol, pine bark, berries secondary
- Red grape skins (thicker) → more resilient growing season → richer proanthocyanidins

### 2C. Four Drug Classes That Deplete CoQ10
Most integrative clinicians only remember **statins**. de Silva stresses four:

| # | Drug class | Typical patients | Clinical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Statins** | CVD / lipids | Classic CoQ10 depleter |
| 2 | **Beta blockers** | HTN, post-MI, stent, CABG | Cardiac drugs that steal cardiac fuel |
| 3 | **Oral hypoglycemics** | Diabetes (incl. older sulfonylureas / glyburide class) | Diabetics already have severe mito dysfunction |
| 4 | **SSRIs** | Depression / anxiety | Fourth major depleter often missed |

**Also noted (Nario):** **Thiazide diuretics** further deplete CoQ10 / electrolytes.

**Time-horizon pearl:** Post-MI/procedure drugs look good for ~3–5 years; years 6–10 morbidity/mortality rise again when nutrient depletion catches up (B vitamins, CoQ10, PQQ, carnitine, Mg). Canadian transplant-wait myocardial biopsy data (~25 years ago): lower EF (Killip class) ↔ greater myocardial nutrient deficiency.

### 2D. CoQ10 Dosing Framework
- Ubiquinone vs ubiquinol: bioavailability marketing is overstated; **take CoQ10** (may need higher dose of ubiquinone)
- de Silva personal dose: ~**500–600 mg/day** (travel / oxidative stress)
- Practical stack taught with Clearfield:
  - Base **100 mg** if age >50
  - Statin: add **200–300 mg** (double or more)
  - Diabetes: +**100 mg**
  - Hypertension: +**100 mg**
- Parkinson’s literature: often cited **~2,000 mg/day** (not 3,000)
- Injectable CoQ10: orphan-drug niche for pediatric **mitochondrial cytopathies**; prefer oral for typical adults

### 2E. Vitamin K2 — Gatekeeper for Calcium / Vitamin D (≠ CoQ10 substitute)
- **Cannot replace CoQ10 with vitamin K** — different mechanisms
- Forms: **K1** (phylloquinone, plants); **K2** (menaquinone — MK-4 / MK-7 by isoprene side chains); **K3 toxic** (avoid)
- **K2 made in the gut**; activates **osteocalcin** + **matrix Gla protein (MGP)**
- Without K2: calcium + vitamin D deposit in **intimal** vessel layers → atherogenic plaque risk
- With K2: calcium directed to **medial** layers → vessel flexibility
- Japan: K2 (MK-4 or MK-7) is a treatment of choice for osteoporosis
- BMJ calcium concern: largely **calcium carbonate without K2**
- Calcium forms (7): citrate, gluconate, lactate, malate, aspartate, MCHA, carbonate — carbonate worst
- **D:K2 ratio = 10:1** → e.g., 5,000 IU D needs **500 mcg K2**
- **K2 has no known toxicity**
- **K2 vs warfarin myth:** clotting concerns apply to **K1**, not gut-derived K2 (no clotting effect per de Silva)

### 2F. Mitochondrial Stack Beyond CoQ10
- **PQQ (pyrroloquinoline quinone):** mitochondrial **biogenetic** agent (“new engine”); fermented soy is a classic food source; patented use with CoQ10 (PQQ rebuilds, CoQ10 fuels)
- **NAD / NADH:** redox couple (electron donate/receive)
- **Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA):** underrated cellular energy nutrient; diabetic neuropathy **300 mg TID**; prefer **R-isomer**; basic safe dose frame **100 mg TID**; de Silva rejects ALA → hypothyroidism claim (confuse with alpha-*linoleic* acid)
- Methylated **B12 + folate**, **B1 100 mg**, **B6 100 mg** (neuropathy dosing)
- **NAC:** “cleaner” / glutathione precursor (ER Mucomyst for acetaminophen OD); not primarily a mito fuel
- **Glutathione** for detox / cellular cleaning
- **NT Factor:** lipid membrane support potentiating cellular energy nutrients

### 2G. Non-Supplement Mitochondrial Strategies
- **#1 intervention overall: exercise** (especially intermittent / racket sports — on/off resets mitochondria; US Open court messaging cited)
- Intermittent fasting → **AMPK** → uses stored fat glucose; activates **sirtuins**, **FOXO**, **p53** (tumor suppressor) → autophagy / cellular cleaning
- Also: altitude, HBOT, cold exposure (de Silva declines cold plunge personally — Sri Lankan origin)
- Mitochondrial volume/energy inheritance: **maternal** line
- Age frame: if born with “1,000 mitochondria,” by ~60 may be down to ~**20** without rebuilding

### 2H. Measurement & Clinical Reality
- New California lab test can quantify mitochondrial volume/function (name deferred for CME; text after)
- Organic acid / biological aging clocks ≠ direct mito function assay
- Subjective feel: only the empty-tank patient “feels” CoQ10/PQQ; half-full tanks don’t notice until they relapse (vitamin D analogy)

### 2I. Berberine Caution
- GLP-1–like effect → **do not start at 1,000 mg**; prefer **dihydroberberine** (less GI irritation)

### 2J. One-Intervention Answers
- Best lifestyle intervention over 60: **exercise**
- Best single pill: **CoQ10**
