## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Gut peptides are pathology-specific.** BPC-157 = damage/repair; KPV = inflammation; larazotide = zonulin/barrier. Ask “what’s dominant?” not “which peptide is trendy?”

2. **Fasting insulin ~50 with glucose 89 is not “fine.”** Hyperinsulinemia drives weight, HTN risk, and androgenization — even when endo/dietitian dismiss pediatric A1c 5.7.

3. **Treat TPO early.** Clearfield jumps on even one-point positives; Moducare/Cholestoff + gluten-aware diet can cut Abs ~50%/year in his experience.

4. **Spearmint tea is a practical first androgen move.** Two bags (~≥100 mg), ~5 days/week → ~20% testosterone drop if the patient actually drinks it.

5. **Ferritin 48 stalls thyroid and weight goals.** Aim ~80–100 with B12/folate/vitamin D on board before declaring diet failure.

6. **Estrogen-free lifestyle (Tano).** Cut six grains + soy + dairy + estrogenic personal care; estrogenic xenotoxins can feed the insulin–estrogen loop in kids.

7. **LDN is thyroid-sensitive.** Cap near 3 mg for thyroiditis; usually second/third line; may blunt Ab titers if timed poorly.

8. **CGM + islet Abs beat reassurance.** Continuous glucose data and type-1 autoantibody panels clarify “type 1.5 / LADA-adjacent” risk the pediatric endo skipped.

9. **Tonsillar scars can be a rheumatic block.** Neural therapy injection (German tradition; Dr. Gerber locally) may be a cheap unlock before endless autoimmune stacking.

10. **Vitamin D: linear, not heroic.** ~+8 ng/mL per 1,000 IU over months; mega-dosing has real hypercalcemia/seizure risk in Clearfield’s career memory.
