## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Gut Peptide Triad (14:45–19:10)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 4)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Stop treating “gut dysfunction” as one bucket. Map dominant pathology → peptide:
> **Damage → BPC-157 · Inflammation → KPV · Permeability → larazotide acetate**

### Mechanisms (as presented)

| Peptide | Dominant job | Mechanism highlights | Clinical niches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BPC-157** | Rebuild / repair | Angiogenesis, mucosal repair, ulcer healing via VEGF & NO pathways | Structural GI injury; “shotgun” gut peptide |
| **KPV** | Put out the fire | α-MSH–derived; ↓ NF-κB, TNF-α, IL-6; PepT1 uptake upregulated in inflamed gut | UC / Crohn’s–type inflammatory dominance |
| **Larazotide acetate** | Lock the doors | Blocks zonulin-mediated tight-junction disassembly; strongest human data (celiac / gluten permeability; phase 2–3 framing) | Gluten-sensitive / barrier-dominant disease |

### Clinical pearl
- House metaphor: **BPC rebuilds · KPV extinguishes · larazotide secures doors**
- Most patients need combinations; common pair = BPC + KPV; larazotide as add-on for barrier/gluten
- Prefer **oral / luminal** contact for gut targeting — injectables less ideal if the goal is GI lining exposure
- Cost note: larazotide described as relatively expensive

## Segment 5 — Hormone / Skin / Androgen Interventions (1:00:00–~1:13:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield; skin clinician (Speaker 7); panel

### Spearmint tea protocol (Clearfield go-to)
- **Two tea bags / cup**, ~**5 days/week**
- Typical bag ~70 mg spearmint → need **≥100 mg** total
- Expected ~**20%** testosterone reduction if adherent
- Peppermint less effective; aromatherapy/oils weaker; **compliance** is rate-limiter (cycle q 2–4 months)
- May influence LH and 5α-reductase / DHT — **measure DHT** before long spearmint courses if hairline concern

### Adjuncts mentioned
- Low-dose **saw palmetto** (~**100 mg**) for age
- DIM / calcium-D-glucarate; **trans-resveratrol**
- Cruciferous vegetables for aromatization support

### Skin
- **2% ketoconazole** shampoo twice weekly to scalp + brows / T-zone (seborrhea → acne cascade)
