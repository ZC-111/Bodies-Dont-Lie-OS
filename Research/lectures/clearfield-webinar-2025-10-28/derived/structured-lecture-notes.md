## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Vaginal Hormones for Recurrent UTI Prevention (17:53–24:50)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 3)  
**Commentary:** Bill Clearfield, Caroline (Speaker 5)

### ★ Core Ranking (evidence for UTI prevention in postmenopausal women)
1. **Vaginal estrogen** — strongest / guideline-backed
2. **Vaginal DHEA (prasterone)** — promising; FDA-approved for GSM/atrophy; UTI data still maturing
3. **Vaginal testosterone** — interesting / experimental for UTI prevention specifically

### Mechanism (estrogen)
- Restores vaginal epithelium → ↑ glycogen → feeds lactobacilli → ↓ pH → ↓ uropathogen colonization
- Landmark 1990s RCT (rings/creams): fewer UTIs over 6 months vs placebo
- Cochrane + later studies: fewer infections, improved pH, restored protective flora
- Societies recommend **low-dose local estrogen** as first-line non-antibiotic UTI prevention in postmenopausal women

### Practical Dosing Cited
| Agent | Dose / schedule (as presented) |
|---|---|
| **Estrogen cream** | 0.1 mg/g — 1 g daily × 2–4 weeks → 1–3×/week maintenance; rings/tablets similar; minimal systemic absorption when local |
| **DHEA cream** | 10 mg/g — 1 g daily × 2–4 weeks → 1–3×/week |
| **Testosterone** | ~300 µg–1 mg per application, several days/week (broad range; experimental for UTI) |

### Clearfield / Caroline Pearls
- Clearfield prefers **intravaginal testosterone** 1 mg/g: nightly × 14 nights → 3×/week × 14 → 1–2×/week PRN; notes vasomotor / GSM benefits beyond UTI
- Caroline: introitus / vestibule rich in androgen receptors; dyspareunia after OCP / high SHBG; bladder/urethra also androgen-receptor rich; apply to vestibule + clitoris when indicated
- Clitoral hypertrophy rare at usual doses; one clinician saw hypertrophy at **6 mg/g** cream → dose back

### Hormone-Avoiding Option
- Meletis: **hyaluronic acid** — European data ≈ estrogen for tissue quality in some studies; may not restore glycogen pathway; possible prebiotic effect for lactobacilli

## Segment 3 — Chris Meletis: Mitochondrial Fatigue, Adrenals & Rising All Ships (24:50–56:20)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis, ND

### ★ Core Thesis
> Long COVID, adrenal fatigue, CFS/ME, and other post-viral syndromes share overlapping pathophysiology — **mitochondria** are the common theme. “Rise all the ships in the harbor” (ATP tide) while finding holes in under-fueled tissues.

### Epidemiology Framed
- Science 2024 cited: ~**7% of U.S. adults** (~17M) with some long COVID burden
- >70% of COVID survivors with some ongoing post-infection symptoms; ~200 diagnosable signs/symptoms catalogued
- Same mitochondrial lens applies to EBV, CMV, HHV-6, Lyme-like post-infectious fatigue (extrapolation)

### Adrenal Cortex + Medulla (treat both)
- Not Addisonian “deficiency” — relative insufficiency (~**20 mg/day** physiologic cortisol equivalent as teaching floor)
- **Cortex:** cortisol/cortisone; hypocortisolism → ↑ pain sensitivity, breakthrough inflammation, cognitive dulling
- **Medulla:** adrenaline / norepinephrine; often ignored while clinics only push adaptogens or hydrocortisone
- Support medulla with **N-acetyl-tyrosine** and related catecholamine substrates alongside adaptogens (rhodiola, ashwagandha, ginseng, schisandra) — but adaptogens need mitochondrial energy to “adapt”
- **Locus coeruleus:** norepinephrine release; adequate cortisol **dampens** NE dumps — low cortisol → “dead then suddenly ON” adrenaline surges → harder crash
- Peer literature cluster: ~20–25% of stress-related disorders (CFS, fibromyalgia, PTSD, IBS, burnout, atypical depression) share hypocortisol patterns

### “Deficit Spending” Metaphor
- Borrowing tomorrow’s energy for today (Wimpy / charge-card analogy)
- Night cortisol drop → relative hypoglycemia → adrenaline dump → anxiety / poor sleep → vicious cycle
- Salt / carb cravings as self-medication when cortisol fails to stabilize glycemia

### Thyroid as Metronome of Mitochondria
- T3 (and interest in **T2**) accelerate mitochondrial activity / AMPK-related pathways
- Clinical pearl: some patients prefer **desiccated porcine thyroid** vs pure T4/T3 — possible non-standardized **T2** contribution (provocation, not proven)
- Warning: uptitrate thyroid **without** adrenal/mitochondrial support → “bug on the windshield” — feels better briefly, then crashes harder
- Pan-pituitary case: slight T4 excess ↑ cortisol clearance → functional Addisonian picture until hydrocortisone adjusted

### Cholesterol → Steroidogenesis → Mitochondria
- CYP11A1 / StAR protein: cholesterol → pregnenolone on mitochondrial membrane
- Aggressive statin + ADT case: cholesterol ~97, testosterone ~2, low pregnenolone/DHEA — “poisoning the pathway”
- Estradiol supports mitochondrial biogenesis (ties tip-of-week pelvic tissue energy to systemic mitochondrial support)

### Mitochondria-Burdening Meds (slide themes)
- Acetaminophen (glutathione), propofol/lidocaine, fluoxetine, anxiolytics, muscle relaxants, sleep meds, **statins** (assume CoQ10 hit), **metformin** (Complex I)

## Segment 4 — Oxaloacetate: Stabilized TCA Intermediate for CFS / Long COVID (56:20–1:12:40)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis, ND

### Product Framing
- **Stabilized oxaloacetate** required for therapeutic effect
- FDA **medical food** status for chronic fatigue syndrome (medical oversight; not Rx drug)
- Also discussed in **glioblastoma** context (orphan-drug research status; Meletis clinical use)
- Higher-dose form: **500 mg** capsules (e.g., oxaloacetate CFS / related brand family; Benagene lower-dose line mentioned)
- Cost: ~**$300 wholesale / ~$500 retail** per 90-count bottle — not cheap; ~2 bottles may reveal responder status

### Mechanisms Claimed
- TCA / Krebs intermediary → supports mitochondrial biogenesis
- Restores NAD/NADH balance; ↓ neuroinflammation; antioxidant / cellular stress mitigation
- Synergy concept with NAD precursors (not automatic overkill — body modulates; measure if pushing hard)

### Trial Snapshots Cited
| Study flavor | Dose / design | Signal (as presented) |
|---|---|---|
| RCT long COVID | 2000 mg/day vs placebo × 42 days (n=69) | Less fatigue; symptom burden improved by day 21; cognition improved |
| Non-RCT ME/CFS / long COVID | 500 mg BID / 1000 mg BID / 1000 mg TID × 6 weeks | Dose-dependent fatigue reduction (~21% / 27% / 33%); ~48% symptom decline near 6 weeks |
| “Enhanced responders” | Same program | **40.5%** with >25% fatigue improvement; **mean 63%** improvement among enhanced responders; faster reaction times |

### Clinical Response / Safety (Meletis experience)
- Typical: ↑ energy, mental clarity
- Side effect: **~3–5% headaches**
- Start thoughtfully; higher dose for strong responders (e.g., 2 × 500 mg TID discussed in Q&A)
- Longevity curiosity: 2014 *C. elegans* lifespan ↑ ~25% (Nario) — not human evidence

### Related Cognitive Stack Mention
- **LongVita** (UCLA-patented curcuminoid framing): ~65× AUC claim; brain delivery / first-pass avoidance — Meletis adjunct interest for neuroinflammation

## Segment 5 — NAD, CD38, Sirtuins & Mitochondrial Aging (1:03:50–1:29:30)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis, ND

### NAD Fundamentals
- Required for **>500 enzymatic pathways**; critical for mitochondria / redox
- Aging: NAD drops (cited rough folds across adulthood); stress + illness accelerate decline
- Exception cited: seniors walking **>14,000 steps/day** did not show the usual NAD drop (causality unclear)
- **CD38** and PARPs consume NAD — inflammation/aging “Hoover”
- Sirtuins (esp. **SIRT3 / SIRT5** in adrenal mitochondria) are NAD-dependent; steroidogenesis needs NADPH via NNT

### Harvard NR / Long COVID Study (pre-publication at time of talk)
- RCT n=58 long COVID; **nicotinamide riboside (Niagen) 2000 mg/day** × 10–20 weeks
- Outcomes tracked: cognition, depression, sleep; NAD serum rise; trends toward symptom benefit — larger confirmation needed
- Expected mid-November publication (speaker would share when public)
- Scandinavian mindfulness + NAD / long COVID paper also referenced

### Practical Mitochondrial Support Checklist (summary slide themes)
- NAD precursor (NR / NMN / preferred route)
- CoQ10 or PQQ
- Creatine (re-popularized for mitochondria)
- Adrenal support + measure **DHEA-S / pregnenolone**
- Sleep / apnea workup; forest bathing (shinrin-yoku); grounding; stress minimization
- Individualized nutrient + optional neurotransmitter testing (directional, not dogma)

### Spike / Senolytic Mentions (high-level)
- IndianR article by Meletis: oxaloacetate, NAD support, silymarin, periodic fisetin for “zombie” senescent cells (3–4 days/month framing)
