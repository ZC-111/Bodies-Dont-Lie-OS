# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-10-28

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Vaginal estrogen is first-line** non-antibiotic prevention for recurrent UTIs in postmenopausal women — stronger evidence than DHEA or testosterone for the UTI endpoint.

2. **Estrogen works via ecology, not lubrication alone:** epithelium → glycogen → lactobacilli → lower pH → fewer uropathogens.

3. **Long COVID / CFS / post-viral fatigue share a mitochondrial core.** Treat the energy tide (“rise all ships”) while finding tissue-specific holes.

4. **Support adrenal cortex and medulla together.** Low cortisol fails to dampen locus coeruleus NE dumps — patients look “dead, then suddenly wired.”

5. **Don’t uptitrate thyroid in isolation.** Speeding mitochondria without substrate/adrenal support creates the “felt better, then crashed” pattern.

6. **Stabilized oxaloacetate** is the novel therapeutic highlight — medical-food CFS framing; ~40.5% enhanced responders with ~63% average fatigue improvement in cited data; ~3–5% headaches.

7. **NAD decline with age/stress is amplified by CD38 and PARPs.** Walking >14,000 steps/day was associated with preserved NAD in seniors (causality unclear).

8. **Oxaloacetate + NAD can be combined**, but super-physiologic stacking is unwise — start low, go slow, consider measuring NAD if using IV + oral.

9. **Cholesterol is mitochondrial steroidogenic feedstock.** Ultra-low cholesterol + ADT can collapse pregnenolone/DHEA — think before celebrating a total cholesterol of ~97.

10. **Methylene blue and metformin are not magic mito bullets.** Metformin hits Complex I and often depletes B12; methylene blue is highly individual and dose-dependent.
