## Section D — Practice Pearls & Comparisons (3 questions)

### Q18. Why does Mitchell still often recommend CoQ10 alongside MitoQ?
**Answer:** MitoQ is a mitochondrially targeted antioxidant and does **not** replace CoQ10’s electron-transport-chain role (or lysosomal roles). Houston/Nario practice pearl: pair organ-targeted MitoQ products with CoQ10/ubiquinol.

### Q19. How does Mitchell contrast urolithin A with Mitoquinol?
- A) They are chemically identical
- B) **Urolithin A is primarily a mitophagy inducer; Mitoquinol works mainly via antioxidant/redox balance — constant mitophagy may not be desirable** ✓
- C) Urolithin A has superior mitochondrial delivery vs MitoQ
- D) Urolithin A raises plasma NAD more than NMN

### Q20. Mitchell’s preferred dosing strategy for clinical effect vs cost is:
**Answer:** Start higher if needed to demonstrate benefit, then **titrate down to a maintenance dose** once symptoms/biomarkers improve (accumulation/steady state; highest cited safe-ish continuous dose ~160 mg for months; excess mainly causes GI intolerance).
