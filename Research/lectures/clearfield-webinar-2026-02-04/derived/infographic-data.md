## 🌿 Infographic 1: "Kratom Dose Ladder"

**Title:** Same Plant, Different Clinical Personality

| Dose pattern | Patient experience | Clinical stance |
|---|---|---|
| **Low dose** | Focus, energy, sociability, ↓ anxiety | Ask: why / how often / how much |
