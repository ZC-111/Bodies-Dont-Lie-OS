# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-02-04

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Kratom is a gray-zone partial mu agonist** — not an opioid drug, not harmless. Ask patients about café teas/extracts; don’t wait for voluntary disclosure.

2. **Dose defines the drug:** low-dose kratom → stimulant/mood; higher dose → analgesia/sedation/dependence. Worst outcomes cluster with concentrates + alcohol/benzos/opioids.

3. **2020 cold-pressor RCT:** pain tolerance ~11 → 25 seconds post-kratom — analgesia is measurable, not only anecdotal.

4. **Mitochondrial dysfunction starts in the 20s–30s** (muscle ATP/copy number). Treat it as early preventive medicine, not only end-stage disease care.

5. **Immune–mito link:** mtDNA release → cGAS–STING / NLRP3 inflammasome — relevant to COVID, long COVID, and amyloid/inflammaging framing.

6. **Mitoquinol’s edge is delivery:** TPP+ tagging drives up to ~90% mitochondrial localization vs ~5% for oral CoQ10 — but it does **not** replace CoQ10’s ETC role; co-prescribe often.

7. **Take MitoQ 30 minutes before food.** Food collapses bioavailability. Typical clinical capsule ~20 mg; practitioner cost ~$60/month.

8. **Titrate:** higher induction to prove effect → maintenance once pain, BP, energy, fertility metrics, or inflammasome markers improve. Excess mainly causes GI intolerance (up to 160 mg studied for months).

9. **NAD precursor hype > data:** NMN/NR/NMN-H raise plasma NAD; Mitchell’s read of clinical outcomes is underwhelming vs cost — prefer agents with clearer functional signals (PQQ cognition; MitoQ vascular/liver/diabetes signals).

10. **FMD and silent diabetic heart injury** are practical hooks: acute FMD gains (80–160 mg PoC) and ~15% cardiac energy improvement (40 mg × 12 wk T2DM MRS) argue for mitochondrial targeting beyond “energy drinks.”
