## 🧪 Infographic 3: "Ergothioneine — Important, Not Everything"

**Title:** Don’t Chase One Antioxidant Badge

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Unique amino-acid antioxidant from fungi/bacteria |
| Human need | Mammals must get it from diet |
| Transporter | Specific membrane ergothioneine transporter |
| Tissue focus | Eye lens, liver, bone marrow, seminal fluid |
| Stability | More stable in blood than glutathione |
| Pitfall | Reishi can be **low** in ergothioneine yet still potent |

**Rule:** Judge the **full antioxidant profile**, not a single standardized marker.

## ⚠️ Infographic 5: "Reishi Safety Card"

**Title:** Ancient Ally — Modern Monitoring

```
DOSE PEARLS
  Fibromyalgia trials: 6 g/day × 6 weeks
  Anticoagulant caution: ≤1.5 g/day + INR/PT; signal at ~3 g

WATCH FOR
  ↓ systolic BP with antihypertensives
  Energizing → dose before ~2 pm
  Weak estrogen-receptor binding (fruit body ethanol extracts)
  Overactive immunity → prefer mycelium over fruit body
  Cyclophosphamide / transplant interactions

QUALITY ALARM
  Only ~26% of US Reishi products matched labels in cited survey
  Demand AOAC-aligned methods + finished-lot heavy-metal testing
```

## 🚫 Infographic 7: "Hard Stops & Myths"

**Title:** What Practitioners Must Get Right

| Topic | Clinical take |
|---|---|
| **Organ transplant** | No culinary mushrooms / no mushroom supplements |
| **Wild-crafted risk** | Can concentrate As, Pb, Cd, Hg, Cs-137 |
| **Chaga sclerotia** | High oxalate → neuropathy/renal failure case report |
| **Candida myth** | Medicinal mushrooms do **not** feed Candida (debunked) |
| **Beta-glucan fetish** | Whole mushroom / dual extracts beat one fraction |

**Footer:** Safety testing beats marketing claims.

## ☕ Infographic 8: "Extraction & Delivery Cheat Sheet"

**Title:** How You Prepare It Changes What You Get

| Goal | Method |
|---|---|
| Polysaccharides / polyphenols | Hot water extract / long boil (woody spp. **8–12 h** traditional) |
| Terpenes / fatty acids (e.g., ganoderic acids, erinacines) | Ethanol / dual extraction |
| Elderly compliance | Liquid extracts in tea or soup; start with Reishi or 7-blend |
| Instant mushroom coffee | Only as good as prior heat treatment / chitin disruption |
| Maintenance vs acute immune need | Rotate Stamets-7–style blend ↔ broader multi-mushroom formula every **3–6 months** |
