# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-06-10

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Seven mushrooms beat one.** A synergistic mycelium + fermented-rice blend outperformed single species on macrophage and NK assays — formulate for entourage, not monomania.

2. **Longevity levers are shared:** antioxidants (esp. ergothioneine + species-specific terpenes), balanced telomerase, SIRT1, AMPK, mitochondrial ATP, and butyrate/SCFA tone.

3. **Don’t standardize only on ergothioneine.** Reishi can test “low” yet remain clinically potent via ganoderic acids, glutathione, and polysaccharides.

4. **Reishi is the elderly/multi-system starter**, with fibromyalgia data at 6 g/day × 6 weeks — but monitor BP, sleep timing, estrogen-sensitive states, and anticoagulants (≤1.5 g if on blood thinners).

5. **Lion’s Mane needs continuity** for brain goals; Ornish 2024 paired ~2 g mycelium with diet, lifestyle, and omega-3 for Alzheimer’s-related function — multimodal context matters.

6. **Cordyceps before 2 pm.** Energizing adaptogen with lung/VO₂/immune upside; caution in hormone-sensitive tumors and with cyclophosphamide.

7. **Metabolic mushrooms need glucose vigilance.** Maitake (and Blazei hypoglycemia reports) can potentiate insulin/metformin — meter and time doses.

8. **Quality is the therapy.** ~26% label match in a US Reishi survey; wild mushrooms bioaccumulate heavy metals and Cs-137 — demand AOAC-aligned + finished-lot testing.

9. **Candida-feeding mushroom myth is false.** Traditional and clinical use (e.g., Reishi) contradicts the 1970s folklore; mushrooms are biphasic immunomodulators.

10. **Marketing language is clinical risk.** Use support/research-suggests phrasing, soft testimonials with disclaimers, and the standard FDA “not intended to diagnose/treat/cure/prevent” footer.
