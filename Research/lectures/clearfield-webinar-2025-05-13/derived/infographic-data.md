## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Static vs Dynamic Hormone Care"

**Title:** Stop Guessing From Cycle Length Alone

| Old (static) | New (dynamic) |
|---|---|
| “My app says 28–30 days” | Full-cycle hormone maps, cycle after cycle |
| Infrequent serum snapshot | Ongoing urine metabolites (E3G, PDG, LH, FSH) |
| Delayed mailed labs | Same-morning home readout (~21 min) |
| Mucus / BBT proxies only | Direct hormone pattern + optional CGM / wearables |
| Wait 12 months TTC | Spot suboptimal ovulation early and intervene |

**Footer:** Normal length ≠ healthy ovulation. Patterns tell the story.

## 📈 Infographic 2: "The Ideal Cycle Map"

**Title:** What Coordinated Hormones Look Like

```
FOLLICULAR                    OVULATION                 LUTEAL
E3G rises  ────────────────►  LH + FSH surge  ──────►  PDG bell curve
(black)                       (teal + gold)             (purple)

Watch for:
  ✗ Flat / non-dynamic E3G
  ✗ Trigger without robust PDG
  ✗ Short luteal (<11 days)
  ✗ Reverse luteal estrogen (E higher after ovulation than before)
  ✗ Failed LH then second surge (“stutter-step”)
```

**Callout:** Mid-luteal labs = **ovulation + 7 days**, not a fixed calendar day.

## 🧪 Infographic 3: "Urine Metabolites Cheat Sheet"

**Title:** What You’re Actually Measuring

| Code | Reflects | Clinical use |
|---|---|---|
| **E3G** | Estradiol (E2) breakdown | Follicular rise, unopposed E, induction charts |
| **PDG** | Progesterone breakdown | Confirm ovulation, luteal adequacy |
| **LH** | LH surge | Trigger / failed surge detection |
| **FSH** | FSH | Coordination with LH; peri ovarian “effort” |

**Rule:** Serum ≠ same-day urine. Metabolites **lag** — don’t force a match.

## 🩸 Infographic 4: "Glucose Follows the Cycle"

**Title:** Why Cravings Spike After Ovulation

```
FOLLICULAR (estrogen ↑)
  → Insulin sensitivity ↑
  → Glucose lower / steadier
  → CGM mostly in range

LUTEAL (progesterone ↑)
  → Insulin sensitivity ↓
  → Glucose swings ↑
  → Cravings, mood risk ↑
```

**Action:** Prioritize protein-before-carbs, adjust training/diet by phase — don’t gaslight the physiology.

**Evidence cue:** n=49 study pairing hormone monitor + CGM (+ Fitbit).

## 🔄 Infographic 5: "Clomid vs Letrozole on the Chart"

**Title:** Know Your Induction Signature

| Drug | Mechanism (teaching) | E3G chart look |
|---|---|---|
| **Clomid** | Blocks estrogen receptors → circulating E rises | Obvious high E3G climb (“see it from a mile away”) |
| **Letrozole** | Blocks estrogen synthesis → E falls then rebounds | Often subtle; patient must disclose meds |

**Footer:** Same goal (ovulation) — different fingerprints on urine maps.
