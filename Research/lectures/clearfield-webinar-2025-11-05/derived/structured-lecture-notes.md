## Segment 4 — Dr. Joni Collins: PRF Superiority, Glycocalyx & Optimization (19:57–1:13:22)

**Speaker:** Joni Collins — founder, 1228 Wellness / 1228 Protocol & Method

### ★ Core Thesis
> **PRF quality is only as good as the patient’s endothelial glycocalyx.** Optimize microvascular health first — then autologous fibrin delivers sustained growth factors. Baking analogy: bad eggs → bad cake.

### 4A. PRF vs PRP (and PRFM)

| Feature | PRP | PRF (“pure F”) |
|---|---|---|
| Additives / anticoagulants | Yes (burns) | **None** — autologous fibrin matrix |
| Growth-factor kinetics | Surge then drop | **Sustained ~28 days** |
| Scaffold | Lacks fibrin scaffold | Fibrin plug = reservoir + implant-like structure |
| Typical spin | Higher speed (~3000–3500 RPM) | Slower; **L-PRF go-to: 12 min @ 2800 RPM + 2 min @ 2800** |
| Cost of goods | Higher kits | ~**$30**/procedure disposables (DIY kits ~$17) |

- **PRFM** (e.g., Selphyl-class): additive “cell fill”; slower/more expensive; Collins prefers pure PRF for most work
- Platelet concentrates: ~**24 PRF protocols** (Choukroun dental paradigm shift, 2001); L-PRF leukocyte content → antimicrobial / immune modulation
- Horizontal/bucket centrifuge preferred over fixed-angle for reproducible quality post–lipid-envelope virus era

### 4B. Clinical Applications Highlighted
- Facial volumization (½ tsp facial volume loss/year after age 25 → ~½ cup by 50)
- Hair restoration (3–6 sessions q4–8 weeks overlapping 28-day cycle)
- Melasma / allergic shiners / dry eye; scar & boxcar acne remodeling
- Joint/pain (anti-inflammatory; needs residual cartilage)
- Fat transfer adjunct (holds graft pocket); ulcer/burn dressings; post-laser fibrosis reduction
- Sexual wellness / post–pelvic radiation dryness (mentioned)
- **Do not** microneedle *through* PRF (kills cells) — inject first (microcannula), then topical after HA glide cleaned off
- **Lidocaine is cytotoxic** to PRF cells — prefer vibration analgesia for scalp; avoid bolus lidocaine with PRF
- Alternate PDO threads (pro-inflammatory) with PRF (anti-inflammatory) ~2 weeks apart

### 4C. Glycocalyx — Why PRF Failed After COVID Era
- Lipid-envelope viruses / inflammatory states → fibrin clot looks like **albumin**; premature clotting; wasted HA syringes
- Glycocalyx = gel-like endothelial “roots of the tree” (proteoglycans, GAGs, glycoproteins, syndecans)
- Functions: NO signaling, permeability barrier, anti-inflammatory shield, antithrombotic surface (ATIII / TFPI), growth-factor bioavailability
- **Syndecan 1–4 > 40** correlated with **35–50% ↓** growth-factor concentrations in platelet therapies
- Damage associations: diabetes, metabolic syndrome, smoking, chronic inflammation, acute stress, poor sleep
- Microvascular health score / GlycoCheck (Kaposcope): Collins’ own score **1.79** after repeated COVID; **PBR target < 2** (PBR > 2 ≈ 10-year inflammatory cardiac risk signal)

### 4D. Pre-Procedure Optimization Protocol
1. **6–8 weeks out:** Mediterranean diet, sleep, stress, smoking cessation; microvascular support supplement; epigenetic testing; hydration; low-GI; consider resveratrol + garlic extract
2. **Week of:** reschedule if recent illness
3. **Day of:** avoid caffeine 2–4 hours; process blood immediately; ideal draw **08:00–11:00** (circadian platelet peak)
4. Patients *can* start PRF same day, but best outcomes follow optimization

### 4E. Case Study — 50yo Female (T2DM, ex-smoker)
**Baseline:** HbA1c 8.2; 20-year smoking (quit 3 mo); mild HTN; elevated syndecan-1, CRP, fasting glucose; PBR ~2.4  

**12-week protocol:** Mediterranean diet + dietitian; **NAC** (not NAD); vitamin C; walking + mindfulness  

**Outcomes:** syndecan-1 ↓, CRP ↓, HbA1c ↓, fasting glucose 112; PBR → **1.8**; PRF growth factors ↑ (**PDGF +65%, TGF-β +48%, VEGF +73%**); visible texture/volume/quality improvement by week 4; 16-week injectable + topical PRF “sweet 16” remodel  

**Practice metrics:** ~**45%** reduction in repeat procedures when optimized; patient satisfaction ~**9.5 vs 7–8** without optimization; alopecia areata totalis case with dramatic response after **one** optimized session (not guaranteed)

## Segment 5 — Q&A: Scars, Tubes, Combos, Revaska (1:13:22–1:48:00)

**Speakers:** Collins, Clearfield, Burgess (Speaker 5), Nario, others

### Scar & Anesthesia
- For melanated skin: **lidocaine only** — benzene/tetracaine → hyperpigmentation risk
- Scar takedown: microcannula + hyperdilute PRF/saline; stage carefully (don’t prematurely disrupt C-section scars)
- Burgess: scars tend to camouflage toward surrounding skin tone with neural/scar work
- Anecdote: optimized PRF restored pigment in hypopigmented tattoo-removal scar / vitiligo-like areas (anecdotal)

### Blood Draw & Processing
- Typical face microneedle + inject: **2 tubes** (10–13 mL); men need more (higher Hct → less PRF fraction)
- Hair: ~palm-sized treatment zones; up to ~**12 tubes** if large area — don’t over-dilute fertilizer
- DIY sterile tubes (~$1–2); avoid foamy lined tubes; plastic preferred when hypercoagulable post-virus
- Double spin after COVID era to keep RBCs down (hemosiderin stain risk)

### Combos & Products

- Glycocalyx oral: **Revaska** (Glycocalyx Institute; powder + aloe; successor lineage Arterial Soft → Endocalyx Pro → Revaska); start ~6 weeks before procedure
