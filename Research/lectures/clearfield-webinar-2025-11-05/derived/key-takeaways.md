# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-11-05

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **PRF quality tracks endothelial glycocalyx health.** Compromised “roots” (virus, diabetes, smoking, poor sleep) → albumin-like product and weak outcomes.

2. **Pure PRF ≠ PRP.** No additives, no burn, fibrin scaffold, growth factors sustained ~**28 days**. If it burned, it was probably PRP.

3. **L-PRF “1228” spin:** **12 min @ 2800 RPM** + **2 min @ 2800 RPM** (same tube). Horizontal/bucket centrifuges often more reproducible than fixed-angle.

4. **Don’t kill the biologic:** no microneedling through PRF; no bolus **lidocaine** with PRF (cytotoxic). Inject → clean → topical; use vibration for scalp comfort.

5. **Syndecan 1–4 > ~40** associated with **35–50% lower** growth-factor yield. Prefer microvascular score / **PBR < 2**.

6. **Optimize 6–8 weeks when possible:** Mediterranean/low-GI diet, sleep, NAC (+ vit C), hydration, smoking cessation; oral glycocalyx support (Revaska lineage) ahead of series.

7. **Case proof-of-concept:** 50yo T2DM after 12-week optimize → PBR 1.8, PDGF/TGF-β/VEGF markedly higher, visible remodel by week 4; ~**45%** fewer repeats when optimized.

8. **Esthetics is a stealth functional door.** Good faith exam + game plan converts “Botox now” into healing-within care (~98% conversion claimed when education sticks).

9. **Fertility tip toolkit:** ubiquinol CoQ10 200–400 mg, carnitine 1–2 g, myo-inositol 2–4 g + DCI 50 mg, maca 1.5–3 g, NAC 600 mg BID, vit E 400 IU, omega-3 1–2 g.

10. **Realistic lifestyle > perfect protocol.** Personalized, compliant plans beat Slurpee-Sunday hypocrisy; personalization on comorbidities drives success.
