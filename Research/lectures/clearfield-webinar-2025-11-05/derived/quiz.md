### Q7. True or False: Horizontal (bucket) centrifuges generally give more reproducible PRF quality than fixed-angle centrifuges in Collins’ post–lipid-envelope-virus experience.

## Section D — Clinical Scenarios (2 questions)

### Q19. A 48-year-old woman wants full-face PRF “today,” smokes, sleeps 4 hours/night, and had a viral illness last week. How would you counsel using this webinar’s framework?
**Answer:**
1. Explain baking/egg analogy — poor glycocalyx → albumin-like PRF → weak outcomes  
2. Offer same-day start only with **expectation management**, or preferably delay until illness clears  
3. Start 6–8 week optimization: sleep, smoking cessation, Mediterranean/low-GI diet, NAC/vit C/microvascular support (± Revaska), optional GlycoCheck/PBR  
4. Day-of rules when ready: no caffeine 2–4h, morning draw, immediate spin  
5. Use education + written game plan to retain (~70% industry drop-off after 3 toxin/filler visits)

### Q20. A man with androgenetic alopecia wants one large full-scalp PRF session using lidocaine blocks and RF microneedling mixed into the PRF. What corrections would Collins emphasize?
**Answer:**
1. Treat **concentrated palm-sized zones**; don’t hyper-dilute fertilizer across the whole scalp — may need many tubes (up to ~12) for large male patterns  
2. **No lidocaine bolus** with PRF (cytotoxic); use vibration / technique instead  
3. Don’t destroy cells by needling PRF in — inject/topical sequence matters; she is not a fan of RF microneedling for fat-volume preservation  
4. Plan **3–6 sessions q4–8 weeks** overlapping the ~28-day growth-factor window  
5. Optimize glycocalyx first for hair outcomes (alopecia areata totalis anecdote was one optimized session — not a guarantee)
