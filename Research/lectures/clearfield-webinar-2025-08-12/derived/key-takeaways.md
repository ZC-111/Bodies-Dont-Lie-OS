# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-08-12

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Treat fracture risk, not only DEXA.** Bone density predicts only ~44% of women and ~21% of men who will fracture; ~95% of fractures follow a fall.

2. **GI patients are bone patients.** Screen celiac, IBD, GERD/PPI users, post-gastrectomy, and bariatric patients early — osteoporosis can be the sole celiac presentation.

3. **Long-term PPIs raise hip-fracture odds dramatically** (summary OR ~3.49 in GERD). H2RAs are safer-ish, not risk-free. Deprescribe when diet/integrative GERD care works.

4. **Never assume bisphosphonates fix PPI bone loss.** PPI + bisphosphonate associated with ~52% *higher* fracture risk than bisphosphonate alone.

5. **SSRIs are osteoclastic.** ~1 fracture per 19 patients on SSRI for 1–5 years — serotonin receptors on bone matter.

6. **MK-4 45 mg/day has the bone outcome data** (BMD + fractures; Japan-approved). MK-7 marketing often rests on surrogates without fracture RCTs.

7. **Steroids break bone before DEXA falls** via collagen damage + fall risk from muscle injury — don’t wait for the T-score to act.

8. **Protein and fiber are bone drugs.** Floor ≈ lb × 0.6 g protein/day; ≥30 g fiber/day; Mediterranean pattern ~21% lower hip-fracture risk.

9. **Match adaptogens to the stress pattern** (Nario): ashwagandha calms, rhodiola sharpens, Siberian ginseng builds stamina — skip mystery blends when one herb will do.

10. **Calcium citrate > carbonate** when acid is low (hypochlorhydria, PPI). Pair calcium strategy with vitamin D; don’t over-supplement blindly.

## Comparison: MK-4 vs MK-7 for Bone

| Dimension | MK-4 | MK-7 |
|---|---|---|
| **BMD trials** | Repeated positive (25 cited) | Not shown to improve/stop loss |
| **Fracture outcomes** | Yes (large reductions in some RCTs) | None |
| **Typical clinical dose discussed** | 45 mg/day | mcg-range consumer doses |
| **Japan MoH osteoporosis approval** | Yes (~1995–96) | No |
| **Common marketing endpoint** | Clinical BMD/Fx | uc-osteocalcin / MGP surrogates |
| **Best paired with** | Vitamin D (synergy) | Insufficient bone outcome case |

**Consensus from lecture:** For osteoporosis/fracture goals, choose MK-4 on outcomes evidence; be a savvy consumer of K2 claims.

## PPI vs H2RA — Fracture/Fall Lens

| | PPI | H2RA |
|---|---|---|
| Hip fracture signal | Stronger, dose/duration related | Weaker; often NS in GERD-only |
| Falls / fall hospitalization | Increased | Generally not significant |
| With bisphosphonate | ↑ fracture risk (~+52%) | Not the focus of that meta-analysis |
| Clinical move | Deprescribe; fix GERD root causes | Preferable step-down if acid suppression unavoidable |
