## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Adaptogens by Energy Profile (18:12–22:47)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 3)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Stop prescribing mystery “adaptogen blends.” Match each herb’s energy profile (calming vs stimulating vs stamina) to the patient’s stress pattern.

### Mechanism Frame
- Adaptogens modulate the **HPA axis** / cortisol (raise if low, lower if high) — “personal trainer for the adrenals”

### Three-Herb Cheat Sheet

| Herb | Role | Best for | Dose (cited) | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ashwagandha** (*Withania somnifera*) | Calming restorer | Anxiety, insomnia, burnout, “wired but tired” | 300–600 mg/day standardized root | High dose + hyperthyroidism or sedatives |
| **Rhodiola** (*R. rosea*) | Mental energizer | Mental fatigue, brain fog, cognitive stamina | 200–400 mg/day (3% rosavins / 1% salidroside) | Jitteriness if late-day or high dose |
| **Siberian ginseng** (*Eleutherococcus*) | Stamina builder | Low physical endurance, chronic illness recovery, athletes | 300–1200 mg/day standardized | Steady energy, less overstimulation |

### Evidence Snippets Cited
- Ashwagandha RCT (Salve et al. 2019 framing): KSM-66 extract 125–300 mg BID → improved sleep quality
- Siberian ginseng: 8-week RCT in 20 trained male endurance athletes → ↑ VO₂ max and time to exhaustion

### Clinical Pearl
- Blends cost more and obscure which herb is working; single-target herb often better for both clinical clarity and patient cost

## Segment 3 — John Neustadt: Gut–Bone Interaction & Enteropathic Osteoporosis (26:34–~1:22)

**Speaker:** John Neustadt, ND (Speaker 2) — NBI; *Fracture Proof Your Bones*

### ★ Core Thesis
> Patients with GI disease, chronic PPIs/SSRIs, steroids, bariatric surgery, and related comorbidities are undertreated for fracture risk. Treat the **patient and fall risk**, not only the DEXA T-score.

### 3A. Why Bone Screening Matters

| Fact | Number / point |
|---|---|
| Hip fracture 1-year mortality (women ≥65) | Commonly cited **~20%**; range up to **~36–60%** in studies |
| vs breast cancer | Even 20% 1-year hip-fracture mortality > combined SEER 5-year breast cancer mortality framing |
| Men | Often **~2×** women’s post–hip-fracture mortality (comorbidity severity) |
| Global burden | Osteoporosis impact second only to CVD; fracture ~every 30 seconds worldwide |
| DEXA predictive value | Predicts only **~44%** of women and **~21%** of men who will fracture |
| Fall mechanism | **~95%** of fractures follow a fall — strength/balance critical |

**US adults ≥50 (general):** women ~20% osteoporosis / ~43% any bone loss; men ~4.4% osteoporosis (summary table cited).

### 3B. GI / Medication Fracture Risk Snapshot

| Condition / exposure | Cited risk signal |
|---|---|
| **Celiac** | Up to **60%** low BMD; up to **35%** osteoporosis at diagnosis (ages 15–35); osteoporosis can be **sole** presentation |
| **Long-term PPI (GERD)** | Hip fracture odds ratio up to **~3.49 (~350%)** vs non-users |
| **SSRI 1–5 years** | NNH ≈ **19** for fracture; serotonin receptors on bone → osteoclast activation |
| **IBD (no surgery)** | Hip fracture RR ↑ **41%** (UC) / **68%** (Crohn); OR fractures ~**32%**; osteoporosis prevalence up to **77%** |
| Current steroids | Reversible **~35%** ↑ hip fracture risk |
| ≥1 opioid | **~67%** ↑ hip fracture risk |
| **Post-gastrectomy** | Median osteoporotic fracture **~3.1 years** post-op; AGA 2003 suggests BMD wait **≥10 years** — Neustadt flags mismatch |
| **Bariatric surgery (2022)** | ~280K procedures; 1-year: **~71%** vit D deficient, **~21.5%** iron deficient; ↓ leptin / ↑ PYY → ↑ bone loss |

### 3C. Mechanisms of “Enteropathic Osteoporosis”
1. Medications (PPIs, SSRIs/SNRIs, glucocorticoids)
2. Chronic inflammation / “immunoporosis” (cytokines → osteoclastogenesis)
3. Gut hyperpermeability → systemic osteoclastogenic load
4. Nutritional deficiencies (Fe, folate, B6, D, Cu, Zn, vitamin K…)
5. Microbial dysbiosis + endocrine–bone signals (leptin, PYY, IGF-1, RANKL)

**Glucocorticoid pearl:** Collagen damage → fracture risk rises **before** DEXA drops; also damages muscle → falls.

**Fall + DEXA interaction:** When patients are strong/stable, DEXA’s ability to predict fracture falls to ~**5%** in women (study cited) — holistic fall prevention matters.

### 3D. MK-4 (Vitamin K2) vs MK-7

| | **MK-4** | **MK-7** |
|---|---|---|
| Origin frame | Mammalian product (UBIAD1) | Bacterial menaquinone |
| Bone density | 25 trials; stops loss / improves BMD across conditions | Not shown to stop loss or improve BMD; one study faster hip loss |
| Fractures | Reductions **>70%** in some studies | **No** fracture outcomes |
| Regulatory | Japan MoH approved **45 mg/day** since ~1995–96 for osteoporosis / bone pain | — |
| Example RCTs | PBC: 45 mg/d × 24 mo + UDCA → BMD ↑; GIO: preserves BMD vs steroids | Marketing often cites undercarboxylated osteocalcin (surrogate only) |

**Also:** Estrogen supports gut absorption of vitamin K forms; **2nd-gen N-containing bisphosphonates** (e.g., Fosamax) **block endogenous MK-4 production**.

### 3E. PPIs, H2RAs, Bisphosphonate Cautionary Tale
- FDA warning (2010); JAMA 2006 association: ~1 year PPI → crude OR hip Fx **~1.85**, adjusted **~1.44**
- H2RA: hip Fx OR **~1.23** (safer than PPI but not risk-free); in GERD-only, H2RA association NS overall
- Longer PPI duration → higher risk (4 years OR **~2.17**); stronger in **men**; GERD-restricted PPI OR ~**3.5**
- PPIs ↑ falls and fall-related hospitalizations; H2RAs generally not
- **PPI + bisphosphonate:** meta-analysis of 4 studies → **+52%** fracture risk vs bisphosphonate alone — do not assume postmenopausal osteoporosis drugs work for PPI-induced bone loss

### 3F. Osteoimmunology & Anti-TNF
- Pro-osteoclast cytokines: TNF-α, IL-1/6/7/8… → more/longer-lived osteoclasts
- Infliximab 5 mg/kg in Crohn: lumbar BMD ↑ ~**8%** in one trial; mixed durability with concurrent prednisone

### 3G. Microbiome, Fiber, Mediterranean Diet, Protein
- Probiotic human data still heterogeneous (strain, sex, inflammation, estrogen status); animal signals for *L. helveticus*, *L. reuteri*, LGG, multi-strain — correct dysbiosis, don’t overclaim
- **SCFAs** (butyrate, propionate): ↑ osteoblasts / mineralization; ↓ osteoclastogenesis; lower intestinal pH → better Ca absorption; high-fiber diet may help without probiotics
- **Mediterranean diet:** higher adherence → **~21%** ↓ hip fracture risk + higher BMD (postmenopausal osteoporosis meta-analysis framing)
- **Protein:** US RDA 0.8 g/kg inadequate with aging; European position: protein alone ≈ **2–4%** of BMD variance; aim **≥1–1.3 g/kg** (Neustadt floor: **lb body weight × 0.6** grams/day); front-load protein earlier in day
- Fiber floor: **≥30 g/day** dietary fiber (most patients)
- Vegans/vegetarians: higher osteopenia/osteoporosis risk in most studies — often **inadequate protein**, not ideology per se

### 3H. Clinical Bottom Line (Neustadt)
- Screen GI / PPI / SSRI / steroid / bariatric / celiac patients for bone loss & fracture risk
- Fix gut, inflammation, nutrition, fall risk; prefer outcome-proven nutrients (MK-4 ± D) over surrogate-only marketing
- Ask companies for **BMD + fracture** evidence, not only osteocalcin carboxylation

## Segment 4 — Q&A: MK-4/D3, Testing, Hormones, Peptides, Minerals (1:22–~2:20)

**Speakers:** Neustadt (Speaker 2), Clearfield, Speakers 3–6

### MK-4 + D3 / MK-7 CV Claims
- Strongest fracture/BMD data: **MK-4 ± vitamin D**; synergistic but MK-4 can work alone
- MK-7 CV claims often rest on surrogate markers (e.g., matrix Gla protein / PIVKA); inconsistent coronary calcification benefit — prioritize clinical outcomes

### BPC-157
- Clearfield practice use for GI calming / leaky gut interest; Neustadt: limited rigorous oral-form clinical data

### Bone Turnover Markers (CTX, P1NP, etc.)
- Useful for engagement / trend awareness; analytical variability and strict pre-analytics; not a direct fracture-risk reduction tool

### Estrogen & Bisphosphonates
- Estrogen FDA-approved for **prevention**; most benefit near perimenopause
- Bisphosphonates: poor real-world adherence; limited hip fracture prevention in primary prevention framing Neustadt emphasizes

### Calcium
- Often over-supplemented; follow current intake targets + vitamin D
- Prefers **calcium citrate** (better with hypochlorhydria / achlorhydria / PPI users; citrate preferred with oxalate stones)

### GLP-1 & Bone
- Current evidence: **no clear bone-loss effect** from GLP-1 itself; weight loss may temporarily affect density until intake stabilizes — more research needed

### Calcitonin, Mg, Boron, Zn, Strontium
- Intranasal calcitonin: mainly acute vertebral fracture pain/healing niche
- Mg/B/Zn: supportive physiology but **no fracture-reduction RCTs** comparable to D + Ca / MK-4 story
- Strontium citrate: inflates DEXA readings; ranelate had fracture data but clot risk; citrate lacks robust efficacy/safety RCTs

### Holistic Close
- Diet, sleep, exercise, fall prevention + evidence-based nutrients beat T-score chasing alone
