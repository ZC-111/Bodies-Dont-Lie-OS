## 🎯 Infographic 1: "DEXA Is Not Destiny"

**Title:** Why T-Scores Miss Most Fractures

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Fractures predicted by DEXA (women) | **Only ~44%** |
| Fractures predicted by DEXA (men) | **Only ~21%** |
| Fractures that follow a fall | **~95%** |
| DEXA predictive power if patient is strong/stable | Drops to **~5%** (women, study cited) |

**Footer:** Screen for falls, meds, gut disease, and nutrition — not only the densitometry number.

## 💀 Infographic 2: "Hip Fracture Mortality Reality Check"

**Title:** Osteoporosis Kills

```
Woman ≥65 + hip fracture
  → ~20% dead within 1 year (commonly cited)
  → studies range higher (up to ~36–60%)

Men: mortality often ~2× women (more/severe comorbidities)

Global: a fragility fracture every ~30 seconds
Burden: second only to cardiovascular disease
```

**Callout:** Surviving 90 days lowers risk — the early window is deadly.

## 🦠 Infographic 3: "GI Patients = Bone Patients"

**Title:** Enteropathic Osteoporosis — Who to Screen

| Population | Bone signal cited |
|---|---|
| Celiac (new Dx, young adults) | Up to **60%** low BMD; **35%** osteoporosis |
| Long-term PPI (GERD) | Hip Fx OR **~3.49** |
| SSRI 1–5 years | **1 fracture per ~19** patients |
| IBD | Osteoporosis up to **77%**; hip Fx RR ↑ 41–68% |
| Post-gastrectomy | Median Fx **~3.1 years** after surgery |
| Bariatric (1 year) | **~71%** vit D deficient; **~21.5%** iron deficient |

**Footer:** Osteoporosis can be the *only* presentation of celiac disease.

## 💊 Infographic 4: "PPI Caution Stack"

**Title:** Acid Blockers & Broken Bones

```
PPI ≥1 year     → hip Fx risk ↑ (adjusted OR ~1.44; crude ~1.85)
PPI ~4 years    → OR ~2.17
PPI + GERD      → OR ~3.5 for hip fracture
PPI + falls     → more falls & fall hospitalizations
H2RA            → safer-ish, not risk-free

⚠ PPI + bisphosphonate → ~52% MORE fractures
   than bisphosphonate alone (meta-analysis of 4 studies)
```

**Action:** Deprescribe PPIs when possible; don’t “cover” PPI bone loss with Fosamax by default.

## 🦴 Infographic 5: "MK-4 vs MK-7 Showdown"

**Title:** Same “Vitamin K2” Category — Different Evidence

| | MK-4 | MK-7 |
|---|---|---|
| BMD outcomes | ✅ Repeated positive trials | ❌ Not shown to stop loss / improve BMD |
| Fracture outcomes | ✅ Some studies **>70%** ↓ | ❌ None |
| Japan approval | ✅ **45 mg/day** since ~1995–96 | — |
| Marketing trap | — | Surrogates (ucOC, MGP) ≠ fractures |

**Dose callout:** Clinical bone protocols discussed → **MK-4 45 mg/day** (± vitamin D synergy).

## 🥗 Infographic 6: "Plate Protocol for Bones"

**Title:** Protein + Fiber + Mediterranean Pattern

```
Protein floor (US practical):
  pounds × 0.6 = grams/day minimum
  (research often 1–1.3 g/kg; RDA 0.8 is too low with aging)

Timing: front-load earlier in the day — don’t stack only at dinner

Fiber: ≥ 30 g/day

Mediterranean adherence → ~21% lower hip fracture risk
  + higher BMD (lumbar, whole body, trochanter)

Vegan/vegetarian: higher osteoporosis risk in most studies
  → usually not enough protein
```

## ⚖️ Infographic 8: "Mechanisms of Enteropathic Osteoporosis"

**Title:** Five Levers Between Gut & Bone

```
1. Medications — PPI, SSRI/SNRI, glucocorticoids
2. Inflammation — TNF-α / ILs → osteoclastogenesis (“immunoporosis”)
3. Leaky gut — osteoclastogenic cytokines enter circulation
4. Deficiencies — D, K, Fe, folate, B6, Cu, Zn…
5. Dysbiosis — ↓ SCFAs (butyrate/propionate), ↑ RANKL signaling

Bonus traps:
  • Steroids wreck collagen before DEXA drops
  • N-bisphosphonates block endogenous MK-4
  • Low estrogen ↓ vitamin K absorption
```
