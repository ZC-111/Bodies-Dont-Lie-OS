## Segment 3 — Estrogen Timing Controversy: KEERS/KEEPS, ELITE & Beyond Age 65 (18:17–36:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield; Carol (Speaker 5); Melvin Nario; panel

### ★ Core Thesis
> Academic “10-year / age-60 window” for HRT is built on **synthetic/CEE-heavy trials** and leaves women >60 with systemic estrogen deficiency untreated — vaginal atrophy, cognition, bone, and CV systems still have estrogen receptors.

### 3A. Estrogen as Master Hormone (organ systems)
- **Brain:** hippocampus, PFC, hypothalamus — mood, cognition, sleep, thermoregulation
- **CV:** arterial flexibility, lipids, inflammation
- **Bone:** inhibits osteoclasts
- **Metabolic:** insulin sensitivity, fat distribution, mitochondria

### 3B. Men need some estrogen too
- Bodybuilder-era TRT → T 1500–2500 + AI to “zero estrogen” = disaster
- Low estrogen in men ≈ menopausal phenotype: CVD, osteoporosis (♂ osteoporotic fracture year-1 mortality ~**37%**), cognitive decline

### 3C. Trials discussed
| Trial | Claimed narrative | Clearfield critique |
|---|---|---|
| **KEEPS (2012)** | Early HT in recent menopause helps CV/mood | CEE + limited endpoints; industry sponsors (Novo, AstraZeneca, Janssen, Merck, Pfizer board ties); ancillary: mood/hot flashes/sleep/BMD; **transdermal E2** ↑ sexual function; no severe VTE signal in trial as presented |
| **ELITE (2016)** | Early (<6 yr) initiation slows carotid thickening; late neutral/↑ risk | Drives “<60 and <10 years” candidate language; silent on what to do for women past the window |
| **WHI** | Historic fear driver | Dismissed as synthetic E + synthetic progestin disaster — not bioidentical practice |

### 3D. Medicare cohort beyond age 65 (Nario, 2024)
- ~**10 million** U.S. women ≥65
- Low-dose **transdermal or vaginal estrogen** associated with ↓ endometrial Ca, ovarian Ca, IHD, HF, even VTE signal favorable in report as presented
- E + progestin: similar reductions in some domains; **no broad ↑ breast cancer or dementia** in this framing
- Supports non-oral routes when continuing/starting later

### 3E. Panel clinical pearls

## Segment 4 — Progesterone After Hysterectomy (36:00–1:04:00)

**Speaker:** Bill Clearfield, DO (CME-style slide deck)

### ★ Core Thesis
> **Never estrogen without progesterone** (pinky-swear rule). Hysterectomy removes the uterus, not progesterone receptors in brain, bone, breast, vessel wall, thyroid axis, or immune system.

### 4A. Physiology & metaphor
- Produced: ovaries/corpus luteum, placenta, adrenals; luteal serum peak ~**20 ng/mL**; draw days **19–21** if cycling
- **Estrogen = Ralph (Honeymooners)** — havoc; **progesterone = Alice** — cleanup crew
- Estrogen dominance: headaches, breast/uterine/ovarian cysts, moodiness, gallstones, cramps, PMS
- Progesterone: sleep, calming, ↓ BP, diuresis, scalp hair, antidepressant, anti-inflammatory, balances estrogen

### 4B. Progesterone ≠ progestins
| Natural progesterone | Synthetic progestins |
|---|---|
| Maintains pregnancy; improves sleep/mood | Opposite metabolic/mood profile |
| Diuretic; no direct IR worsening; better lipids | Weight gain, fluid retention, depression, acne, hair loss, insomnia |
| No ↑ CVD evidence as framed; ↓ breast stimulation | Do **not** balance estrogen the same way |
| Local cream on breasts → soften fibrocystic change in ~6–8 weeks | WHI-style combinations drove breast-cancer fear |

### 4C. Key evidence points (as presented)
- Long-term study (**33 yr, n=722**): luteal low progesterone → **10×** cancer deaths; **5.4×** premenopausal breast cancer
- Post-WHI comparative framing: no HT ~10/100k; synthetic E alone ↑~20%; synthetic E+progestin ↑~40%; **natural E + natural P → ~10% ↓** vs no HT (and large relative ↓ vs synthetics)
- Estrogen alone post-hysterectomy → ↑ prolactin → ↑ PTH pathway → bone loss (Carol)
- TBI Phase 2: IV progesterone within 24h → **~50%** better 30-day outcomes at **8 mg/kg**; Phase 3 failed after dose cut to **4 mg/kg** (parallel drawn to under-dosed ivermectin trials)

### 4D. Neurosteroid pathway
```
Cholesterol → Pregnenolone → Progesterone → 5α-DHP → Allopregnanolone → GABA tone
```
- Low P → low allopregnanolone → low GABA → agitation, insomnia, anxiety (matches deficiency symptom list)
- Astrocytes/oligodendrocytes make neurosteroids locally; regulate glutamate/GABA; neuroprotective in MS demyelination models with E2+P

### 4E. Systemic receptor sites outside uterus
Kidney (diuresis, aldosterone block), CV, lung, GI, bone (osteoblast), brain (sedation, analgesia, visual memory), thyroid (P ↑ thyroid output vs E ↑ TBG), immune (↓ NF-κB / IL-1β / TNF-α)

### 4F. Ratios & dosing
- **(E1 + E2) / P** — symptoms low when ratio **< ~250**; worse with estrogen dominance
- Older rule: **P × 1000 / E2** — <100 estrogen-dominant; >500 progesterone-dominant (usually iatrogenic overdose)
- Oral micronized P **100–200 mg QHS** most potent systemic; cream less potent but good locally (breast, ovarian pain); vaginal needs high dose for systemic effect
- Oral preferred when goal is **allopregnanolone / GABA** (first-pass metabolism) — take at night; topical daytime for local effect
- Cycle restart: 100–200 mg × 10 nights then withdraw → menses in 5–10 days; repeat once; then ultrasound if no bleed
- Escalation to 200 mg: breakthrough bleeding, breast tenderness, fibroids, endometriosis, panic/agitation

### 4G. “Drunk” on progesterone (Carol)
- Excess allopregnanolone → foggy/dizzy; check **sulfation + glucuronidation** detox capacity before abandoning therapy

### Clinical Pearl
> Conventional Mayo/Cleveland/FDA labeling: “no uterus → no progestin.” That logic treats progesterone as a uterine drug only. Bioidentical practice treats it as a whole-body neurosteroid and estrogen balancer.

## Segment 5 — Hormone Testing as a Dating App (1:04:15–1:21:00)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO

### ★ Core Framework
Three “bachelors” — pick the match for the clinical question; ideal is a **Dream Team combo**.

| Test | Persona | Strengths | Red flags | Swipe right when… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Serum** | Reliable classic | Gold standard; total free+bound; insurance; thyroid/IR/FSH/LH/prolactin/SHBG/IGF; best for injectable/pellet BHRT baseline | Misses diurnal cortisol; venipuncture stress; weak for some cream dynamics; doesn’t “see” many synthetics | Full baseline, peri-menopause, insurance-covered monitoring of bioidenticals |
| **Saliva** | Free-spirited adventurer | Free/bioavailable; at-home; multi-sample cortisol/CAR/Cushing night; good early transdermal tracking; symptom correlation | Gingivitis skews androgens/DHEA; cream residue; weak long-term HRT monitoring; limited cycling reference ranges | Adrenal rhythm, ovulation tracking, needle-phobic, early topical HRT |
| **24-h urine** | Deep-dive storyteller | Full steroid metabolome (GC-MS); estrogen detox pathways; oral HRT first-pass; anabolic/catabolic; GH output; dried strips option | Kidney disease/hematuria invalid; averages diurnal peaks; collection burden; weak as sole baseline/androgen | Cancer-risk estrogen metabolism, detox capacity, oral HRT, enzyme activity |

### Practice pearls (Nario + Clearfield)
- Combo: **serum baseline + saliva cortisol/early topical + urine estrogen metabolism**
- Saliva panel ~**$200–300**; not for endless serial dosing once tissues saturated with topicals
- Urine alone = metabolites, not circulating levels — don’t dose solely off metabolites
- Serum T on pellets may read 100–300 while “ideal” female range cited ~**30–40** — **treat symptoms**, not panic ranges
- Bioidenticals show on serum; many **synthetics/progestins do not** — “If you’re my student and you use synthetics, you fail”
- Vaginal DHEA (audience): converts toward T → lubrication/tissue thickening
