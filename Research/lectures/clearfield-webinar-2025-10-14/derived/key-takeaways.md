# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-10-14

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Never estrogen without progesterone** — even after hysterectomy. Pinky-swear rule; uterus is not the only progesterone target.

2. **Timing hypothesis ≠ absolute contraindication after 60.** KEEPS/ELITE/WHI-era synthetics drove the “10-year window”; 2024 Medicare ≥65 data (as presented) favor low-dose transdermal/vaginal estrogen for many outcomes.

3. **Vaginal dryness / failed mucosal health is a red flag** for whole-body estrogen deficiency — not a cosmetic footnote.

4. **Progesterone ≠ progestin.** Micronized progesterone calms, diureses, and balances estrogen; synthetics drive many WHI-era harms and side-effect lists.

5. **Neurosteroid pathway matters:** progesterone → allopregnanolone → GABA. Oral bedtime dosing intentionally uses first-pass metabolites for sleep/anxiety.

6. **“Drunk” on progesterone?** Think impaired sulfation/glucuronidation of allopregnanolone before abandoning therapy (Carol).

7. **Hormone tests are tools, not religions.** Serum = baseline/insurance/BHRT; saliva = free hormone & rhythms; 24-h urine = metabolites/detox. Combine; correlate with symptoms.

8. **Don’t dose solely off urine metabolites or panic pellet serum T** (100–300 printouts vs ~30–40 symptom targets cited).

9. **Faith can heal — or delay care.** Watch prayer-as-substitution and negative religious coping; keep medicine in the plan.

10. **Men need some estrogen.** Crushing E with AIs while pushing T sky-high recreates menopausal risk (bone, brain, heart).
