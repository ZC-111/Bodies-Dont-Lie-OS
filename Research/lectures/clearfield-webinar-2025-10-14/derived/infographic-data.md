## 📊 Infographic 6: "Dosing & Ratio Cheat Sheet"

**Title:** Practical Progesterone Moves

```
Oral micronized P: 100–200 mg QHS (systemic + GABA)
Cream: local breast / ovarian pain (less potent systemic)
Vaginal: needs higher dose for systemic effect

Cycling draw: days 19–21
Ratio cue: (E1+E2)/P  < ~250 → happier symptoms
Old rule: P×1000/E2  <100 E-dominant | >500 P-dominant (often overdose)

Amenorrhea restart: 100–200 mg × 10 nights → withdraw → bleed 5–10 d
```

**Drunk/foggy on P?** Check sulfation + glucuronidation (Carol).
