## Segment 6 — Modalities Compared: Cap Systems, ILF, Dynamical Neuro Optimal (23:21–25:55)

**Speaker:** Marley Jones

### 6A. Cap / Network-Targeted Systems
- Full cap (not just a few sensors); deeper pickup of cortical frequencies
- Targets networks (e.g., amygdala emotional regulation; prefrontal focus)
- Example use: OCD / trauma — improve emotional regulation, ↓ OCD behaviors

### 6B. Infra-Low Frequency (ILF)
- Trains ~**0.1 Hz** (slower than delta)
- Modulates large-scale networks for mood stability / self-regulation
- Claimed help when stuck in hyperarousal (PTSD / complex PTSD) → more balanced resting state
- Also discussed for anxiety, autism

### 6C. Dynamical Neurofeedback — Neuro Optimal ★
- Does **not** target specific frequencies or preset goals
- Monitors continuous activity; detects sudden fluctuations/oscillations
- Triggers brief **auditory interruption** (click / pause) → brain self-corrects
- Principle: **non-linear dynamics** — invitation to CNS, not intervention
- Outcome framing: adaptability, flexibility, resilience under chronic stress

## Segment 8 — Val Brown Five-Phase Model & Do-No-Harm Framing (30:28–36:36)

**Speaker:** Marley Jones

### Five Phases (as mapped to Neuro Optimal)
1. **Instability** — system detects turbulence in cortical activity
2. **Reinforcement** — real-time feedback helps self-correction
3. **Integration** — brain adopts more efficient pathways (path of least discomfort)
4. **Flexibility** — greater adaptability; ↓ cell-danger / stress rigidity

## Segment 10 — Deep Dive Appendix: Linear vs Dynamical Feedback Loop & Consumer Gadgets (~48:05–1:03+)

**Speaker:** Marley Jones (continued lecture / slide narration)

### Linear / Historical Neurofeedback — Limitations
- Amplitude + frequency band training (delta/theta/alpha/beta/gamma)
- Assumes universal ideal cortical states
- Risk of **overtraining**, new imbalances, delayed feedback
- Speaker personal history: QEEG protocol helped anxiety but **worsened chronic fatigue** — felt like a “marionette”

### Dynamical Joint Time–Frequency Analysis
- Continuous real-time monitoring across time **and** frequency (proprietary)
- Processes ~**256×/second** (hardware/compute limit cited)
- No assumed ideal state — only moment-to-moment fluctuation feedback
- Claims: no fatigue/headache/emotional dysregulation from pushing bands

### Car Analogy (development stages)
1. Crank-start → early rigid manual neurofeedback  
2. Manual transmission → guided five-phase specialist protocols  
3. Automatic → semi-structured systems still dictating phases  
4. Self-driving → **Neuro Optimal** auto-navigation: real-time self-correction without forced states

### Feedback Loop (simplified)
```
EEG voltage fluctuations
  → Software analyzes @ 256 Hz (duration, intensity, frequency, shifts)
  → Ear sensors reject external noise
  → Amp: analog → digital
  → Brief media-stream interruptions
  → CNS adjusts → ↑ flexibility/resilience, better interoception, ↓ allostatic load
```
