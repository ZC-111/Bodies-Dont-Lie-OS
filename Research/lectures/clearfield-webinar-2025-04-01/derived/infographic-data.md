## ⚖️ Infographic 3: "Three Neurofeedback Families"

**Title:** Cap Networks vs ILF vs Dynamical

| Type | What it does | Best mental model |

| Cap / network systems | Target amygdala, PFC connectivity | Structured network coaching |

## 🧠 Infographic 4: "Five-Phase Self-Regulation Arc"

**Title:** Val Brown Model Mapped to Neuro Optimal

```
1 INSTABILITY   → Detect cortical turbulence
2 REINFORCEMENT → Real-time feedback / self-correct
3 INTEGRATION   → More efficient pathways emerge
4 FLEXIBILITY   → Adaptability ↑; rigid stress patterns ↓
5 RESILIENCE    → Stable self-optimization under stress
```

**Key insight:** Goal is fluid adaptability — not locking the brain into “good” bands.

## 🚨 Infographic 7: "When Protocols Backfire"

**Title:** Overtraining Risk in Rigid Nervous Systems

```
Stuck fight-or-flight / deep “wagon-rut” pathways
        │
        ▼
Force amplitude/frequency protocol
        │
        ├── May help one symptom (e.g., anxiety)
        └── May worsen another (e.g., chronic fatigue)

Prefer: information-only dynamical feedback
        → No assumed universal ideal state
        → Avoid fatigue / headache / emotional dysregulation
           from pushing bands too far
```

**Footer:** Jones: QEEG protocol helped anxiety, worsened CFS — felt like a marionette.

## 📱 Infographic 8: "Clinic-Grade vs Consumer Gadgets"

**Title:** Signal Quality Matters

| Device | Promise | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Muse / Mendi | App-guided focus/relaxation | Motion & artifact contamination |
| Narbus glasses | Lenses track “focus” | Blink proxies ≠ cognition |
| Clinical dynamical EEG | Artifact rejection + real-time CNS mirror | Higher cost, higher fidelity |

**Clinical outcomes cited on slides:**
- ADHD: **30–50%** symptom improvement signal vs stimulants
- Sleep: often **first** noticeable win
- PTSD/anxiety: emotional regulation / ↓ amygdala hyperactivity framing
- Pain: cortical “stuck pattern” reorganization after tissue healing
