### Q8. How does dynamical Neuro Optimal differ from traditional frequency-band neurofeedback?
**Answer:** It does **not** target specific frequencies or predetermined ideal states. It continuously monitors activity, detects sudden fluctuations/oscillations, and delivers a brief auditory interruption so the brain **self-corrects**. Framing is invitation/non-linear dynamics, not forced protocol training.

### Q13. Why does Jones warn against some traditional protocol-based neurofeedback in highly distressed, rigid patients?
**Answer:** An already hyper-aroused / “guard up” nervous system can be **over-stimulated** by medical-style intervention protocols, increasing cost (worsened dysregulation) rather than calming. Dynamical feedback aims to restore flexibility without imposing states.

## Section D — Clinical Integration (2 questions)

### Q19. A veteran with PTSD, poor sleep, and hypervigilance asks about neurofeedback vs TMS. Using this webinar’s framing, how do you counsel?
**Answer:**
1. PTSD / first-responder “stuck” cortical patterns are highlighted as strong Neuro Optimal candidates
2. Sleep is often the first domain to improve
3. Dynamical neurofeedback is framed as non-stimulatory invitation (self-regulation), whereas TMS is stimulatory/augmenting — Jones prefers non-augmenting tools as a naturopath but acknowledges TMS can help depression
4. Combine with trauma-informed care; do not promise cure — emphasize adaptability / ↓ allostatic load
5. Consider home rental if clinic visits are a barrier

### Q20. A parent of three children on the autism spectrum cannot afford weekly in-clinic sessions for each child. What practice model does Jones recommend?
**Answer:** Prioritize **system rental or purchase** so the whole family can train at home (Zengar buyers are often moms helping kids; multiple neurodivergent children in one household is increasingly common). Clinicians can earn via rentals + sales/referral commissions; personal preloaded units (~100 sessions) are usually poorer value than licensed professional/unlimited systems.
