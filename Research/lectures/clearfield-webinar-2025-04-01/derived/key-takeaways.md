# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-04-01

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Neurofeedback is cross-specialty.** Neurology, psychiatry/therapy, pain, and sleep practices all have citation-backed entry points (concussion, epilepsy, ADHD, PTSD, migraine, insomnia).

2. **Dynamical ≠ protocol training.** Neuro Optimal does not chase ideal frequency bands; it mirrors turbulence with micro auditory interruptions so the brain self-corrects.

3. **Interoception + allostatic load are the integrative bridge.** Training is framed as restoring internal sensing and lowering cumulative stress load — root-cause nervous-system work.

4. **Stuck patterns are “wagon ruts.”** Trauma, ACE scores, mold, TBI, Lyme, and chronic fight-or-flight deepen rigid cortical pathways; flexibility is the therapeutic target.

5. **Sleep is often the first win.** Across Zengar’s reported millions of sessions, sleep improvement is the most consistent early signal.

6. **Overtraining is a real harm mode.** Amplitude/frequency protocols can help one domain and worsen another (Jones: anxiety ↑ but chronic fatigue crashed).

7. **ILF and cap systems still have roles** (0.1 Hz self-regulation; amygdala/PFC network targeting) — know the family of tools, then choose philosophy.

8. **Economics enable adherence.** ~$79 clinic sessions vs family rentals ≈ $5/effective session; ~12–15 adult sessions; certification ~$660; pro system ~$10K.

9. **Consumer EEG gadgets are not clinic substitutes.** Muse/Mendi/Narbus-style devices struggle with artifact; blink or motion can drive false feedback.

10. **Theory matters for consent.** Pribram holonomic / dynamical framing + Val Brown five-phase arc (instability → resilience) help patients understand *why* you are not “programming” their brain.

## Comparison: Traditional vs Dynamical Neurofeedback

| Dimension | Traditional / Linear | Dynamical (Neuro Optimal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Detection** | Amplitude + frequency bands | Joint time–frequency; moment-to-moment shifts |
| **Goal setting** | Practitioner/QEEG ideal state | No assumed universal ideal |
| **Feedback** | Reward toward target bands | Auditory micro-interruption |
| **Risk called out** | Overtraining, new imbalances, delayed feedback | Framed as low side-effect, non-stimulatory |
| **Patient fit** | Protocol-tolerant | Hyper-aroused / rigid / trauma-heavy |
| **Home use** | Often trainer-dependent | Auto-navigational / plug-and-play |
| **Philosophy** | Fix the machine | Invite self-organization |

**Consensus from lecture:** Prefer dynamical systems when the nervous system is already guarded; reserve forced protocols carefully.
