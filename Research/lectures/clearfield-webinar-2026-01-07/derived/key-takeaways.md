# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-01-07

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Collagen is signaling therapy** — hydrolyzed peptides cue repair cells; dose **10–20 g/day × 8–12+ weeks** with C/Zn/Cu. Match type 1/2/3 to tissue goal; species matters less once hydrolyzed.

2. **Collagen has BMD data, not fracture outcomes** — useful adjunct; Neustadt prefers food protein first (≥0.6 g/lb/day) and will not treat collagen as anti-fracture monotherapy.

3. **~95% of fractures follow a fall** — gait, balance, chair-rise, one-leg stand, and **indoor sneakers** often beat another densitometry debate.

4. **Invert FRAX for counseling** — a 10% 10-year risk is a **90% non-fracture** probability; abnormal DEXA is seldom an emergency.

5. **DEXA alone is weakly predictive** — ~44% of osteoporotic women / ~21% of men who fracture are “caught” by DEXA framing; ΔBMD on drugs poorly predicts anti-fracture effect.

6. **Vitamin D 30–44 ng/mL** is the hip-fracture / fall-reduction band Neustadt emphasizes (~2000 IU D3 for many) — distinct from higher immune targets.

7. **MK-4 45 mg/day divided** is the vitamin K form with BMD **and** large fracture-reduction data (>70%); K1 and MK-7 improve ucOC without comparable Fx proof.

8. **BTMs (prefer CTX + P1NP)** monitor adherence, not destiny — high pre-analytical noise; “fast bone loser” prediction ≈ coin toss.

9. **Men’s bone workup:** NLR, D, PTH, T **and E2 (>20)**; HRT physiologic ranges; strength/stability still foundational; occult celiac if unexplained.

10. **No strontium citrate extrapolation** from European ranelate trials — vertebral-leaning efficacy, DEXA artifact, clot/stroke/MI safety signal, zero citrate safety/Fx RCTs.
