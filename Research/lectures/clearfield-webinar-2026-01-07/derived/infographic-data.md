## 🧬 Infographic 1: "Collagen = Messenger, Not Brick"

**Title:** Stop Thinking “Drink Skin / Joints / Bone”

```
Hydrolyzed collagen
  │
  ├── Digested → bioactive peptides
  │     (glycine, proline, hydroxyproline fragments)
  │
  ├── Signal → fibroblasts / chondrocytes / osteoblasts
  │
  └── Clinical ask:
        Which type?  Which dose?  How long?  Cofactors?
```

**Footer:** “You’re not becoming a cow — you’re borrowing its amino acids.” — Nario

## 📦 Infographic 2: "Match Collagen to Tissue Goal"

| Goal | Prefer | Typical evidence niche |
|---|---|---|
| Skin, hair, nails, bone matrix | **Type 1** (±3) — bovine / porcine | Skin RCTs; BMD (König) |
| Cartilage / joints | **Type 2** | Joint-focused products |
| Skin elasticity / vessels | **Type 3** | Often paired with type 1 |
| Fine wrinkles / hydration | **Marine type 1** | Skin; weak joint/bone data |

**Dose card:** **10–20 g/day** hydrolyzed peptides × **8–12+ weeks** + vitamin C / zinc / copper  
**Fail mode:** half-scoop in coffee twice a week

## 🦴 Infographic 3: "Fracture Risk Is Multivariable"

**Title:** DEXA Is One Tile, Not the Whole Mosaic

```
FRACTURE RISK
  ├── Fall risk (≈95% of fractures follow a fall)
  ├── Strength & balance (one-leg stand, gait, chair rise)
  ├── Footwear at home
  ├── Meds that destroy bone / increase falls
  ├── Hormones (E2, T, PTH)
  ├── Inflammation (NLR) & vitamin D
  └── Bone density / quality (DEXA, TBS, REMS, QCT…)
```

**Callout:** DEXA alone predicts fracture in only **~44% of osteoporotic women** and **~21% of men**.

## 👟 Infographic 4: "Home Footwear Fix"

**Title:** Most Falls Happen Indoors

| Fact | Number |
|---|---|
| Falls in adults >70 while barefoot/socks/no shoes | **>50%** |
| Fall risk barefoot/socks vs sneakers (age ≥65) | **11.2×** |
| Surgical hip-Fx patients in socks/slippers/barefoot | **72%** (mean age 78) |

**Action:** Lace-up **indoor sneakers** with support — open-heel slippers also predict injurious falls.

## 🦵 Infographic 5: "10-Second One-Leg Stand"

**Title:** Office Test with Mortality Signal

```
Study: ~1,700 adults, ~10-year follow-up

Age 51–55:  95.3% could hold 10 seconds
Age 71+:    <50% could hold 10 seconds

Cannot hold 10 sec → +84% all-cause mortality
Cannot hold 5 sec  → +213% injurious fall risk
```

**Bridge:** Use results to open protein, resistance training, and micronutrient conversations.

## 💊 Infographic 6: "Vitamin K — Surrogate vs Outcome"

**Title:** Lower ucOC ≠ Proven Fracture Protection

| Form | Lowers ucOC? | Improves BMD? | Fracture RCTs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **K1** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ (no reduction) |
| **MK-7** | ✅ | ❌ (may worsen hip BMD at high dose) | ❌ none |
| **MK-4 45 mg/day divided** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ **>70% fewer fractures** |

**Footer:** Demand fracture endpoints — not just prettier lab numbers.

## 🧪 Infographic 7: "Lab Ladder for Bone Context"

**Title:** What Neustadt Actually Uses / Trusts

| Test | Clinical use |
|---|---|
| **25-OH vitamin D** | Hip Fx + falls sweet spot **30–44 ng/mL** (~2000 IU D3 for many) |
| **NLR** | ≥3.17 osteopenia signal; ≥4.68 osteoporosis signal |
| **PTH / T / E2 (men)** | Keep male **E2 >20**; bone gain from T partly via aromatization |
| **CTX + P1NP** | Monitor **drug adherence**; weak standalone Fx crystal ball |
| **Celiac serology** | Unexplained osteoporosis (can be sole clue) |

**BTM warning:** Morning fasting; same lab/season/cycle phase; high assay CV — don’t over-interpret single values.

## ⛔ Infographic 8: "Strontium Decision Card"

**Title:** Why Neustadt Says No to US Strontium Citrate

```
Studied drug:   Strontium RANELATE (EU) — not US OTC
US supplement:  Strontium CITRATE — no Fx RCTs, no safety data

Ranelate scorecard:
  • ~45% vertebral RRR in most trials
  • Hip benefit in only 1/5 large trials
  • Falsely elevates DEXA (US machines don’t correct)
  • Vascular clot / stroke / MI signal ≈ 1 serious event per Fx prevented

Prefer: MK-4 + D3 (± Ca) with fracture outcomes + Strong Bones framing
```

**Competitor callout:** Algae calcium + strontium citrate stacks ≠ ranelate evidence.
