# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-06-09

## Top 10 Clinical / Practice Pearls

1. **Arginine, melatonin, and GABA are low-cost GH-axis adjuncts.** They mainly remove somatostatin brakes and support slow-wave sleep; peptides still “press the accelerator.” Expect acute GH spikes more than proven long-term body-composition miracles.

2. **Dose anchors from the tip:** arginine 5–10 g empty stomach; melatonin 0.5–5 mg 30–60 min before bed; GABA 250–750 mg qHS (Powers study used 3 g).

3. **MYCIN-era lesson:** infection decision support is decades old — modern value is usable, clinician-shaped depth (functional + traditional), not novelty branding.

4. **Taya Pro Med pricing frame:** $99 Pro vs $397 clinical suite vs free trial — positioned far below many scribe-only products.

5. **Polypharmacy > med list.** Demo “triple whammy” (ACEI + diuretic + NSAID) plus nutrient depletions and CBD–drug pairs — have staff enter full Rx/OTC/supplement lists before the visit.

6. **Condition modules beat generic chatbots** when you need functional + traditional layers, cases, patient booklets, protocols, and IRB drafts in one workflow.

7. **Trust but verify AI MCQs.** Live Hashimoto’s screening question returned different correct letters/explanations to different users — content needs Bayesian QA before teaching from it.

8. **Navigation matters:** for dosing/mechanism monographs use **By supplement**, not only AI Search (berberine lesson).

9. **Education stack is part of the product:** weekly newsletter, AI podcasts (epigenetics demo), course marketplace, optional publishing add-on, University/Mediverse library.

10. **Platform philosophy:** practitioner-built, feedback-responsive (~weekly updates), medical-scope guardrails, marketplace equity vs gig-telemed extraction — AI assists; it does not replace the clinician.
