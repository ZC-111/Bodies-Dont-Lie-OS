## Infographic 1: "GH Axis — Accelerator vs Brakes"

**Title:** Peptides Aren’t the Whole Play

```
ACCELERATOR                    BRAKES / SUPPORT
GHRH / GHRP peptides           Somatostatin tone
Sermorelin / ipamorelin / CJC  Sleep debt / poor SWS
Exogenous GH                   Nutrient gaps
```

**Support stack (Nario tip):**
| Tool | Role | Example dose (as taught) |
|---|---|---|
| Arginine | ↓ somatostatin | 5–10 g empty stomach |
| Melatonin | ↓ somatostatin + ↑ SWS | 0.5–5 mg qHS |
| GABA | ↑ GH pulse (Powers 2008) | 250–750 mg qHS (study used 3 g) |

**Footer:** Strongest evidence = acute GH rise. Long-term body-comp claims = still uncertain.

## Infographic 2: "Arginine Evidence Strip"

**Title:** The Most Studied Amino-Acid Secretagogue

| Source | Signal |
|---|---|
| Alba-Roth et al. 1988 | ↑ GH; amplifies GHRH response |
| Goli 2022 meta-analysis | Stimulates GH mainly via somatostatin suppression |
| Acute studies | Often ~2–4× baseline GH (esp. fasting) |
| Corpas et al. | Short-term Arg+Lys ≠ sustained GH/IGF-1 in older men |

**Callout:** Use as adjunct to peptides — not a guaranteed long-term anabolic substitute.
