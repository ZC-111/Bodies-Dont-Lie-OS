## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Natural Growth-Hormone Axis Support (10:01–15:21)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 4)

### Framing
Patients chase GH or peptides (sermorelin, ipamorelin, CJC). Inexpensive nutrients can support the axis as the “offensive line” while peptides press the accelerator.

### 1) Arginine (strongest amino-acid GH secretagogue cited)
- **Mechanism:** Suppresses somatostatin → removes a primary brake on GH release; complements GHRH/GHRP without competing with peptide signaling.
- **Evidence cited:** Alba-Roth et al. 1988 (arginine ↑ GH, amplifies GHRH response); Goli 2022 systematic review/meta-analysis (somatostatin-suppression pathway).
- **Acute effect:** Often ~2–4× GH above baseline (esp. fasting).
- **Caveat:** Corpas et al. — short-term oral arginine + lysine did **not** significantly raise long-term GH/IGF-1 in older men → strongest signal is **acute**, not sustained anabolism.
- **Practical note:** Arginase competes with eNOS for arginine → may limit NO benefits.
- **Dose:** 5–10 g pre-bed or pre-workout, empty stomach.

### 2) Melatonin
- Suppresses somatostatin; supports slow-wave sleep; augments GH pulse.
- **Evidence cited:** Valcavi 1993 (↑ spontaneous GH; ~doubled GH response to GHRH); Nassar et al. (~2007/2020s) — 0.5 mg and 5 mg ↑ GH around resistance exercise; higher dose larger effect (~150% GH response often quoted).
- Synergistic with bedtime GH secretagogues (supports largest physiologic GH pulse in SWS).
- **Dose:** 0.5–5 mg, 30–60 min before bed.
- **Pearl:** Sometimes the best “anti-aging” move is sleep before midnight.

### 3) GABA
- **Evidence cited:** Powers et al. 2008 — 3 g oral GABA → ~400% ↑ resting GH; ~200% greater GH response post–resistance exercise.
- Mechanism frame: somatotroph activity via pathways involving Pit-1 transcription (as stated).
- Useful clinically for insomnia, anxiety, high cortisol / chronic stress.
- **Dose:** 250–750 mg at bedtime; pairs well with melatonin.

### Take-home
> Peptides press the accelerator; amino acids, melatonin, and GABA help remove brakes. Literature supports **acute** GH enhancement; long-term body-composition/longevity translation is less certain — but low-cost, generally safe adjuncts to a full GH-optimization plan.
