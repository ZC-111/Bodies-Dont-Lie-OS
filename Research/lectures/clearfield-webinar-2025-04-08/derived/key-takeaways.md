# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-04-08

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Homeopathy treats the patient who has the illness** — same “sinusitis” label can map to different symptom pictures and different medicines; SOAP alone is the wrong primary map.

2. **Three principles, always:** law of similars, high dilution, individuality. Bulk supplements at material doses are not homeopathy.

3. **Clinical constitution = three lenses used together:** (a) reactional mode / elimination, (b) morphology & connective tissue, (c) sensitive type.

4. **Psoric = open elimination with return to baseline**; **sycotic = blocked elimination after an insult** (“After what?”) with weight, growths, chronic catarrh, and lost baseline; **syphilitic = destructive**; **tuberculinic = fast, weak psoric subset**.

5. **Morphology predicts trajectory:** fluoric (lax) → sprain/prolapse themes; phosphoric (normal) → inflammatory cycling; carbonic (tight) → sclerosis/constipation/inflexibility themes.

6. **Sensitive types are multi-axial portraits** — Lycopodium (belt bloating, groggy naps, meal-clock migraines, scratch-to-bleed, contradiction flush) vs Nux vomica (Type-A, 3 a.m. worries, refreshed post-meal naps).

7. **Homeopathics are FDA-regulated medications since 1938** — not casual “remedies.” Direct toxicity is rare; harm is mainly delayed emergency care.

8. **Use as first agent and/or combination care** — acuity rules (possible pyelo → antibiotic + homeopathy; simple sinusitis → environment + homeopathy first, antibiotic contingency).

9. **Outcome aim of constitutional work:** fewer and shorter eliminations/illnesses → less reliance on stacked “anti-” drugs that can themselves push physiology toward sycosis.

10. **Evidence Hakimi emphasized:** EPI3 (~8,559) — similar outcomes with less med burden; LBP follow-up with halved drug use; Arnica gene-expression / repair signals. Skip unexperimented “COVID remedy” marketing.
