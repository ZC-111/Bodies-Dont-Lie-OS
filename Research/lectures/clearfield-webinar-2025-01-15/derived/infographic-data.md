## 🧬 Infographic 1: "What Is Metabolomics?"

**Title:** From Molecules to Phenotype

```
GENOME  →  TRANSCRIPTOME  →  PROTEOME  →  METABOLOME
 (DNA)        (RNA)           (proteins)    (metabolites)
   risk          activity         machines      phenotype NOW
```

| Matrix | What you see |
|---|---|
| Blood / urine / stool / saliva | Pathway activity in real time |
| Retestable | Unlike fixed DNA sequence |
| Best with | Subjective + objective clinical exam |

**Footer:** Don’t confuse genetic *risk* (e.g., MTHFR) with current metabolite dysfunction.

## ⏱️ Infographic 2: "Pathway Red Flags"

**Title:** How to Read a Metabolite Pattern

| Pattern | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| All metabolites ↑ or all ↓ in a pathway | Global pathway stress — investigate |
| High upstream + low downstream | Enzyme block (genetic or chemical) or low cofactor |
| ↑ Glycolysis + ↓ TCA | Mitochondrial / redox / O₂ junction problem → lactate risk |
| Wrong time of day | Circadian artifact — retest with consistent timing |

**Callout:** Methylfolate example — missing cofactor stalls Hcy → methionine.

MHO (metabolically healthy)     MUO (metabolically unhealthy)

## 🧪 Infographic 4: "MetS Diagnostic Snapshot"

**Title:** Any 3 of 5 = Metabolic Syndrome

1. ↑ Waist circumference  
2. ↑ Triglycerides  
3. ↓ HDL  
4. ↑ Blood pressure  
5. ↑ Fasting glucose  

**Also watch:** microalbuminuria · ↑ uric acid · abnormal OGTT / 2-hr glucose

**Drivers:** TNF-α / IL-6 / CRP · excess FFA · high leptin / low adiponectin · ROS · RAAS

## 🦠 Infographic 5: "Gut–MetS Axis"

**Title:** Microbiome Signals That Move Risk

| Higher risk pattern | Lower risk pattern |
|---|---|
| Dysbiosis; ↓ Bifido / Lactobacillus | Mediterranean-associated flora |
| ↓ / absent **Akkermansia** | Akkermansia supported (foods: cranberry, pomegranate) |
| LPS + pro-inflammatory tryptophan mets | ↑ SCFAs → ↓ inflammation |
| Choline → TMAO (worse in dysbiosis) | Healthier handling of diet metabolites |

**Rule:** Test before empiric Akkermansia — mucin regulator; dose/presence is not “more is better.”
