# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-01-15

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Metabolomics shows phenotype now; genomics shows risk.** An MTHFR variant is not proof of methylation failure — look at homocysteine/methionine and related metabolites.

2. **Always pair metabolite panels with subjective + objective exam.** Isolated omics without energy, sleep, diet, vitals, and nutrition-focused PE is incomplete (and irresponsible).

3. **Circadian timing is a pre-analytic variable.** Same pathway can look different midday vs midnight; standardize collection time.

4. **Visceral fat — not BMI alone — separates MHO from MUO.** WHR >0.85 (women) / >1.0 (men) flags apple-shape risk.

5. **MetS = any 3 of 5 classic criteria**, driven by inflammation, excess FFA, and mitochondrial ETC leak → ROS → insulin resistance.

6. **Gut dysbiosis amplifies MetS risk** via LPS/endotoxemia, unfavorable tryptophan metabolites, and TMAO handling; Akkermansia is often missing — **test before supplementing**.

7. **Health scores beat raw metabolite dumps for busy clinics.** Score dyslipidemia / BP / glucose / composite MetS, then drill into contributing metabolites.

8. **Digital twinning** simulates stacked diet/lifestyle/supplement moves against predicted health-score change — framed as bias reduction vs favorite-supplement prescribing.

9. **BAIBA + cold + spices** are practical white→brown adipose levers; bergamot and cinnamon are highlighted food bioactives for atherogenic lipids / MetS markers.

10. **Rapamycin and “anti-aging” drugs are not first-line.** Exhaust lifestyle and safer mitophagy supports (e.g., urolithin A in his experience); watch immune and autoimmune blowback with rapamycin. Statins/metformin treat signals, not always root mitochondrial/lifestyle drivers.
