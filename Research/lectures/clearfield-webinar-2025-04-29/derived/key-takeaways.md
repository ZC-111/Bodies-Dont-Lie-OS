## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Fix foundations before peptides.** Testosterone, GH, metformin, desiccated thyroid, Mg/electrolytes, and taurine turn a 3–4/10 peptide response into ~10/10.

2. **Fat loss is multi-pathway, not Ozempic escalation.** Combine GH-axis (tesamorelin), mito (SLU-PP-332, MOTS-c), NAD (5-Amino-1MQ), and insulin (retatrutide) at lower doses for 12–24 weeks.

3. **Retatrutide is the presenters’ metabolic “master” agent** — GLP/GIP/glucagon tri-agonist used as a **microdose**, not a weekly megabolus. Split dosing flattens side effects.

4. **MOTS-c does not expire in 15 minutes.** Cited LC-MS data: ~99% potency at 30 days post-reconstitution — ignore the “must inject entire vial now” rumor.

5. **SS-31 = mito hardware; MOTS-c = mito software.** SS-31 also framed as an “amplifier” (including androgen-receptor sensitivity).

6. **GHK-Cu topicals need ≥3% copper peptide** or they are underdosed marketing. Injectable GHK-Cu may burn (skin/melanin-related variability).

7. **Immune stack ladder:** TA1 for travel/baseline → LL-37 for infection → KPV for gut/systemic inflammation → VIP only for critical illness → microdose retatrutide when autoimmunity looks like insulin resistance.

8. **Cartalax (“cartilage” bioregulator)** is the early breakout healing add-on to BPC-157/TB-500/GHK-Cu for joints and soft tissue — early Western data, high enthusiasm.

9. **Sleep: mitigate EMF before DSIP.** Shielded paint/canopy beats peptides; metal-coil / Eight Sleep mattresses called EMF antennas.

10. **Long-term GLP success = microdose + metabolic flexibility + optional LDN cycling.** Carb-phobic high-fat diets amplify gastric-emptying side effects; clean carbs/fiber matter.

## Comparison: Weekly Megadose GLP vs Microdose Multi-Pathway

| Dimension | Weekly megadose (semaglutide-style) | Microdose multi-pathway (this webinar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary lever** | Appetite suppression | Insulin + mito + GH + NAD |
| **Dosing pattern** | Large once-weekly peak/trough | Split 0.15–0.5 mg EOD / 2–3×/wk |
| **Side-effect profile** | Nausea, fatigue, muscle loss if misused | Lower peaks; still start low/slow |
| **Diet advice** | Often paired with extreme low-carb | Stay metabolically flexible; clean carbs |
| **Duration mindset** | Escalate forever | 12–24 wk stacks; then taper maintenance |
| **Best for** | Insurance-driven appetite only | Metabolic disease + biomarker repair |
| **Presenters’ view** | “Antiquated” if alternatives exist | Preferred clinical framework |

**Consensus:** Match stack to priority (fat → immune → longevity). Don’t inject everything at once.
