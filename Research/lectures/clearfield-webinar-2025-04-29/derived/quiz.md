## Section C — Protocols, Q&A & Practice Systems (6 questions)

### Q15. Preferred retatrutide microdosing pattern from Q&A:
**Answer:** Roughly **0.15–0.5 mg** split across the week (e.g., every other day / M-W-F), **not** a large once-weekly bolus (2–3 mg weekly called a “recipe for disaster”).

### Q16. Why do split GLP doses reduce side effects vs weekly megadoses?
- A) They shorten half-life to hours
- B) **They flatten peak/trough exposure so nausea/fatigue are less extreme** ✓
- C) They eliminate all immunogenicity forever
- D) They convert peptides to orals automatically

### Q17. For mold/mycotoxin patients, which immune peptides are prioritized before SS-31?
**Answer:** **Thymosin Alpha-1, Thymalin, LL-37, and KPV** for immune clearance/inflammation; SS-31 addresses secondary mitochondrial impairment/fatigue, not mold clearance itself.

### Q18. LDN’s role in long-term peptide use per Hunter:
- A) Cure opioid addiction only at 50 mg
- B) **Modulate immunogenicity / restore peptide sensitivity; often 0.5–1.5 mg; e.g. 3 months on / 3 months off** ✓
- C) Replace all cycling forever
- D) Must stop the peptide entirely while on LDN

### Q19. What dietary error worsens GLP side effects according to the presenters?
**Answer:** **Carb-phobic keto/carnivore + high fat** while on GLPs → delayed gastric emptying without fiber/roughage → gallbladder and bowel complications. Advise metabolic flexibility with clean carbs (e.g., quinoa, yams, rice, oats).

### Q20. What is the legal/practice takeaway on research peptides for clinicians?
**Answer:** Research peptides remain a gray zone (“clear as mud”). Clinicians generally **cannot** stock/resell research peptides or tell patients to buy them; patients may independently purchase for research with vendor indemnification. Prefer counsel (e.g., Jeff Cohen) and compounding/legal channels where available.
