## Segment 1 — Opening & TB-006 Alzheimer's Infusion (00:00–13:18)

**Speakers:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 3), panel

### Key Points
- Nario reporting from A4M / West Palm Beach conference pipeline
- **TB-006** (company: **True Binding**): infused antibody targeting **galectin-3** — framed as the “glue” holding tau tangles; infusion aims to restore neuronal connectivity
- Clinicians already using in IRB/study context; many started with family members (parent as patient #1)

- Homeopathic Alzheimer’s protocols exist but are harder to match these video-level results

### Clinical Relevance
- Emerging anti-galectin-3 antibody pathway for dementia; access via IRB enrollment, not FDA approval yet
- Cost and genetic eligibility counseling essential before referral

## Segment 3 — Foundations Before Peptides (18:19–23:09)

**Speakers:** Jay Campbell, Hunter

### ★ Core Thesis
> Peptides work best when foundations are optimized. With foundations → ~10/10 response; without → ~3–4/10. Peptides never replace hormones or minerals.

### Six Foundational Agents (“God Stack” framing)
1. **Testosterone** (men and women) — peptides underperform if T is absent/low
2. **Human growth hormone** — anti-aging / thymus support at therapeutic doses
3. **Metformin** — studied longevity agent; daily protocol staple for presenters
4. **Desiccated thyroid** — HRT slightly suppresses thyroid; optimize thyroid so peptides work
5. **Magnesium + electrolytes**
6. **Taurine**

### Clinical Pearl
- Metabolic peptides raise metabolism → mineral depletion → tachycardia, insomnia, “staring at ceiling”
- Many “peptide side effects” are dehydration/electrolyte failure, not the peptide itself

### Agents & Pearls

| Agent | Role | Dosing / notes (as presented) |
|---|---|---|
| **AOD-9604** | GH fragment; looks good on paper | Experientially weak as monotherapy; oral/effervescent + mito stack more interesting |

| **SLU-PP-332 (“Sloop”)** | Oral ERR agonist / mito mimetic | **100–200 µg start → 400–600 µg/day**; 8–12 wk on / 4–8 off; pill form; endurance + RMR without HR spike for most |
| **5-Amino-1MQ** | NNMT inhibitor → ↑ NAD | Better in obese/unfit; Jay: strong first cycle then tachyphylaxis; 150 mg AM historically |
| **MOTS-c** | Mitochondrial peptide | **1–2 mg/day**; best when mito-impaired / prediabetic; **stable ~30 days post-reconstitution** (debunks “use immediately” rumor) |
| **Retatrutide** | GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon **tri-agonist** | Not FDA-approved yet (Lilly; projected ~2026); **microdose**; mild appetite suppressant vs tirzepatide/semaglutide; major biomarker + addiction/compulsion signal claimed |

### Stack Philosophy
- Hit **multiple pathways** (GH, mito, NAD, insulin) at **lower doses for longer** (12–24 weeks) instead of escalating one GLP weekly
- Avoid defaulting to **semaglutide/Ozempic** when stronger multi-agonists exist
- Syringe compatibility: fat-loss injectables mostly **separate**; orals (SLU, 5-Amino) not mixed

### Retatrutide Clinical Framing
- Improves lipids (HDL, ApoA/B particle profile), hsCRP, homocysteine (experiential + research-use community)
- Start low/slow; women often lower; example: **0.15 mg** twice weekly in supervised adolescent athlete case
- May pair microdose appetite agent (tirzepatide or **cagrilintide**) if RMR↑ outpaces satiety

## Segment 11 — Q&A: Dosing, Mold, Neuropathy, Regulation, LDN (1:53:15–2:41:17)

**Speakers:** Jay Campbell, Hunter, Melvin Nario, panel

### Clinical Answers
- **Retatrutide microdose:** ~**0.15–0.5 mg** every other day / split weekly (not 2–3 mg once weekly)
- Split dosing flattens peak/trough → fewer GLP side effects vs weekly megabolus
- **SS-31:** possible adjunct in PKD / mycotoxin mito injury; primary mold immune stack = TA1 + Thymalin + LL-37 + KPV
- **Oral gut:** KPV > BPC alone for gut; **both + Metformin** preferred
- **Neuropathy stack:** ARA-290 ± BPC/TB-500 ± Cartalax ± microdose GLP-1 (treat insulin resistance upstream)
- **MCAS / SIBO:** Bio Gut Pro kitchen-sink oral promoted
- **Cerebrolysin sourcing:** FDA letters; ampules via **pctzone.ru** (Indian warehouse) among few remaining sources
- **Regulatory:** attorney Jeff Cohen — “clear as mud”; clinicians must not stock/resell research peptides or direct-order for patients; patients may self-order research with indemnification; Lilly aggressive on tirzepatide / retatrutide
- **LDN receptor reset:** 0.5–1.5 mg may restore peptide sensitivity; Hunter **3 mo on / 3 mo off**; also androgen-receptor sensitivity + gut/immune longevity
- **Long-term GLP microdose:** after months, taper to **¼–½ dose 1–2×/month** to quiet food noise
- **Carb phobia + GLP = problem:** need clean carbs/fiber (quinoa, yams, rice, oats); high-fat + GLP doubles gastric emptying delay → gallbladder/bowel issues
- **Tirzepatide / cagrilintide microdose window:** same **0.15–0.5 mg** EOD or 2×/week
- Semaglutide framed as outdated appetite-only agent vs metabolic multi-agonists
- Future: microneedle peptide patch (~7 mm now; need 10–12 mm for larger peptides)
