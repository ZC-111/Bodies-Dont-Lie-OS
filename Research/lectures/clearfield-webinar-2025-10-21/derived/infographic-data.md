## 🚨 Infographic 8: "Who Should Consider EGX Support?"

**Title:** Broader Than “Heart Patients Only”

1. Prior CABG, stents, or known plaque
2. Family history of early cardiovascular disease
3. Hypertension / insulin resistance / neuropathy
4. High inflammatory burden or poor lifestyle (incl. regular alcohol)
5. Esthetic/PRF patients with poor microvascular scores (GlycoCheck)
6. Frequent IV therapy patients (repeated endothelial trauma cited)

**Footer:** Support can create early wins that finally unlock lifestyle change — still not a free pass for junk habits.
