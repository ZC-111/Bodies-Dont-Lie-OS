## Section D — Clinical Integration (2 questions)

### Q19. A 58-year-old with prior stenting, controlled lipids on an integrative program, but unchanged carotid plaque on CIMT asks what to add. Outline Crowley’s approach from this webinar.
**Answer (key elements):**
1. Add studied **rhamnan sulfate / ArterialCell** at **2 BID** × 3–6 months → then **1 BID**
2. Check/optimize **nitric oxide** (saliva stick; AMS or studied NO booster)
3. Continue lifestyle: glucose, BP, inflammation, movement; consider plasmalogens, D+K2, omegas
4. Reassess plaque with **CIMT** (~6 months) and/or **CCTA/AI cardiac CT** rather than relying only on early CAC change
5. Expect months for elasticity/plaque signals; counsel on cost and lifelong maintenance until imaging clears risk

### Q20. A patient drinks daily, has family history of early MI, refuses lifestyle change, and asks whether ArterialCell is “worth it.” How does Crowley frame this clinically?
**Answer:**
1. EGX is damaged by alcohol even at “social” doses — candidate for support despite imperfect lifestyle
2. Supplement is not a substitute for lifestyle, but early symptomatic/functional benefit can motivate later behavior change (Crowley: many stop drinking once they feel the difference)
3. Still address roots: inflammation, microbiome/food sensitivities, NO status, imaging risk
4. Screen for seaweed allergy / GI intolerance; discuss cost (~$110–125/month with NO in his practice banding)
5. Pair with counseling — Speaker 1’s point: repair microvascular “roots” before stacking endless other supplements
