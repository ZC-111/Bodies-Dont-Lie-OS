## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Glycocalyx damage precedes endothelial damage.** Treat the EGX as the first casualty in the vascular cascade — not an afterthought.

2. **Rhamnan sulfate from *Monostroma nitidum*** is Crowley’s go-to glycocalyx regenerator (clinically since 2021; Houston introduction). Microfluidic data: restore EGX up to **~103%** after glycemic insult.

3. **Stubborn carotid plaque can still move** when RS is added to an already-optimized program — 6/6 CIMT cases improved; mean burden ↓ **5.55 mm** at 6 months.

4. **Dose ladder for significant CVD:** ArterialCell **2 BID × 3–6 months → 1 BID** maintenance; expect ~**3 months** before clear elasticity/plaque signals (Houston: elasticity ↑ **~89%**).

5. **Always pair with nitric oxide assessment** (salivary dipstick). Crowley prefers cost-effective **AMS** NO (arugula/K-nitrate) over pricier Vascanox unless you want Houston’s published combo protocols.

6. **Hypertensives on meds + EGX/NO stack:** average **~11 mmHg diastolic** drop cited — useful adjunct narrative, not a reason to abandon antihypertensives blindly.

7. **Neuropathy signal:** T2DM peripheral neuropathy pilot improved conduction + symptoms with RS as the *only* added change; largest gains in **month 1**.

8. **Natural chelator tip (Nario):** MCP 5 g TID, chlorella 3–10 g, ALA 300–600 mg, GSH 500–1000 mg, NAC 600–1200 mg — gentler than unsupervised EDTA/DMPS/DMSA mineral stripping.

9. **Labs that fit the story:** CIMT for structure; Lp-PLA2 + MPO for rupture biology; ADMA/SDMA + saliva NO for the NO axis; gut-barrier panels when inflammation is the driver.

10. **Lifestyle still wins — EGX support can unlock it.** Alcohol damages the glycocalyx; early “felt better” on RS often precedes willingness to stop drinking or train. Repair microvascular roots before stacking endless supplements or expecting PRF/esthetics to shine.
