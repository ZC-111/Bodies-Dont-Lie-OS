## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: IGF-1 LR3 (11:35–21:46)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 4)

### ★ Core Thesis
> IGF-1 LR3 is a long-acting IGF-1 analog with strong preclinical anabolic signals — but **human RCT evidence is thin**, cancer/hypoglycemia risk is real, and it is **not FDA-approved** for anti-aging. Prefer secretagogues over direct IGF/GH substitution when possible.

### Molecular Design (as taught)
| Feature | Native IGF-1 | IGF-1 LR3 |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Native sequence | +13 N-terminal AA; Glu→Arg at position 3 (“LR3”) |
| IGFBP binding | Bound / buffered | Largely escapes IGFBPs (“stealth”) |
| Half-life | ~10 minutes | ~20–30 hours |
| Receptor potency | Baseline | ~3× harder at IGF-1R |

### Preclinical Signal
- Thomas et al. 1994 (tumor-bearing rats): ↑ muscle protein turnover **and** tumor growth
- Sheep / neonatal rat models: selective organ growth (heart, kidney, intestine); myoblast proliferation > native IGF-1
- Yoshida & de la Fontaine 2020 review: blocks atrophy pathways; drives skeletal muscle hypertrophy

### Human Evidence (borrowed from native IGF-1)
- Short-term elderly trials: ↑ whole-body and muscle protein synthesis (2014-era reviews cited)
- Thompson 1998: GH + IGF-1 in obese postmenopausal women → better fat loss while preserving lean mass; small mood signal
- **Punchline:** 1 year IGF-1 in postmenopausal women failed to meaningfully increase muscle mass
- 2026-era reviews still call for proper LR3 human trials — **no large RCTs** for anti-aging or sarcopenia

### Safety Reality Check
- Large epidemiology (~400,000): higher endogenous IGF-1 linked to ↑ prostate, breast, colorectal, thyroid cancer risk
- **Hypoglycemia** = most common short-term adverse effect
- Not FDA-approved for adult anti-aging; approved IGF-1 use framed around pediatric GH deficiency contexts
- Clinic pearl: bodybuilders / biohackers often unaware of cancer + glucose monitoring needs

### Clinical Protocol Framing
- Careful patient selection; baseline glucose; IGF-1 + cancer screening; cycling; rock-solid informed consent
- Prefer **GH secretagogues** (CJC / ghrelin-pathway agents) over direct GH or IGF substitution (Nario clinical opinion)
- Circulating IGF-1 may **not** track LR3 tissue delivery well — still monitor to avoid overstimulation; IGFBP-3 or 24-hour urine GH framed as more informative in some contexts
- **Do not let GH “flat-line” to zero** in aging patients — intervene early (sleep, nutrition, stress, peptides) before crash

### Side Panel
- Tano: arginine-based commercial GH-support stacks sometimes move clinical markers
- Peptide educator (Georgia): epigenetic SNPs help prioritize pathways / avoid clogging; hesitate if high tumor burden; protect HPA axis
- Clearfield: CJCs often fail to move the needle in his long experience
