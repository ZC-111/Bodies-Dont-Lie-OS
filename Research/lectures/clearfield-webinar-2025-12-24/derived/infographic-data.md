## 🧪 Infographic 1: "Detox vs Biotransformation"

**Title:** Say What You Mean Clinically

| Term | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| **"Detox" (medical literature)** | Coming off addictive substances |
| **"Detox" (patients)** | "Clean me out" / feel better / lose weight |
| **Biotransformation** | Convert fat-soluble toxins → water-soluble → excrete |

**Footer:** Use patient language, teach precise physiology.

## 🛣️ Infographic 2: "The 3-Phase Clearance Highway"

**Title:** When Phase 2 Is the Bottleneck

```
Phase 1 (CYP450)
  Lipid toxin → reactive intermediate
        │
        ▼
Phase 2 (conjugation)
  Methylation · Glutathione · Acetylation …
  Water-soluble package
        │
        ▼
Phase 3 (GI / microbiome)
  Clear or recirculate healthier form
```

**Callout:** Backup at Phase 2 = **6 lanes → 2 lanes** → recirculation + nutrient drain.

**Support cues:** B vitamins / crucifers (P1) · GSH/NAC/Ca-D-glucarate (P2) · probiotics + fix dysbiosis (P3)

## 🧲 Infographic 5: "MCP — Start Low, Watch Excretion"

**Title:** Modified Citrus Pectin as a Binder Tool

```
Pediatric study (As / Pb / Cd):
  ~15 g/day × 6 days (day 6 → 20 g)
        │
        ├── Cadmium urinary excretion ↑ ~150%
        └── Lead urinary excretion ↑ >500% by day 6
```

**Practice tips:**
1. Empiric binder: metals, plastics, PFAS
2. May improve galectin-3 / cystatin C on longevity panels
3. **Titrate slowly** — jumping to 15 g → extreme bloating

## 🏠 Infographic 6: "Mold Rule #1"

**Title:** Spores ≠ Mycotoxins

```
MOLD SPORES          MYCOTOXINS
Allergic reaction    Immune + hormone disruption
Skin/sinus allergy   IL-1β · MMP-9 · TGF-β signals
```

**#1 Intervention:** Remediate the building **or get the patient out**. Binders/antifungals are secondary to leaving the source.

**Case proof:** 27yo — crawl-space rental mold → moved → MSQ 89→20 with phased gut/thyroid/MCP plan
