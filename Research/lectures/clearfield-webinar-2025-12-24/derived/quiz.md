## Section A — Creatine Tip of the Week (5 questions)

### Q1. Which creatine form does Nario recommend as first-line based on clinical outcomes evidence?
- A) Creatine pyruvate
- B) **Creatine monohydrate** ✓
- C) Creatine ethyl ester
- D) Creatine HCl

> *Pyruvate is more soluble and may raise plasma levels, but lacks proven superior tissue uptake vs monohydrate.*

### Q2. What daily monohydrate dose range is typically cited for muscle saturation vs brain/cognition goals?
**Answer:** Muscle ~**3–5 g/day**; brain/neuroprotection studies often use **10–20 g/day** (neurons: slower transport, higher stress demand).

### Q3. True or False: Creatine causes kidney damage in healthy adults.
**Answer:** **False.** It raises **serum creatinine** via creatine→creatinine conversion, which can mimic renal injury. Avoid/monitor in advanced CKD, AKI, or with nephrotoxins.

### Q4. What lab does Hartman recommend ordering in creatine users to assess kidney function without creatinine artifact?
- A) BUN alone
- B) eGFR from creatinine only
- C) **Cystatin C** ✓
- D) Spot urine protein

> *~$40 at LabCorp; may prevent unnecessary nephrology referral.*

### Q5. Approximate creatine content by weight: monohydrate vs pyruvate?
**Answer:** Monohydrate ~**88%** creatine; pyruvate ~**60%** creatine / **40%** pyruvate.

## Section D — Q&A Clinical Applications (4 questions)

### Q17. Preferred routes for EDTA when aiming to lower metals/uranium per Nario/Hurst discussion?
**Answer:** **IV superior**; rectal used; **oral EDTA absorption questionable** — do not rely on oral marketing claims. DMSA may be IV/rectal/oral.

### Q18. Why is ApoE4 alone insufficient to predict Alzheimer’s in Hurst’s framing?
- A) ApoE4 is protective
- B) **It needs epigenetic/environmental co-factors (e.g., TOMM40, APOC1 “switch”) to express risk** ✓
- C) ApoE testing is always wrong
- D) Only homozygotes matter and nothing else does

### Q19. Glutathione maintenance vs treatment dosing ranges Hurst described?
**Answer:** Maintenance ~**100–200 mg/day** (especially with glutathione SNPs); treatment/detox pushes often **500–1500+ mg** — counsel patients for feeling worse during “cleanup.” Monitor RBC glutathione.

### Q20. When boosting T3 for migraines/brain fog in a high reverse-T3 patient, what must be supported concurrently?
**Answer:** **Adrenal / systemic fight-or-flight support** — otherwise energy rebounds briefly then reverse T3 / “parking brake” returns and patient declares therapy failed. Also consider DIO2 / CNS T3 availability and trial glutathione.
