# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-12-24

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Biotransformation, not trendy “detox.”** Teach Phase 1 → 2 → 3 clearance; a Phase 2 bottleneck recirculates reactive intermediates and burns nutrients.

2. **Never aggressive-detox the chemically sensitive frail patient first.** Rebuild mitochondrial/nutrient capacity; otherwise they “react to everything” and abandon care.

3. **Creatine monohydrate remains first-line.** Pyruvate is soluble marketing without superior outcome data. Muscle 3–5 g/day; brain goals often need 10–20 g/day.

4. **Creatine raises creatinine, not (usually) kidney injury.** Order **cystatin C** on users before scaring them into dialysis workups.

5. **Mycotoxins ≠ mold allergy.** Spores allergenic; mycotoxins immunogenic/hormonal. **#1 Rx = leave or remediate the environment.**

6. **MCP is a practical binder** (metals, plastics, PFAS) with pediatric excretion data (Pb ↑ >500% by day 6 at study doses) — **titrate slowly** to avoid bloating.

7. **Reverse T3 is the parking brake.** Case rT3 23 — support adrenals/limbic system when starting T4/T3 or the brake returns on day 3.

8. **ApoE4 is not destiny.** Needs epigenetic co-factors (e.g., TOMM40, APOC1). Use genomics as a motivational map (~40% of Hurst’s patients; cost-limited).

9. **Glutathione dosing is intentional:** maintenance 100–200 mg/day; treatment 500–1500+ mg with Herx counseling. Prefer GSH when conversion SNPs block NAC. Monitor RBC GSH.

10. **Weight loss mobilizes adipose toxin stores.** With GLP-1 / fasting, prep Phase 1–3 pathways *before* expecting patients to feel well during fat loss.
