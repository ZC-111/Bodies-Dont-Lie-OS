# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-11-27

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **40% of dementia is preventable** — and changes start ~30 years before diagnosis. Don’t wait for age 65 to act.

2. **Amyloid plaques are symptoms, not the root cause.** Target vascular health, inflammation, oxidative stress, mitochondria, toxins/infection, insulin, and hormones upstream.

3. **Women’s ~2× dementia risk is biologic**, not longevity: pregnancy brain changes, lifetime estrogen exposure, menopausal fuel-shift/white-matter catabolism (worse in APOE4), hot-flash microintensities, and volume loss.

4. **Measure it, mend it, maximize it.** Cheap repeatable metrics (Trail Making, HOMA, homocysteine, vitamin D, NO, contrast sensitivity, P300) drive adherence better than advice alone.

5. **Exercise is the closest “magic pill.”** ≥4,000 steps for gray matter; complex movement + true HIIT for BDNF; strength/grip for aging resilience; sauna as heat adjunct.

6. **Cognitive reserve ≠ crossword puzzles.** Social engagement and complex novel challenges beat domain-locked games; meditation/plants restore mood more than cognition scores.

7. **Homocysteine is a modifiable neurotoxin** — run it tighter than lab “normal” (e.g., ~≤5 at 40, ~≤6 at 50) and fix methylation/GSH pathways.

8. **HRT is brain-relevant** — midlife use linked to lower dementia risk; prefer peri-menopausal start for bone; past the 10-year window use low/slow individualized protocols (including older adults and intravaginal therapy), not automatic refusal.

9. **Sudden psychiatric change = think PANDAS/PANS.** Overnight OCD/psychosis/insomnia after infection needs antibody panels and anti-infective trials, not only psych meds.

10. **War on sugar > war on fat for brain insulin resistance.** Mediterranean/Paleo cleanup often beats unsustainable keto in stressed menopausal women; omega-3s ~2→3g (up to ~5g if tolerated).

## Debate Summary: Dogma vs. Individualized Brain Care

| Dimension | Old Dogma | Smurz / Panel Framing |
|---|---|---|
| **Alzheimer cause** | Amyloid primary | Upstream metabolic/inflammatory/toxic drivers |
| **Depression model** | Serotonin deficit | 2022 review: not empirically substantiated |
| **Sleep purpose** | Must be glymphatic detox | 2024 clearance data challenges dogma; still prioritize sleep |
| **HRT after 10y** | Never start | Case-by-case low/slow + monitoring |
| **Keto in menopause** | Ideal for all | Often too stressful; Med/Paleo first |
| **Omega-3 testing** | Always index first | Often skip test; just supplement |
| **PANDAS** | “Not real” / anxiety only | Abrupt onset + infection workup essential |

**Consensus:** Prevention is multi-modal; meet the patient (stress load, age, receptors, infections) rather than single-protocol absolutism.
