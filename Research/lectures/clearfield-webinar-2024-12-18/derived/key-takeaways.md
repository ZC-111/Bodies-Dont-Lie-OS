# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-12-18

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Urinary metabolite tracking is pattern medicine**, not a single-number serum replacement — use it for coordination, response to therapy, and life-stage transitions.

2. **Bleed ≠ ovulation.** Confirm with E3G rise → LH (± FSH) surge → PDG rise; withdrawal/breakthrough bleeds mislead.

3. **LH-only OPKs are incomplete.** Track estrogen, LH, progesterone, and FSH when the clinical question is hormone health, not just a yes/no surge.

4. **Ideal LH surge lasts 1–3 days.** Chronically high baseline LH desensitizes the ovary; multi-day marathon surges are suboptimal even if PDG eventually rises.

5. **Watch for the reverse estrogen pattern** (weak follicular E, high luteal E) — PMS/heavy menses; continuous data beats one timed draw or mid-luteal DUTCH.

6. **Luteal phase <11 days** raises early miscarriage / implantation-failure risk; normal luteal length ~11–16 days.

7. **PCOS has multiple urinary phenotypes** (high E, suppressed E, reverse E, failed then successful LH surges). Don't time luteal labs off a non-ovulatory surge.

8. **Cooperative HRT:** if she still surges, dose progesterone **after LH** (~3 days post-surge) rather than blind day 15–28 — until coordination is lost.

9. **Route matters for monitoring:** oral/vaginal/injectable P often ceilings urinary PDG; transdermals barely move urine — underlying ovarian pattern remains readable.

10. **~3 months of near-daily Mira ≈ one cycle map in cost**, with real-time, flexible sampling; still add T, cortisol, or full metabolite panels when those questions matter.

## Comparison: Continuous Urinary Monitor vs Mail-In Cycle Map

| Dimension | Mira-style home monitor | DUTCH / mail-in cycle map |
|---|---|---|
| **Technology** | Immunofluorescence (Mira) | Dried urine metabolite panel |
| **Timing** | Real-time; patient/provider choose cadence | Predetermined sample days |
| **Ovulation indexing** | Chart centered on observed LH | May not re-index if early ovulation |
| **Data density** | Daily or qOD across months | ~11 samples / one cycle typical |
| **Cost framing** | ~$330 ≈ 3 months near-daily | One cycle ≈ same dollar ballpark |
| **Androgens / adrenals** | ❌ Not included | Partial (depends on panel) |
| **Best for** | Irregular cycles, TTC timing, peri P timing | Pathway/metabolite deep dive |
| **HRT on chart** | Transdermal = slight change; oral P = ceiling | Can also saturate / flatten patterns |

**Consensus from panel:** Strongest fit for irregular cycles, fertility timing, and LH-guided progesterone — cheaper first pass than repeated cycle maps; interpretation still needs a clinician.
