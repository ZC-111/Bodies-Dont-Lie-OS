# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-09-23

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Brain optimization = four pillars** — lifestyle, nutrient repletion, pain resolution, hormone optimization. SSRIs are last-line, not first-line.

2. **Cold plunge at 35–36°F for 2–4 minutes** often yields near-instant pain relief and large mood jumps via “earned” dopamine (and large NE/DA spikes lasting hours).

3. **Finnish sauna** (e.g., 175°F × 10 + 10) supports detox, GH, and dementia-risk literature — but **pre-load salt/water** to blunt RAAS from relative hypovolemia.

4. **Sulodexide** targets endothelial glycocalyx repair (EU drug; US personal-import discussion). Bridge with **rhamnan sulfate / Arterosil HP** + NO support (~10–11 mmHg diastolic signal).

5. **Iron deficiency is a mental-health emergency** in young women — neuropsychiatric symptoms can occur **without anemia**; offer office IV iron or aggressive oral strategies (EOD + vit C; ferric citrate).

6. **Leaky gut defeats oral vitamins** — cellular micronutrient failure + zonulin/gliadin/LPS → use sublingual/IM/IV and repair permeability.

7. **Lithium orotate 5–20 mg/day** can mood-stabilize within 1–2 weeks; do not confuse with lithium carbonate (toxicity/ECG baggage).

8. **Progesterone first for insomnia** (allopregnanolone pathway). Avoid chronic Benadryl — anticholinergic hit to acetylcholine/memory.

9. **LDN 1–5 mg** (TLR4 / microglial calm) and **methylene blue** are practical immunomodulatory brain tools; watch SSRI interactions with methylene blue; compounding quality can make or break response.

10. **Nandrolone microdosing** (~4 mg/wk women; ≤20 mg/wk men) can improve pain, muscle, and mood without bodybuilder-dose side effects.
