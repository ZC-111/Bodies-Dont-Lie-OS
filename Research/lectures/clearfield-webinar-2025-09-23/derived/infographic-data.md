## 🩸 Infographic 4: "Iron Deficiency = Mental Health Crisis"

**Title:** Ferritin 4 Can Feel Like “Going Crazy”

| Clue | Action |
|---|---|
| Anxiety, palpitations, weird thoughts | Check **Hb + ferritin** even if “not anemic-looking” |
| Neuropsychiatric sx **without anemia** | Still replete (Amerling) |
| Oral fails / severe depletion | Office **IV iron** (crash cart ready) |
| Oral strategy | **Every other day** + **vitamin C**; consider **ferric citrate** |
| Root drivers | Leaky gut, malabsorption, menses, autoimmunity, vegan gaps |
