## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Sulodexide & Endothelial Glycocalyx (11:11–17:41)

**Speaker:** Dr. Nario (Speaker 3)

### ★ Core Concept
> Damaged **endothelial glycocalyx** = sticky, cranky vessels → stiff arteries, ↑ BP, leaky brain vessels, brain fog, kidney/eye damage, inflammaging.

### Sulodexide
- Pharmaceutical-grade GAG blend: **~80% heparin-like fraction** + **~20% dermatan sulfate**
- Mild anticoagulant / vessel-repairing; better tolerated than heparin/warfarin; GI side effects most common (nausea, pain, diarrhea)
- Evidence signals cited: diabetic nephropathy (proteinuria ↓), vessel function (2018), post-COVID/sepsis vessel stabilization (2021)
- **Not FDA-approved in US**; Europe available; US patients may import **90-day personal supply** with Rx under FDA personal-use policy

### Natural Glycocalyx Support
- **Rhamnan sulfate** (from green algae *Monostroma nitidum*) — closest US-available repletion; Mark Houston / Mario Institute data on carotid calcium reversal (Doppler; changes as early as ~12 weeks; multi-year reverse calcium claims)
- Omega-3s, vitamin C/E, polyphenols (green tea, cacao, resveratrol), NO boosters (L-arginine)

### Clinical Pearl — Arterosil HP
- Speaker 4 (cardiovascular practice): **Arterosil HP** (rhamnan sulfate product) + nitric oxide stack → average **~10–11 mmHg diastolic BP drop**
- Ken Crowley scheduled **Oct 21** for hypertension talk

## Segment 3 — Stefan Hartman: Brain Optimization — Bologna Preview (19:11–1:12:50)

**Speaker:** Stefan Hartman, PA — Iron Direct Primary Care (anti-aging / functional medicine)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Mental health and brain optimization rest on **four pillars**: (1) lifestyle, (2) nutrient deficiencies, (3) pain (physical + emotional), (4) hormone optimization. SSRIs are last-line after the pillars.

**Context:** Preview of bilingual English/Italian lecture for Bologna brain-optimization conference (Nov 7–8); organizer Dr. Paolo Conforti visited clinic.

### 3A. Pillar 1 — Lifestyle: Cold Plunge

**Protocol / observations (clinic case series):**
- Plunge temp **~35–36°F**; duration typically **2–4 minutes**
- Near-instantaneous pain relief (acute or chronic); mood often jumps dramatically (e.g., 0→10, 4→9, pain 9→1)
- “Healthy dopamine” — earned, not cheap stimulant dopamine
- Converts **white adipose → brown adipose**; immunomodulatory / cytokine-lowering in arthritis
- Wet cold plunge reported as harder than walk-in cryotherapy (−240°F nitrogen chambers)
- Membership model (~$100/mo 24/7 access) vs cryo sessions (~$50 each); Cadillac plunge unit ~$30K
- Gender: Hartman sees pain and mood benefit in women as well; mother does **3 min @ 36°F** with euphoria
- Duration of benefit: person-dependent — daily for severe pain vs 3×/week for maintenance
- Nario add-on: dopamine spike ~**200–250%**, norepinephrine ~**500%+** for hours → antidepressant / focus effect

### 3B. Pillar 1 — Sauna, Red Light, Strength, Diet

**Finnish sauna (preferred over infrared based on dementia literature):**
- Detox via sweat; every patient toxic panel abnormal in his practice
- **175°F × 10 min → break → 10 min** → growth hormone boost
- Endocrine: GH, noradrenaline, ADH, beta-endorphins
- Chronic fatigue support via detox + heat stress
- Do **not** reflexively suppress fever with Tylenol Q4H — fever is adaptive (treat misery/sleep disruption, not the number)

**Red light / infrared:**
- Case: trigeminal neuralgia (post-herpetic) — near **99% pain resolution**; avoided Mayo rhizotomy; patient near suicide
- Mechanisms cited: nerve regeneration, ↑ cellular energy, ↓ inflammation, ↑ blood flow, neuromodulation
- Depth: ~**688 nm** reaches fascia/muscle/ligament

**Strength training:**
- Spine/pain patients almost never sarcopenia-free athletes — frailty collapses facet joints → arthritis + inflammatory cytokines
- Muscle mass improves inflammatory signaling and insulin resistance (ubiquitous in psych population)

**Diet / omega-3 quality:**
- House-call kitchens: seed oils, HFCS, near-zero real protein
- Prefer **wild-caught** fish; farmed salmon often fed rapeseed → omega-6 meat profile (Quinn: fish get EPA/DHA from algae, not pellets)
- Don’t overcook fish (oxidizes delicate PUFAs)
- Grass-fed vs grain-finished meat profile; grain finish → omega-6 skew; liver abscesses in feedlot cattle
- “Mediterranean diet” in US often fails because Italian sourcing (local, glyphosate/bromide bans, al dente pasta, slower meals) isn’t replicated

| Agent | Key use / notes |

### 3D. Pillar 2 — Leaky Gut, Micronutrients & Iron

**18yo F case:** anxiety + brain fog despite oral multivitamin; cellular micronutrient panel low B1/B3/C; food allergies (peanut/soy/wheat); + anti-gliadin, anti-zonulin, anti-actin, LPS; estrogen dominance (low P day 21, high E1/E2)
- Fix: sublingual (or IV/IM) vitamins + gut repair — oral capsules not absorbing

**Iron deficiency (young women epidemic):**
- Neuropsychiatric symptoms can occur **without anemia** (Amerling)
- Cases: ferritin **4**, Hb **9.7** → “insane asylum” thoughts; vegan Hb ~3 history; gluten avoidance → fastest marathon, dysphagia resolved
- Offer **IV iron in office** (safer access than hematology infusion chairs); prepare for rare anaphylaxis
- Oral pearls: **ferric citrate** can replenish stores in weeks–month (Amerling); **every-other-day** dosing ↓ hepcidin blockade (Nario); add **vitamin C**

### 3E. Pillar 4 — Hormones for Brain & Pain

- **DHEA + E2 + T vaginal** — 60yo horse rider incontinence → mental health embarrassment resolved with tissue regeneration
- **Topical P + E2** — 45yo perimenopause: night sweats, insomnia, low salivary T/E2/P → “best in a year,” sleeping 7–8h
- **Topical estradiol on hands** — idiopathic sclerodactyly-like fibrosis reversed; ended social isolation
- **Progesterone first-line for insomnia** (→ allopregnanolone); preferred over alcohol, cannabis, or Benadryl (anticholinergic → memory hit — Speaker 9)
- **Nandrolone (low dose):** ♀ ~**4 mg/week** (0.02 mL / ~2 units once weekly); ♂ ~**≤20 mg/week**; lasts ~14 days, trough ~day 5–6
  - Cases: tennis knee pain + mood; drywall Dupuytren-like fibrosis; steroid osteoporosis/asthma; psoriatic arthritis (couldn’t afford DMARD); mother’s back pain / confidence / hang-time

### 3F. Clinical Sequence (Avoid Overwhelm)
1. Master lifestyle basics (train, diet, sun, sauna, cold)
2. Replete common deficiencies (Mg, lithium orotate, B vitamins — absorbable forms)
3. Optimize hormones
4. Advanced: LDN, methylene blue, ketamine, peptides (dihexa, cerebrolysin — mostly old Soviet data)

**Creatine note (Quinn):** emerging cognition/mood data at ~**20 g/day**; Hartman notes neonatal ICU safety precedent.

## Segment 4 — Extended Q&A Clinical Pearls (1:12:58–1:32:20)

**Speakers:** Hartman, Amerling, Clearfield, Nario, Arnot, Hall, Quinn

### Sauna & Volume (Amerling)
- Sauna ↑ aldosterone via vasodilation → relative hypovolemia → RAAS activation
- **Pre-load salt/water** before sauna; Roman baths = early “dialysis” only if electrolytes replaced

### Cold Plunge vs Cryo Chamber
- Prefer plunge for full experience; use walk-in cryo if mobility limits, open wounds, or cost/access issues

### Emerging Trends
- Psychedelic therapy expansion; peptide revival; Bredesen-adjacent protocols; galectin-3 infusions for Alzheimer’s risk (Nario)

### Lithium Orotate Titration
- Load for quick patient-perceptible effect → lower maintenance (analogy to iodine saturation)
- Bundle branch block concerns → lithium **carbonate**, not orotate
