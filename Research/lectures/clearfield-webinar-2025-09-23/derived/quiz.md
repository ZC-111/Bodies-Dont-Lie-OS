## Section A — Framework & Vascular Tip (5 questions)

### Q1. According to Hartman, what are the four pillars of mental health / brain optimization?
**Answer:** (1) Lifestyle (diet, training, sunlight, cold, sauna, light), (2) nutrient deficiencies, (3) pain (physical and emotional), (4) hormone optimization.

### Q2. Sulodexide is best described as:
- A) A PCSK9 inhibitor
- B) **A GAG blend (~80% heparin-like + ~20% dermatan sulfate) that supports endothelial glycocalyx repair** ✓
- C) An SSRI augmentation agent
- D) A form of intravenous iron

### Q3. True or False: Sulodexide is FDA-approved and widely stocked in US pharmacies.
**Answer:** **False.** It is available in Europe; US clinicians discussed legal **90-day personal-use import** with a prescription under FDA personal-use policy. Natural bridge: rhamnan sulfate / Arterosil HP.

### Q4. What average blood-pressure change did Speaker 4 report with Arterosil HP + nitric oxide support?
- A) 2–3 mmHg systolic only
- B) **~10–11 mmHg diastolic** ✓
- C) No change in BP; only carotid IMT
- D) Orthostatic hypotension requiring discontinuation

### Q5. Why does Hartman argue “Mediterranean diet” recommendations often fail US patients?
**Answer:** Mediterranean study contexts include local sourcing, glyphosate/bromide bans, al dente pasta (lower GI), and meal culture — variables not replicated by copying a US “Mediterranean” plate from supermarket ingredients.

### Q14. An 18-year-old with brain fog is on oral multivitamins but has low cellular B vitamins and positive zonulin/gliadin/LPS markers. What is the teaching point?

### Q15. True or False (Amerling): Neuropsychiatric symptoms of iron deficiency only appear after frank anemia.

## Section D — Hormones, LDN, Ketamine & Sequencing (4 questions)

### Q17. Hartman’s approximate nandrolone microdose for women vs men:
**Answer:** Women ~**4 mg once weekly** (~0.02 mL / 2 units); men ~**≤20 mg weekly**. Injectable; effect lasts ~14 days with trough around day 5–6.

### Q18. Why is progesterone preferred over Benadryl for insomnia in this discussion?
**Answer:** Progesterone → allopregnanolone → natural sedation/sleep. Benadryl is anticholinergic; acetylcholine is needed for memory — linked to cognitive harm (Speaker 9), and patients often self-medicate with alcohol/cannabis instead of proper hormone care.

### Q19. Low-dose naltrexone’s CNS anti-inflammatory mechanism highlighted by Hartman:
- A) Direct dopamine agonism
- B) **TLR4 antagonism → ↓ microglial TNF-α / IFN-β signaling** ✓
- C) NMDA blockade only
- D) Acetylcholinesterase inhibition

### Q20. Correct order of Hartman’s “don’t overwhelm the patient” sequence:
- A) Ketamine → hormones → lifestyle
- B) **Lifestyle basics → nutrient repletion → hormones → advanced (LDN, methylene blue, ketamine)** ✓
- C) SSRIs first, then cold plunge if needed
- D) Peptides before diet or training

> *SSRIs remain available but are not first-line in this framework.*
