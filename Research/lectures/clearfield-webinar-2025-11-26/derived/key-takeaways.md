# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-11-26

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Vitamin D induces CYP450 (esp. 3A4 / 2C9 / 2B6).** Optimizing levels can lower serum concentrations of many drugs — re-titrate narrow-TI meds when 25-OH-D rises past ~40–50 ng/mL.

2. **Fresh Spanish mackerel outperformed pills, drinks, canned fish, and previously frozen options** for subjective brain/omega-3 effect in the featured case — processing and freezing destroy fragile PUFAs.

3. **Farmed fish often lack meaningful omega-3** when fed rapeseed/canola instead of algae; sushi is legally frozen and similarly compromised.

4. **~15 fish-oil capsules may be needed for ~2 g EPA+DHA** — food-first counseling is more practical than pill math alone.

5. **Visceral fat is an independent disease risk** often omitted from “normal” abdominal imaging reports — learn to see it and teach patients the hard-belly clue.

6. **Diet precedes exercise for brain optimization.** Carb-driven late-day rumination and mood crashes block training adherence; fix food first.

7. **Ice plunge 35–38°F × ~3 minutes** was ranked the single highest-yield evening modality (dopamine, norepinephrine, adaptive cortisol, vagal training); toe covers improve compliance.

8. **Low-dose nandrolone** was used primarily for bone density, with secondary gains in joint/back comfort and libido/confidence — tiny doses only (~1 unit weekly in this case).

9. **HRT failure is usually dose, time, adherence — or inflammatory lifestyle sabotage**, not “hormones don’t work.”

10. **DHEA may show negative feedback once female testosterone is adequate**; Clearfield often stops DHEA around one year at goal free-T / androgen index.

## Comparison Snapshot: Pills vs Food Omega-3

| Dimension | Fish-oil pills / Rx concentrates | Fresh high–omega-3 fish |
|---|---|---|
| **Processing** | Heat, bleach, blanch common | Minimal if lightly cooked |
| **Practical 2 g dose** | Many capsules | Food portions (e.g., mackerel meals) |
| **Brain “feel” (anecdote)** | Flat / modest | Stronger (Spanish mackerel) |
| **When Rx still useful** | HyperTG, CVD indication, fish refusal (Vascepa etc.) | Default teaching target |
| **Hidden pitfalls** | Oxidized oil | Freezing, farm feed, TSP on cheap canned fish |

**Consensus from session:** Prefer real fish; use concentrates selectively.
