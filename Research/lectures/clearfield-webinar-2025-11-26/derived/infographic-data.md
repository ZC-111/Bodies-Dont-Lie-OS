## 💊 Infographic 1: "Vitamin D Is a Medication Mixer"

**Title:** Sunshine Vitamin = CYP450 Inducer

```
↑ Vitamin D (optimize / high-dose)
        │
        ▼
   VDR activation
        │
        ▼
 CYP3A4 · CYP2C9 · CYP2B6 ↑
        │
        ▼
 Faster clearance of >60% of Rx drugs
        │
        ▼
 Serum drug levels may DROP
```

**Watch list:** Statins (atorva/simva) · Amlodipine · Benzos · Prednisone · Phenytoin/carbamazepine · Warfarin (INR) · Ethinyl estradiol OCPs · Some HIV PIs

**Footer:** Re-check narrow-TI drugs when 25-OH-D crosses ~40–50 ng/mL — don’t blame “aging.”

## 🐟 Infographic 2: "Omega-3 Hierarchy (Lived Experience)"

**Title:** Fresh Fish Beats the Pill Bottle

| Source | Relative brain/omega “oomph” (anecdotal) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh Spanish mackerel** | ★★★★★ | Target food; ~300 g/day split |
| Wild salmon (fresh) | ★★★★ | Still strong if truly fresh |
| King mackerel (often frozen) | ★★★ (~70%) | Availability bias |
| Previously frozen salmon | ★★ (~40%) | Freezing damages fragile omega-3 |
| Fish-oil pills / cod drinks | ★–★★ | Heat/bleach/blanch processing |
| Canned sardines/herring | ★ / variable | Heat + possible TSP |
| Farmed fish (rapeseed-fed) | near ✖ | Little algae → little EPA/DHA |

**Callout:** ~15 fish-oil pills ≈ 2 g EPA+DHA (Swiss lecture cited) — food is the practical path.
