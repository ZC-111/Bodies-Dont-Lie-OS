## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Vitamin D as a CYP450 Inducer (12:13–15:50)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 3)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Vitamin D is not only bones/immunity/mood — it acts as a **CYP450 enzyme inducer**. Optimizing levels can quietly lower serum levels of many prescription drugs, especially in polypharmacy patients.

### Mechanism
- Vitamin D → vitamin D receptor (VDR) activation → upregulates CYP enzymes, especially **CYP3A4, CYP2C9, CYP2B6**
- These enzymes metabolize **>60% of prescription medications**
- When 25-OH-D rises from deficient → optimized (~80–90 ng/mL integrative targets), hepatic clearance of some drugs accelerates → **serum drug levels can drop**

### Drugs Commonly Affected
| Class | Examples | Clinical risk when CYP induced |
|---|---|---|
| Statins | Atorvastatin, simvastatin | Faster clearance → reduced lipid-lowering effect |
| Calcium channel blockers | Amlodipine | Reduced BP control |
| Benzodiazepines | Midazolam, triazolam | “Meds don’t work anymore” |
| Steroids | Prednisone | Reduced anti-inflammatory effect |
| Antiepileptics | Phenytoin, carbamazepine | Lower drug levels; these drugs also deplete vitamin D (bidirectional) |
| HIV protease inhibitors | — | May reduce therapeutic levels |
| Warfarin | — | CYP2C9 induction → INR instability |
| Oral contraceptives | Ethinyl estradiol–based | Possible reduced efficacy |

### Clinical Pearl
> Re-evaluate dosing for **narrow-therapeutic-index** drugs when vitamin D is corrected or high-dose supplementation starts (typically 5,000–10,000 IU/day). CYP induction effects often appear once levels rise above ~**40–50 ng/mL**.

**Cited:** Braselowski et al., *Osteoporosis* 2012 — functional CYP3A4 upregulation as vitamin D normalizes above ~40 ng/mL.

### Action Checklist When Optimizing Vitamin D
1. Screen for CYP3A4 / CYP2C9 substrates
2. Reassess symptoms / drug efficacy after dose increases
3. Document BP, sedation, INR, and lipid response where relevant

## Segment 4 — Stefan Hartman + Mom: Longevity Protocol (20:37–1:08:00)

**Speakers:** Stefan Hartman, DO (Speaker 6); Mom (Speaker 5)  
**Title framing (Clearfield):** HRT, ice plunges, omega-3s, and dead hangs

### ★ Core Thesis
> Brain optimization and longevity come from **diet first**, then cold/heat/light/load — not from reaching first for ketamine, CBD, or LDN. Patients (and doctors) often don’t know what “feeling good” feels like because baseline lifestyle is so inflammatory.

### 4A. Diet Journey — Trial & Error
- Raised on Chinese medicine / Tai Chi / food-as-cause-of-illness model; Stefan: no antibiotics, delayed vaccines until university
- Diets tried: **macrobiotic** (extreme; high iodine via sea vegetables; period synced to full moon / less pain — anecdote) → **Gerson** (juicing, high potassium; excess fruit → mood crashes) → lower-carb / high–omega-3 fish pattern
- Mood best when carbs **<100 g/day** (even better ~50 g for Mom); best of all on **high–omega-3 fish + vegetables**
- Food-as-medicine framing: childhood illness traced to what was eaten, not separate from diet

### 4B. Omega-3 Strategy — Fresh Spanish Mackerel > Pills
- Processed fish-oil pills: fish often un-iced → heated → bleached/blanched; omega-3 fragile (Paolo Conforti teaching referenced)
- **Spanish mackerel** (fresh, husband-caught): strongest subjective brain “euphoria” response; ~**300 g/day** split meals for protein + omega-3 goals
- King mackerel often previously frozen → ~70% of Spanish mackerel effect
- Previously frozen salmon → roughly ~40% omega-3 value vs fresh
- Canned herring/sardines: FDA allows **trisodium phosphate (TSP)** on fish (often cheaper fish); Mom reports strong adverse reaction; briny taste = TSP clue; omega-3 also damaged by canning heat
- Farmed fish: often fed **rapeseed/canola oil**, not algae → **little/no omega-3**; sushi legally frozen → omega-3 loss
- Clinic pearl (Stefan): everyone tested is high omega-6 / low omega-3, especially mental-health patients; Italian functional medicine conference emphasized omega-3:6 ratio for cytokines, mood, hormones
- Swiss lecturer note: ~**15 fish-oil pills** to reach ~**2 g** EPA+DHA — impractical vs food

### 4C. Visceral Fat & Body Composition
- Mom: zero visceral fat on DEXA via HRT + high–omega-3 fish/veg pattern; flexibility improved vs stiff 50s
- Stefan critique: radiologists often call abdominal MRI “normal” while ignoring visceral fat — independent risk for cancer, CVD, kidney disease, diabetes, viral hyper-inflammation
- Hard, expanded belly = visceral fat compressing organs/diaphragm

### 4D. Raw Dairy, Pasteurized Dairy & Carbs–Mood Link
- Childhood: pasteurized dairy → nasal congestion / mucus; warm salt bag TCM earache remedy (no antibiotics)
- Adult Mom: **raw dairy** with coffee OK if milk not heated **>108°F** (espresso cooled first)
- Intrusive ruminative thoughts (“balled-up brain”) attributed to carb load / inflammation — often late-day after carbs; amygdala↑ / prefrontal↓ framing (Quinn)
- Seed oils / restaurant carbs pull mood down hours later → hard to connect to food
- Protein target discussed: ~1 g protein per ideal lb; Mom aims ~300 g mackerel/day scaled on kitchen scale before “earning” carbs

### 4E. Human Performance Arena (HPA) Protocol
Evening unwind replacing alcohol/restaurant culture:

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Red light | ~8 min each side; hyaluronic acid + C + A + collagen (prefers fish-skin collagen over beef powder / scale collagen) before |
| Massage chair | Added later; valued recovery tool |
| **Ice plunge** | **35–38°F**, **~3 minutes**, submerged to neck/arms in; **#1 if only one modality**; toe covers help adherence |
| Resistance / hangs | Dead hangs (~6 min/day valued), rings, squats, bands, vibration plate |
| Sauna | After cold; GH↑, vasopressin/ADH effects; sunlight exposure when possible |
| HBOT | Cracked heels heal after ~day 3 of sessions (anecdote); cognition / inflamed brain support; thyroid balance also discussed for heel callus (Warren Levin teaching) |

**Cold physiology (Stefan):** dopamine, norepinephrine, cortisol (framed as adaptive), vagal training via carotid-level submersion / breath control.

**Priority order (Mom + Stefan):** **Diet > exercise**. You can’t out-exercise a bad diet; depressed patients often can’t start exercise until diet unballs the brain.

### 4F. Boot-Camp Vision for “Sick Doctors / Patients”
- Morning: coffee → high–omega-3 breakfast (Spanish mackerel, squash, dandelion, miso soup pictured)

## Segment 5 — HRT Deep-Dive: Nandrolone, Testosterone, Progesterone, Estradiol, DHEA (1:08:30–1:36:00)

**Speakers:** Mom (Speaker 5), Stefan Hartman, Bill Clearfield; Q&A from Quinn and chat

### 5A. Mom’s Hormone Stack (Lived Protocol)
- Started hormone-skeptical (no vaccine since 1967 narrative; natural births) → now multi-hormone under Stefan’s guidance
- **Progesterone (oral)** first for sleep (while still drinking whiskey — alcohol was waking her at 2am); dose titration critical — too much → arm numbness from immobility; too little → poor sleep; keeps multiple bottle strengths
- **Testosterone:** very diluted (~20 mg/mL SIP); ~4 units every third day; excess aromatizes → spotting / uterine symptoms → cut back
- **Nandrolone (“Narlo” / compounding pharmacies):** **1 unit once weekly**; day-after libido/confidence effect; Stefan’s primary indication = **bone density**; also reduced inflammatory back/joint pain; ~10× more anabolic than testosterone; can convert to DHT — keep doses tiny; half-life similar to testosterone cypionate (~7–14 days); effect wears ~day 6
- **Estradiol:** topical on face (and lately other sites); excess → uterine “funny feeling”
- Also: NP Thyroid, pregnenolone, tiny melatonin at night; considering pausing DHEA
- Small HGH dose post-workout → next-day muscle recovery without lingering soreness
- Vascepa / Rx omega-3: Stefan uses for patients who won’t eat fish or have hypertriglyceridemia/CVD indication; otherwise prefer food fish or liquid fish oil

### 5B. Clearfield Clinical Pearls (Panel)
- Testosterone “didn’t work” usually = underdosed, insufficient time/delivery, or non-adherence — plus lifestyle inflammation (binge carbs) can sabotage HRT
- **DHEA:** useful as cortisol counterbalance (cortisol:DHEA ~5–6:1); FDA history for depression; OTC quality varies
- Observation: with adequate female testosterone for 6–12 months, adding DHEA may show **negative feedback** (levels drop despite supplementation) → Clearfield often **stops DHEA ~1 year** once free T / androgen index at goal
- Female free testosterone target discussed ~**2–4%**; free androgen index formulas shared (male optimal ~50–110; female normal ~0.8–6, ideal toward mid-range)
- Dr. Gersh view referenced: estradiol is central; she speaks at upcoming conference

### 5C. Immune Aging Narrative (Mom)
- Rejects “immune system inevitably dies with age” teaching; credits raw dairy switch, alcohol cessation, low inflammation, omega-3 diet
- Anecdote: methylation/immune testing previously scored ~40-year-old immune age
