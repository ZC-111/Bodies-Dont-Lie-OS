- A) 20 ng/mL

- C) **40–50 ng/mL** ✓

### Q3. Which statin pair did Nario specifically flag for faster clearance / reduced effect when vitamin D induces CYP enzymes?
**Answer:** Atorvastatin and simvastatin.

### Q4. True or False: Antiepileptics like phenytoin and carbamazepine have a bidirectional problem with vitamin D.
**Answer:** **True.** Vitamin D induction can lower antiepileptic levels, and these drugs also lower vitamin D — “perfect storm.”

### Q5. What should clinicians do when correcting vitamin D in a patient on a narrow-therapeutic-index CYP3A4/2C9 drug?
**Answer:** Re-evaluate dosing; reassess symptoms/efficacy; document BP, sedation, INR, and lipid response as relevant. Do not assume “aging” when a previously effective drug suddenly fails.
