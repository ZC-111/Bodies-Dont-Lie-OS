## 🧪 Infographic 2: "Breath Tests — Know the Limits"

**Title:** Sensitivity Isn’t High Enough to Overrule the Patient

| Method | Sensitivity (cited) | Blind Spot |
|---|---|---|
| Glucose breath | **~54%** | Misses distal SI (glucose absorbed proximal 3–6 ft) |
| Lactulose breath | **~42%** | Interpretation noise; still imperfect |
| DNA CFU correlation (2018) | **None significant** | Breath ≠ cell-count truth |
| Duodenal aspirate | “Gold standard” | Samples ~1–2 of ~19 feet |

**Thresholds:** CH₄ > **10 ppm** · H₂ > **20 ppm**  
**Hill bias:** Prioritize **methane** clinically; hydrogen has physiologic roles (~70% of microbiota produce H₂).

**Callout:** Empiric antimicrobial trial + clinical picture often beats a false-negative breath test.

## 🧱 Infographic 3: "Three-Phase SIBO Model"

**Title:** Treat Once — Don’t Whack-a-Mole Every Quarter

```
PHASE 1 — ANTIMICROBIALS (~30 days)
  Broad botanical (or Rx) combinations
  Address H. pylori + SIBO together when both present

PHASE 2 — SET THE STAGE RIGHT (~up to 9 months)
  HCl / enzymes  ·  bile  ·  motility (MMC)
  Stop all-day grazing; support vagal / serotonin pathways

PHASE 3 — COLLATERAL DAMAGE (≥ 6 months)
  Rebuild micronutrients (A, D, E, K, B12, Fe, Zn, Se, Ca…)
  Nutrients refill lymphocyte stores → fewer bounce-backs
```

**Footer:** Antimicrobial-only care explains the revolving door.

## 🛡️ Infographic 4: "The Small Bowel Safety Net"

**Title:** What Normally Keeps the SI Inhospitable to Overgrowth

```
1. Migrating motor complex (Phase III sweep)
2. Hydrochloric acid
3. Pancreatic enzymes (also antimicrobial)
4. Bile acids
5. Competent ileocecal valve
6. Mucosal immunity (sIgA; vitamin A–dependent)
7. Healthy commensals
```

**Breakers:** PPIs · opioids · aging/atrophic gastritis · gastric surgery · chronic stress/sympathetic tone · H. pylori

## 💊 Infographic 6: "Targeted Probiotics — Not Megadoses"

**Title:** Acute Tools, Not Lifelong Prevention

| Agent | Role in Lecture |
|---|---|

## 🥗 Infographic 7: "Diet Myths — FODMAP vs Reality"

**Title:** SIBO Is Not an Excuse for Permanent Restriction Theater

| Approach | Hill Framing |
|---|---|
| **FODMAP** | Strongly **against** for SIBO — strips oligosaccharides, polyphenol foods, lactose/raw dairy supports; IBS diet; bail by **6 weeks** if no help |
| **Fermented foods** | Often pause during active Rx (uncontrolled variables) |
| **Carnivore** | Optional short tool; prolonged zero-carb may ↑ sympathetic drive → counterproductive |
| **Meal spacing** | Leave ~1–2h between feedings to protect MMC |

**Callout:** Raw dairy (when appropriate) framed as microbial + immune supportive — opposite of classic FODMAP logic.
