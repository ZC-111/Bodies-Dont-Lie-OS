# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-03-11

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **~Half of SIBO patients relapse by 9 months** (~44%); many relapse by 3–6 months. Plan for root causes, not endless antibiotic rounds.

2. **SIBO ≠ “too many bugs only.”** Expect colonic-type / gram-negative shift in the small bowel plus the classic symptom cluster — and ~75% IBS overlap without identity.

3. **Breath tests are weak decision tools** (glucose ~54% sensitivity; lactulose ~42%). Don’t let a negative test veto a classic clinical picture — empiric therapy is often more rational.

4. **Anatomy explains false negatives:** ~19 ft of small bowel; glucose samples the proximal few feet; aspirates sample ~1–2 ft.

5. **Three-phase care is mandatory:** (1) antimicrobials, (2) motility + digestive secretions, (3) nutrient collateral damage for ≥6 months.

6. **H. pylori and SIBO often travel together** — treat simultaneously with a ~30-day multi-botanical protocol rather than sequencing forever.

7. **Targeted probiotics beat megadoses:** *L. plantarum* and *S. boulardii*; useful acutely, not proven as lifelong prevention. Start after the antimicrobial month for many patients.

8. **Reject FODMAP-as-SIBO-diet.** It strips oligosaccharides, polyphenols, and lactose/raw-dairy supports; stop by ~6 weeks if no benefit — it’s an IBS tool.

9. **PPIs, opioids, aging hypochlorhydria, gastric surgery, and chronic sympathetic tone** set the stage; replace HCl/enzymes/bile and protect the MMC (stop grazing).

10. **Compliance kits matter:** Biotics SBO kit (Phase 1) + upcoming Hydrozyme / Beta Plus / Motilityzyme Phase-2 stack (~1 each per meal, framed up to ~9 months).

## Comparison: Kill-Only vs Three-Phase SIBO Care

| Dimension | Kill-only / antibiotic cycles | Hill three-phase model |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary goal** | Symptom clearance | Eradicate + maintain remission |
| **Diagnostics** | Breath-test gatekeeping | Clinical + labs + selective testing |
| **H. pylori** | Often separate courses | Same botanical sweep |
| **Diet** | Long FODMAP common | Avoid SIBO-as-FODMAP; meal spacing |
| **Probiotics** | Random high-CFU blends | Strain-specific, often post-antimicrobial |
| **Secretions / motility** | Afterthought | Core Phase 2 |
| **Nutrients** | Spotty | ≥6 months rebuild |
| **Relapse expectation** | High / revolving door | Designed around the 9-month window |
| **Best for** | Acute flare triage | Failed prior antimicrobial-only care |
