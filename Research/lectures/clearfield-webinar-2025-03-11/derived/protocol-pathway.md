## 1. Indication & Scope

**For:** Patients with SIBO / IMO-pattern GI symptoms (bloating, gas, distension, pain, diarrhea ± malnutrition), including relapse after prior antimicrobials; concurrent suspected *H. pylori* + SIBO.

**Not for / refer out:** Acute abdomen, GI bleed, severe malnutrition requiring hospitalization, unexplained weight loss without workup, suspected obstruction, or cases needing Rx antibiotics the speaker does not personally use (e.g., rifaximin) — coordinate with a prescribing clinician.

**Lecture framing:** ~20% population burden (clinical estimate); ~half relapse by 9 months if drivers (secretions, motility, nutrients) are ignored.

## 2. Entry Criteria

### History / clinical picture
- Classic SIBO constellation: bloating, gas, distension, abdominal pain, diarrhea ± weight loss / malnutrition
- Risk: female sex, aging hypochlorhydria, PPIs, opioids, hypothyroidism, Parkinson’s, diabetes, IBD, celiac, hepatic dysfunction, recurrent iron deficiency, etc.
- ~75% IBS overlap — related but not identical

### Labs / imaging (as stated)
- Prefer **clinical diagnosis ± empiric antimicrobial trial** over breath-test gatekeeping
- Breath tests (cited): glucose ~54% sensitivity; lactulose ~42%; anatomy (~19 ft SI) explains false negatives
- Supportive chemistries: B12 / Fe deficiency, fat-soluble vitamin deficits, hypoalbuminemia, ↑ CRP/AST
- Stool: not diagnostic for SIBO; may hint hypochlorhydria / digestive dynamics
- Pearl: B12 deficiency → classic SIBO; symptomatic **without** B12 deficit → consider **IMO**

### Red flags → escalate / do not start pathway
- Alarm features (bleed, obstruction, severe dehydration, cancer concern)
- Prep-related harm if forcing low-yield breath testing with long washouts in frail patients

## 3. Pathway Overview

```text
[Clinical entry ± selective labs]
        ↓
 Phase 1 Antimicrobial (~30 days; SIBO ± H. pylori together)
        ↓
 Phase 2 Motility + secretions (ideally Day 1; often staggered; up to ~9 mo)
        ↓
 Phase 3 Nutrient rebuild (≥6 months)
        ↓
 [Maintain / reassess relapse window]
        ↓ fail after good compliance → change antimicrobial stack
        ↓ fail after poor compliance → redo + double down on Phase 2
```

| Phase | Goal | Typical duration (as stated) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Suppress / eradicate overgrowth (± *H. pylori*) | ~30 days |
| 2 | Restore HCl / enzymes / bile + MMC / motility | Up to ~9 months (relapse window) |
| 3 | Rebuild micronutrient (± protein) collateral damage | ≥6 months |
| Maintain | Avoid chronic FODMAP / endless kill cycles | Ongoing counseling |

## 7. Cautions, Interactions & Controversies

- Breath testing: low sensitivity; prep burden may harm compliance (`cited_study` / `speaker_claim`)
- Hill does **not** use rifaximin personally (scope/cost); may combine botanicals with Rx abx if another clinician prescribes
- Strong **anti-FODMAP-as-SIBO** stance — contrasts with common IBS pathways
- Product pathway centered on Biotics kit — treat as `product_pitch` until independently verified
- Oral *H. pylori* niche: dental referral if GI relapse persists

## 8. Adjuncts Mentioned (non-core)

- Nario tip: nutrient synergies (iron+C; B6+Mg; E+A; iron+A testosterone pearl) — not part of SIBO core pathway
- Postbiotic: butyrate most SIBO-relevant among options discussed
- High sIgA → find trigger (dysbiosis/diet/environment), not suppress blindly
