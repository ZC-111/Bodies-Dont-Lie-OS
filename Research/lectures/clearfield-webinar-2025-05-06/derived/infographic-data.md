## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Treat the Man, Not the Cutoff"

**Title:** Testosterone Deficiency Is Clinical

| Signal | Conventional reflex | Sammons framing |
|---|---|---|
| Total T “normal” at 500 | Reassure / dismiss | Still low if no drive, fatigue, no gains |
| Total T 2000 | “Optimized athlete” | Super-physiologic → aromatize, mood, gut |
| Prevalence quotes | 2.1% of US men | Practice reality closer to **30–40%** |
| Target window | Minimal sufficiency | Physiologic **~700–1100** |

**Footer:** Labs confirm. Symptoms decide. More is not always better.

## 🧪 Infographic 2: "Labs Before You Dose"

**Title:** Baseline Checklist for Male HRT

```
AXIS          FSH · LH · Prolactin · Total/Free T
UPSTREAM      DHEA-S · (Pregnenolone) · Progesterone
ESTROGEN      Estradiol · consider estrone / total E
THYROID       TSH · Free T4 · Free T3 (± antibodies)
METABOLIC     A1c · fasting insulin · glucose
CLEARANCE     AST/ALT · Homocysteine (<10) · MTHFR
FOUNDATION    Vitamin D · CBC · Lipids · SHBG · (± IGF-1, iodine)
```

**Callout:** Homocysteine 12–26 = clear hormones poorly → fix methylation first.

## ⚖️ Infographic 3: "The Aromatization Decision Tree"

**Title:** Who Actually Needs an AI?

```
Start TRT → optimize liver, sleep, body comp
        │
        ▼
Recheck ~3 months (clinical + labs)
        │
        ├── E2 ~30–45, feels good → NO AI
        ├── E2 >65 OR >42 + symptoms → consider AI
        └── Gynecomastia / tearfulness / central fat gain
                    → lower T dose ± AI (don't wait for man boobs)
```

**Study punchline:** ~1,700 men → only **~2.5%** needed AI; average start **~1 year** in.

## 💊 Infographic 4: "Anastrozole Dosing Map"

**Title:** Not Breast-Cancer Dosing by Default

| Use case | Starting pattern discussed |
|---|---|
| With TRT | **0.5 mg 3×/week** |
| Monotherapy to raise endogenous T | Up to **1 mg daily** (24h physiology) |
| Moderately low T (~450–500), chubby/low drive | Trial 0.5 mg 3×/week → titrate |
| Letrozole | Similar 3rd-gen AI class |
| Tamoxifen | SERM — more male side effects; not interchangeable |

**Result in small treated cohort:** 88% to physiologic E2; none crashed sub-physiologic.

## 🔄 Infographic 5: "T:E2 Ratio — The Missing Metric"

**Title:** Absolute Levels + Ratio

```
Tool: Omni Calculator (T ÷ E2)

Literature band often cited:  ~10:1 to 13:1
Halasa preference voiced:     closer to ~30:1 for men

Low ratio  → too much estradiol relative to T
High ratio → may need E2 support (bone, libido, CV)

Combined T+E2 signals: libido · cognition · muscle · fat loss · CV markers
```

**Footer:** Crushing E2 to zero was the gym-rat era mistake.

## 🌿 Infographic 7: "Clear Estrogen Without Always Using AI"

**Title:** Detox & Metabolite Tools

1. **DIM** — cruciferous pathway push toward clearer metabolites (food dose impractical)
2. **Calcium D-glucarate** — less estrogen reuptake / phase-2 support
3. **Liver stack** — milk thistle, dandelion, liver–gallbladder blends
4. **Methylated Bs** — support methylation exit pathways
5. **Lifestyle** — glass storage, less plastic/receipts/alcohol/UPFs
6. **Cyclodextrin** (Halasa) — microplastics / xenoestrogen scavenger adjunct

**Rule:** Limit inbound estrogenizers while upgrading outbound clearance.
