# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-05-06

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Treat clinical hypogonadism, not a single lab cutoff.** Symptomatic men with “normal” totals still deserve a full workup; aim for physiologic T (~700–1100), not gym-rat super-physiologic levels.

2. **Only ~2.5% of TRT patients needed an aromatase inhibitor** in the cited ~1,700-man cohort — and average AI start was nearly **a year** in. Stop reflex anastrozole for every new start.

3. **Men need estradiol.** Bone, tissue repair, libido sweet spot, and CV protection suffer when E2 is crushed to zero. Intervene for high E2 + symptoms (often >65, or >42 with symptoms), not folklore.

4. **Track the testosterone:estradiol ratio** (Omni Calculator) alongside absolutes — literature often ~10–13:1; some faculty prefer higher (~30:1).

5. **Fix clearance and comorbidities** (liver enzymes, homocysteine <10, OSA, diabetes, disruptors) or TRT becomes aromatized sludge after a short honeymoon.

6. **Troches are Sammons’ preferred delivery** (~$55–75/mo compounded; often with DHEA) — easier physiology than injectables/pellets; pellets rank last (can’t remove).

7. **Anastrozole with TRT: think 0.5 mg 3×/week**, not automatic 1 mg daily breast-cancer dosing. As monotherapy it may raise endogenous T via LH/FSH for moderately low men.

8. **DIM + calcium D-glucarate** support estrogen metabolism/clearance when you want natural adjuncts; tamoxifen is a SERM with more male side-effect baggage.

9. **Optimized TRT ≠ cardiac poison** when balanced — FDA removed the CV black-box (Feb 2025); risk rises when dumping T into obese, unmonitored, aromatizing physiology.

10. **OS01 AMPK activator** (100 mg; 6 weeks on / 4 off) is the tip-of-week metabolic tool — but don’t leave AMPK stuck on; pair with mTOR/repair cycles.

## Comparison: Reflex AI vs Selective AI

| Dimension | Reflex AI (urban legend) | Selective AI (this webinar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who gets it** | Nearly all men on TRT | ~2.5% with high E2 ± symptoms |
| **When started** | Weeks 4–8 by default | Often ~12 months if needed |
| **E2 goal** | Near zero / very low teens | Physiologic ~30–45; protect bone/CV/libido |
| **First move if “estrogenized”** | Add anastrozole | Lower overdose, fix liver/OSA/adiposity |
| **Natural tools** | Often ignored | DIM, Ca-D-glucarate, methylation, cyclodextrin |
| **Clearfield current practice** | Historical block >40 | Rarely block unless gyno/mood; individualize libido |
| **Best for** | Oversimplified protocols | Art + labs + ratios |

**Consensus:** AI is a tool, not a TRT co-pay. Match to labs, ratio, and the man in front of you.
