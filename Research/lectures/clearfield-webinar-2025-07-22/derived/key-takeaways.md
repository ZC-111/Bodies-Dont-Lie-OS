# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-07-22

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Wait 4–6 weeks after illness before trusting a lipid panel.** Acute infection drops LDL/HDL ~10–30% and raises TG ~10–50% via IL-6 / acute-phase physiology.

2. **Stubborn lipids can be a signal, not a statin failure.** Hunt chronic inflammation, hormones, occult infection, and steroid effects before escalating lipid drugs.

3. **Membrane lipid replacement (MLR) targets a root aging lesion:** free-radical damage to unsaturated phospholipid tails in mitochondrial membranes → falling ATP.

4. **Cited 8-week mito rebound:** ~70-year-olds’ mitochondrial energy production rose toward levels of healthy ~29-year-olds (study as presented by Pelton).

5. **MLR + CoQ10 roughly doubled CoQ10 plasma levels** in cited work — relevant because CoQ10 is both electron-transport cofactor and fat-soluble antioxidant.

6. **Metabolic markers in a 6-month study:** ~32% ↓ homocysteine and ~59% ↓ fasting insulin (as presented).

7. **Dose matters in toxin-burdened patients.** Gulf War protocol escalated from ~⅛ tsp to **~6 g/day** for 6 months with broad symptom recovery; expect notice in weeks, not days.

8. **Plan MLR as maintenance, not a 2-week cleanse.** Oxidative membrane damage is continuous; Pelton takes ~1 tsp powder daily (± wafers when traveling).

9. **Prefer crystal-free CoQ10** (Pharma Nord framing): ordinary CoQ10 crystallizes above body temp; split **100 mg BID with fat** for higher needs.

10. **Extreme serum iodine ≠ automatic diagnosis.** Repeat the lab, prefer urine iodine, consider contrast exposure, taper high-dose iodine carefully, and optimize vitamin D / sex hormones alongside thyroid Abs.
