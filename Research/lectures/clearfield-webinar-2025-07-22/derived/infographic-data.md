## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Don't Chase Sick Lipids"

**Title:** Timing Your Lipid Panel

| Situation | What lipids do | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Acute illness** (flu, COVID, infection) | LDL/HDL ↓ 10–30%; TG ↑ 10–50% | Do **not** titrate therapy on this draw |
| **0–4 weeks post-illness** | Still unstable | Wait |
| **4–6 weeks recovered** | ★ True baseline | Decision labs OK |
| **Stubborn high LDL/TG** | May signal inflammation | Hunt sinusitis, gums, NASH, IR, steroids |

**Footer:** IL-6 tells the liver to make CRP/fibrinogen — not your usual lipid package.

## 🩺 Infographic 7: "Iodine Panic Card"

**Title:** Serum Iodine 1245 — Now What?

```
Ref range ~40–92
Patient: 1245 on Tri-Iodine 12.5 mg × 1.5 yr
TSH low-normal / Free T3 mid; TPO ~100
```

**Checklist:**
1. Repeat level (error? recent CT contrast?)
2. Prefer **urine** iodine for monitoring
3. Taper iodine — don’t ignore extreme serum values
4. Optimize vitamin D (TPO inverse relationship; target ~80–100)
5. Check sex hormones / perimenopause at 42
