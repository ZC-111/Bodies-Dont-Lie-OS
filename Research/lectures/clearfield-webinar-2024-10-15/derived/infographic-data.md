## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Recording Consent Checklist"

**Title:** Before an Industry Guest Goes Live

```
□ Confirm speaker identity & title
□ Ask: Is recording allowed today?
□ If NO → document reason (e.g., marketing approval)
□ Offer recorded make-up Tuesday
□ Announce no-record policy to attendees
□ Disable host/cloud recording
□ Ask attendees to disable local recording
□ Verify no “Recording…” banner remains
```
