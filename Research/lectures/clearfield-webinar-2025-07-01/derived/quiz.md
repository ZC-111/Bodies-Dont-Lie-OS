## Section A — Tip of the Week: Garlic Chelation (3 questions)

### Q1. In the lead-chelation RCT Nario cited, garlic tablets were dosed at:
- A) 600 mg/day
- B) 1.2 g/day
- C) **3.6 g/day** ✓
- D) 10 raw cloves only (no tablets)

> *117 industrial workers; garlic matched D-penicillamine for blood-lead reduction with fewer adverse effects. (Basic & Clinical Pharmacology & Toxicology)*

### Q2. Why does Nario prefer standardized garlic tablets/extract over “just eat garlic” for chelation dosing?
**Answer:** Matching the study dose requires ~**6–10 raw cloves/day**, which is impractical and poorly tolerated; heat cooking also degrades allicin. Standardized tablets or aged garlic extract at **1.2–3.6 g/day** give reliable dosing.

### Q3. True or False: Aged garlic extract is the same product as black garlic.
**Answer:** **False.** Nario explicitly distinguished aged garlic from black garlic.

### Q8. Why does Shear compare “optimal physiological ascorbate” to optimized hormones?

### Q9. True or False: Shear teaches that vitamin C can be accumulated and stored for weeks like fat-soluble vitamins.
**Answer:** **False.** Uptake must be gradual and continuous; stores deplete rapidly under stress, infection, toxins, and inflammation.

### Q12. Before initiating high-dose IVC (especially >~25 g), which screen is non-negotiable?
- A) ApoB
- B) Homocysteine
- C) **G6PD** ✓
- D) ANA titer

> *G6PD deficiency impairs glutathione regeneration in RBCs → hemolysis risk under oxidative burst. High G6PD is not the problem — low is.*

### Q13. What is the SIP seven-to-seven maintenance starting dose Shear teaches?
**Answer:** About **1 teaspoon ≈ 4 grams** of pure sodium ascorbate daily, sipped every **20–30 minutes** from morning through evening (not used as the sole hydration bottle).

### Q14. After acute infection symptoms resolve on oral ascorbate, what is the critical compliance rule?
- A) Stop immediately to avoid kidney stones
- B) Switch to ascorbic acid for 24 hours
- C) **Continue a lower dose for at least 72 hours post-resolution to prevent rebound/relapse** ✓
- D) Double the dose for one more day then stop

### Q15. Why does Shear advise against calcium ascorbate for pharmacologic dosing?
**Answer:** Titration to high ascorbate doses would **overdose calcium** (or magnesium in similar salts) while still **underdosing ascorbate**. Use pure sodium ascorbate when pushing pharmacologic amounts.

### Q16. In integrative oncology IVC, why is co-administering glutathione during the cytotoxic window problematic?
**Answer:** Pharmacologic IVC relies on a **pro-oxidant / H₂O₂** selective-kill window (Fenton chemistry). Glutathione can blunt that oxidative burst and undermine cytotoxic intent — a common sequencing error Shear flags.

### Q18. How does Shear respond to the “>500 mg vitamin C is only 17% absorbed” claim?
