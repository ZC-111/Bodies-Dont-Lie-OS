# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-02-19

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Thymic involution is a clinical story, not trivia** — aging + chronic illness leave many patients unable to recognize/clear viruses, mold, and abnormal cells.

2. **TA1’s job is immune literacy** — accelerate dendritic-cell maturation and T/NK competence so the host does the clearing.

3. **Hartman default: 1.5 mg SQ every 3 days** — matches observed ~3-day wear-off in practice cases.

4. **Thymogen is the oral on-ramp** — less data than TA1, but real-world responders (URI/mold, energy, even thyroid-dose reduction in one case).

5. **Nario ladder:** thymogen (mild/oral) → thymulin (inflammation, **zinc-required**) → TA1 (viral/cancer “biggest gun”).

6. **Safety profile is the selling point** — studies show minimal toxicity; watch injection sites and possible transient ALT rise; defer peds/pregnancy/nursing advocacy.

7. **Use as adjunct in oncology narratives** — melanoma/NSCLC/HCC data cited are combination/support contexts, not monotherapy cures.

8. **Complex chronic = high yield** — long COVID, mold/mycotoxin, POTS-like illness, EBV, stealth infection, cancer-support patients often “perk up.”

9. **Labs that matter:** CBC screen → Cyrex Lymphocyte Map + Array 12 when patterns are opaque; TGF-β1 in biotoxin stories.

10. **Budget pearl:** Knox gelatin 10–15 g/day + vitamin C (± glucosamine) when patients can’t afford branded collagen peptides.

## Peptide Comparison: TA1 vs Thymogen vs Thymulin

| Dimension | Thymosin alpha-1 | Thymogen | Thymulin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Route** | Injectable (SQ) | Oral | Injectable / extract (practice-dependent) |
| **Evidence depth** | Strongest (incl. labeled-use framing) | Weaker (Russian bioregulator literature) | Moderate / clinical niche |
| **Best use (heuristic)** | Viral, cancer, complex stealth | General/respiratory immune support | Chronic inflammation / neuroinflammation |
| **Zinc dependency** | No special rule stated | No special rule stated | **Yes — check & replete zinc** |
| **Hartman dosing** | 1.5 mg q3d (higher in some trials) | 1–2× daily | Not Hartman’s focus |
| **Cost** | ~$170 / 10 mg vial | Similar / slightly less | Variable / access-limited |
| **Main barrier** | Cost + compounding/access history | Adherence + thinner data | Zinc management + availability |

**Consensus:** Prefer data-heavy TA1 for hard cases; use oral thymogen for ease; reserve thymulin when cytokine/neuroinflammation is the dominant story and zinc is addressed.
