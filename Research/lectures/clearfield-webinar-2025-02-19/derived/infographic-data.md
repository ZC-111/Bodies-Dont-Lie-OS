## ⚖️ Infographic 4: "Thymic Peptide Ladder"

**Title:** Nario’s Easy Clinical Heuristic

|---|---|---|---|
| **Thymogen** | Mild | Oral general / flu / URI support | Empty stomach; 1–2×/day |

| **TA1** | Heavy | Viral, cancer, complex stealth illness | 1.5 mg SQ q3d (Hartman default) |

**Footer:** Weakest → strongest is a practice mnemonic, not an RCT ranking.

## 📋 Infographic 5: "Hartman Default Protocol Card"

**Title:** Outpatient TA1 Starter Pattern

```
DOSE:     1.5 mg subcutaneous
INTERVAL: Every 3 days (benefit often wears ~day 3)
COURSE:   Multi-week / multi-round; disease-dependent
ORAL ALT: Thymogen 1–2× daily (titrate by feel)
COST:     ~$170 per 10 mg vial; oral ≈ similar
ACCESS:   Jillian (injectable Rx accounts) /
          Integrative Peptides (oral)
```

**Note:** Melanoma/sepsis literature may use higher mg doses; Seeds chat note used **mcg** scale — verify units.

## 🧪 Infographic 6: "What to Order Before/During"

```
1. CBC + differential

      Cyrex Lymphocyte Map

      TGF-β1 (biotoxin), thyroid dosing, metabolic markers
```

## 🛡️ Infographic 7: "Safety Gate"

**Title:** “Barrels Won’t Kill You” — Still Use Judgment

| Green flags (lecture) | Yellow / red flags |
|---|---|
| No clear organ toxicity in studies | Injection-site reaction possible |
| Used even in advanced liver/renal/PID contexts (cited) | Transient ALT bump (~2×) then fall |
| Low threshold in complex chronic adults | **Peds / pregnancy / nursing — don’t advocate yet** |
| Main drawback = cost/access | Not a substitute for root-cause workup |
