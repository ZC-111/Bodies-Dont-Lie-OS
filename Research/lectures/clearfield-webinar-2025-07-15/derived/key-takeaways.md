# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-07-15

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Invisible TBI looks “normal.”** Visual, memory, dizziness, cognitive fatigue, and balance complaints with a clean outward exam are still TBI until proven otherwise — not automatic primary psychiatry.

2. **Misdiagnosis → meds → spiral.** Routing undiagnosed TBI into psychotropics can be counterproductive and contribute to suicidal ideation; substance use often masks neurologic pain.

3. **Interdisciplinary > stovepipe.** NICoE/Avalon-style models (full-team “fishbowl,” daily clinician communication, 30-day intensive) keep service members in the fight and treat TBI as primary with MH comorbidities.

4. **Suicide rates in veterans with TBI rose dramatically** in the cited neurology data (<10 → >90 per 100,000 over ~15 years). Prevention that ignores brain injury is incomplete.

5. **Cu:Zn 0.7–1.0 on standard labs** is a cheap inflammatory/mineral window. Don’t megadose zinc without copper planning (metallothionein trap); separate doses.

6. **Pituitary/hypothalamic vascular anatomy explains hormone collapse** after blast — GH first (~20% acute → up to ~35–40% at 1 year if untreated).

7. **Prefer IGF-1 (~200–250) over finicky GH draws**; use rT3 as a ~$10 cortisol-stress screen; cash ~10-hormone panel ~$350.

8. **Testosterone thresholds matter for mood/suicide risk:** total T <300 risk, ~150 frank depression in men; prolactin >6 helps flag treatment-resistant anxiety/depression.

9. **Neurosteroids are therapeutics, not trivia:** progesterone/allopregnanolone/DHEA/pregnenolone protect myelin and calm GABA tone; early IV progesterone research cited for mortality reduction.

10. **Stack modalities thoughtfully:** hormones + HBOT/TMS/photobiomodulation/neural therapy/Synapsin/regenerative tools beat any single “miracle” — and VA Whole Health / disability advocacy are part of care navigation.

## Comparison Table: Symptom-Only Psychiatry vs Physiologic TBI Care

| Dimension | Symptom-only MH path | Physiologic TBI + interdisciplinary path |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary frame** | PTSD / depression / anxiety | Brain injury ± pituitary injury |
| **First tools** | Psychotropics, more meds for side effects | Hormone panel, inflammation, team modalities |
| **Risk** | Missed TBI; SI from meds/spirals | Requires time, access, often cash pay |
| **Model example** | Fragmented consults | NICoE / Avalon “fishbowl” |
| **Success signal** | Symptom checklists only | Function, retention, cognition, hormones normalize |
| **Policy lever** | MH suicide prevention spend | Chronic TBI designation, baseline cognition, Mission Act access |

**Consensus from the night:** Mental health comorbidities are real — but TBI is physiologic. Hormones and interdisciplinary care belong in the first conversation, not the last.
