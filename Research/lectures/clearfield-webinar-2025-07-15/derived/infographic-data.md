## 🎯 Infographic 1: "The Invisible Wound Trap"

**Title:** Why TBI Gets Missed — and Why That Matters

```
LOOKS FINE
  → walks / talks OK
  → no obvious physical injury
       ↓
SYMPTOMS
  → brain fog, dizziness, visual changes
  → memory, balance, cognitive fatigue
       ↓
MISROUTE
  → labeled “mental health / PTSD”
  → psychotropics that may worsen TBI
       ↓
SPIRAL
  → substance use as pain mask
  → suicidal ideation risk ↑
```

**Footer:** Treat the physiologic brain injury — don’t only medicate the behavior.

## ⚖️ Infographic 4: "Zinc : Copper — The 0.7–1.0 Window"

**Title:** A Free-ish Inflammatory Window on Standard Labs

| Cu:Zn Ratio | Meaning | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **0.7–1.0** | ★ Optimal balance | Maintain |
| **> 1.0** | High Cu / low Zn | Inflammation, psych, CVD risk signals |
| **< 0.7** | Copper deficiency | Anemia, immune, connective tissue, hormones |

**Supplement rule:** ~**8–15 mg Zn : 1 mg Cu** — separate by hours (metallothionein / competitive absorption).

## 🧬 Infographic 5: "Hormone Collapse After TBI"

**Title:** What Shakes Loose When You Shake the Pituitary

```
WITHIN HOURS–DAYS
  Cortisol ↓ >50%
  Free T3  ↓ >50%
  Testosterone — nearly all males change
  LH ↓ ~50%

GROWTH HORMONE
  Acute deficiency ~20%
  Untreated at 12 months → ~35–40%
```

**Anatomy callout:** Hypothalamus (thin innervation, poor blood supply) + pituitary (tiny arteries) — blast/IED force tears supply easily.

## 💊 Infographic 7: "Depression Toolkit After TBI"

**Title:** Hormones Before the Fourth Antidepressant

```
FIRST-LINE PHYSIOLOGY
  ├── Growth hormone (sermorelin / CJC-1295 / MK-677)
  ├── Thyroid (NP Thyroid preferred over Armour coating)
  ├── Testosterone (mood, cytokines, seizures, cognition)
  ├── DHEA 10–25 mg♀ / 25–100 mg♂ (cortisol temper)
  ├── Pregnenolone (memory; watch glucose)
  └── Estradiol as adjunct (brain flow, PTSD risk, HDL)

AVOID ASSUMPTION
  High-dose anxiolytics/antidepressants can suppress
  LH / FSH / GH further — stacking meds ≠ fixing root cause
```
