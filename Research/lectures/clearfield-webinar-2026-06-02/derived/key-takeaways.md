# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-06-02

## Top 10 Clinical / Practice Pearls

1. **Residual inflammatory risk matters after LDL is optimized.** Nario’s tip: low-dose colchicine hits NLRP inflammasome / IL-1β–IL-6 signaling — LoDoCo2 (~31% ↓ MACE) and COLCOT (~23% ↓ post-MI events).

2. **Brand 0.5 mg colchicine can be a cash-pay trap.** Prefer discussing **generic 0.6 mg daily** when insurance won’t cover branded LoDoCo dosing.

3. **High CAC is a practical colchicine cue** in this group’s framing — especially when the patient already has ASCVD overlap or gout/hyperuricemia.

4. **Yearly CRP/hsCRP is a cheap early-warning habit** in St. John’s prevention talk — not diagnostic, but a reason to dig when the number climbs.

5. **Thermography is pushed hard as earlier, noninvasive breast screening** vs mammogram (3–5 year claim) — know the advocacy position even if your practice standard differs.

6. **Kit design = compliance design.** Eight core agents beat a 100-step “beat cancer” lifestyle stack if the patient will actually finish the protocol.

7. **DMSO is the recurring force-multiplier** — carrier into tumor shell, topical pain mix (80/20 with water), temples for headache, stroke kit backbone; small human stroke series + large animal literature.

8. **Tippens ≠ clean fenbendazole monotherapy proof.** Long remission, but **Keytruda trial exposure** is a major confounder Nario correctly flagged.

9. **Gut peptide adjuncts for colitis:** BPC-157, **KPV**, larazotide — still treat CMV when CMV is the driver.

10. **Private health association ≠ licensed compounding.** Alt One / Body Health Care framing is legal/structural, not an FDA green light — understand the model before partnering or referring.
