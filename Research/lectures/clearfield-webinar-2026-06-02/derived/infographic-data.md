## Infographic 5: "DMSO — The Great Carrier"

**Title:** Why It Shows Up in Every Protocol

```
DMSO = small polar molecule
  │
  ├── Temporarily disrupts membrane lipids
  ├── Crosses skin in minutes
  ├── Carries dissolved agents into tissue / tumor shell
  └── Claimed extras: clot effects, stem-cell activation signals
```

**Practical tips from Q&A:**
- Oral: cold water ± pomegranate juice; often bedtime with CDS
- Topical pain mix: **80% DMSO / 20% water**
- Temples for headache; scalp anecdotes in post-stroke / cognitive protocols
- Don’t casually dump into hot CDS baths — burn/sensitivity risk
