**Answer:** It won’t diagnose the cause, but rising systemic inflammation is a check-engine light that may prompt workup earlier — illustrated by a friend with chronic headaches who was diagnosed with cancer only weeks before death.

### Q12. Standard curcumin dose in the typical Alt One kit vs super kit?
**Answer:** **2,000 mg/day** standard; **4,000 mg/day** in the super kit (often 4 capsules AM + 4 PM).

### Q13. Why is ivermectin in the super kit / optional add-on rather than every standard kit?
