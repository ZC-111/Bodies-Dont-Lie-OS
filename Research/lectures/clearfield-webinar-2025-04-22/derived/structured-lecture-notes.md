## Segment 4 — Meletis Core Lecture: Muscle, Myostatin & Fortetropin (16:32–1:21:49)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis, ND

### ★ Core Thesis
> Patients starting GLP-1s (or any sharp calorie cut / bariatric path) are often **overfed and undernourished**. Eating *less of the same poor diet* accelerates **muscle, myokine, metabolic, mental-health, morbidity, and mortality** risk. Preserve lean mass deliberately — or “skinny fat” replaces obesity.

### 4A. Why Muscle Mass Is Non-Negotiable
- Skeletal muscle ≈ **40%** of metabolically active body mass → loss sabotages weight-loss maintenance (“calories stick like Velcro”)
- Lowest-third strength after age 60 → **~50% higher all-cause mortality** vs upper third (literature cited from ~2008 onward; revalidated 2024)
- Muscle strength inversely associated with death risk even after controlling for cardiorespiratory fitness
- Sphinx riddle framing: crawl → walk → cane/scooter — age-related sarcopenia as the clinical plotline

### 4B. Grip Strength as a “New Vital Sign”
- 2024 literature proposes **hand-grip dynamometry** alongside pulse ox / BP / temp
- Correlates with: psychological health, functional ability, morbidity, hospitalization length, mortality
- Disease associations: diabetes, CVD, stroke, kidney/liver disease, some cancers, MSK pain
- Practical: non-invasive, sit-down vitals workflow; engages patients with a trackable metric
- Vs get-up-and-go: literature supports grip as a solid comparator metric; convenience is the clincher
- Meletis uses a ~$300 dynamometer; notes Amazon ~$40–50 units are reproducible enough for trend tracking

### 4C. Disuse, Myostatin & Anabolic Resistance
- **Myostatin** = negative regulator of muscle growth; rises with **age, obesity, disuse** (injury, joint replacement, sedentary life)
- High myostatin → poor gym ROI; low myostatin → “Eddie Hall” phenotype (self-described myostatin defect / World’s Strongest Man anecdote)
- **Anabolic resistance** ≠ steroids — muscles fail to take up leucine / BCAAs / amino acids efficiently
- Clinical pairing suggestion: Fortetropin **+ leucine/BCAAs + balanced amino acids**
- Rapid weight loss (GLP-1 / bariatric / crash diets) ↑ muscle loss because baseline nutrition is usually poor
- Pre-conception caution: avoid encouraging **>5 lb loss in 6 months** before pregnancy (toxin mobilization into zygote environment)
- Bone caveat: after large weight loss, walking may deliver *less* load → consider intentional weight-bearing (philosophical weight-vest question)

### 4D. Thyroid–Mitochondria Axis Under Calorie Stress
- Up to **~75%** of hospitalized patients show **euthyroid sick syndrome** pattern: low/normal free T3 + low/normal T4; TSH often normal/low
- Severe calorie restriction = perceived hostile environment → conservation / plateau
- Prefer **T4 → T3** pathway; illness + H2 blockers/PPI patterns + low Zn/Cu/Fe push toward **reverse T3** (“park ranger / brake pedal”)
- T3 signals mitochondrial DNA tempo; thyrocytes need mitochondria and vice versa
- Warning slide theme: **“Don’t die from dieting”** — personal sister case (extreme plant-based protein elimination → massive weight loss → later inflammatory breast cancer context; framed as toxin-mobilization caution, not proven causation)
- Feather-duster metaphor: fat loss without phase I–III detox support redistributes lipophilic burden (DDT/DDE, forever chemicals)

### 4E. Exercise Snacks, Gut–Muscle Crosstalk & Myokines
- 2024 resistance-training “minimal dose” literature: single-set / short-session “exercise snacks” beat pure sedentary life
- Preprint gastroenterology graphic: healthier gut ↔ healthier muscle (SCFAs / butyrate feed colon + TCA + muscle)
- **Myokines / “exerkines”** (IL-6 reframed): released with contraction → CV, metabolic, immune benefits; multi-organ crosstalk
- Irisin: myostatin deletion/inhibition → ↑ irisin + **browning of white fat**; cold plunge / NE / exercise also promote thermogenesis (mitochondria / UCP-1)
- Historical pearl: 1953 London bus study — sedentary **drivers** had ~2× coronary disease vs walking **conductors**
- Bonus MSK pearl: tight hamstrings / myofascial planes linked to reduced cervical ROM & deep neck flexor endurance

### 4F. Fortetropin (Myos MD / consumer “Yoked”)
- Fertilized raw egg, lyophilized / freeze-dried; **10 patents**; independent educator disclosure (not paid for this talk)
- Claims / cited trial themes:
  - ↓ serum myostatin
  - ↑ protein synthesis; ↓ protein-breakdown markers
  - College athletes (n=37): lean mass ↑; doses **6.6 g** and **19.8 g** (higher dose → greater gains); ↑ muscle thickness
  - 21-day RCT (n=20): **~18% ↑ protein synthesis** vs placebo
  - Vet dogs post-immobilization: placebo ↑ myostatin + ↓ thigh circumference; Fortetropin group preserved circumference
- Clinical vignettes: osteoporosis/sarcopenia after trauma; pre/post ACL-type repair in soccer dad; 86yo physicist after fall; 35yo chiropractor post-GLP-1 muscle loss + OSA; Duchenne teen regained toileting/shower independence at heroic dosing
- Working with large Duchenne/Becker social community (~5,900) to explore dosing for future formal study interest
- Caveat: egg allergy absolute contraindication; do **not** cook (heat destroys activity); mix into yogurt/pudding/smoothie
- Typical adult dose discussed: **~11.6–19.8 g once daily** (scoop-and-a-half of original); DMD case used ~3× typical
- Retail original canister ~**$99/month**; HCP channel via Myos MD; negotiate discounts; vanilla has slightly lower yield/scoop
- Does **not** routinely lab myostatin; notes mild mTOR ↑ in exercising skeletal muscle in one study — avoid if active tumorigenesis / pregnancy without data
- CHF/LVH concern: myostatin effects more skeletal-centric; small data; Meletis personally comfortable supporting skeletal muscle in many cardiac patients

### 4G. Clinical Stack for Muscle Retention
1. Oxygen / rule out **OSA** (Lofta.com remote sleep test ~$189 cited)
2. Calories sufficient to avoid T3→rT3 conservation
3. Protein + EAAs; leucine adjunct
4. Mitochondrial support (NR / NAD pathways, CoQ10, PQQ; avoid mitochondrial toxins when possible — propofol, some anesthetics, frequent lidocaine examples)
5. Resistance training + step tracking
6. Phase I–II–III detox when liberating adipose toxins
7. Hormonal milieu: cortisol rhythm, DHEA (avoid “age-adjusted broken” ranges), total/free T (± SHBG in women), thyroid cluster + rT3, IGF-1 (~50% of mid-50s/60s patients “abysmal” IGF-1 in his practice)
8. Adaptogens / physiologic hydrocortisone buffer if low cortisol → breakthrough inflammation
9. Fortetropin for myostatin / anabolic-resistance support
