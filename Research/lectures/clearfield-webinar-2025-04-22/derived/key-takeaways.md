# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-04-22

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **GLP-1 success without muscle strategy is incomplete.** Patients are often overfed/undernourished; cutting poor calories accelerates sarcopenia, myokine loss, and “skinny fat.”

2. **After 40, expect ~1–2% muscle and ~1.5–5% strength loss per year** unless you intervene — frame this for every longevity and weight-loss consult.

3. **Add grip strength to vitals.** 2024 literature links low grip to cardiometabolic disease, hospitalization, and mortality; it’s cheap, sit-down, and motivational.

4. **Myostatin is the brake.** It rises with age, obesity, and disuse; lowering it (training ± Fortetropin) improves the training response and supports irisin / white-fat browning.

5. **Anabolic resistance = failed amino-acid uptake**, especially leucine — not a steroid lecture. Pair protein/EAAs (+ leucine) with myostatin support.

6. **Aggressive dieting can push T4→reverse T3** (euthyroid sick / conservation physiology). Plateaus may be protective biology, not patient noncompliance.

7. **Fat loss liberates toxins.** Support phase I–II–III detox when weight drops fast; don’t “feather-dust” lipophilic burden into systemic circulation.

8. **Fortetropin (Myos MD / Yoked)** — patented fertilized raw egg — ↓ myostatin, ↑ protein synthesis (~18% in a 21-day RCT). Dose ~11.6–19.8 g daily; **never cook**; egg allergy contraindicated.

9. **Oxygen before aminos.** Screen OSA (Lofta remote testing cited); Meletis’s own weightlifting + mid-400s AST/ALT story was bicep infarct unmasked by nocturnal hypoxia.

10. **Elderberry juice (~6–12 oz/day)** is a simple metabolic adjunct (WSU n=18: −24% glucose, −9% insulin, +27% fat oxidation) — useful tip, not a miracle.

## Comparison: Scale-Only Weight Loss vs Muscle-Centric Protocol

| Dimension | Scale-only (GLP-1 / crash cut) | Muscle-centric (this webinar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary goal** | Lower weight | Preserve lean mass + metabolic rate |
| **Protein** | Often incidental / inadequate | Intentional EAAs + leucine |
| **Myostatin** | Ignored (rises with disuse) | Train + Fortetropin to normalize |
| **Thyroid** | Plateaus blamed on “willpower” | Watch T3 vs rT3 under restriction |
| **Detox** | Rarely planned | Phase I–II–III when liberating fat |
| **Oxygen / OSA** | Often missed | Screen early (Lofta pathway) |
| **Tracking** | Scale + BMI | Grip strength + function + labs |
| **Best for** | Short-term optics | Longevity, independence, insulin sensitivity |

**Consensus:** Use GLP-1s if you must — but never without a muscle, mito, protein, and oxygen plan.
