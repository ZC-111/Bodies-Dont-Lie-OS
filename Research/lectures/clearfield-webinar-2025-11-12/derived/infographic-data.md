## ⚖️ Infographic 5: "Lidocaine vs Procaine — Choose by Goal"

| Dimension | Lidocaine (Burgess default) | Procaine (some schools) |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiac profile | Stabilizing (anti-arrhythmic lineage) | More dysrhythmic historically |
| ANS feel | Normalizing | More stimulatory (panel) |
| Reactions (Burgess) | Low over decades | High in his early exposure |
| Stem-cell mix | Avoid mixing (panel caution) | Marcaine discussion separate |
| Best mental model | Opening / regulation | Stimulus vs numb debate |

**Consensus in room:** Both used historically — match agent to objective and safety data you trust.
