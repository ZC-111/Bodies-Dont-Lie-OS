# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-11-12

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Neural therapy reframes local anesthetics as voltage/regulation tools** — lidocaine stabilizes membranes, raises cellular voltage, and restores segmental communication for pain *and* systemic dysregulation.

2. **The therapeutic effect lasts days, not minutes.** Numbness ~30 min; “opening” ≈ **3–5 days** → follow-up about weekly.

3. **Surgical scars are interference fields.** German rule: inject scars first. Midline abdominal/lumbar scars can drive remote pain via Ren/Du / segmental blockade.

4. **Scar-only protocol helped ~53% of selected women** (39 of ~5,000 screened) with lasting pain reduction despite pain *not* being in the belly.

5. **Prefer 1% lidocaine with hard safety habits:** aspirate every time (especially upper cervical), usually ≤5 cc/point and ≤10 cc total (≤20 cc select cases).

6. **Procaine vs lidocaine is goal-dependent.** Burgess: fewer reactions and cardiac-stabilizing profile with lidocaine. Panel: procaine may be more ANS-stimulatory; don’t mix lidocaine into stem-cell injectates.

7. **IRR (infraspinatus respiratory reflex)** — intradermal scapular blebs can abort asthma crises in trained hands (Gerber/Burgess clinical lore).

8. **Homeopuncture = homeopathic + point/scar application**, often with **DMSO** driver; can defer needles when micro-drop point therapy already drops pain.

9. **Traumeel injectable is largely blocked in the US** — plan alternatives (other injectables, oral-pellet extracts in DMSO, legal international sourcing). Bicarb can reduce lidocaine burn on C-section scars.

10. **Expect emotional release** when unlocking ANS fields; neural therapy is also used as a precursor before acupuncture, detox, or IVs when major scars are present.

## Agent Snapshot: Lidocaine vs Procaine

| Dimension | Lidocaine | Procaine |
|---|---|---|
| **Burgess default** | ✅ Yes (1%) | ❌ Rarely |
| **Reaction experience** | Low over ~30 years | High in early exposure |
| **Cardiac framing** | Stabilizing | More dysrhythmic historically |
| **ANS framing (panel)** | Normalizing | Stimulatory |
| **Session effect window** | Opening 3–5 days | School-dependent |
| **Stem-cell context** | Avoid co-mixing | Marcaine discussed as alternative anesthetic story |

**Consensus:** Skill, aspiration, scar-first sequencing, and dose discipline matter more than brand loyalty to one ester/amide story.
