### Q14. Approximate dosing guardrails Burgess describes for 1% lidocaine:
**Answer:** Prefer total body load **≤10 cc** (up to ~**20 cc** in strong patients if needed); usually **≤5 cc per point**. Lightheadedness can occur with multi-facet loads; he reports no syncope with his lidocaine practice.

**Answer:** Injectable Traumeel (Heel; ~14 remedies including Arnica) is largely **unavailable/blocked in the US**. ### Q20. A new clinician wants to start neural therapy next week for a patient with a large C-section scar and chronic neck/back pain. Outline a Burgess-aligned first approach.
**Answer (model):**
1. Full history/exam (1–2 h initial) — map scars, segments, red flags

7. Warn about possible emotional release; do not advance to upper cervical until aspiration/safety habits are solid
8. Document that Wikipedia “pseudoscience” labels do not replace informed consent, training, and outcomes tracking
