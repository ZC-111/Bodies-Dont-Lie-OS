# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-09-17

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Start with the endocannabinoid system, not the dispensary menu.** Anandamide/2-AG + CB1/CB2 fine-tune other neurotransmitter systems; plant THC is a longer, broader partial-agonist overlay.

2. **NIH-scale ambition, clinician-scale humility.** Pacher & Kunos: ECS modulation may touch almost every disease — but Block insists on evidence tiers, not anecdotes.

3. **Best evidence first:** conclusive for chemo nausea/vomiting (THC); substantial for chronic pain and MS spasticity (2017 National Academies).

4. **Modern cannabis ≠ landrace cannabis.** Pre-1970 balances (~≤5% THC with CBD companionship) mutated under prohibition into mega-THC flower and 90%+ concentrates.

5. **No brainstem CB receptors ≠ zero harm.** Respiratory arrest is unlikely; accidents, acute hypotension from potent products, psychosis risk in vulnerable patients, and adolescent IQ signals are real.

6. **Protect developing brains.** Dunedin: heavy continuous use from ~15 → ~6 IQ points lower by 30. Prefer delaying THC until ~25.

7. **Edibles demand kinetic counseling.** Oral path → 11-hydroxy-THC (~4× stronger/longer). Never stack brownies because “nothing happened yet.”

8. **CBD markets need label literacy.** 2018 Farm Bill + Epidiolex descheduling flooded CBD; acid-converted **delta-8** often skirts into gas stations with thin safety data.

9. **Cancer claims stay disciplined.** Cannabinoids may ease physical/psychological/spiritual suffering; RSO anecdotes and petri-dish kill assays ≠ proven human cures.

10. **Rescheduling’s clinical prize is research + education.** Leaving Schedule I (toward III) unlocks training and studies; industry logistics, chemovar complexity, and age limits still need careful rulemaking.

## Comparison: Landrace Balance vs Modern Mega-THC

| Dimension | Traditional landrace pattern | Modern recreational pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Typical flower THC** | Often ≤5%, balanced with CBD | Often 20–30%+ |
| **Concentrates** | Uncommon historically | Oils/waxes/shatter ~90–95% THC |
| **Anxiety signal** | CBD buffers THC | High-THC novices → anxiety/paranoia |
| **Microdosing** | Easier at low % | Hard without metered devices |
| **Evidence fit** | Closer to historical human coevolution | Outruns component-level medical research |
| **Clinical stance** | Prefer balanced / low effective dose | Avoid concentrates in naive/cardiac patients |

**Consensus from the room:** Science and reason over politics; start low/go slow; don’t pretend “cannabis” is a single drug.
