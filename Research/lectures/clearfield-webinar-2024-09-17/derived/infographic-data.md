## 🎯 Infographic 1: "Three Lenses — Don't Confuse Them"

**Title:** Medical Research ≠ Decriminalization ≠ Regulation

| Lens | Core question | Clinician role |
|---|---|---|
| **Research / education** | What does the ECS do? What does evidence support? | Learn mechanisms; follow NASEM ladder |
| **Decriminalization** | Should people be jailed for use? | Public-health / ethics voice |
| **Regulation** | Who grows, tests, ages, labels, sells? | Demand purity, dosing, COAs |

**Footer:** Block’s opener — only some of the “cannabis space” is the practice of medicine.

## 🧬 Infographic 2: "The ECS Fine-Tuner"

**Title:** Lipid Signaling That Supervises Other Systems

```
ENDOCANNABINOID SYSTEM
  │
  ├── RECEPTORS
  │     CB1 → brain / nerves (memory, reward, movement, judgment…)
  │     CB2 → periphery / gut / immune (injury-activated)
  │
  ├── LIGANDS (on-demand, short-lived)
  │     Anandamide (“bliss”) — THC’s closest cousin (partial agonist)
  │     2-AG — fuller endogenous agonist tone
  │
  └── ACTION
        Retrograde synapse control → fine-tunes glutamate, dopamine, etc.
```

**Callout:** NIH authors (Pacher & Kunos): modulating ECS may help in **almost all human diseases**.

## 🫁 Infographic 3: "Why Opioids Stop Breathing — Cannabis Usually Doesn't"

**Title:** Receptor Geography Matters

| System | Brainstem respiratory centers | Overdose pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Opioids** | Dense target | Apnea / fatal respiratory depression |
| **Cannabinoids** | **Absent** | Fall asleep / impaired driving — accidents still kill |

**Footer:** “Can’t stop breathing” ≠ “can’t die from cannabis.” Counsel impairment and cardiac hypotension with concentrates.

## 📊 Infographic 4: "2017 National Academies Evidence Ladder"

**Title:** Lead With What Is Actually Known

| Evidence tier | Clinical use |

| ★★★ **Conclusive** | Chemo-induced nausea & vomiting (THC) |

**Rule:** Anecdotes ≠ plural of data. Horse (evidence) before cart (hype).

## ⚗️ Infographic 5: "Then vs Now — The Potency Problem"

**Title:** Not Your Mother’s Marijuana (Adams + Block)

```
PRE-1970 LANDRACE BALANCE
  THC ≈ CBD, often <5% flower THC

MODERN RECREATIONAL BREEDING
  Flower ~20–30% THC common
  Concentrates / shatter / dabbing → 90–95% THC

CLINICAL TRANSLATION
  Naive perceptible dose ~2.5–5 mg THC
  Eraser-tip of 25% flower can hold many mg → hard to microdose
```

**Footer:** Traditional fields evolved toward balance; prohibition economics evolved toward concentrates.

## 🧠 Infographic 6: "Adolescent Brain Guardrail"

**Title:** Dunedin Study — Why “Wait Until 25” Shows Up in Clinic

```
Age 15 → continuous heavy cannabis use → retested ~age 30
IQ change cited: ~6 points lower (100 → 94)
Percentile shift: ~50th → ~26th

Brain cannabinoid circuitry matures into mid-20s
```

**Action:** Screen teens/young adults; delay THC when possible; never normalize school-age recreational access as “safe because legal.”

## 🍪 Infographic 7: "Edibles — The 11-Hydroxy Trap"

**Title:** Why the Second Brownie Wrecks the Night

```
INHALED / SUBLINGUAL     ORAL EDIBLE
Fast to brain            Delayed 30–90+ min
Δ9-THC dominant          Liver → 11-hydroxy-THC
                         ~4× stronger, ~4× longer

FAILURE MODE
  “I feel nothing” → redose → peak hits late → ER / paranoia
```

**Counseling line:** Start low, go slow — one dose, wait it out.

STATE LICENSE          FEDERAL DEA LICENSE
