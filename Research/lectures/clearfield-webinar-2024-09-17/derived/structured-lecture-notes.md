## Segment 2 — The Evolving Cannabis Space: History, Law & Plant Evolution (11:15–34:56)

**Speaker:** Jeffrey Block, MD

### ★ Framing Thesis
> Separate **medical research/education**, **decriminalization**, and **regulation** — only some of this is clinical medicine. Start with the body’s **endocannabinoid system**, not industry hype.

### 2A. Regulatory Axes (FDA vs DEA)
- **FDA:** safety & efficacy (pharma vs nutraceutical standards — cannabis sits awkwardly between)
- **DEA / DOJ:** risk–benefit scheduling
  - Schedule I = high abuse potential + **no currently accepted medical use in the US** + lack of accepted safety under medical supervision
  - Schedules II–V = controlled but progressively less restricted
- Schedule I research is stifled when clinicians fear license risk for studying the plant

### 2B. Plant History & Landrace Chemistry
- *Cannabis sativa* documented with humans ≥6,000 years; archaeological evidence ~10–12,000 years; origin eastern Himalayan refugia
- Ancient landrace fields (e.g., Middle East) often grew **balanced THC ≈ CBD**
- THC tends to provoke anxiety in novices; CBD tends to relieve anxiety → traditional balance
- **Controlled Substances Act (1970)** → modern breeding chased potency (alcohol-prohibition analogy: beer/wine → spirits)
- Afghani Kush / short indica hybrids selected partly because they hide from helicopters; high UV / altitude → high THC + **myrcene**
- Modern flower: THC approaching ~25% (vs ~5% pre-1970); CBD can reach ~15% in some cultivars — but recreational market skewed mega-THC

### 2C. Medical Education Gap
- Cannabis in USP ~1850–1942; removed after 1937 prohibition → generations of physicians untaught
- Not on national boards → medical schools devote little curriculum time
- Block predicts ~**10-year lag** after rescheduling before newly trained clinicians are community-ready
- Osteopathic / integrative lens: body–environment integration aligns with ECS thinking better than purely allopathic training

### Clinical Pearl
> Modern “pot” is not landrace pot. Dose, delivery, and chemovar matter as much as “cannabis yes/no.”

## Segment 3 — Endocannabinoid System Mechanisms (34:56–51:51)

**Speaker:** Jeffrey Block, MD

### ★ Core Thesis
> NIH framing (Pacher & Kunos): modulating ECS activity may have therapeutic potential in **almost all diseases affecting humans**. Phytocannabinoids are a late evolutionary blip interacting with a half-billion-year lipid signaling system.

### 3A. CB1 vs CB2
- **CB1:** CNS / peripheral nerves — memory, reward, judgment, movement, sensation, vision, coordination
- **CB2:** periphery; often dormant until injury/disease; PET mapping shows dense abdominal/gut signal
- Endogenous ligands: **anandamide** (Sanskrit “bliss”; closest to THC as partial agonist) and **2-AG** (fuller agonist tone)
- Endogenous action: **site-specific, on-demand, short-lived**; plant cannabinoids: broad coverage, longer duration
- **No CB receptors in brainstem respiratory centers** → fatal respiratory depression essentially impossible (unlike opioids) — but intoxication can still kill via accidents/impaired driving

### 3B. Synaptic Mechanism
- Retrograde signaling: postsynaptic → anandamide / 2-AG → presynaptic CB1 → fine-tunes neurotransmitter release (glutamate, dopamine, adrenaline, etc.)
- ECS “fine-tunes” other receptor systems; other systems largely water-based; ECS is **lipid-based**
- Regulation spectrum: full agonists, partial agonists (THC), inverse agonists; pharma interest in modulating **endocannabinoid tone**
- Block is cautious about labeling “endocannabinoid deficiency syndrome” as a formal syndrome — lifestyle may restore tone

### 3C. East–West Bridge
- CB2 gut dominance maps conceptually onto acupuncture / **qi** flowing through gut rather than brain/heart/lungs
- Intact immune system ≈ intact ECS for survival framing
- Integrative medicine = meeting Western receptor science with Eastern balance traditions

### 3D. Pain Systems & Historical Polypharmacy
- Three modern ligand discoveries: **endorphins** (1970s / opium), **endocannabinoids** (1990s / cannabis), **endovanilloids** (capsaicin / chili)
- Early 20th-century botanical formularies (e.g., Parke-Davis) combined small amounts of morphine + cannabis + capsaicin → mitigated single-agent side effects
- Lowest effective dose remains the clinical rule; excess of any pathway can hijack pain perception / create dependence

## Segment 4 — Bioethics, Public Health & Evidence Hierarchy (51:51–1:12:15)

**Speaker:** Jeffrey Block, MD (with Surgeon General video clips)

### 4A. Physician Double Bind
- AMA ethics: respect the law **and** seek change when laws harm patients
- Many clinicians hold state medical + federal DEA licenses → conflict under Schedule I
- Bioethical framing Block uses with his children:
  1. Seeking relief from intolerable symptoms is instinctive (self-soothing)
  2. Society designates healers to alleviate suffering
- Block himself does not authorize cannabis clinically; speaks from science/ethics/botany platform

### 4B. Public Health Contrast
- Tobacco smoking deaths ↓ (public-health win)
- Lethal opioid overdoses ↑ over same era (Joint Commission “smiley face” pain culture + OxyContin era cited)
- Industry dynamics volatile around DEA rescheduling rumors (stock swings)

### 4C. National Academies 2017 Evidence Ladder
Federally commissioned meta-analysis (NAS/NASEM):

| Evidence level | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Conclusive** | Chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting (**THC**) |
| **Substantial** | Chronic pain; MS spasticity |
| Limited / little | Glaucoma, ALS, Parkinson’s (among others cited as weak) |

### 4D. Surgeon Generals’ Arc
- Historical flip-flops track politics as much as science
- **Vivek Murthy (2015):** let science guide; some medical benefit signals
- **Jerome Adams (2019):** “not your mother’s marijuana” — 5% → 20–30% flower → 90–95% concentrates (oils/waxes/shatter/dabbing); pregnancy use concern; “no such thing as medical marijuana” as crude plant product — research **components**
- **Murthy (2021):** medical benefits + harms; decriminalization ≠ ignore science; don’t incarcerate for simple use
- HHS recommended Schedule III; DEA public process expected ~December (as of talk date); Florida recreational ballot Nov 2024

### Clinical Pearl
> “Anecdotes are not the plural of data.” Lead with horse (evidence + bioethics), not cart (stakeholder bias).

## Segment 5 — Extended Q&A: Psychiatry, Adolescence, Delivery, Cancer, Scheduling (1:12:15–2:25:40)

**Speakers:** Block; Dr. Leonard; Melvin Nario; Dr. Quinn; Dr. Siegel; Bill Clearfield

### 5A. Psychiatry / Schizophrenia Risk
- Psychiatry most polarized specialty: addictionologists vs clinicians comparing cannabis to benzos/SSRIs
- Genetic psychosis predisposition + heavy use → worse outcomes possible
- Need consensus on therapeutic CB1 approaches; Alzheimer’s/dementia research interesting but evidence-thin

### 5B. Potency, BP Drop, Pregnancy
- Habitual use → receptor downregulation → higher doses needed
- High-potency / concentrates can cause acute **hypotension** (afterload reduction) — absolute concern in advanced cardiac disease
- Smoking any substance in pregnancy impairs fetal oxygenation (CO) — delivery method matters
- Contrasting ethnopharmacology: dilute cannabis **teas** used in Jamaica for first-trimester hyperemesis (volume + low dose) ≠ smoking a spliff

### 5C. Adolescent Brain — Dunedin Study
- Brain circuitry develops with cannabinoids into ~**mid-20s**
- Dunedin (NZ) longitudinal: continuous use from age 15 → age 30 associated with ~**6-point IQ drop** (100 → 94; ~50th → ~26th percentile)
- Block’s personal stance for his twins: prefer waiting until ~25

### 5D. Hemp Farm Bill 2018, Epidiolex, Delta-8
- 2018 Farm Bill separated hemp (< intoxicating THC framing) from marijuana → CBD flood → price collapse
- **Epidiolex** (CBD): Schedule I → V → descheduled; nutraceutical-like availability
- Acid conversion of CBD → **delta-8 THC** (gas-station loophole); little US safety data vs delta-9
- Historical bright spot: Israeli delta-8 chemo-nausea pediatric study terminated early for dramatic antiemetic effect
- **Marinol** (synthetic/oral THC): legal but often flop — slow onset, paranoia in naive users vs inhaled relief

### 5E. Product Safety / Interstate Spillover
- CBD supplement safety = what’s **actually in the bottle** (nutraceutical labeling weaker than pharma)
- Case: elderly woman given infused gummy mistaken for plain candy
- Recreational legalization spills across state lines (MA → VT example; money follows)

### 5F. Cancer / RSO
- Rick Simpson Oil = anecdotal; cancer pain is physical + psychological/spiritual
- Petri-dish cannabinoid cytotoxicity ≠ controlled human cancer cures
- Possible dermatologic/epigenetic interest (e.g., psoriasis as hyperproliferative disease; plant oils evolved as UV protection)
- Cannabis may improve quality of dying/living with cancer pain — do not sell as cure vs Whipple-level interventions

### 5G. Indica vs Sativa (Chemovar Reality)
- All derived from *C. sativa*; “sativa” often more energetic; “indica” = “in the couch” (myrcene-heavy sedating pattern)
- Amotivational syndrome risk with chronic heavy indica-type use
- Extreme-athlete anxiolysis anecdotes ≠ endorsement

### 5H. Oral Kinetics & Edibles
- Sublingual / mucosal absorption faster than GI
- Oral Δ9-THC → hepatic **11-hydroxy-THC** (~**4×** stronger, ~**4×** longer) — classic brownie redose disaster → ER visits
- Naive perceptible dose may be as low as **2.5–5 mg THC**; modern flower concentrates make microdosing hard without metered devices
- Prescribing language illegal under CSA; states use **recommend / order**

### 5I. Schedule III Practicalities (Quinn + Block)
- Moving off Schedule I is a **huge** research/education unlock (not a baby step)
- Schedule III enables interstate commerce industry needs; Schedule II still research-constrained
- Society of Cannabis Clinicians interest in a possible **Schedule VI** acknowledging multi-cannabinoid/terpene complexity (not single-molecule pharma model)
- **Sativex** (≈1:1 CBD:THC) used in ~30 countries for MS spasticity — US lag; “start low, go slow”
- Future: patented partial agonists inspired by anandamide; CBD as **negative allosteric modulator** of CB1
