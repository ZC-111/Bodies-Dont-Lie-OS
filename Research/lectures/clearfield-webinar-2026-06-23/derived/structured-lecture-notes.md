## Segment 1 — Tip of the Week: Traumeel Evidence (05:53–11:41)

**Speaker:** Melvin Nario, DO (Speaker 5)

### Context
- Nevada Homeopathic Medical Board appeared before the legislature; senators pressed for study evidence on homeopathy.
- Framing: welcome scientific challenge; move from anecdote → RCT language.

### Traumeel evidence cited
1. **Thiel & Borojerdi (classic RCT)** — 73 patients, post-traumatic knee arthrosis  
   - 3 intra-articular injections every 8 days vs saline  
   - Greater swelling reduction (~73% vs ~51%), better mobility, less pain/effusion; no adverse events reported
2. **Traumed / multicenter ankle-sprain RCT (2024; ~625 patients)** — Traumeel gel vs placebo gel vs diclofenac gel  
   - Superior pain reduction vs placebo; non-inferior to diclofenac on several measures  
   - Time to ~50% pain reduction shortest with Traumeel (~6 days vs ~7 for placebo/diclofenac)

### Clinical pearl
- Not “throw away NSAIDs” — show where homeopathic and orthopedic practice can agree.
- When legislators ask “where are the studies?”: answer is neither “homeopathy has all answers” nor “there is no evidence.”
