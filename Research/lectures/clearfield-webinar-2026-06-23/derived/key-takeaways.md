# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-06-23

## Top 10 Clinical / Teaching Pearls

1. **TOBIN is a bridge filter, not a replacement system.** Toxicity, Oxidative stress, Biologicals, Inflammation, Nutrition/neuroendocrine — map Ayurveda / Thai / Native American practice into these buckets without discarding lineage integrity.

2. **Ama / garavisha = “unintelligent” toxic residues** that confuse digestive fire and distort doshas — useful Ayurvedic frame for GMOs, PFAS, xenoestrogens, ultra-processed burden.

3. **Prakriti vs vikriti is the diagnostic fork.** Innate constitution (“year/make/model”) vs acquired deviation. Healthy-in-spec patients are nearly “invisible”; disease is distance from baseline.

4. **Joint = energy valve.** In Thai medicine, greatest potential for gain/loss of energy is at the joints (*lom*) — explains early joint symptoms across infectious and immune pictures.

5. **Treat all five bodies for holism.** Physical + energetic/matrix + mental-emotional + identity/ego layers — physical therapy alone is incomplete.

6. **Somatic expression precedes labs.** Every imbalance has a personality (Thomas Hanna). Extreme anger + noncompliance can be the somatic face of cardiac/liver crisis — read the room before the CBC.

7. **Terrain over seed (preference).** Western medicine often treats the pathogen/seed; Ayurveda/indigenous frames prioritize soil/terrain/bio-individuality — both eyes needed.

8. **Ahimsa as practice ethic.** Prefer non-lethal pathways; incidental mortality is not spiritually neutral. Ayurveda = science of *life*, not attrition.

9. **Traumeel has RCT language for legislators.** Intra-articular knee data + 2024 multicenter ankle gel RCT (~625): better than placebo, competitive with diclofenac on key endpoints — without discarding NSAIDs.

10. **No medicine without consent.** Intervention without consent is definitionally not medicine; health sovereignty is the clinic goal (“temple of wellness”).
