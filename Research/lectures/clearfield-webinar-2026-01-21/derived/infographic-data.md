## 🎯 Infographic 1: "The Telomere Countdown"

**Title:** Your Chromosomal Ride Tickets

| Stage | Approx. telomere length | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Conception | **~15,000 bases** | Full starting aglets (TTAGGG repeats) |
| Birth | **~10,000 bases** | ~Half the functional “tickets” already spent |
| Advanced age | **~5,000 bases** | Cells lose function → organismal aging |
| Hard ceiling | **~125 years** | Max lifespan/healthspan if only basal shortening |

**Footer:** Every division spends a ticket. Lifestyle can slow *accelerated* loss — not erase the basal end-replication problem.

## 🧪 Infographic 3: "Vendor BS Detector"

**Title:** 3 Questions Before You Believe “Lengthens Telomeres”

1. Does the product **express telomerase**? (If no → it does not lengthen.)
2. Is expression **greater than HeLa** (tie-level)? (If no → won’t reverse aging.)
3. Do you have **Sierra Sciences / Andrews-validated** assay data — not only “blood longer after than before”?

**Trap artifacts**
- Toxicity → kills short-telomere cells → ↑ blood average
- Immune flare → marrow cells with long telomeres enter blood → ↑ average
- Net body telomere budget can still **fall**

## 📏 Infographic 4: "Measure What Matters"

**Title:** Average Length Is Not Enough

| Method / vendor cue | What you get | Andrews take |
|---|---|---|
| Average-only assays | One mean number | Misleading — hides short-telomere tail |

**Callout:** Aging biology tracks **critically short** telomeres more than the mean.

## 🧬 Infographic 6: "Telomerase & Cancer — Myth Bust"

**Title:** Telomerase Does Not Cause Cancer

```
Short telomeres
  → Mutation storms (chromothripsis / kataegis)
  → Cancer cells may mutate to make their OWN telomerase

Longer immune-cell telomeres
  → Better cancer surveillance

Inducing telomerase in normal cells
  → More “ride tickets” when division is appropriate
  → Does NOT equal loss of growth control
```

**Resource cue:** Tokyo CME 2017 talk (~25:25–1:02); book *Telomere Lengthening*
