# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-01-21

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Hayflick / telomere shortening caps human lifespan ~125 years** — no lifestyle stack removes the basal end-replication problem without a true lengthener.

2. **Telomere lengths:** ~15k bases at conception → ~10k at birth → trouble near ~5k. Reproductive cells use **telomerase** to tie the tug-of-war; somatic cells do not.

3. **Only telomerase lengthens telomeres in normal human cells.** “Blood longer after than before” is not proof — demand telomerase-expression data (ideally &gt; HeLa tie-level).

4. **Measure % short telomeres (FISH), not averages alone.** Life Length (Spain) was the preferred commercial lab cited.

5. **Betty White / QQ test:** reversing methylation or mean telomere biomarkers ≠ reversing aging. Phenotype (look/feel/behave younger) is the bar.

6. **Slow accelerators now:** consistent exercise, antioxidants, sugar control, toxin/inflammation reduction, gut health, no obesity/smoking, stress reduction.

7. **Telomerase ≠ cancer cause** in Andrews’ framing — critically short telomeres drive mutation storms; longer immune telomeres support surveillance. See Tokyo 2017 deep-dive.

8. **Senolytics: caution** until potent lengthening exists — mass killing of senescent cells forces replacement divisions and further shortening.

9. **Melatonin–HF headlines (AHA 2025 observational):** treat as phenotype/marker signal in severe insomnia cohorts (esp. Rx-only UK context) — not a ban on clinical melatonin.

10. **Televital** (humans) and **Telo Dash** (pets) were Andrews’ strongest current nutraceutical directions; gene therapy remains PoC / high-risk; funding (~$2M/mo) framed as the bottleneck to a war-winning agent.
