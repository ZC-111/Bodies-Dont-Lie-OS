# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-02-18

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **You cannot manage what you cannot measure.** Treat stress like hypertension/diabetes: identify → measure diurnal salivary cortisol → manage.

2. **75–90% of primary-care visits are stress-related** (Taylor’s cited figure; he suspects higher now). Become a stress expert and you can help most patients.

3. **Cortisol is capacity, not the villain.** Goal is a responsive diurnal rhythm (high AM, low night)—not chronically high, flat, or inverted “tired and wired.”

4. **Blood / saliva / urine answer different questions.** Blood = delivery; saliva ≈ tissue; urine/DUTCH = metabolites. Mismatch does not invalidate saliva.

5. **Perimenopause is often progesterone failure under rising FSH**, not simple estrogen deficiency—saliva frequently shows elevated estrogen with low progesterone.

6. **Target ~200:1 progesterone:estrogen on saliva** (lab Taylor uses). ~80% of his menopausal patients do well on progesterone alone; thinner patients more often need estrogen.

7. **Stress breaks TSH-only thyroid logic** — HPA can suppress TSH and block T4→T3; check free T3, reverse T3, and the cortisol curve.

8. **Four-prong stress care:** remove stressor → change response → repair damage → build resilience. Severe flat curves may need a short hydrocortisone bridge plus IV nutrient/NAD support.

9. **Unopposed estrogen to “lower FSH” is not Taylor’s physiology play** — elevated FSH is expected in menopause; prioritize E/P balance and avoid Premarin-era mistakes.

10. **Ayahuasca is pharmacologically potent** (DMT + reversible MAO-A-i). Screen psych/CV/serotonergic meds; counsel without judgment; US use is legally constrained.
