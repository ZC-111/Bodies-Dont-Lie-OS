### Q11. True or False: After menopause, 100% of women stop producing estrogen.
**Answer:** **False.** All stop ovulating (↓ progesterone). Many continue tissue estrogen via aromatase (especially adipose). Blood may miss tissue estrogen that saliva can reflect.
