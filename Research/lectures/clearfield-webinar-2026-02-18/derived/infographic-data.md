## 🧪 Infographic 4: "Three Compartments — Stop Comparing Apples to Oranges"

**Title:** Same Hormone, Different Question

| Matrix | What it answers | Misuse |
|---|---|---|
| **Blood** | Delivery / circulating total | “Must match saliva” |
| **Saliva** | Tissue physiology (fat-soluble steroids) | Dismissed because ≠ blood |
| **Urine / DUTCH** | Metabolites & detox pathways | Used alone to dose HRT |

**Pearl:** Keys lost in the bedroom won’t be found in the kitchen — wrong compartment ≠ bad test.

## ⚖️ Infographic 5: "The 200:1 Rule"

**Title:** Balance Is Good. Imbalance Is Bad.

```
Target (Taylor’s saliva lab framing):
  Progesterone  ≈  200 × Estrogen

Menopause reality check:
  ✓ All women ↓ ovulation → ↓ progesterone
  ✗ Not all women ↓ estrogen (aromatase in fat/tissue)

Practice pattern cited:
  ~80% of his menopausal patients → progesterone alone
  Thin / younger → more often need estrogen too
```

**Retest:** ~3 months; hold progesterone 48–72h before saliva to catch oversaturation.

## 💊 Infographic 6: "Four-Prong Stress Protocol + Adrenal Bridge"

**Title:** Fix Upstream Allocation First

```
1. REMOVE stressor (job, food, relationship, glucose chaos)
2. CHANGE response (meditation, Quantum breathwork, environment)
3. REPAIR damage (gut, neurotransmitters, nutrients)
4. BUILD resilience (adaptive capacity)
```

**Bridge for severe flat cortisol:**
- Mild → adaptogens (e.g. rhodiola stack)
- Moderate → weekly IV × 3: Vit C 15g + Mg + B5 + B6 + NAD 100mg
- Severe → HC ~10 mg AM + 5 mg noon × 2–3 months (*Safe Uses of Cortisol*)
- Low DHEA → often 5–10 mg (women)
- High night cortisol → phosphatidylserine to reboot nocturnal rhythm

## 🌿 Infographic 8: "Ayahuasca — Clinician Brief (Not an Endorsement)"

**Title:** Know Enough Not to Blink Twice

```
Leaf (Psychotria)  → DMT
Vine (B. caapi)    → β-carbolines = reversible MAO-A-i
Together           → 4–8h 5-HT2A / network experience

2019 RCT (n=29 TRD): day-7 response ~64% vs ~27% placebo

Screen hard:
  ✗ SSRIs/SNRIs/stimulants (serotonin syndrome)
  ✗ Bipolar / psychosis / active cardiac disease

Legal: US Schedule I; ceremonial/religious pathways; travel common
```

**Footer:** Pharmacologically potent. Setting + screening decide safety.
