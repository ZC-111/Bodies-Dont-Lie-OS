# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-09-16

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Oil pulling is a low-cost longevity adjunct**, not dental care. 1 Tbsp oil, 10–20 min swish, spit in trash, then brush + tongue scrape.

2. **Match oil to dosha when practicing classical Ayurveda:** Vata → sesame; Pitta → coconut; Kapha → sunflower (James refinement of Nario’s tip).

3. **Oral inflammation is systemic.** Periodontal disease feeds IL-6/TNF-α “inflammaging” linked to heart, brain, and metabolic risk; *P. gingivalis* appears in Alzheimer brains in cited literature.

4. **~80% of the world relies partly on traditional medicine** (WHO framing). Integrative clinicians who ignore cultural context will lose compliance even with “correct” protocols.

5. **Medical AI systematically omits indigenous/traditional corpora** — missing databases, oral traditions, and non-English formularies produce Western-only answers by default.

6. **Never defer clinical judgment to the model.** Practitioner liability stays with the human; “AI told me” is not a defense.

7. **Hallucinations are dangerous, not cute** — fabricated citations, bad scribe notes, and confident wrong protocols. Verify or omit references.

8. **Prompt engineering is a clinical skill:** layered logic flow, patient fingerprint, explicit request for integrative/plant-based/cultural options — or you will not get them.

9. **Bias is measurable:** spending-as-need algorithms underestimating Black patients (2019 *Science*); dermatology AI failing dark-skinned cancer detection.

10. **AI scribes need informed consent and line-by-line review** (Freed and peers hallucinate). Chart what you verified, not what the tool drafted.

## Debate / Tension Summary: AI as Tool vs Authority

| Dimension | Unsafe pattern | Safer pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge** | Ask AI first, learn later | Know disease → then query |
| **Prompts** | One-line “best treatment for X” | Layered, contextual, culturally explicit |
| **Citations** | Paste model references | Verify every link or don’t request them |
| **Culture** | Assume Western protocol = universal | Ask for traditional equivalents / origins |
| **Liability** | Defer to “cutting-edge AI” | Clinician owns outcome |
| **Scribes** | Auto-file transcript | Consent + human edit |

**Consensus (James + Tano):** AI has promise; current clinical tools are “dull knives.” Expertise + prompting + oversight required.
