# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-12-04

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Most ear infections don’t need antibiotics.** Placebo comparisons show ~16 hours faster resolution — educate parents instead of Candyman prescribing.

2. **Fever is therapy; Tylenol is not automatically therapy.** Acetaminophen depletes glutathione; febrile seizures are usually not the pathology — trauma from falling is.

3. **Early NSAIDs (and aggressive ice) can delay soft-tissue healing.** Prefer BPC-157 + thymosin β4; use cold for pain; ibuprofen last.

4. **TRT belongs in primary care with monitoring.** Mood thresholds (~295/148 men), longevity signal &gt;600 after 60, target often 700–900; separate erythrocytosis from PV.

5. **Vasectomy is not a free lunch.** Meta-analysis links to higher prostate cancer risk; counsel beyond “best contraception” soundbites.

6. **Low-salt dogma is weak.** ~0.4 mmHg MAP change; Armerling — normal kidneys excrete excess; many hypertensives are volume depleted; fix hyperinsulinemia.

7. **Fiber is not a universal gut cure.** Mg + salt beat bulking for constipation; fiber often worsens IBS; diverticulosis/cancer protection claims overstated.

8. **Strep antibiotics buy ~1 day.** Iodine gargles/sprays, Biocidin, neti, ± steroid gargle can keep antibiotic rates very low.

9. **Tamiflu is the cautionary tale.** Patient-level data fight showed limited symptom benefit without hard outcome wins — template for pharma skepticism.

10. **Iodine deficiency was universal in Hartman’s ~150-patient sample.** Dose with selenium (~400 mcg) + B vitamins; pair high vitamin D with **K2 + magnesium**.

**Consensus:** Individualize — “evidence-based” labels without patient-level data are often judgment-based medicine in disguise.
