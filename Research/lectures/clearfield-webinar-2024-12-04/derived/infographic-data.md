**Title:** Testosterone Is Primary Care — With Eyes Open

## 🧂 Infographic 5: "Salt & Blood Pressure"

**Title:** The 0.4 mmHg Intervention

```
Public low-salt advice
        │
        ▼
MAP change ≈ 0.4 mmHg (white participants, ~90 trials)
        │
        ▼
Volume ↓ → SNS + RAAS stay ON
        │
        ▼
Many hypertensives are salt-/volume-DEPLETED
```

**Actions:** Fix insulin resistance first • Prefer mineral salt • Bedside volume exam (JVP) • Elderly cuff HTN may be pseudo-HTN from stiff arteries

## 🧪 Infographic 8: "Iodine + D/K2 Stack"

**Title:** Two Micronutrient Non-Negotiables

**Iodine**
- 150/150 patients deficient (serum + 24h urine)
- Example dose: 12 mg tablet daily (range 12.5–50+ mg)
- Always: Se ~400 mcg + B vitamins
- Bonus: halide detox (F−/Br−), thyroid, infections, estrogen metabolism

**Vitamin D**
- Target upper range / ~70–100 (Clearfield)
- Must pair **K2 + magnesium** → block soft-tissue calcification

**Watch-outs:** Finasteride psych/libido risk • High-D without K2 • Spray sunscreen endocrine load
