## Segment 3 — Muscle as Endocrine Organ & Sarcopenia Creep (27:18–43:57)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis

### Muscle Biology
- In non-obese adults, **skeletal muscle is the largest organ** — glucose uptake, fat oxidation, myokines / exerkines communicating with liver, adipose, brain
- Muscle–brain axis: sarcopenia linked to Alzheimer’s / dementia risk; falls risk without lean mass
- After ~30: **~3–5% muscle mass loss per decade** if unmanaged; age-60+ lowest strength tercile → ~50% higher all-cause mortality (BMJ 2008 cited)
- “Skinny fat”: arm girth may look similar at 25 vs 75; quality of muscle differs dramatically

### Euthyroid Sick / Non-Thyroidal Illness
- Present in ~**75%** of hospitalized (or hospital-worthy) patients
- Also triggered by intentional calorie deprivation / extreme weight-loss programs
- Pattern: **↓ free T3**, **↑ reverse T3**, T4 low/normal, TSH low/normal → TSH+T4-only panels look “euthyroid”
- Reverse T3 = 0× metabolic activity vs T3 (3–5× vs T4); competes at mitochondrial DNA receptors for ETC / thermogenesis
- Drivers of T4→rT3: low Fe / Se / Zn, starvation, inflammation, injury
- Without adequate T3 → impaired brown-fat thermogenesis / UCP1 uncoupling → weaker weight-loss response

### Toxins & “Skinny but Unhealthy”
- Fat loss mobilizes lipophilic burden: DDT/DDE, PCBs, organochlorines, PFAS (“forever chemicals”)
- Need working mitochondria for phase I–II detox while calories/micros drop
- Meletis: >60% of NDs still under-support detox during massive weight loss
- GLP-1 / bariatric / crash loss disproportionately robs muscle → lower BMR → regain + insulin resistance

## Segment 4 — Adipocytes, Myostatin–Irisin Axis & NAD Decline (43:57–1:02:07)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis

### Fat Browning & Thermogenesis
- Brown / beige adipocytes: dense mitochondria + UCP1 → heat
- Stimuli: cold exposure, exercise, irisin, norepinephrine
- **Elevated myostatin** (aging, obesity, inactivity) → **↓ irisin** → impaired browning → obesity predisposes to regain
- Historical thermogenics (ephedra / synephrine) had adrenergic risks; modern frame favors mito + training + myostatin support

### Anabolic Resistance
- With age / disuse: skeletal muscle fails to take up amino acids well (not just “low testosterone”)
- Common thread: ↓ physical activity / contraction → anabolic resistance → hard to rebuild after rapid loss
- IL-6: context-dependent — excess inflammatory vs exercise-induced myokine signaling

### NAD Biology
- NAD can drop ~**65%** between ages 30–70; ~half of skin NAD by ~40
- ~**500** NAD-dependent enzymatic pathways (compare ~300–600 Mg-dependent)
- Similar age curves: melatonin, nitric oxide
- 1953 Lancet London bus study: conductors healthier than drivers (activity vs sedentary)
- ATP trivia: ~60 kg (~130 lb) ATP turnover/day at age 30; ~**10% less ATP production per decade** thereafter
- Phone-battery analogy: DNA/apps useless without cellular charge

### Mitochondrial Support Stack (lecture framing)
- Prefer **nicotinamide riboside (NR)** as NAD precursor (NRK1/2 stress-inducible uptake; NMN / NAD do not freely cross membrane without breakdown)
- CoQ10, PQQ (biogenesis), resveratrol
- Organic acids / Krebs intermediates highlight recurring B3 / Fe / Mg / lipoic acid gaps

## Segment 5 — Clinical Protocol: Weight Loss + Detox + NAD Evidence (1:02:07–1:19:29)

**Speaker:** Chris Meletis

### Selected NR Evidence Touched
- 1000 mg NR × 3 weeks (overweight adults): ↓ inflammatory markers; ↑ muscle NAD signals
- 1000 mg/day × 6 weeks RCT: skeletal muscle markers / NAD flux bioavailability
- Twin / discordance studies: 250–1000 mg escalating × 5 months → whole-blood NAD **↑ ~2.3-fold** lean & obese twins; ↑ mito biogenesis / gut microbiota signals (limitation: no control in one cited design)
- Cross-sectional aging metabolome: NAD among most depleted metabolites in older adults — **exception:** seniors walking ~**14,000 steps/day** retained better NAD
- Physically impaired older adults: muscle NAD markedly depleted vs active youth

### Meletis Weight-Loss Optimization Protocol
1. **NAD precursor** (preference: NR) + CoQ10 + PQQ + resveratrol  
2. **Sufficient calories** to avoid starvation-driven T3→rT3 conversion  
3. **Protein / EAAs** with emphasis on leucine / BCAAs for anabolic resistance  
4. **Exercise** (resistance + walking / endurance — both support NAD)  
5. **Fortetropin** (fertilized egg yolk) to inhibit myostatin / mitigate anabolic resistance  
6. **Detox support** — phase I (mobilization) → reactive intermediate → phase II conjugation → elimination (bile, kidney, skin/sauna)

### Detox Caution
- Liberating toxins without conjugation/elimination can precede cancer diagnoses after massive loss (anecdotal clinical warning — coincidence vs causation unknown)
- Support gallbladder, kidneys, skin; rule out **sleep apnea** (case: lean polycystic kidney disease patient with nocturnal hypoxia worsening renal trajectory)
- Minimum endocrine panel framing: 4-point salivary (or urinary) cortisol, DHEA, free/total T, IGF-1/GH signals, full thyroid ± antibodies; HbA1c / cortisol dysregulation with weight loss

### NAD Depletors
- Viral illness (ex vivo: dramatic drop within ~25 h), overtraining, sun exposure, overnutrition/undernourishment, sedentary lifestyle, environmental load
- Finger-stick NAD testing available (True Niagen / contracted lab) to titrate rather than guess

## Segment 6 — Q&A: NR vs NAD, Cyclodextrin, Fortetropin, Cancer (1:19:29–1:50:47)

**Speakers:** Meletis, Clearfield, Halasa (Speaker 5), Nario (Speaker 4), panel

### IV NAD vs IV NR
- Extracellular NAD may be misread as cellular catastrophe → inflammatory response; cardiac sensitivity; must run slowly
- IV NR: shorter infusion, better tolerated in cited early work, converts to NAD; Meletis scheduled separate Coronagen talk (Jan 28) comparing IV NR vs IV NAD
- NMN: does not freely cross membrane; FDA has treated NMN as drug (supplement status contested)

### Cyclodextrin (Halasa)
- Cyclodextrin loading can enable endocytosis of large molecules (NAD/NMN/peptides/drugs)
- Also framed for clearing fat-soluble toxins / mycotoxins / microplastics / xenoestrogens
- Compounding / Strive pharmacy pipeline; book copies planned for Nevada conference

### Phase II Detox (chat question)
- Phase II = acetylation, glucuronidation, glutathione conjugation, methylation, etc. → water-solubilize for elimination
- Elevated stool β-glucuronidase → re-liberates estrogens/xenoestrogens; support flora + calcium-D-glucarate

### Fortetropin (Myos MD)
- Lyophilized fertilized egg yolk; patents; lowers myostatin, ↑ protein synthesis (~**18% in 21 days**, Berkeley / Evans investigator cited), ↓ anabolic resistance
- Canine disuse study: less atrophy post knee procedures; also bone signal (↓ myostatin → ↑ osteoblast / ↓ osteoclast activity)
- Meletis treats **presumptively** (rarely assays myostatin); contraindication: **egg allergy**; caution/avoid in active cancer (mTOR signal in one rodent/athlete study)
- Source: Myos MD (myosmd.com); Meletis email naturalmedman@hotmail

### NR Dosing
- Most RCTs at **1000 mg/day**; 300 mg PD study ~51% NAD rise; 1000 mg ~141% (supra-physiologic)
- Clinical practice: often start **500 mg** (“nudge not shove”); higher in Parkinson’s / Alzheimer’s / cardiomyopathy / serious kidney disease per risk–benefit
- True Niagen: most human trials; consumer 300 mg / professional 500 mg capsules common

### Cancer & Mitochondria
- Meletis cautious: mito support *could* fuel unwanted cells — medical-legal caution even for family cases
- Halasa counter: cancer suppresses mitochondria / mitophagy; activating mitochondria (polyphenols, methylene blue, etc.) can be anti-tumor — but cancer is multi-layered (fibrosis, immune polarization, etc.); single interventions underperform
- True Niagen SAB member (Brody / Feldon framed) reportedly uses NR support in oncology contexts — Meletis still conservative

### Exercise for NAD
- Both resistance and endurance/walking matter; not only HIIT
- Tryptophan→niacin is ~**60:1** then multi-step to NAD; polymorphisms (Meletis homozygous MTHFR 677T example) argue for precursor shortcuts (Brenner NR pathway)
