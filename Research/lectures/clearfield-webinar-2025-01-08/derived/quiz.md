### Q5. Classic euthyroid sick / non-thyroidal illness pattern under calorie stress includes:
- A) High free T3, low reverse T3, high TSH
- B) **Low free T3, elevated reverse T3, T4 and TSH low or normal** ✓
- C) Isolated elevated free T4 only
- D) Suppressed reverse T3 with high free T3

### Q6. Why does measuring only TSH + T4 miss the problem Meletis describes?
**Answer:** Patients can look “euthyroid” on TSH/T4 while free T3 is low and reverse T3 is high. Reverse T3 has essentially **0×** metabolic activity and competes with active T3 at mitochondrial targets, so energy, thermogenesis, and detox capacity fall.

### Q7. Roughly what fraction of hospitalized (or hospital-worthy) patients does Meletis say show euthyroid sick patterns?
- A) 10%
- B) 25%
- C) **~75%** ✓
- D) Nearly 100%

### Q8. True or False: Obesity-related myostatin elevation can impair irisin signaling and fat browning, helping explain weight regain.
**Answer:** **True.** High myostatin → lower irisin → less white-to-beige/brown conversion → weaker thermogenic response and easier regain.

## Section B — NAD, Mitochondria & Detox (6 questions)

### Q9. Between ages 30 and 70, NAD levels may fall by roughly:
- A) 10%
- B) 25%
- C) **Up to ~65%** ✓
- D) They typically rise

### Q10. Why does Meletis prefer oral nicotinamide riboside (NR) over NMN or intact NAD for membrane entry (physiologic framing)?
**Answer:** NAD and NMN are too large to freely cross the cell membrane and must be broken down; NR can use stress-inducible **NRK1/2** pathways, convert intracellularly toward NMN → NAD. (Halasa notes cyclodextrin may bypass this for large molecules.)

### Q11. In the twin NR study Meletis highlighted, whole-blood NAD increased about:
- A) 10%
- B) 50%
- C) **2.3-fold** ✓
- D) 10-fold

### Q12. Which older adults were the “exception” who retained better NAD in the aging metabolome discussion?
- A) Those on high-dose niacin alone with zero steps
- B) **Those already walking ~14,000 steps/day** ✓
- C) Only those on GLP-1 agonists
- D) Only bedbound patients

### Q13. Meletis’s mitochondrial weight-loss support stack explicitly includes which combination?
- A) Only caffeine and ephedra
- B) **NAD precursor (NR) + CoQ10 + PQQ + resveratrol**, plus calories/protein/exercise/Fortetropin/detox  
- C) Levothyroxine monotherapy only
- D) NMN IV exclusively

### Q14. Why must phase II detox keep pace when fat is lost quickly?
**Answer:** Phase I mobilizes toxins into more reactive intermediates; without conjugation (glutathione, methylation, glucuronidation, etc.) and elimination (bile, kidney, skin), lipophilic chemicals redistributed from adipose can increase systemic burden — Meletis warns of post–rapid-loss cancer diagnoses as a clinical caution (causation unproven).

## Section C — Fortetropin, Dosing & Clinical Judgment (6 questions)

### Q15. Fortetropin (Myos MD) is best described as:
- A) A synthetic follistatin peptide injection
- B) **Lyophilized fertilized egg yolk product that lowers myostatin and increases protein synthesis** ✓
- C) A pure leucine powder
- D) An IV NAD formulation

### Q16. What protein-synthesis result did Meletis cite from the Berkeley / Evans-related Fortetropin work?
- A) 2% in 6 months with diet overhaul
- B) **~18% increase within 21 days without diet/lifestyle change (older adults)** ✓
- C) No change in synthesis
- D) 50% decrease in myofibrillar synthesis

### Q17. Absolute contraindication Meletis stresses for Fortetropin:
- A) Mild osteoarthritis
- B) **Egg allergy** ✓
- C) History of walking 5,000 steps/day
- D) Taking vitamin D

### Q18. Why is Meletis cautious about Fortetropin in active cancer?
**Answer:** At least one rodent/young-athlete signal of **↑ mTOR**; with metastatic disease he treats it as a “no-fly zone” until clearer safety data — egg allergy is the other hard stop.

### Q19. Typical NR dosing discussion from Q&A:
- A) Always start at 2000 mg
- B) **Most trials use 1000 mg/day; Meletis often starts ~500 mg clinically; higher in selected neuro/cardiac/renal cases** ✓
- C) Only 50 mg is effective
- D) Oral NR never raises NAD

### Q20. Short answer: Name one reason IV NR may be preferred over IV NAD in Meletis’s comparison.
**Answer (any one):** Shorter infusion time / better tolerability / less cardiac sensitivity / less immune “hazmat” response to extracellular NAD / improved compliance for return visits — while still moving NAD biology in the desired direction.
