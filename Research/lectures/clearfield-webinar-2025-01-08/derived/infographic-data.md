## 🦋 Infographic 3: "Euthyroid Sick Under Calorie Stress"

**Title:** Why TSH + T4 Lie During Diets

```
Starvation / illness / inflammation
        │
        ├── ↓ Free T3  (active)
        ├── ↑ Reverse T3  (0× activity)
        └── TSH / T4 often "normal"
```

**Musical chairs:** T3 vs rT3 compete at mitochondrial DNA receptors → ETC + brown-fat thermogenesis suffer.

**Action:** Order free T3 + reverse T3 when patients plateau or crash-diet.

## 💉 Infographic 8: "NR vs NAD Delivery"

**Title:** Getting Building Blocks Inside the Cell

```
Oral / IV NR  →  NRK1/2 uptake  →  intracellular NMN  →  NAD
IV NAD        →  must break down; extracellular NAD may trigger "hazmat" immune response
NMN alone     →  membrane problem (unless delivery hack)
Cyclodextrin  →  Halasa: endocytosis loader for big molecules + toxin clearance angle
```

**Practical:** Start oral NR ~500 mg clinically; many trials at 1000 mg. IV NR often shorter/better tolerated than IV NAD (per Meletis).
