# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-01-08

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Rapid weight loss without a muscle plan is incomplete.** GLP-1s, bariatric surgery, and crash diets often produce sarcopenia plus liberated lipophilic toxins — “skinny” can still be metabolically fragile.

2. **Muscle is an endocrine organ.** Myokines/exerkines link muscle to liver, adipose, vessels, and brain; sarcopenia tracks with falls, mortality, and neurodegeneration risk.

3. **Expect ~3–5% muscle loss per decade after ~30** if unmanaged; lowest strength tercile after 60 ≈ **50% higher** all-cause mortality — train early, not only in geriatrics.

4. **Euthyroid sick physiology shows up in calorie cuts.** Pattern: ↓ free T3, ↑ reverse T3, TSH/T4 often normal — order free T3/rT3 when patients plateau or “starve to lose.”

5. **Obesity raises myostatin → lowers irisin → impairs fat browning**, which helps explain regain after dieting even when the scale briefly cooperates.

6. **NAD falls hard with age** (up to ~65% from 30→70) across ~500 dependent pathways; highly active seniors (~14k steps/day) were the protective exception in cited metabolomics.

7. **Prefer NR as the practical NAD precursor** when membrane biology matters; NMN/NAD need breakdown (or advanced delivery). Start ~500 mg clinically; many trials use 1000 mg.

8. **Mito stack for intentional loss:** NR + CoQ10 + PQQ + resveratrol + adequate calories/protein (leucine) + resistance/walking + detox + OSA screen.

9. **Fortetropin (Myos MD)** — lyophilized fertilized egg yolk — ↓ myostatin, ~18% ↑ protein synthesis in 21 days; avoid with egg allergy and use cancer caution (mTOR signal).

10. **IV NAD ≠ automatically better.** Extracellular NAD can provoke inflammatory/cardiac sensitivity; IV NR may be shorter, better tolerated, and still raise NAD — cyclodextrin is an emerging delivery/toxin-clearance tool (Halasa).

## Comparison: Scale-Only Loss vs Muscle–Mito Protocol

| Dimension | Scale-only rapid loss | Muscle–mito protocol (this webinar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary goal** | Lower weight fast | Preserve lean mass + cellular energy |
| **Thyroid lens** | TSH ± T4 only | Free T3 / reverse T3 under restriction |
| **Myostatin / irisin** | Ignored | Train ± Fortetropin to restore browning |
| **Protein** | Often inadequate | EAAs + leucine for anabolic resistance |
| **NAD / mito** | Rarely addressed | NR + CoQ10 / PQQ / resveratrol |
| **Detox** | Unplanned toxin dump | Phase I→II→III + elimination routes |
| **Oxygen** | Often missed | Screen sleep apnea even in lean patients |
| **Best for** | Short-term optics | Durable metabolism, strength, longevity |

**Consensus:** Use GLP-1s or calorie cuts if indicated — but never without muscle, mito, thyroid nuance, and detox support.
