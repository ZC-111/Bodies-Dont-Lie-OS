# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-05-19

## Top 10 Clinical / Teaching Pearls

1. **Ivermectin: calibrate, don’t evangelize.** Fascinating pathways (mTOR/AMPK/autophagy) and odd wins (BOHEMIA mosquito toxicity) exist — mammalian longevity and human outcome evidence still lag the hype.

2. **“Evidence indifferent” is the failure mode.** Stay open to mechanism while refusing to invent protocols from rotifers, cell culture, or animal Alzheimer models.

3. **Label milligrams ≠ systemic exposure.** Botanical performance lives in Cmax, AUC, half-life, and residence time — not the bottle claim alone.

4. **Ashwagandha QA PK headline:** ~**18× AUC**, ~**5.6× Cmax**, ~**4.1×** mean residence vs 2.5% withanolide standard (human crossover PK as presented).

5. **Berberine QA PK headline:** ~**18× Cmax**, ~**4× AUC** vs berberine HCl at equal 500 mg — classic poor-bioavailability molecule gets a second look.

6. **Curcumin QA PK headline:** ~**20× AUC**, ~**22× Cmax** vs 95% curcuminoids in a **fed** single-dose design (more real-world than fasted PK).

7. **Higher bioavailability demands dose humility.** Clearfield’s toxicity question is correct — down-titrate; long residence is the feature, not an excuse to keep old gram doses.

8. **Piperine is not a free lunch.** Raising botanical AUC by suppressing clearance/detox pathways may violate “do no harm”; resonance approaches claim a different mechanism (ordering without marker chemistry change).

9. **PK is layer one.** Clinical outcome trials (Nerve QA, Female QA) and microbiome/safety studies are still required before outcome superiority claims.

10. **Ayurveda bridge without cosplay.** Tri-dosha language maps to individual variability / systems biology; current QA work optimizes **herbs’ resonance**, not yet patient-specific dosha prescriptions.
