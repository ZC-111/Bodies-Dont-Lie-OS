# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-08-26

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Ozone’s job is electron movement** — O₃ vanishes in seconds; **lipid peroxides (~7–10 days)** carry the lasting biology.

2. **Dose defines poison** — inhaled ozone is toxic because lungs lack clearance; properly dosed medical ozone is a different category (Paracelsus).

3. **Add ozone to what you already do** — framed as a near–slam-dunk **potentiator** for meds, herbs, antibiotics, chemo, and chelation — not usually monotherapy ideology.

4. **NRF2 > megadose oral antioxidants alone** — vignette of advanced AIDS patients still oxidized on huge C/E/carotene until day-7 ozone revived enzyme systems.

5. **TH1 is the chronic-infection lever** — ozone called the “king” TH1 stimulant alongside vitamin D, melatonin, fish oil, mushrooms, quercetin, etc.

6. **Pain = failed local electron flux** — periarticular / soft-tissue ozone (or oxidized ascorbate) can abolish pain even when anatomy cannot be rebuilt.

7. **Never direct-IV gas** — oxygen emboli / PFO cerebral risk; every major ozone society opposes it. Use MAH / proper extracorporeal methods.

8. **Stack order: ozone → vitamin C → Myers** — never ascorbate in the bag before ozonation; oxidized C (dehydroascorbate) may cross BBB via GLUT1.

9. **G6PD + acute CVD are the hard stops** — screen G6PD before blood work; hold ozone in acute stroke/MI ~1 week; heparinize blood methods.

10. **Beta-carotene supplements + smoking = risk** — ATBC-era ~18–28% ↑ lung cancer signal; **ask about smoking before carotenoid maintenance plans**.
