### Q13. Absolute timing contraindication emphasized for systemic ozone:
**Answer:** **Acute stroke or acute MI** (and acute cardiovascular events generally) — use standard acute care first; consider ozone ~**1 week** later once stable / reperfusion risk settled.

### Q14. Correct order for same-day MAH + high-dose vitamin C:

- C) **Ozone / MAH first, then immediately piggyback vitamin C (and Myers after)** ✓

> *Preloading C into the ozonation bag antidotes the desired oxidant hit on blood cells.*

### Q15. Name three contraindications / cautions he listed for ozone.
**Answer (any three):**
- G6PD deficiency (check before blood ozone; mild lows → micro-doses)
- Acute stroke / acute MI
- Acute alcohol toxicity
- Never inhale
- Avoid direct IV gas
- (Pregnancy and iron-storage disease were **not** listed as contraindications in this talk)
