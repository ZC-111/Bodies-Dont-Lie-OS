## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Vitamin A & Beta-Carotene in Smokers (~14:50–18:49)

**Speaker:** Dr. Nario (Speaker 2)

### ★ Core Thesis
> Vitamin A is powerful for immunity, mucosa, vision, and gut repair — but **supplemental beta-carotene in smokers can raise lung cancer risk**. Never skip the social history.

### Two Forms of Vitamin A
| Type | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Preformed retinoids** | Retinyl palmitate, acetate, propionate | Animal/synthetic; already active; excess → toxicity |
| **Provitamin A carotenoids** | Beta-carotene, alpha-carotene, beta-cryptoxanthin | Plant-based; must convert to retinol; generally safer |

### Clinical Uses (integrative)
- Immune support; mucosal barriers (gut, lungs); T-cell support
- Vision / retinal health, night vision, dry eyes, macular support
- Skin / epithelium: wound healing, acne, eczema, psoriasis (oral or topical)
- Gut integrity (one of the “4 Rs” repair tools) — leaky gut / mucosal absorption

### Smoking Gun — ATBC Trial Context
- Male smokers (>5 cigarettes/day; often long smoking history) on **20 mg/day beta-carotene** → **~18–28% ↑ lung cancer risk** (1996 ATBC framing; follow-up literature through ~2013 for former smokers)
- Mechanism framed as **antioxidant flip**: smoke oxidant stress turns beta-carotene into a **pro-oxidant**
- **Food sources safer** than synthetic supplements; non-smokers generally OK / sometimes helped

### Clinical Pearl
> Do not skip social history. Maintenance carotenoid plans can backfire if smoking (or heavy past smoking) is missed.

### Adjunct Mention (Clearfield)
- Short high-dose A + D cold protocol discussed anecdotally (150k / 100k / 100k IU vitamin A over 3 days with tapering vitamin D) — **distinct from chronic beta-carotene maintenance**; Nario’s warning targets ongoing carotenoid supplementation in smokers

## Segment 3 — Frank Shallenberger: Ozone Therapy Foundations (21:54–~1:02:00)

**Speaker:** Frank Shallenberger, MD

### ★ Core Thesis
> Ozone is a controlled oxidant that **moves electrons**. Disease (especially chronic/degenerative) is marked by poor electron movement and mitochondrial failure. Dose defines poison vs medicine (Paracelsus).

### 3A. What Ozone Is
- O₃ = three oxygen atoms sharing electrons that stabilize two → unstable, electron-hungry oxidant
- Generator: electrical discharge splits O₂ briefly → ~**2–3% ozone / 97–98% oxygen** mix exits
- Nice clinical generator ~**$2,500** (Southern Star / ozonegenerator.com cited later)
- **All ozone does clinically is move an electron** — then ozone is gone in seconds

### 3B. Lipid Peroxides = The Real Effectors
- Ozone grabs electrons mainly from membrane PUFAs → creates **lipid peroxides**
- Peroxides persist **~7–10 days** in tissue (bladder example)
- Clinical “magic” attributed to peroxides, not lingering O₃ gas

### 3C. Electron Movement & Mitochondria
- Life = electron movement; aging/disease = slowed electron flux (noticed ~age 55; athletes notice earlier)
- Therapies work when they improve electron movement: oxidants **take** electrons; reductants (carotenoids, vitamin C) **give** electrons
- Bio-energy testing (Shallenberger patent): VO₂ / respiratory gas metrics → mitochondrial / biological age framing
- Optimal cytosolic NAD:NADH framed as ~**700:1** (oxidant-dominant milieu for cellular work)

### 3D. Dose Principle
- “Ozone is poison” arguments usually cite **inhaled / lung** toxicity literature
- Lungs have **no protective clearance for ozone** — **never inhale**
- Therapeutic dosing established over ~120 years (Tesla medical generators ~1904); taught in AAOT courses

### 3E. Delivery Routes (gas is versatile)
- Major autohemotherapy (MAH): ~200 mL blood + gas, shake, reinfuse — most common systemic route
- Local gas: joints, soft tissue, periosteum, CSF (done), dental/cavitation, bladder, vagina, rectum/colon → liver
- Ozone water (gas trapped like soda CO₂); ozonated olive oil (Tesla method) for skin/fungus/impetigo/bites
- Limb bagging for diabetic/varicose ulcers (high topical concentration → rapid microbial kill; **no known microbial resistance**)
- **Never** direct IV gas injection (Academy + international societies oppose) — oxygen emboli / PFO risk

### 3F. Systemic Mechanisms Cited
1. Endothelium → ↑ nitric oxide + heme oxygenase-1 → circulation
2. Powerful **NRF2** stimulation → endogenous antioxidant enzymes (case series: AIDS patients on massive oral antioxidants still oxidized until day-7 ozone revived redox)
3. **TH1 immune stimulation** — “king” among TH1 tools (alongside vitamin D, melatonin, fish oil, medicinal mushrooms, cannabis, quercetin, echinacea)
4. Mitochondrial resuscitation via peroxide-driven NADH → NAD
5. Pain relief via restored local electron movement (prolozone lineage from mid-1980s Germany)

### Clinical Pearl
> Add ozone to whatever you already do — medications, herbs, antibiotics, chemo, chelation — as a **potentiator**, not usually a standalone religion.

## Segment 4 — Clinical Applications Walkthrough (~1:02:00–1:15:42)

**Speaker:** Frank Shallenberger

### Viral Illness
- Franzini (Italy): 50 frail COVID patients >75 on respiratory support — standard care alone ~high mortality vs **2/50 deaths (~4%)** with added ozone (vs ~85% US comparator mortality cited)
- Clinic vignette: obese diabetic (A1c ~12), prior stents, RR 34, SpO₂ 82 → high-dose C (~50 g), ~40 mg ozone into blood, IM vitamin D ~500,000 IU × 2–3 days → out of danger ~3 days
- Claimed universal viral response in his practice (influenza, Hanta, West Nile, bird flu, viral meningitis) when ozone ± methylene blue ± vitamin C used aggressively
- Shingles: three consecutive large ozone doses → rapid resolution vignette
- Colleagues’ Sierra Leone Ebola ozone papers referenced

### Bacterial / Soft Tissue
- Post–rotator cuff revision with arm cellulitis facing amputation: daily joint/subQ ozone + MAH + continued IV antibiotics → limb saved (potentiation framing)

### Autoimmune / CVD / Cancer / Diabetes
- Autoimmune framed as often virus-driven → TH1 + antimicrobial oxidant strategy
- CVD / Buerger’s / CAD: “slam dunk” circulating/endothelial support
- Oncology: potentiates chemo, may ↓ resistance; NRF2 protects healthy cells → fewer chemo symptoms; radiation vignette (Fred, tonsillar SCC) — ozone + C concurrent with XRT → oncologist amazed at lack of xerostomia/dysphagia/tooth loss
- Diabetes complications (neuropathy, retinopathy, fatty liver, CKD): eGFR recovery vignette if treated before eGFR ≪ 20; occasional dialysis-sparing anecdotes

### Pain / Prolozone Framing
- Degenerative joints improve via regeneration **and** direct analgesia
- Thesis: **pain = local electron-movement failure**; ozone or oxidized ascorbic acid into the tender point can abolish pain even when anatomy cannot be “fixed” (multi-level lumbar hardware example)

## Segment 5 — Q&A: Safety, Consent, Prostate, Biofilm, Combos (1:15:42–~2:00+)

**Speakers:** Shallenberger, Clearfield, Nario, panel

### Malpractice / FDA / Consent
- FDA “no medical use” line arose in **ambient inhaled ozone** environmental language — not a fair clinical summary
- AAOT library ~**4,200–4,300** published papers searchable by condition (aot.us)
- Academy does **not** issue official consent forms (liability); use practice-attorney consents (~30 forms in his office)

### Stroke / Acute CVD / Anticoagulation
- Blood ozone requires anticoagulation: **heparin** (his default) or citrate/phosphate dextrose–type solutions
- **Contraindicated in acute stroke / acute MI** — wait ~1 week until reperfusion risk settles, then ozone helps recovery
- Bubbling/oxygen in *proper* MAH ≠ stroke risk; **direct IV gas** is the dangerous pattern

### Oxidized Vitamin C (Dehydroascorbate)
- Ascorbate needs oxidation → dehydroascorbate to use **GLUT1** (vs SVCT for reduced C) → BBB / mitochondrial access
- Hemorrhagic-stroke literature on oxidized C stopping bleed if early
- Clinic trick: ~50 g vitamin C bag + ~40 mg ozone into bag → run oxidized C IV
- **Order rule:** ozone (MAH) **first**, then immediately piggyback vitamin C / Myers — **never** put C in the blood bag before ozonation (antidotes the oxidant hit)
- Stack he uses: ozone → vitamin C → Myers; hesitant with concurrent lipoic acid

### Prostate Delivery
- Midline perineal point (scrotum–anus midpoint), lithotomy-like position
- 25G × 1.5″ needle parallel to table → tip ~⅛″ from capsule (**do not pierce prostate**)
- ~3 mL 1% **procaine**, then **20 mL ozone @ 20 γ** peri-prostatic
- Strong for prostatitis; adjunct for BPH / selected prostate cancer; 150 g prostate hematuria case shrunk over ~3 months BIW without surgery

### Biofilms / IC / Spine & Knees
- Interstitial cystitis: repeated bladder ozone gas via catheter → ~**85–90%** success in his series — biofilm hypothesis
- Low back / Modic changes: infectious drivers (mycoplasma etc.); large-volume paravertebral gas (~240 mL) + pain effect
- Cited antibiotic trial (~half of Modic/herniation cohort cured in 6 months) — residual failures attributed to biofilm
- Knees: occult colonizers under biofilm (mycoplasma / Neisseria / strep framed) → cytokine persistence

### Other Combos
- **DMSO + ozone:** compatible / mutually potentiating; he rarely uses DMSO (odor)
- Chlorine dioxide / H₂O₂: valid oxidants but narrower routes & weaker evidence base vs ozone’s ~4–5k papers
- **Keyzone:** MAH then immediately piggyback **EDTA chelation** — preferred CVD stack; mutual potentiation claim

### Contraindications (stated)
1. **G6PD deficiency** (hemolysis / renal risk) — check before blood ozone; mild low → start tiny doses (ozone can induct G6PD)
2. **Acute CVD events** (stroke/MI)
3. Acute **alcohol toxicity**
4. Pregnancy / iron-storage disease: **not** listed as contraindications in his talk

### EBU & Devices
- **EBU** (extracorporeal blood ozonation + filtration): dual-arm large-bore circuit ~1 hour; filter catches biofilms/clots/bacteria/WBC; may capture spike protein (case mentioned); prefers over multipass as potential game-changer
- Learn from **Brendan Cochran, ND** (AAOT faculty); **buy only ozone-resistant filter systems** (microplastic risk with cheap units)
- Cost contrast: EBU ~$30k, multipass ~$17k, ozone sauna ~$8k, solid Southern Star generator ~$2,500
