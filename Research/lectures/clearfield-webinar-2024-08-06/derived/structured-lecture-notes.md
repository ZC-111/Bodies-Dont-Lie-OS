## Segment 2 — Ross Pelton: Gut Microbiome Ecosystem & Oral Probiotic Dilemma (14:23–34:00)

**Speaker:** Ross Pelton, RPh

### ★ Core Thesis
> **It is not the probiotic bacteria themselves that regulate health — it is the postbiotic metabolites they produce from dietary fibers and polyphenols.**

### Background & Framing
- Career: pharmacy → anti-aging (~40 yrs) → last ~12 yrs primary focus on human gut microbiome
- Books/papers cited:
  - *Drug-Induced Nutrient Depletion Handbook* (1999/2001) — OCPs deplete more nutrients than any other drug class → *The Pill Problem*
  - Free DIND quick reference: naturalpharmacist.net/dind
  - *Postbiotic Metabolites: The New Frontier in Microbiome Science* (June 2019) — first paper with “postbiotic metabolites” in the title
  - *The Microbiome Theory of Aging* (Integrative Medicine, Jan 2023)

### Anatomy & Immune Geography
- **>99.9%** of GI bacteria reside in the **colon** (primary clinical target)
- **>70%** of immune cells live in the GI tract → dysbiosis rapidly impairs immunity
- Healthy mix framed as ~**85–90%** beneficial / **10–15%** potentially pathogenic (pathogens only problematic when overgrown)

### Dysbiosis Drivers
- Environmental toxins, antibiotic overuse, poor diet
- Pathogen metabolic products = highly inflammatory

### The Oral Probiotic Dilemma (3 parts)
1. **Stomach acid** — 10,000–100,000× more acidic than small intestine/colon; kills most oral probiotics (enteric coats / spores help survival but not guarantee effect)
2. **Small intestine** — bile acids + pancreatic enzymes destroy bacterial membranes
3. **Diet failure** — fibers + polyphenols are the primary “food” for probiotic bacteria; almost no one eats enough diversity

### Population Diet Failure Stats (compiled from cited studies)
- **90–95%** of US children/adults miss fiber RDA
- **80–91%** fall short across phytonutrient/polyphenol color categories
- **73%** of US food supply is ultra-processed (*Nature Food*); ultra-processed foods ~**52%** cheaper than healthy alternatives
- Americans spent ~**$17.4B** (2022) on oral probiotics — largely wasted per Pelton

### Ecosystem Analogies
- Amazon rainforest / auto plant: bacteria = workers; fibers/polyphenols = parts; high-dose mono-strain probiotics = flooding plant with windshield-wiper specialists → **hurts balance & diversity**
- High-dose CFU marketing (50B / 100B / 200B) can provoke immune “alarm” reaction — not because strains are “bad,” but because excess of few strains imbalances the ecosystem
- Optimal colonic pH **6.2–6.8** (weakly acidic); maintained by postbiotic acids (SCFAs, organic/nucleic/amino/fulvic acids)

## Segment 3 — Japanese Multi-Year Fermentation & Diet Diversity (34:00–42:50)

**Speaker:** Ross Pelton

### Manufacturing Model (Dr. Ohira–lineage process)
- Southern Japan sterile facility; ~**80-gallon** aerobic fermentation vats + mammoth **~10,000-gallon** anaerobic vessels
- Starter: **12 probiotic strains** + Japanese fruits/vegetables/mushrooms/seaweeds (diverse fibers + polyphenols) + spring water
- **~3 years** natural fermentation → **>500 postbiotic metabolites**
- Additional **~2 years** anaerobic fermentation (mirrors anaerobic colon environment)
- Encapsulated product: mostly postbiotics, some residual probiotics + prebiotic residual from fermentation; Fuji patent capsule stays hard in acid, dissolves in alkaline SI

### Salad Buzz Protocol (practical diet diversity)
- Pre-chop ~**16** fiber/polyphenol vegetables weekly; lemon juice (Vit C) keeps mix fresh ~1 week in fridge
- Daily: handful lettuce + handful salad buzz ± protein (wild salmon / garbanzo) — ~2 minutes
- YouTube: search “Ross salad buzz” (~8 min); also in weekly Clearfield email

### Categories of Postbiotic Metabolites
- B vitamins, vitamin K, folate
- Essential amino acids
- SCFAs (esp. **butyrate**)
- Immune regulators; antimicrobial peptides (**bacteriocins**, **defensins**) — kill pathogens without harming commensals
- Neurotransmitters (**~95% of serotonin** made in gut)
- Antioxidant / cell-signaling compounds

### Clinical Actions of Postbiotics
- Anti-inflammatory activity in inflamed colon
- Pathogen kill via antimicrobial peptides
- Accelerate epithelial renewal (healthy turnover every **6–10 days**; ~**10%** of daily energy expenditure)
- Butyrate = primary fuel for colonocytes
- Restore acid–base balance; support intestinal barrier; detox support; rapid GI symptom improvement
- **Proposal:** recognize bacteria-derived postbiotics as a class of **essential nutrients**

### Antibiotic Resistance Context
- Bacteria double ~every **20 min** → ~**72** doublings/day → ~**504** doublings over a 7-day antibiotic course
- WHO ranks antibiotic resistance a global crisis → proactive microbiome health reduces antibiotic need

## Segment 4 — Barrier Science, LPS Study & Clinical Evidence (42:50–1:09:16)

**Speaker:** Ross Pelton (+ Harvard mega-plate video)

### Mega-Plate Video (Harvard)
- Visual of E. coli evolving across antibiotic concentration bands to **1000×** wild-type resistance in ~11 days via successive mutations
- Teaching point: be proactive about microbiome health so antibiotics are needed less often — **not** an instruction to stop indicated antibiotics

### Zonulin / Leaky Gut / LPS
- Alessio Fasano / zonulin: inflammation → zonulin → degradation of tight-junction “Velcro” proteins → paracellular leak
- Lipopolysaccharides (LPS) among most inflammatory substances known

### Landmark LPS Human Model Study
- Double-blind placebo crossover: low-dose LPS IV in healthy volunteers (36h inpatient ×2 with washout)
- **No clinical symptoms** at the low dose used
- Bloodwork: **~25× ↑ TNF-α** and **~100× ↑ IL-6** → asymptomatic LPS leak still drives systemic inflammation / accelerates biological aging

### Why Oral Probiotics Fail Damaged Ecosystems
- Replanting animals into a destroyed Amazon does not regrow the forest
- Even if bacteria survive transit, they still need fibers/polyphenols — which 80–95% of people lack
- Brian Mayer (GI/microbiome scientist): CFU mega-dose “shortcut” will not do the job
- Direct postbiotic delivery → immediate anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, acid–base, and epithelial-repair signaling

### Evidence Snapshots Cited
| Study / model | Outcome highlighted |
|---|---|
| Vitamin C knockout mice | Postbiotic prep restored microbiome/ATP; higher ATP than wild-type despite no Vit C synthesis |
| Constipation (female college students) | 3 caps/day × 2 weeks → all chronic-constipation subjects normalized stools (adaptogenic bowel effect) |
| Australian distance runners | After 3 caps/day: **+8.4% hemoglobin**, ↑ VO₂ / lung function, ↑ lactate threshold, ↑ cardiac output |
| Japan bone density (ages 20–60, 24 mo) | Significant ↑ bone mineral content & density (dose 3–10 caps by body size); theory: weak acids + B vitamins aid mineral absorption |
| Fatigue RCT (healthy adults, 12 wk) | Significant ↓ fatigue on VAS + chronic fatigue score — bigger bang expected in already-ill patients |
| Mouse swim-to-exhaustion | Postbiotic group swam **~2×** longer than placebo |

### Practical Protocols Mentioned
- **During antibiotics:** take postbiotics, but not in stomach at same time as antibiotic dose (~few hours apart)
- **After antibiotics:** double/triple dose **10–14 days**
- **Pediatrics:** when child can swallow capsule; **C-section infants:** bite capsule, rub paste inside mouth (no vaginal microbiome exposure)
- **Oral/dental:** bite, swish ~20 sec at bedtime for oral microbiome; anecdotes of abscess / MRSA topical paste (cannot make public claims)
- **Trial box:** start **2 capsules BID × ~1 week** (30-count ≈ 1 week at that dose); US attendees promised sample kits

### Origin Story (Shiro Ohira)
- Landscape architect → shaman-fermented Malaysian fruit paste cured acute food illness (1980) → abandoned career at 49 for PhD microbiology → ~10 years developing multi-year fermentation → claimed #1 direct-delivery postbiotic product globally

### Closing Framing
- 2022 lit: probiotic benefits typically from **metabolites/secreted compounds**, not live microbes per se
- Cleveland Clinic 2016 top-10 medical innovations: **#1 = gut microbiome to prevent/diagnose/treat disease**
- 2021 lit: depletion of beneficial microbial metabolites linked to rise in NCDs
- “Epidemic of epidemics” (CVD, cancer, diabetes, obesity, autism, Alzheimer’s, IBD) tied to gut failure; Hippocrates framing: all disease begins in the gut

## Segment 5 — Clinical Q&A: Fulvic Acids, Candida, SIBO, FMT, H₂O₂ (1:09:16–1:29:00)

**Speakers:** Ross Pelton, Bill Clearfield, panel

### Fulvic / Humic Acids
- Largest single ingredient class in multi-year ferment (per Dr. Muneki Takahata, Vancouver 2017 scientific meeting)
- Fulvic acids smaller than humic; **12–15 mineral binding sites/molecule**; nano-size → easy membrane transport
- Soil bacteria normally produce fulvic acids → mineral delivery into plant roots; pesticides kill soil bacteria → weaker plants → more pesticide cycle
- Multi-year ferment gives bacteria time humans don’t have to generate fulvic acids in food matrix

### Candida / Thrush
- Strain **Enterococcus faecalis TH-10** (discovered by Ohira in Malaysian tempeh) produces **phenyllactic acid**
- 1% phenyllactic acid cell-culture data: membrane disruption of E. coli, Candida, MRSA, etc.
- TH-10 spiked into final batch before encapsulation
- Practical: chew/swish for oral Candida, then swallow; no formal treatment claims; also antifungal activity noted
- Food poisoning: bite **10–15 capsules**, squeeze paste into stomach (capsule otherwise bypasses stomach)

### Akkermansia (oral)
- Good studies exist; Pelton wants mechanism clarified — live bacterium vs its postbiotic metabolites
- Same transit/diet caveats as other oral probiotics

### Gerson Coffee Enemas
- Pelton consulted for Gerson (San Diego / Mexico clinic)
- Caffeine via rectal hemorrhoidal vein → liver → rapid toxin dump; also **flushes distal microbiome**
- Short-term detox useful; long-term not ideal for microbiome; fiber loss from juice protocols is a gut concern

### Leaky Gut / Brain Dose
- Marketed in Philippines; optimal dose/duration for barrier healing **unknown** (biochemical individuality)
- Mechanism known: ↓ inflammation, clear pathobionts, restore butyrate/epithelial fuel

### SIBO
- Colon bacteria relocated to distal SI (“wrong housing development”) — not always “bad bugs”
- Most SIBO patients **cannot tolerate live probiotics** (gas/bloating from SI fermentation)
- FODMAP / SCD: short-term only; ~**20%** may resolve on diet alone; many need **rifaximin** then rebuild microbiome + lifestyle
- Postbiotic ferment usually better tolerated (low live CFU, acid-stable metabolites)
- **Titration:** 1 capsule QOD → 1 daily → standard 2/day if tolerated

### FMT
- Fastest way to rewrite severe dysbiosis; certified donor programs → often capsules; homebrew exists but not promoted
- Dramatic clinical turnarounds described (malabsorption / extreme weight loss cases)

### Keystone Species
- **E. faecalis TH-10** candidate keystone
- **Lactobacillus fermentum ME-3** synthesizes **glutathione** (postbiotic) — strongest oral GSH-boosting strategy Pelton knows
- Concept accepted; exact keystone list still incomplete

### Food-Grade Hydrogen Peroxide
- Pelton historically marketed **35% food-grade H₂O₂**; now hard to source → uses **15%**
- Prior experience: IV low-dose H₂O₂ every other day in Mexican non-toxic cancer hospital
- Current home use: dilute (distilled water) to wash produce / occasional gargle; **extremely caustic — extreme caution**
- Nario: also uses clinically for GI + systemic infection support

## Segment 6 — Extended Q&A: Custom Probiotics, Delivery, Diet Habit, Rapamycin (1:29:00–2:03:24)

**Speakers:** Ross Pelton, Melvin Nario, Bill Clearfield, panel

### Customized / Personalized Oral Probiotics (IFM booth company)
- Pelton: customization doesn’t fix the oral probiotic dilemma; prioritize matching **fibers/polyphenols to desired strains**

### Pro + Pre + Post Combined
- Can combine; oral live probiotics still unlikely to survive; prebiotics + postbiotics will
- Diversity of **foods** (not CFU bottles) builds bacterial diversity
- Heat-killed / lysed probiotics release intracellular compounds (debated whether true “postbiotics”)
- Spore probiotics survive acid but still need fiber/polyphenol substrates in colon

### Product Timing
- Patent capsule: **any time**, with/without food
- ME-3 glutathione strain: Pelton takes at night (theory: liver detox during sleep) — no hard evidence for timing

### Rectal / Vaginal Delivery
- Rectal probiotics after bowel movement can colonize distal colon; some insert Ohira-type capsules rectally or vaginally

### Habitual Same-Food Diet
- Habit of meat + potatoes + one broccoli serving ≠ diversity
- Goal: **little bits of many** plants daily (10–15 types), not one large serving of one vegetable

### High-Dose Probiotic Immune Alarm
- Immigration analogy: healthy diversity of few arrivals OK; flooding with 50–100B of few strains overburdens systems

### Antibiotics Duration vs Resistance Video
- Clarification: do **not** skip indicated antibiotic courses; video motivates prevention via microbiome health

### Polyphenol Capsules
- Quercetin etc. marketed; many polyphenols are huge molecules humans don’t digest → reach colon as bacterial food (with fibers)
- >8000 structurally identified polyphenols; bacterial **CAZymes** (carbohydrate-active enzymes) enable strain-specific metabolism → distinct postbiotics

### Gas / Reflux Tools
- Simethicone helpful for drug-related gas
- Preferred product: **RX Vitamins Acid Block** — DGL + sodium alginate wafers
  - DGL coats esophagus; alginate forms floating foam barrier vs reflux
  - Some take 6–8×/day; bedtime dose common for nocturnal reflux
- Bonus (Clearfield): DGL is aromatase inhibitor → may raise T / help control E

### Other Mentions
- **Saccharomyces boulardii** — useful yeast (not bacteria); benefits acknowledged
- Frequency-specific microcurrent for GI — unknown to Pelton; Clearfield to share materials
- Vaginal birth: infant covered in maternal vaginal mucus → swallows → seeds gut; C-section misses this (links to earlier infant paste tip)

### Rapamycin Mini-Protocol (follow-up from prior lecture)
- Most users (~**85–90%**): **5–6 mg once weekly** (Mannick elderly study: ~**20%** immune function boost at 5 mg/wk)
- Pelton experimented **12 mg every 2 weeks × ~6 months** (theory: higher peak autophagy) then returned to weekly
- Core rationale: modern constant feeding → chronic mTOR on / autophagy off; historically humans spent most of day fasting → autophagy dominant
- Autophagy = remove damaged “flat tire” before installing new parts; rapamycin partially inhibits mTOR → pharmacologic autophagy assist
- Muscle paradox: continuous mTOR without autophagy = Formula 1 without pit stops; need breakdown/rebuild cycles
- Expectation management: day-to-day “feel” often minimal (like Vit D); possible visceral fat loss; benefits = long-term cellular optimization, not acute stimulant effect
