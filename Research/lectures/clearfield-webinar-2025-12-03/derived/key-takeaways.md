# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-12-03

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Coimbra Protocol ≠ Coimbra Group.** The therapy is Dr. Cicero Coimbra’s high-dose vitamin D3 protocol for autoimmunity (São Paulo) — not the European academic consortium.

2. **Dose by PTH, not by 25-OH-D.** 25-OH-D rises even with receptor resistance. PTH suppression into the lower third of the reference range (~10–13 pg/mL sweet spot; keep detectable) marks biological effect.

3. **Guardrails are the protocol.** ≥2.5 L fluid/day, calcium <500 mg/day with no cheat days, magnesium + riboflavin, and serial labs are mandatory — not optional add-ons.

4. **Starting framework ~1,000 IU/kg/day** for many autoimmune cases (range cited 40,000–300,000 IU/day); Hashimoto’s often lower (150–300 IU/kg/day). Vitiligo literature often starts at 35,000 IU/day.

5. **Expected 25-OH-D on therapy is often 150–200+ ng/mL.** Do not panic-titrate down solely because levels exceed conventional “optimal” 50–80 if PTH/Ca/renal are appropriate.

6. **24-h urinary calcium is the most sensitive pause switch.** >250 mg/L → hold vitamin D 3 days, restart at half dose; <250 mg/L → may continue.

7. **Toxicity is real and can be fatal.** Clearfield’s unsupervised mega-dose case (Ca ~15, seizures, arrhythmia, death) is the cautionary frame for every informed consent.

8. **Never DIY and never abandon DMARDs casually.** OTC vitamin D mega-dosing and partial adherence (skipping Mg/diet/fluids) drive nephrocalcinosis, stones, and arrhythmia risk.

9. **Patient selection beats protocol enthusiasm.** Reserve for severe/refractory autoimmune disease with proven compliance capacity — not mild asymptomatic antibody positivity.

10. **Adjuncts still matter.** K2 helps steer calcium; treat comorbidities; acupuncture/homeopathy (Vargas) and acupuncture-point injection technique (Clearfield) were discussed as complementary tools — especially for vitiligo.

## Protocol Snapshot: Monitoring Targets

| Domain | Target / action |
|---|---|
| **PTH** | Lower third of lab range; ≥30–40% drop from baseline; never zero |
| **25-OH-D** | Often 150–200+ — informational, not primary titration |
| **Diet Ca** | <500 mg/day |
| **Fluids** | ≥2.5 L/day |
| **Urine Ca (24h)** | <250 mg/L continue; >250 hold 3d → ½ dose |
| **Imaging** | DEXA; annual kidney US if long-term |
| **Evidence posture** | Pilot/observational promise; no long-term powered RCTs |

**Consensus from the room:** Fascinating and potentially useful — but insurance, liability, irreversible kidney risk, and compliance make this a high-bar, supervised adjunct only.
