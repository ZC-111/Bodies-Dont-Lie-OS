## 📉 Infographic 3: "Dose by PTH, Not by 25-OH-D"

**Title:** The Therapeutic Compass

| Marker | What it tells you | Protocol use |
|---|---|---|
| **25-OH-D** | Substrate in blood | Will rise anyway (often 150–200+) — **not** the titration target |
| **PTH** | Functional receptor effect | Suppress to **lower third** of lab range (~10–13 pg/mL sweet spot; keep detectable) |

**Rules:**
- Drop PTH ≥30–40% from baseline
- Never drive PTH to **zero**
- Recheck ~every 4 weeks while titrating

## 🚫 Infographic 5: "The Non-Negotiables"

**Title:** Three Rules That Prevent Kidney Disaster

```
1. FLUIDS     ≥ 2.5 liters / day (mandatory)
2. CALCIUM    < 500 mg / day diet — NO cheat days
3. SUPERVISION  Serial labs + informed consent — never OTC DIY
```

**Avoid:** dairy, fortified plant milks, nuts/seeds, canned fish with bones, Ca antacids

## 🧪 Infographic 6: "Cofactor Stack (Minimum Viable)"

**Title:** D Alone Is Not the Protocol

| Must-have | Role |
|---|---|
| Magnesium (glycinate) | Hydroxylase cofactor; check **RBC Mg** |
| Riboflavin B2 50–200 mg | Immune + ↓ stone risk |
| Omega-3 2–4 g | Membrane / anti-inflammatory |
| Vitamin K2 100–400 mcg | Steer Ca to bone (≠ Ca-diet substitute) |

**Also consider:** methylated Bs, selenium 200 mcg, zinc 15–30 mg, iron if deficient  
**Bowles note:** some push Mg to 1,000–1,500 mg elemental + boron/B5

## 🚨 Infographic 7: "Safety Tripwires"

**Title:** When to Pause — and What Can Go Wrong

**24-h urinary calcium**
- **<250 mg/L** → continue
- **>250 mg/L** → stop D × 3 days → restart at **½ dose**

**Catastrophic risks**
1. Nephrocalcinosis (often irreversible)
2. Kidney stones / obstruction
3. Arrhythmia / seizures / hypercalcemia death (Clearfield “Tom” case ~150k IU/day unsupervised)

**Baseline must-haves:** total + ionized Ca, PTH, renal panel (± cystatin C), consider genetics (e.g., MEN1 case lesson)

## 📊 Infographic 8: "Evidence Snapshot (Honest)"

**Title:** Promising Pilots — Not Pharma RCTs

| Signal | Detail |
|---|---|
| Vitiligo / psoriasis (Cinnamoré 2013) | 35k IU × 6 mo; **>80%** significant improvement; no toxicity under protocol |
| Observational autoimmune series | Mean ~35k IU (MS ~53k); Ca/renal/urine Ca stayed in range with strict diet/fluids |
| VDR genetics subgroup | ~**75%** carried multiple relevant mutations |
| MS Society | Does **not** endorse these doses |
| Long-term RCTs | **Missing** — durability, bone, CV, cancer unknowns |

**Positioning:** Adjunct for selected severe/refractory patients — not a replacement for DMARDs.
