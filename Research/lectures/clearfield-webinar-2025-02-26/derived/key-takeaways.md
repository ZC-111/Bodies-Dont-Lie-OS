# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-02-26

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Treat the endocannabinoid system as real physiology** — receptors, ligands (anandamide/2-AG/PEA), and enzymes (FAAH). It is not “just getting high.”

2. **CBD is psychoactive but not intoxicating**; THC drives intoxication. “CBD has no side effects” is false — watch sedation, GI effects, and drug interactions.

3. **Balanced THC:CBD ratios beat mega-THC marketing.** Classic research plants were ~1:1; modern recreational hybridization skewed THC upward.

4. **Entourage ≠ orchestral.** Entourage = plant matrix synergy. Orchestral = add nutraceuticals/pharma (theanine, melatonin, curcumin, PEA, BioPerine) for targeted outcomes.

5. **For anxiety: CBD-dominant, THC-sparing.** High-THC alone is often a temporary mask that can worsen baseline anxiety.

6. **Cancer counseling must be precise:** THC can kill cancer cells; CBD mainly slows growth. Always ask *what is in the product* — like asking which antihypertensive class.

7. **Demand a third-party COA** covering contaminants, full cannabinoid totals (not only Δ9), and terpenes. Sum heavy metals; don’t trust single-metal “pass” theater.

8. **Oral Δ9 → 11-hydroxy-THC** (~3–4× potency and duration). Counsel against early redosing; useful for sleep/pain, dangerous for edibles novices.

9. **Drug interaction vigilance is mandatory** — especially anticoagulants, CNS depressants, SSRIs, antipsychotics in youth, stimulants, and opioids (where cannabinoids may allow dose reduction).

10. **VCO tip:** virgin ≠ refined; may raise HDL *and* LDL. Interpret lipids by particle subclass; don’t statin-chase large buoyant LDL blindly.

## Comparison: Isolate CBD vs Full-Spectrum Strategy

| Dimension | CBD isolate (e.g., Epidiolex-style) | Full-spectrum / orchestrated product |
|---|---|---|
| **Composition** | Single molecule | Cannabinoids + terpenes ± added agents |
| **Dose needed** | Often very high (epilepsy example) | Often lower via synergy |
| **Anxiety** | Useful | Better with calming terpenes; low THC |
| **Cancer cytotoxicity** | Growth inhibition mainly | THC adds kill potential (adjunct only) |
| **Legal/hemp optics** | Easier <0.3% THC story | Must track total THC carefully |
| **Patient failure mode** | “CBD did nothing” after junk isolate | Wrong ratios / dirty COA / bad extraction |
| **Best for** | Narrow regulated indications | Personalized integrative adjuncts |

**Consensus:** Prefer matrix literacy over isolate hype; personalize ratios; never abandon standards of cancer/epilepsy/pain care.
