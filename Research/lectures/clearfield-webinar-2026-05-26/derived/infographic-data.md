## Infographic 1: "What Morgellons Is (and Isn't)"

**Title:** Dermopathy, Not Delusion-by-Default

```
IS                              ISN'T
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Filaments in / from skin        Dust, cat hair, couch lint
Associated with Borrelia ±      “The infection itself”
  other pathogens               Automatic DSM primary delusion
Peer-reviewed lesion science    Substack synthetic-biology lore

```

**Footer:** Keep the definition tight — embedded cutaneous filaments. Orifice “worms/poop” ≠ Morgellons per group evidence rules.

## Infographic 2: "Microscope Diagnostic Ladder"

**Title:** Why 10× Misses the Diagnosis

| Tool | Typical mag | Morgellons fibers? |
|---|---|---|
| Naked eye | 1× | Usually invisible |
| Dermatoscope | ~10× | Often insufficient |
| Phone macro + USB scope | **≥60×** | Characteristic filaments visible |
| In-lesion observation | ≥60× in tissue | Differentiates embedded vs surface artifact |

**Callout:** Scraping alone can harvest environmental debris. Confirm **in-tissue** origin.

## Infographic 4: "Serology Trap Map"

**Title:** Why Tests Fail

| Trap | What happens | Clinical risk |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-reactivity | Syphilis → false ⊕ Lyme (± blot) | Lyme label; syphilis missed |
| Prozone | Ab flood → false ⊖ nontreponemal | False security |
| Late Ab fade | Chronic infection, weak serology | False ⊖ screens |
| Lifelong treponemal ⊕ | Exposure ≠ activity | Over/under-treatment confusion |
| L-forms / cysts | Wall antigens shed | Standard assays blind |

**Callout:** Negative RPR ≠ “cannot be syphilis” (antenatal aortic-rupture case).

## Infographic 6: "2023–2025 Syphilis Reality Check"

**Title:** Standard of Care Is Fraying

| Finding (as presented) | Number |
|---|---|
| Early syphilis failing 4× titer drop @ 12 mo after penicillin | **10–20%** |
| Contemporary isolates with penicillin resistance signal | **~61%** |
| SS14 subset resistance (cited) | **~74%** |
| Secondary syphilis with intense pruritus (1980 n=105) | **~42%** |

**Action:** Higher clinical suspicion + better direct detection — do not equate “got a shot” with cure in every patient.
