# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-05-26

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Morgellons = dermopathy with tissue-embedded filaments**, associated with Borrelia (± *H. pylori*, *T. denticola*) — not synonymous with the infection itself and not automatically a primary delusional disorder.

2. **Diagnose with ≥60× microscopy in the lesion.** Dermoscopes (~10×) often miss fibers; surface scrapings can harvest environmental artifact.

3. **CDC 2012 abstract ≠ data.** Cotton/cellulose headline conflicts with majority-protein fiber findings on a tiny biopsied subset; syphilis serology was never done.

4. **Syphilis can false-positive Lyme tests (including Western blot); Lyme does not false-positive syphilis.** Cross-reactivity + stigma may “launder” some cases into a Lyme-only pathway.

5. **Negative nontreponemal serology does not exclude life-threatening syphilis** (antenatal mother, repeatedly negative RPR, fatal aortic rupture — diagnosis by IHC).

6. **Prozone, late Ab fade, L-forms/cysts, and emerging penicillin resistance (~61% isolates cited)** explain persistent disease after “adequate” short courses.

7. **Only ~6% of Lyme cohorts present with Morgellons** in cited series — real but not universal; keep diagnostic criteria tight.

8. **ILADS-capable antimicrobials + functional medicine** (hormones, nutrition, stress, worthiness) outperform the revolving 2-week antibiotic + steroid loop.

9. **Carnivore can be a potent elimination/metabolic tool** (Nario tip) — not a proven longevity diet; watch LDL and remember much benefit may be food *removal*.

10. **Refractory facial flaking/rosacea-like disease** may be Staph + Candida overlap — Tano: mupirocin + Lamisil (not iodine alone).
