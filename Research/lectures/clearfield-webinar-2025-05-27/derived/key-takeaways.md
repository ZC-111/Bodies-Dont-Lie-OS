# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-05-27

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Muscle is the organ of longevity** — ~40% of body mass, endocrine (myokines), nutrient sensor, and the main glucose sink (~80%). Frame patients as under-muscled, not merely over-fat.

2. **Prescribe minutes they have, not guidelines they ignore.** Two–six minutes of isometrics / squats / doorway pushes beats an unread 150-minute walking prescription.

3. **1RM guides intent:** ~50–80% / low reps → strength; lower relative load / higher reps → hypertrophy; aging adults usually need **both**.

4. **Lactate ≠ lactic acid toxin.** ~10% of work into the burn; lactate fuels and signals heart, liver, and brain.

5. **Myokine stack:** BDNF (brain/mood), irisin (metabolic + bone), exercise IL-6 (acute anti-inflammatory), myostatin (necessary brake). Resistance + some aerobic work maximizes the broadcast.

6. **GLUT4 translocation during exercise** moves glucose/insulin into muscle without carb-loading and helps lower circulating insulin / IR.

7. **Protein targets:** ~30–50 g/meal, ~2.5 g leucine/meal, ~1 g/day per lb **goal** weight. Hospital Boost/Ensure-style drinks: poor absorption + sugar. Protein alone does not build muscle.

8. **mTOR is contextual.** Leucine → mTORC1 → MPS; mTORC2 favors survival; endothelium needs angiogenic tone. Don’t blanket-suppress for “longevity” if you want muscle; in cancer, don’t feed insulin/sugar excess.

9. **Sarcopenia (~3–5%/decade) is reversible at any age.** Start isometrics → bands → weights. Sessions ~≤1 hour favor testosterone over cortisol. Pathologic multi-day DOMS → check anabolic hormones (Hartman).

10. **Assess function, not vanity.** Grocery carries, hangs, chair stands, floor get-ups, 2×waist < height; grip helps but is not destiny. Pair training with integrative tools (TRT, sleep, TRE balanced with protein).

## Comparison Table: Passive Aging vs Muscle-Centric Longevity

| Dimension | Sedentary / cardio-only excess | Muscle-centric plan (Quinn) |
|---|---|---|
| **Glucose fate** | Recirculates → visceral fat | ~80% disposed in muscle |
| **Signaling** | Low myokine output | BDNF, irisin, beneficial IL-6, etc. |
| **Bone** | Osteopenia risk (incl. men/youth) | Irisin → osteoblasts |
| **Insulin** | Rising IR | GLUT4 + sensitivity ↑ |
| **Hormones** | Drift + sarcopenia loop | Training ± TRT/optimization |
| **Injury recovery** | Slow (empty AA reservoir) | Faster repair capacity |
| **Starter tool** | “Join a gym Monday” | Isometrics between patients |
| **Endurance extreme** | Marathon sarcopenia risk | Strength + sensible VO₂ |

**Consensus from the night:** Integrative medicine uses all tools — resistance stimulus is non-negotiable medicine; hormones and quality amino acids amplify it.
