### Q6. Why does Tamati now discourage ultra-distance training for her own genetics?
**Answer:** Genetic testing (e.g., poor detox/GST and endothelial inflammatory risk) showed long ultras drove excess inflammation and raised stroke/aneurysm vulnerability; she is better suited to shorter high-intensity sessions (~45 min) and now prioritizes muscle for longevity.
