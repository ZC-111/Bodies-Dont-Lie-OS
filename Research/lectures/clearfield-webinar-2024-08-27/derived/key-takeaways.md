## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Worst-pain / thunderclap neuro presentations are not “migraine” until imaged.** Isabelle lost critical hours on analgesics before CT showed blood throughout the brain.

2. **Time-to-OR matters — and age bias is real.** ~18 hours to intervention (including ~12 h air-ambulance delay) vs. the “golden hour” ideal framed the family’s advocacy forever after.

3. **Screen post-SAH/stroke patients for severe sleep apnea.** SpO₂ to ~70% with hundreds of nightly apneas was missed until a smuggled sleep study; CPAP was likely life-saving.

4. **HBOT can be part of late neuro-rehab stacks.** After ~33 sessions she saw early language/motor flicker; home chamber + years of grind produced driver’s-license–level recovery against “no QoL” prognosis.

5. **Caregiver mindset transfers from endurance sport:** compartmentalize, one more step, protect the patient from fear-mongering, and refuse institutional fatalism when the family can safely care at home.

6. **CNS lymphoma “weeks to live” is not always the end of options.** RGCC-guided temozolomide + rituximab plus keto, IVC, HBOT, peptides (thymosin α1), and a large team cleared MRI disease in ~12 weeks in this case report.

7. **RGCC is a budgeting/selection tool, not a magic cure.** Nario: CTC burden + ex-vivo drug/natural/hormone/mAb sensitivity reduces shotgun supplement waste.

8. **IV vitamin C remains a contested hospital adjunct.** Tamati’s father’s sepsis fight (16 days vs ethics committee; Marik-type rationale) illustrates need for Right-to-Try–style pathways with waivers.

9. **Muscle is the currency of longevity — ultras are not.** Tamati retired from extreme distance after rhabdo, hypokalemic seizure, and genetics showing excess endothelial inflammation risk from overtraining.

10. **Personalize training with genetics + labs:** chronotype, ATP recovery, detox genes, and nutrient panels beat one-size-fits-all CrossFit-at-6am advice.
