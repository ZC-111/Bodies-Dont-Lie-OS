## 🚨 Infographic 4: "18 Hours to the OR"

**Title:** System Delays That Nearly Wrote Her Off

   → Coil/stent @ ~18 h total

**Action for clinicians:** Thunderclap / worst-pain presentations get urgent imaging — not a migraine label by default.
