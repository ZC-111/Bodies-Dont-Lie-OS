# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-03-31

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Vitamin K is not just “take with D.”** 2026 RCT meta-data links K supplementation to better fasting glucose, HbA1c, and HOMA-IR — pointing to peripheral insulin sensitivity.

2. **Prefer K2 MK-7 for tissue retention** (longer half-life vs MK-4). Classic jobs remain: osteocalcin for bone + matrix GLA protein to keep calcium out of arteries.

3. **Undercarboxylated/total osteocalcin ratio marks K insufficiency** and tracked ~2.5× frailty odds in older adults — K story extends to mobility and resilience.

4. **Warfarin patients need consistent K intake, not zero K.** DOACs are largely outside the vitamin K pathway worry.

5. **Always pair D + K with magnesium (300–500 mg elemental)** — activator for the mineralization / anti-calcification pathway (glycinate/citrate emphasized).

6. **MCG is an AI frequency-domain 2-lead EKG (~10 min)** that scores early/soft plaque, small-vessel disease, mito dysfunction, stiffening, congenital flags, and VF/AF risk — domains CAC cannot fully cover.

7. **CVD score &gt;4 is significant; ~4.6 ≈ average ACS.** Florida ER subset: scores &lt;4 had zero ACS events (n=455; larger study pending). Use yearly scores like a vital sign.

8. **Small-vessel disease is common in women and clinically sneaky** — extreme fatigue + DOE + “normal cath” can convert to large-vessel ACS within months. MCG can detect and trend it.

9. **Inflammation control beats chasing cholesterol alone.** Run the 11-state checklist; diabetes inflammation starts with hepatic carb intolerance (&gt;~25 g flips a multi-day lipogenesis/JNK1 switch), not “glucose is a toxin.”

10. **Daily aerobic work is the equalizer:** ~50 min at ~70% HR, most days, clears ~93% of circulating inflammatory factors that day — then turn off each inflammatory “spigot” (sleep/MTHFR, MCAS, gut, etc.).
