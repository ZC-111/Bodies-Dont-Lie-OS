## 🧬 Infographic 2: "K1 vs MK-4 vs MK-7"

**Title:** Pick the Right Vitamin K Tool

```
K1 (phylloquinone)
  └── Leafy greens → hepatic clotting factors

K2 MK-4 (short chain)
  └── Animal foods → extrahepatic; short half-life → higher/more frequent dose

K2 MK-7 (long chain) ★
  └── Natto / fermented → days-long half-life, tissue retention
  └── Nario’s standout for osteocalcin / CV-bone benefits
```

**Dose callout (glucose meta-analysis range):** **90–200 µg** — lower doses often effective
