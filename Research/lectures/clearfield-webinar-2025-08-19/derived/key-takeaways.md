# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-08-19

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Black cohosh is not the estrogen villain we were taught** — neurotransmitter/thermoregulation pathway; 20–40 mg/day; studied with tamoxifen; still loop oncology.

2. **Fasciolopsis buski is a high-yield “IBS” mimic** sitting at the ampulla of Vater — Gerber finds it in roughly 1 of every 2–3 IBS patients.

3. **Praziquantel 25 mg/kg ÷ 3 doses in one day** can end years of vomiting/RUQ pain overnight when the fluke is the driver; don’t drive that day.

4. **Follow antiparasitics with a short liver–gallbladder flush** (magnesium citrate + olive oil/grapefruit) to handle die-off and inspect stool for stones/odd debris.

5. **EAV (Voll) expands the differential** when labs/imaging are “normal” — parasites, dental foci, mycotoxins, vaccines, hormones, metals — and is lawful for NV homeopathic licensees (NAC 630A.015).

6. **Babesia often feels worse than Lyme** — azithromycin + atovaquone × 10 days with fat; any insect bite can count.

7. **Lyme protocol used here:** azithromycin 250 daily × ~30 days + Cowden (Samento/Cumanda/Burbur); titrate drops; back down + Burbur for Herx.

8. **Scabies is chronically missed** — permethrin and/or ivermectin plus enzyme spray/laundry protocol; believe patients who feel movement under skin.

9. **H. pylori clinic alternative to standard quadruple therapy:** mastic + berberine + bismuth citrate + zinc carnosine (~1 month BID).

10. **Chronic fatigue stack:** think EBV (IV C / ozone / H₂O₂ + SP-6 homeopathic shots), mold/azoles (itraconazole preferred), and occult dental infection — not only “adrenal fatigue.”
