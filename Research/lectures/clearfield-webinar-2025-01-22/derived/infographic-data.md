## 👃 Infographic 4: "Nose Protocol — Irrigate Then Nebulize"

**Title:** Direct Sinus Delivery Beats Oral Alone

```
1. Saline / Citra / SSKI wash (clean the snot first)
2. Nebulize antifungal (Rhino Sprint, one nostril at a time)
3. Repeat BID
```

| Drug | Mix (distilled water — not saline) |
|---|---|
| Amphotericin | 3 mg / 30 mL |
| Voriconazole | 12.5 mg / 30 mL (better brain Rx) |
| SSKI wash | 8–10 drops / 8 oz saline |

**Why it works:** Antifungal → hyphae retract → biofilm/suctionable debris clears → cognition often improves quickly.

## 🧠 Infographic 5: "Urine → Brain Rule"

**Title:** One Toxin in Urine Means Many in Brain

```
Urine mycotoxin positive
        │
        └── Expect ≥10 mycotoxins in brain tissue load
```

**Imaging:** AI CT/MRI algorithms (gliotoxin vial–trained) can visualize toxin; biopsy-confirmed in Dennis series.  
**Clinical shortcut:** You don’t need imaging to act — urine positive already implies CNS involvement.

**Oxygen assist:** 100% O₂ @ 8–10 L/min × ~2 h/day (or HBOT 1.4–2.5 ATA) to mobilize clearance.
