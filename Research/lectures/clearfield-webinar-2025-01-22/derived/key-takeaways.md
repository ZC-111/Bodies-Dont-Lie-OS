# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-01-22

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Air first, always.** Patients breathe ~3,000 gallons/day. Nasal washes and antifungals cannot outrun ongoing mold exposure — “drying off in the shower.”

2. **~20% of people are genetic hyper-reactors** (T-cell docking defect → ~9,000× inflammation per spore). Colony-count targets for them are near-zero (settling plate 0–2).

3. **Humidity 40–50%.** Drier is not better — cilia/mucosa fail below 40%. Pair HEPA with central dehumidification and a no-ozone bipolar ionizer (D6 Stereonizer).

4. **Fiberglass in HVAC is a fungal scaffold.** Remove it; particles seed eosinophil destruction of sinus (and sometimes lung) mucosa down to bone.

5. **Brown halo on SDA agar = mycotoxins leaching from tissue.** Matches tissue mycotoxin assays; a practical OR/clinic proof marker after hundreds of cases.

6. **Amphotericin in distilled water (3 mg/30 mL), irrigate then nebulize** (Rhino Sprint). Hyphae retract → suctionable; saline mixes fail. Voriconazole 12.5 mg/30 mL preferred when brain penetration matters.

7. **IGF-1 alone misses GH deficiency.** Even IGF-1 above the mean of normal → ~53% chance of failed stimulation; do full pituitary workup in mold-toxic patients.

8. **Urine mycotoxin + clothing plate beat blood Ig alone for “is it now?”** Blood IgG/IgE lack temporal resolution; one urine mycotoxin implies multi-toxin brain load.

9. **MARCoNS, MCAS, and biofilm are usually fungal consequences.** Kill fungus / fix air first; BEG spray and silver–EDTA treat surfaces, not the cause.

10. **HBOT (1.4–2.5 ATA) and 100% O₂ (8–10 L/min × 2 h/day) mobilize brain mycotoxins** — only after housing is safe, or relapse wastes the investment.

## Debate Summary: Mold Testing Camps

| Dimension | Urine mycotoxins (Dennis preference) | Serum mold IgG / IgE (Campbell camp) | Clothing SDA tap / settling plate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Window** | Recent excretion (~1–3 days) | Chronic / historical exposure | Real-time environmental load |
| **Best for** | “Is toxin exiting now?” | Documented immune sensitization | Intake triage before deep workup |
| **Limitation** | Excretion ≠ full body map | No temporal stamp | Colony ≠ species/toxin ID |
| **Dennis use** | Primary biologic | Secondary / optional | Always — smells + plate before debate |

**Consensus from lecture:** Environment first. Prefer urine when choosing one blood-vs-urine assay; never skip housing/HVAC. Imaging AI is confirmatory/compliance, not required once urine is positive.
