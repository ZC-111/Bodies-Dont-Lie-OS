# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-03-10

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Chronic disease is mostly extrinsic intoxication, not inevitable aging.** Vestigial populations (Hadza, Tsimané) lack coronary fatty streaks, age-rising BP, and hippocampal shrinkage despite comparable lifespan.

2. **Four drivers link industrial diet to disease:** chronic inflammasome stress, dysbiosis, type B malnutrition (empty calories + depleted micros/phytonutrients), and glycemic mismatch.

3. **Target omega-6 : omega-3 ≤ 5 : 1.** At that ratio Clayton’s ~1.5M-sample experience shows chronic inflammasome stops while acute response remains. N. America often sits at 25–45 : 1.

4. **Omega-3 alone is not enough** — pair LCPUFA with polyphenols/lipophenols to shut down chronic inflammasome stress.

5. **Polyphenols brake MMPs** (direct active-site inhibition + epigenetic downregulation). Spices are the densest daily source; clearance is rapid so intake must be continuous.

6. **Prebiotic fibers > probiotic capsules** for durable eubiosis. Modern intake ~4 g/day vs mid-Victorian ~40 g vs Mesoamerican ~140 g. Use a sequential blend (FOS → inulin → β-glucans → resistant starch).

7. **Type B malnutrition** (depletion + calorie excess) is the dominant modern pattern — not classic scurvy/beriberi. Ultraprocessed foods show dose-related harm across cancer, T2DM, dementia, aging clocks, and early death.

8. **Intervene before the event horizon.** Atheroma, BMD, and joint-space gains are reported when enough tissue remains; late irreversible loss cannot be dialed back.

9. **GHK-Cu (glycine–histidine–lysine–copper)** is emerging as a regenerative signaling peptide beyond cosmetics — topical cream has the best clinical footing; liposomal if oral; injectables work but hurt.

10. **Food addiction is engineered** via MRI bliss-point research. Intense sweeteners are not one pharmacology; Clayton rates sugar-sweetened beverages as the clearer metabolic villain vs aspartame’s murky epi signal.
