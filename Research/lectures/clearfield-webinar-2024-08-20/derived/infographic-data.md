## 🎗️ Infographic 2: "Cancer Bone Loss — Know the Numbers"

**Title:** Survivorship Without Fracture Surveillance Is Incomplete

| Setting | Signal |
|---|---|
| Breast Ca + aromatase inhibitor | ~**20%** fracture / 5 years; ~**80%** AI bone loss |
| Prostate Ca + ADT | BMD drop by **3–6 months**; hip Fx RR ↑ ~**30%** |
| Chronic glucocorticoids | **30–50%** fracture |
| Breast Ca fracture → hospital | up to **83%** ↑ relative mortality |
| Hip Fx, woman ≥65 | up to **36%** dead in 1 year |

**Callout:** ASCO: non-metastatic cancer + risk factor → **central DEXA** — yet screening rates remain abysmal.

## 💊 Infographic 4: "Meds That Break Bones (or Cause Falls)"

**Title:** Medication Review = Fracture Prevention

| Class | Pearl |
|---|---|
| Glucocorticoids | Risk before DEXA changes; collagen + muscle hit |
| SSRIs | ~1 Fx / 19 women over 1–5 years |
| PPIs / acid blockers | OR ~1.59 after 4 years |
| Hypnotics | ~33% ↑ Fx; insomnia common in survivors |
| Antihypertensives | Fall risk via over-drop in BP with aging PK |
| AIs / ADT | Oncologic bone loss — screen early |

**Rule:** ~**95%** of fractures follow a fall. Deprescribe fall-risk drugs when you can.

## 🥗 Infographic 5: "Lifestyle Stack That Moves Fracture Risk"

**Title:** What Guidelines Often Skip

```
Mediterranean diet → ~21% ↓ hip fracture
Protein            → ~1.3 g/kg/day (~0.6 g/lb)
Sleep              → <5 h linked to lower BMD; sleep ~17% of Fx variance
Social support     → ≥3 close people; faster recovery after break
Steps              → 7,000–7,500/day (not the 10k myth)
Strength training  → push weight = push aging
Home safety        → grab bars, light, clear paths, footwear
```

## ☀️ Infographic 6: "Calcium & Vitamin D — Right-Size Them"

**Title:** More Calcium Is Not More Protection

| Target | Number |
|---|---|
| Adult women RDA (total) | ~1200 mg/day |
| Upper limit (diet + pills) | ~2000–2500 mg |
| Typical US woman diet | ~800 mg |
| Often-needed supplement | ~**400 mg** (or none if diet adequate) |
| Vitamin D for lowest hip Fx | serum **30–44 ng/mL** |
| Ca + D fracture reduction | ~**16–22%** |

**Prefer:** food-first calcium; **citrate** if low stomach acid / PPI use.

## 🔑 Infographic 7: "MK-4 vs MK-7 — Not Interchangeable"

**Title:** Same “K2” Label, Different Molecules

| | MK-4 | MK-7 |
|---|---|---|
| Improves BMD in trials Newstead cites | ✅ Repeatedly | ❌ No |
| Fracture outcome data | ✅ (e.g., ~60%+ ↓ in metas at 45 mg) | ❌ None |
| Studied OP dose | **45 mg/day** | — |
| Anti-neoplastic research | Phase 2 / orphan paths | Not shown |

**Stack:** MK-4 + vitamin D3 > MK-4 alone for BMD.  
**Add-on math:** Ca+D (~16–22%) → add MK-4 → **>70%** relative Fx ↓ in cited outcomes.

## ⚖️ Infographic 8: "Drug Decisions in Cancer Bone Disease"

**Title:** Match the Drug to the Evidence You Actually Have

```
Ask for THIS indication:
  1) Fracture / SRE reduction?
  2) Overall survival?

Snapshots:
  • PPI bone loss + oral BP → can worsen Fx vs PPI alone
  • AI bone loss: BPs lack Fx data; denosumab has Fx signals + rebound after stop
  • GIO: BPs ↑ BMD, weak Fx proof; MK-4 stops GC bone loss
  • ADT: denosumab labeled for bone mass; protein + exercise vs sarcopenia
  • Anabolics (teriparatide): Newstead prefers for Fx reduction vs antiresorptives
```

**Footer:** Absence of data ≠ absence of effect — but say so honestly to patients.
