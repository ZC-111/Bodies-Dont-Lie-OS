# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-08-20

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Treat fractures (and cancer survival), not T-scores.** DEXA only predicts fracture in ~44% of women and ~21% of men with osteoporosis; drug-driven BMD changes are poor proxies for anti-fracture effect.

2. **Cancer survivorship bone loss is near-epidemic.** ~1 in 5 AI-treated breast cancer patients fracture within 5 years; ADT bone loss appears by 3–6 months. Screen with central DEXA when guidelines say so — real-world rates are abysmal.

3. **Fracture risk from glucocorticoids precedes densitometric change.** Collagen quality and sarcopenia/falls matter as much as mineral density.

4. **Medication reconciliation is fracture prevention.** SSRIs, PPIs, hypnotics, antihypertensives, AIs, and ADT all contribute; ~95% of fractures follow a fall. Post-discharge swaps often trade one fracture-promoting drug for another.

5. **FRAX is a limited tool in oncology.** Not validated for cancer/transplant and ignores AI/ADT/transplant inputs.

6. **Right-size calcium; optimize vitamin D.** Aim ~1200 mg total calcium (often ~400 mg supplement); serum D **30–44 ng/mL** for lowest hip-fracture risk. Prefer **calcium citrate** with hypochlorhydria/PPIs.

7. **Mediterranean diet + protein + strength + sleep + social support.** ~21% ↓ hip fracture with Med diet adherence; protein ~**1.3 g/kg/day**; 7–7.5k steps beat the 10k myth; ≥3 close relationships aid recovery/mortality.

8. **MK-4 ≠ MK-7.** Only **MK-4 (~45 mg/day)** has the BMD + fracture outcome package Newstead emphasizes; lowering undercarboxylated osteocalcin alone is not clinical success.

9. **Don’t extrapolate postmenopausal OP drugs blindly.** Oral bisphosphonate + PPI can **increase** fractures vs PPI alone; AI bone-loss bisphosphonate fracture data are weak; ask every agent for SRE + OS evidence in *this* setting.

10. **Don’t crash estradiol in men (or women).** Case pearl: chronic DIM → male osteoporosis with normal T. On TRT, always co-monitor E2; estradiol drives much of male bone maintenance.
