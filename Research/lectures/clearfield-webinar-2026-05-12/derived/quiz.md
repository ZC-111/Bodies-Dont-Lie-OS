### Q13. True or False: In Hartman’s model, membership fees include labs and Fullscript supplements.
**Answer:** **False.** Membership buys **access** to providers (visits, texting, included procedures). Labs are patient-pay at wholesale + ~$30 processing; supplements via Fullscript.

### Q14. What is the key legal distinction Hartman draws between DPC and concierge?
**Answer:** Concierge often still bills insurance **plus** a cash membership (“double dipping”) and risks Medicare sanctions. DPC is cash membership with **Medicare opt-out** — no insurance billing for primary care services.
