# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-05-12

## Top 10 Practice / Clinical Pearls

1. **DPC membership buys access, not consumables.** Visits, texts, and common procedures are included; labs, Rx, and supplements are patient-pay (wholesale labs ~+$30; Fullscript).

2. **LLC → S-corp + shareholders** is the structural unlock Hartman teaches — fair W-2 wage plus dividends that avoid Medicare/SS/UI on the profit share, with SIMPLE IRA match.

3. **High automation, low front desk.** Self-scheduling (Elation), Google Voice, AI scribes, and provider-roomed patients cut overhead and remove the “worst interaction” layer of non-clinical staff.

4. **$0 paid ads can scale.** ~1,200 patients via trainers, counselors, chiros, gyms, and word-of-mouth — make allied partners look good.

5. **Pay providers to stay.** ~65% of received payments after fees counters corporate turnover culture that empties primary care.

6. **Supervising MD as shareholder > $2–3K/mo fee.** A believing collaborator who shares upside can make a PA/NP DPC financially survivable at launch.

7. **Don’t double-dip Medicare.** Concierge that bills insurance *and* takes cash membership is a compliance trap; clean DPC = opt out + cash membership. Referrals out still work.

8. **Contractual no chronic opiates/benzos** keeps FL PA prescribing limits and overdose risk manageable; short acute courses only when obvious (e.g., fracture).

9. **Acute dermal herbicide rescue:** warm dilute apple cider vinegar bath (5–6 cups, 20–30 min); consider C60 topically / bentonite binder; escalate if systemic.

10. **Obesity medicine is shifting to body composition.** Investigational oral NA-931 (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon/IGF-1) aims to blunt sarcopenia — early data promising, long-term IGF-1 safety unproven.

## Framework Snapshot: DPC vs Concierge

| Dimension | DPC (Hartman) | Concierge (classic) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary revenue | Cash membership | Insurance + membership |
| Medicare | Opt out | Often still participates |
| Compliance risk if mixed | Lower if clean | High (“double dipping”) |
| What’s sold | Time / access / relationship | Access premium on top of insurance |
