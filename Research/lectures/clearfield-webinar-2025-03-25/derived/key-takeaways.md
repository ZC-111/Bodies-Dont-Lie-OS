# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-03-25

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Lp-PLA2 = plaque vulnerability; MPO = plaque rupture.** Treat MPO positives as higher urgency (quercetin, pomegranate, curcumin, aged garlic ± lipid drugs for Lp-PLA2).

2. **hsCRP goal <1** (not merely “lab normal” <3). Pair with omega-3 2–4 g/day and curcumin as a universal anti-inflammatory stack.

3. **CBC pattern recognition beats flags.** High basophils + monocytes + eosinophils → think parasitic gut infection; high MCH → B12; high RDW → oxidative/chronic stress.

4. **Use hormone indices, not single labs.** FAI (total T ÷ SHBG×0.288), E:P <250, free T3×10/rT3 >2.0, and prolactin <6 as a one-time anxiety/depression screen.

5. **Extreme cortisol may need hormones, not herbs.** In catastrophic stress, Clearfield went straight to physiologic cortisone + counseling; DHEA often inverse to cortisol.

6. **Stool β-glucuronidase links gut and estrogen.** Calcium D-glucarate + fiber/biotics — especially postmenopausal high-E / low-P with GI symptoms.

7. **SIBO cutoffs:** H2 ≥20 ppm rise/90 min; methane ≥10 ppm anytime. Rx is short; botanical + low-FODMAP path takes ≥6 months.

8. **Mucosal barrier = zonulin + DAO + histamine + LPS.** High LPS → neuroinflammation risk; high histamine → MCAS toolkit (DAO + stabilizers).

9. **IgG food tests are hypothesis generators.** Eliminate top offenders 3–6 weeks (IgG t½ ~23 days); interpret against history; expect false positives/cross-reactivity.

10. **Toxins: screen cheap, escalate smart.** Hair metals (~$65) first; remove mold exposure before debating urine vs serum; ALA ≥300 mg BID when nerves involved; Epsom baths replenish molybdenum.

## Debate / Camp Summary: Mold Testing

| Dimension | Urine mycotoxins | Serum mold IgG / IgE (Campbell) |
|---|---|---|
| **Window** | Recent excretion (~1–3 days) | Chronic / repeated exposure |
| **FDA-approved urine assay?** | No | N/A (antibody panels) |
| **Best for** | Screening recent exposure | Long-term immune response + MCAS (IgE) |
| **Caveat** | Excretion ≠ proven pathology | Requires camp-specific interpretation |

**Consensus from lecture:** Environment first (get out of mold). Air + swab for the building; choose biologic assay by acute vs chronic question.
