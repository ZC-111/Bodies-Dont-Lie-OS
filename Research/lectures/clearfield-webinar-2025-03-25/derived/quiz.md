## Section A — Serum Markers & CBC Pearls (7 questions)

### Q1. What does an elevated myeloperoxidase (MPO) primarily signal in Clearfield’s framing?
- A) Soft plaque that is still stable
- B) **Plaque rupture risk (worse than Lp-PLA2 vulnerability alone)** ✓
- C) Isolated bone resorption
- D) Pancreatic enzyme deficiency

> *Lp-PLA2 = plaque vulnerability; MPO = plaque rupture — “much worse.”*

### Q2. Which LDH isoenzyme pair maps primarily to cardiac / renal / RBC injury?
- A) LDH 4–5
- B) LDH 3 only
- C) **LDH 1–2** ✓
- D) LDH 5 only

### Q3. ↑ CRP + ↑ sed rate + **normal** LDH suggests:
- A) Tissue damage with cell lysis
- B) **Inflammation or infection without tissue damage** ✓
- C) Bone marrow suppression
- D) Parasitic infection

### Q4. The CBC pattern Clearfield flags for parasitic gut infection is:
**Answer:** Concurrently **high basophils + high monocytes + high eosinophils**.

### Q5. High MCH on CBC is most consistent with:
- A) Iron deficiency only
- B) **B12 deficiency / macrocytic anemia** ✓
- C) Spherocytosis
- D) Mast cell activation

### Q6. What hsCRP target does Clearfield prefer clinically?
- A) <3.0 mg/dL (lab “normal”)
- B) **<1** ✓
- C) <5
- D) Any value if ApoB is normal

### Q7. Free T3 × 10 ÷ reverse T3 should be greater than what value?
**Answer:** **> 2.0** — a clue to thyroid conversion / cortisol-stress imbalance.
