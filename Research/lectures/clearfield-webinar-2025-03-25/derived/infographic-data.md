## 🧮 Infographic 4: "Hormone Math That Matters"

**Title:** Indices Over Isolated Numbers

```
FAI = Total T ÷ (SHBG × 0.288)
  Male optimal ~53–108 | Female free % ~2–4%

E:P = (Estrone + Estradiol) ÷ Progesterone
  Prefer < 250  → rising ratio = estrogen-excess symptoms

Free T3 × 10 ÷ rT3  > 2.0
  Conversion / cortisol-stress clue

Prolactin < 6
  Treatment-resistant anxiety/depression screen (check once)
```

**Male T open target:** 700–900 ng/dL total · 2–4% free

## 🧱 Infographic 6: "Mucosal Barrier Quartet"

**Title:** Zonulin · DAO · Histamine · LPS

```
High ZONULIN     → leaky gut (bacteria / yeast / gluten)
Low DAO          → villous atrophy / ongoing barrier damage
High HISTAMINE   → MCAS → DAO enzyme + mast-cell stabilizers
High LPS         → endotoxin / neuroinflammation → berberine, oregano, garlic + repair
Low LPS          → immune deficiency → Igs + vitamins A & D
```

**Indications strip:** autoimmunity · food sensitivity · headache · arrhythmia · GI · mood · suspected leaky gut

## 🍽️ Infographic 7: "IgG Elimination Done Right"

**Title:** Chronic Food Sensitivity Protocol

```
1. Pick top 1–2 daily offenders (not the whole pink list)
2. Eliminate ≥ 3 weeks (prefer 6) — IgG t½ ≈ 23 days
3. Reintroduce one food at a time
4. Symptoms return → pull for another 6 weeks
5. Map tolerance over ~6 months (± dietitian)
```

**Remember:** IgG ≠ anaphylaxis IgE. Context + history beat printouts.
