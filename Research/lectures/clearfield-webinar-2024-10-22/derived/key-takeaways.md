# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-10-22

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **2 g DHA daily, for life.** Plasma saturates in ~6 weeks; brain turnover takes ~2.5 years. Sub-gram “fish oil” doses underdose the brain.

2. **DHA is 97% of brain omega-3s and 93% of eye omega-3s** — structural membrane lipid, not a boutique add-on.

3. **No RDI for DHA or lutein** despite foundational roles; average intakes (~<100 mg DHA, ~2 mg lutein) force supplementation for most patients.

4. **Lutein + zeaxanthin are the only carotenoids that cross the BBB** to form macular pigment; **MPOD ≈ brain lutein**.

5. **Lutein protects DHA from peroxidation** at inner mitochondrial membranes — they are a pair, not rivals.

6. **Depression can be an early neuroinflammatory signal**, not only a primary psychiatric label — check inflammatory tone before declaring SSRI failure (Miller/Emory framing).

7. **MMP-9 links plaque rupture and leaky BBB** — same low-grade inflammatory machinery in vessels and brain.

8. **AD is a decades-long systems/network disorder** (Bredesen “36 holes”) plus **exposome/allostatic load** — amyloid/tau monotherapy won’t patch the roof.

9. **Women carry disproportionate AD burden** (~2/3 of cases): hormones, caregiving stress, muscle mass/glucose disposal, menopause waist–hip risk (~40% ↑ dementia over 15 years).

10. **Never too late** — start DHA + lutein/zeaxanthin at any age; pair omega-3s with **methylated B vitamins**.

## Comparison: Brain Foundations Stack

| Dimension | DHA | Lutein / Zeaxanthin |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary job** | Membrane fluidity, SPMs/neuroprotectins, glutamate damping | Antioxidant, blue-light filter, MPOD, DHA protection |
| **BBB** | Structural lipid in brain/eye | Only carotenoids forming macular pigment in brain/eye |
| **Typical underdose** | 250–500 mg fish-oil labels | “Pixie dust” mg in eye formulas |
| **Speaker target** | **2 g/day** ongoing | Often **10–20+ mg** lutein with zeaxanthin (~4:1) |
| **Outcome signals cited** | Framingham ~47% ↓ dementia risk at high blood DHA; depression trials at 2 g | Cognition, BDNF, eye strain, UV skin protection, complement/MAC |
| **Safety note** | AFib signal exists; Nelson unconvinced — monitor if lone new AFib | GRAS; no practical OD concern discussed |
| **Product example** | Brain Omega ~460 mg DHA/serving | Lutamax 2020: 10 mg lutein / 2 mg zeaxanthin |

**Consensus:** Stack them. Lutein without enough DHA (or DHA without lutein) leaves oxidation or structure uncovered.
