- A) 2 mg/day

- C) **20 mg/day** ✓

### Q19. Why does Nelson connect lutein to long COVID / thrombo-inflammation?
**Answer:** Lutein modulates the **complement cascade**, including terminal **C5b–9 (MAC)**. Persistent MAC activation is linked to thrombo-inflammation in long COVID; a Netherlands-style observation showed falling MAC with lutein vs rising controls over ~12 months. Adipose is a major complement source — relevant given obesity–COVID comorbidity.

### Q20. A 68-year-old patient asks whether starting DHA + lutein is “too late,” and whether Fatty15 should replace fish oil. What do you counsel based on this webinar?
**Answer:**
1. **Never too late** — start foundational DHA + lutein/zeaxanthin now.
2. Target **~2 g DHA/day** long-term (not 250–500 mg “eye formula” doses alone).
3. Pair with lutein+zeaxanthin (natural ~**4:1**; marigold-sourced OK; GRAS; typical researched ranges 6–40+ mg lutein).
4. Do **not** replace DHA with Fatty15 on current evidence — expensive, low dose, lacking membrane outcome trials; keep EPA/DHA/ALA core.
5. Ensure **methylated B vitamins** for omega-3 utilization.
6. If new lone AFib appears after omega-3 change, reassess levels/causality — Nelson is skeptical of the AFib scare but pragmatic.
7. Optional product construct discussed: Brain Omega (~460 mg DHA/serving × multiple softgels to reach 2 g) + Lutamax 2020 (10 mg lutein / 2 mg zeaxanthin per serving as labeled).
