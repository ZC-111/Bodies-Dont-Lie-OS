# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-06-03

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Fractionate before you statin.** Basic LDL-C panels miss LDL-P, small dense LDL, ApoB, HDL-P, and Lp(a) — the markers that change decisions.

2. **LDL is substrate, not just sludge.** It feeds steroid hormones, vitamin D, membranes, bile, brain, and immunity; over-suppression shows up as fog, myalgia, hormone loss, and worse diabetes.

3. **Insulin is a cholesterol catalyst.** Glycolysis → Acetyl-CoA + insulin-driven HMG-CoA reductase explains diabetic dyslipidemia better than “eat less cholesterol” alone.

4. **Homocysteine is the missing co-villain.** Ox-LDL needs arterial injury; high Hcy “pokes holes,” then foam cells build plaque. Order Hcy with every serious lipid workup.

5. **MTHFR stack:** methylfolate + TMG/betaine + methylcobalamin — not folic acid / cyano-B12 defaults.

6. **Lp(a) is genetic — niacin is the oral workhorse** (~1.2–1.5 g; higher when severe). Statins don’t fix it; flush form may be more effective than non-flush / inositol hexanicotinate.

7. **Particle number drives risk.** Aim LDL-P < ~1000 nmol/L and ApoB preferably < ~80; Hartman uses pharma when LDL-P climbs past ~1800–2000.

8. **Acetyl-L-carnitine (~500 mg)** helps age-related mitochondrial cholesterol shuttle; watch TMAO/gut context; pair with CoQ10/ubiquinol if blocking HMG-CoA.

9. **Root-cause diet beats monoclonal “machine guns.”** Low-GI / Mediterranean / Prophet Daniel vegetable kickstart + omega-3, berberine, curcumin, magnesium — reserve Repatha-class agents for true refractory genetics.

10. **HRV is trainable.** Beyond gadgets: fish oil dose-response, sleep/stress practices, Med diet, and repleting Vit D/B12 move autonomic fitness (Nario tip).

## Comparison Table: Basic Lipid Panel vs Fractionation-First Care

| Dimension | Basic total/LDL panel → statin reflex | Fractionation + root-cause frame (this webinar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary question** | Is LDL-C high? | Which particles + Hcy + Lp(a) drive risk? |
| **Hormone lens** | Often ignored | Protect LDL substrate for steroids / Vit D |
| **Diabetes interaction** | Guidelines push statin harder | Watch statin-worsened glycemia + insulin→HMG-CoA loop |
| **Lp(a)** | Rarely ordered | Niacin-first; estrogen/ASA/ASO pipeline noted |
| **Cost philosophy** | Cheap panel, expensive mAb later | ~$20 advanced panel; cheap nutrients first |
| **Success metric** | Lower LDL-C | Lower ApoB/LDL-P/sdLDL + Hcy + symptoms/hormones |

**Consensus:** Decode cholesterol — don’t just delete it.
