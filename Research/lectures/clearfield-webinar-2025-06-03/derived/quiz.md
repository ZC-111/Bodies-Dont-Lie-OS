### Q9. In the lecture’s cascade, what does homocysteine do that enables oxidized small-dense LDL to drive plaque?

### Q10. Foam cells form when:
**Answer:** Monocytes migrate to inflamed arterial sites, become macrophages, and engorge on oxidized LDL / cholesterol remnants until they die and contribute to plaque.

### Q11. What genetic issue commonly elevates homocysteine, and what triad is recommended?
**Answer:** **MTHFR** mutation → impaired folate methylation. Treat with **methylfolate + trimethylglycine (betaine) + methylcobalamin**.

### Q12. Approximate Lp(a) thresholds called “very high cardiac risk” in the talk?
- A) Any detectable Lp(a)

- C) Only if ApoB < 40
- D) Only after MI

### Q13. Why are statins a poor primary tool for elevated Lp(a) in this webinar’s framing?
**Answer:** Lp(a) is genetically determined; panel consensus is that **statins do not meaningfully treat Lp(a)** — **niacin** is the oral therapy emphasized (plus emerging agents / estrogen / aspirin per Nario).

## Section D — Clinical Integration (2 questions)

### Q19. A 58-year-old man on atorvastatin 80 mg for diabetes has new myalgias, brain fog, rising A1c on multiple agents, and no prior particle panel. Using this webinar, what is your approach?
**Answer:**
1. Order **lipoprotein fractionation** + ApoB + Lp(a) + homocysteine (+ MTHFR if indicated)
2. Replete **CoQ10/ubiquinol** and reconsider whether high-dose statin is worsening glycemia
3. Address diet (carb/sugar → TG and small dense LDL), methyl donors if Hcy high
4. If Lp(a) high → **niacin** pathway; consider ezetimibe as lower-toxicity add/alternative
5. Do not treat total LDL alone; protect hormone/membrane substrate

### Q20. Homocysteine is 12 µmol/L, small dense LDL elevated, ApoB 115, Lp(a) 140 nmol/L. Outline a root-cause stack consistent with the lecture.
**Answer:**
1. Methylfolate + TMG/betaine + methylcobalamin; check MTHFR
2. Anti-inflammatory / low-GI diet; omega-3; curb oxidized fat / seed-oil frying
3. Niacin titration (flush education) for Lp(a) + lipids
4. Particle-number focus; magnesium, CoQ10, acetyl-L-carnitine, berberine/curcumin as adjuncts
5. Recheck fractionation + Hcy; reserve PCSK9 mAbs as expensive last-line, not first root-cause tool
