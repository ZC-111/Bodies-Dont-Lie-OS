## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Don't ditch chemo/RT/IO — engineer them.** Personalize (pharmacogenetics), package (cyclodextrin + platelet nanoparticles / exosomes), dose ~10–25%, and photo-activate.

2. **Mitochondria + immune are non-negotiable.** High-dose cytotoxics that destroy both lose mitophagy and endogenous antitumor immunity even if bulk tumor shrinks.

3. **Acidic TME is a cascade, not a slogan.** Lactate export → Treg/Th2/Th17 skew, fibrin cloak, fibrosis, and CSC de-differentiation. Alkaline *water* does not fix tumor extracellular pH.

4. **“Vascular but hypoxic” is a fibrosis problem.** Kinked vessels exclude RBCs/lymphocytes; ozone / plasma O₂ and antifibrotics reopen the kill zone for radical-dependent therapies.

5. **Platelet nanoparticles flip MDR.** Endocytosis can turn ABC exporters into pumps that concentrate packaged drug inside the tumor cell.

6. **Naked checkpoint inhibitors are blunt instruments.** Loading anti–PD-1/PD-L1/CTLA-4 into platelet NPs aims effect at tumor and reduces systemic autoimmunity drain.

7. **NK exosomes are complementary carriers.** Invitrix framing: secretome > bulk cells; possible wild-type p53 advantage; loadable with chemo/photosensitizers/nutraceuticals.

8. **Read labs after lysis, not during it.** D-dimer/tumor marker spikes mid-cytoreduction often mean tumor breakdown — reassess ~2–3 weeks post-block.

9. **Glutathione timing matters.** Skip reduced GSH during oxidative chemo/RT/ozone windows; after recovery, consider support with NAC + glycine to manage glutamate.

10. **Lavender is an evidence-backed sleep tip.** Olfaction stays online during sleep — diffuser or pillow delivery; chamomile/bergamot/peppermint as adjuncts.
