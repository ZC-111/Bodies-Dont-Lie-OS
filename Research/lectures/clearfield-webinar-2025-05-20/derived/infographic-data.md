## 🩸 Infographic 4: "Vascular but Hypoxic"

| VEGF-driven vessels | Many, but **kinked** by fibrosis | Losartan / Klotho / focused US / PDT |

**Key insight:** Integrative O₂ support can enable — not antagonize — radical-dependent chemo/RT.
