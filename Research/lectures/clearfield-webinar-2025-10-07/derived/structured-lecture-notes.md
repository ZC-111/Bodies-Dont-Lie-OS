## Segment 3 — Dr. Tanno: The Business of Health & the Productivity Equation (≈14:50–1:26:00)

**Speaker:** Dr. Tanno  
**Framing:** Medical school teaches skills, not business — yet almost no one practices for free. Title focus: **business of health + productivity equation** (money equation deferred).

### ★ Core Thesis
> Practice viability = **skills × preeminence × productivity**, priced to what the market can bear, with psychic cost managed so you don’t burn out.

### 3A. Why Private Practice
- Autonomy to introduce therapies without institutional gatekeeping
- Drawback: you become the practice → work *in* it instead of *on* it → burnout risk

### 3B. First Decisions Before Revenue
1. **Who do you want to serve?** (market selection)
2. **Where?** (location)
3. List services / offers / website
4. Model both **provider side** and **buyer (patient) side**

### 3C. Revenue & Profit Definitions
- **Revenue** = Price × Quantity demanded
- Services demanded depend on **skills, preeminence, productivity**
- **Total profit** = Total revenue − Total cost
- **Total cost** = Fixed + Variable + **Psychic cost** + **Opportunity cost**

### 3D. Skills vs Preeminence
- **Skills:** shared training outcomes (e.g., all well-trained internists can place lines)
- **Preeminence:** skills so valuable you become **irreplaceable** — Tuesday webinars exist to build this differentiation
- Example: allergist who added hormone literacy → transformed allergy/hives care (skill-gap honesty)

### 3E. “21st-Century Medical Preeminence” Domains
Environment, pathophysiology, body systems (≈12), pharmacology, lifestyle, nutrition/supplements, mind–body–soul, basic science, **technology**, and **market intelligence**

**Market intelligence examples:**
- Milk aisle (almond/oat/soy/etc.) signals that conventional cow’s milk is no longer the unchallenged default
- Sugar aisle (organic cane, coconut, monk fruit, stevia vs beet/GMO Roundup-tolerant sugar) signals safer sweetener choices

### 3F. Palmares / Preeminence Report
- Preeminence = community knowledge + total life achievements (**palmares** — Latin “lifetime achievements”)
- CV/resume = short; palmares = full lifetime (incl. family achievements) → Tanno’s Palmares Global / Tear Pro network concept

### 3G. Psychic Cost (Burnout Driver)
Psychic cost rises with:
- Mispriced services vs competitors
- Information asymmetry (Dr. Google patients)
- Thin skill set / lack of preeminence
- Slow **time-to-results**
- Seeing too many patients at too-low price → more work for less margin

**Pricing pearl:** If market can bear $200 and you charge $100, you may need ~2× patient volume for same revenue → burnout. Do competitor research first.

**Clearfield TMS case:** $500 × 6 = $3,000 → zero takers; dropped to $200 × 6 = $1,200 → 15 signups in 6 weeks.  
**Tanno coaching:** Prefer **high start → sequential drop** (e.g., 500 → 300) over dumping to floor immediately; hard to raise prices later. Amazon-style “was/now” psychology.

### 3H. Opportunity Cost
Value of next-best alternative use of capital/time (classmate who left medicine to become a SF chef).

### 3I. Elasticity of Demand
- Definition: % change in quantity demanded / % change in a demand determinant (esp. price)
- Demand curve slopes down: ↑ price → ↓ quantity
- **Elastic (>1):** raise price → revenue can fall (TMS $500 example)
- **Unitary (=1):** 1% price ↑ ↔ 1% quantity ↓
- **Inelastic (<1):** raise price, buyers stay — driven heavily by **preeminence** (+ luxury/status positioning, affluent location)
- Functional medicine often favors fewer patients with higher value vs insurance 10–15 min “burn and turn”

### 3J. High-Ticket Offers & Revenue Streams
High-ticket depends on: preeminence, niche, market size, hungry buyers.  
Safer growth path: **sell more to each buyer** (multiple streams) rather than endless new patients.

**Integrative immunity practice stream example:**
Advertising/funnels → landing page + questionnaire → triage VA → visit → education (fixes #1 gripe: “doctor never explained why”) → allergy testing / SLIT / shots / foods / hormones / supplements (passive recurring)

**Visit cadence (Tanno FM):** initial → 3 mo → 6 mo → 1 yr (~3–4 visits/year) unless complex.

### 3K. True Demand Function Variables
**Buyer:** own price, competitor prices, income, taste, effort (patient must do work — e.g., estrogen-free lifestyle).  
**Seller/provider decision vars:** information asymmetry, skills, preeminence, productivity, time-to-results.  
**Taste expressed via:** referrals, satisfaction surveys, **Google/Yelp reviews**.

### 3L. Outcome Measurement & ICEA
- Pre/post **21-page questionnaire** (~15 min) at baseline and ~3 months → objective improvement vs “I feel the same”
- **Incremental cost-effectiveness analysis (ICEA):** relate Δcost to Δeffect (questionnaire / labs / antibodies)
- Price of ignorance = psychic cost + seller’s remorse (difficult patients, bad reviews)
- Buyer’s remorse if patients choose untrained “Joe FM” without homework
- Call for standardized outcome measurement so functional medicine can show the world it works (like pharma CEA/CBA requirements)

### 3M. Pre-Practice Self-Audit Checklist (selected)
Average own vs competitor price; median income in draw area; Google/Yelp vs competitors; advertising footprint; proprietary know-how; irreplaceability; time-to-first/final results; goals actively pursued; own hormones/supplements/weight/exercise (credibility to coach others).
