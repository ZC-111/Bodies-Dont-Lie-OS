# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-10-07

## Top 10 Clinical / Practice Pearls

1. **Itraconazole can dramatically calm fungal-responsive prostatitis** — symptoms may rebound within days of stopping; consider long courses (~6 months per Campbell mycotoxin teaching) and compounded rectal delivery for local effect.

2. **Tanno’s prostatitis frame:** prostatomegaly ≈ prostatitis ≈ infection (fungus, mycoplasma, parasite, etc.) — informs why antiparasitic/antifungal trials sometimes help.

3. **Biological dentistry is mainstream in European integrative practice.** Titanium = reliable but possible ion/immune issues; zirconia = biocompatible/esthetic with less decades-long data.

4. **Revenue = Price × Quantity; demand rides on skills + preeminence + productivity.** Marketing without preeminence is a leaky bucket.

5. **Preeminence = irreplaceability** — Tuesday learning exists to create skill gaps closure (e.g., allergist + hormones). Palmares > one-page resume.

6. **Psychic cost is the burnout variable** — underpricing forces volume; Dr. Google asymmetry punishes thin knowledge; slow time-to-results breeds bad reviews.

7. **Price to what the market can bear; start high and drop sequentially.** Clearfield TMS: $500 × 6 → 0; $200 × 6 → 15 signups. Elastic demand punishes overpricing.

8. **Inelastic demand is earned by preeminence** (and sometimes luxury positioning / affluent location) — not by wishing.

9. **Grow with streams, not slaughterhouse volume:** education-driven allergy/hormone/supplement/immunotherapy packages; ~3–4 FM visits/year; measure with pre/post questionnaires for ICEA.

10. **Legal/regenerative humility:** no national FM diploma required, but contracts/boards matter; pheresis ≠ mycotoxin cure; stem-cell cowboy practice is high-risk — prefer cleared protocols; NV HMD expands integrative protection.
