**Title:** The Full Cost Stack Practitioners Forget

| **Fixed** | Rent, staff base, devices | TMS machine sitting idle |

## 📈 Infographic 3: "Elasticity Cheat Sheet for Clinic Pricing"

**Title:** Know Where You Sit on the Demand Curve

| Zone | Elasticity | What happens if you raise price | How you get here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left of peak revenue | **Elastic (>1)** | Volume collapses; revenue may fall | Commodity care, weak differentiation |
| Peak | **Unitary (=1)** | 1% ↑ price ↔ 1% ↓ quantity | Balanced market |
| Right of peak | **Inelastic (<1)** | Buyers still pay | **Preeminence**, niche, affluent location |

**Case callout:** TMS at $500 × 6 → 0 takers (elastic). At $200 × 6 → 15 signups in 6 weeks.

## 🧠 Infographic 4: "Psychic Cost — The Hidden Burnout Tax"

**Title:** Why Smart Clinics Still Burn Out

```
Psychic cost ↑ when:
  • Price << competitors (you absorb volume)
  • Patients arrive armed with Dr. Google
  • Skill gaps go unfilled
  • Time-to-results is slow
  • You work IN the practice, not ON it
```

**Antidote:** Market research → price to what market can bear → invest in preeminence → educate every visit.
