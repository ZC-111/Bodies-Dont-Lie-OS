# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-08-05

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Pharmacogenomics ≠ root-cause care.** PGx guides metabolism/dosing; remission still ~16–20% in cited trials. Ask *why* the patient is depressed or anxious.

2. **HTR1A is the “faulty serotonin thermostat.”** Overactive auto-receptors shut off synthesis; rebuild serotonin (5-HTP, saffron, cofactors, light, targeted probiotics) before expecting SSRIs to work. Consider SNRIs.

3. **IL-1β / NLRP3 inflammasomes explain “brain on fire” depression** — especially in seniors, post-infection, Lyme/mold, and amyloid contexts. Resolvins and phenotype-matched polyphenols matter when antidepressants stall.

4. **GPR50 = high cortisol + low melatonin**, stronger in women (X-linked). Ashwagandha 300–500 mg BID has striking stress/cortisol data; Relora is a backup.

5. **ADORA2A carriers can panic at 100–150 mg caffeine.** Audit energy drinks and “decaf”; use theanine/NAC/DHA and fix B12/D.

6. **GCH1 is MTHFR’s upstream partner.** Without BH4 you cannot make monoamines *or* nitric oxide. Low-dose BH4 (~2.5–5 mg BID), not PKU/Kuvan megadoses, was pivotal for Jasmine.

7. **Genomics without an action map is trivia.** Clinical decision support that pairs each SNP with diet/lifestyle/supplement/medication options turns reports into plans.

8. **Inflammation subtype changes the stack:** CCL2 → PEA; TNF-α → Lion’s Mane; IL-1β → resolvins — not one anti-inflammatory for all.

9. **Nutrigenomics is most useful for transporters/receptors** (e.g., TCN2 and brain B12), not for nutrients you can already measure in blood.

10. **Rosemary ~750 mg culinary dose supports cognition; 6 g can impair it.** Never ingest essential oils; caution with anticoagulants.

## Comparison: Pharmacogenomics vs Clinical Genomics

| Dimension | Pharmacogenomics | Clinical / root-cause genomics |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary question** | Drug metabolism & interactions | Etiology of symptoms |
| **Typical genes** | CYPs, transporters for meds | HTR1A, IL1B, GPR50, ADORA2A, GCH1, COMT, DIO2, BDNF… |
| **Remission boost alone** | Modest (~16–20% in cited trials) | Case-series outcomes when paired with interventions |
| **Best use** | Avoid over/under-dosing | Build precision stacks for treatment resistance |
| **Example tool framing** | GeneSight-style med matching | Intellx DNA clinical decision support |
| **Who it’s for (this talk)** | Broad prescribing | Licensed clinicians (meds included) |

**Consensus:** Use both when helpful — never stop at PGx for complex mental health.
