## 🎯 Infographic 1: "PGx vs Root-Cause Genomics"

**Title:** Two Different Questions

| Approach | Question it answers | What it misses |
|---|---|---|
| **Pharmacogenomics** | How do I metabolize this drug? | Why am I depressed/anxious? |
| **Clinical genomics** | What root pathways drive my symptoms? | Nothing if paired with action plans |

**Callout:** Even with PGx, remission was only **~16–20%** in cited trials — **~80% still unwell**.

**Footer:** Dose the drug *and* treat the etiology.

## 🌡️ Infographic 3: "HTR1A — Fix the Thermostat"

```
NORMAL AUTO-RECEPTOR
  Sense serotonin → make enough → stop

OVERACTIVE HTR1A
  Sensor reads "too hot" when cold
  → Serotonin production shuts off
  → SSRIs have little to recycle
  → Slow, weak antidepressant response
```

**Rebuild kit:** saffron · 5-HTP · vitamin D · *L. helveticus* + *B. longum* · 10,000-lux light  
**Med pearl:** SNRIs often beat SSRIs for this genotype

## 🔥 Infographic 4: "IL-1β → NLRP3 → Brain Inflammation"

**Title:** Why Antidepressants Miss Remission

```
Peripheral hit (surgery, dental infection, Lyme, mold, virus)
        ↓
     IL-1β ↑
        ↓
  NLRP3 inflammasomes
        ↓
  Inflammatory signal crosses to brain
        ↓
  Depression / bipolar / cognitive decline / poor remission
```

**Tools:** resolvins · omega-3s · sulforaphane · resveratrol · apigenin · luteolin · quercetin  
**Match agent to phenotype** (e.g., apigenin for anxiety/sleep; sulforaphane for autism/ADHD signals)

Integrative BH4 (not PKU doses):
