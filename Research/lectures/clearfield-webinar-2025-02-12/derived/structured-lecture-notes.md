**Speaker:** Melvin Nario (Speaker 2); comments from Speaker 4, Benoit Tano, Bill Clearfield

### ★ Core Framing
> Lp(a) is a **~90% hereditary** coagulation/endothelial-risk factor present in ~**20%** of the population. Statins can **aggravate** Lp(a). ApoB is now trumping NMR particle panels as the preferred CV marker.

### Management Notes (University of Arizona fellowship teaching)
| Approach | Claimed Lp(a) effect |
|---|---|
| Aspirin ≥150 mg | ~26–50% lowering |
| Fibrates | ~40% |
| Niacin 1–3 g/day (flush preferred) | ~20–30% |
| L-carnitine 2 g | ~10% |
| CoQ10 60 mg BID | supportive |
| Repatha (PCSK9i) | indirect Lp(a) modulation via ApoB hybrid particle |

### Additional Points
- Do not expect Lp(a) to normalize — any reduction helps
- Family-member testing when elevated
- ApoB preferred over standard cholesterol panel / NMR
- CAC vs CT angio debate: Speaker 4 notes lean-mass hyper-responders with high ApoB can still have CAC zero; jury still out

## Segment 5 — Safety, Contraindications & Device Quality (36:49–1:08:33)

**Speakers:** Allison Beardsley, Melvin Nario, Bill Clearfield, panel

### Eye Safety Paradox
- Literature caution: retinal exposure / rare detachment risk; also used for childhood myopia, macular degeneration, cataracts
- Studio: optional “Terminator” goggles; neurosurgeon member encourages staring; staff vision improvements reported
- Clearfield/Andrew: informed consent for eye choice; mouse retinal data = short (90 sec) exposure — do not over-extrapolate

### Clearance / Caution Flags (manufacturer + studio practice)
| Situation | Practice |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy | Doctor’s note required |
| Pacemaker | Doctor’s note (3 Bentonville members cleared) |
| Uncontrolled HTN / compromised liver (fat flush) | Waiver language; avoid |
| Lipodystrophy / HIV ART (Speaker 7) | Avoid — fat-cell mobilization concern |
| Autoimmune | Beardsley: no absolute contraindication; lupus/fibromyalgia anecdotes |
| Open wounds / post-op | Surgeons have green-lit wraps ~2 weeks post tummy tuck |
| Alcohol / dehydration | Encourage hydration, minimize alcohol, “love the liver” |
| Face contouring | Face/neck panel **12–18″ away** — preserve facial fat for youthfulness |

### Device Quality
- Prefer **FDA-cleared** Class II (clearance ≠ drug approval); ~90% market devices not even registered
- Contour Light manufactured in Reno (~$40K system); skin-contact LEDs vs high-intensity box panels (photonics law)
- No burn risk with their FDA-cleared wraps; non-cleared unit once left marks
- Staff training brief (wrap/unwrap) — LEDs, not cutting lasers

### Liability Framing
- Boutique fitness waiver (not medical malpractice model); no vitals taken; member self-reports BP/conditions
- Clearfield caution: medical “clearance” can shift liability onto the clearing physician
