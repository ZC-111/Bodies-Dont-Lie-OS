# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-02-12

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Photobiomodulation works through cytochrome c oxidase → ATP.** Wavelength choice matters; Red Light Method’s Contour Light pairs **635 nm** (contouring) with **880 nm** NIR (pain/inflammation).

2. **Skin contact beats distant panels for this LED wrap model.** Direct wraps reduce energy loss vs high-intensity box panels; session lights are warm (~86°F), not burning, when FDA-cleared.

3. **Standard studio stack:** 25 min wraps → 10–15 min Power Plate → 15 min Pilates; ~3×/week is the real-world average — “rocket booster,” not a diet substitute.

4. **Device quality filter:** demand **FDA clearance** (Class II) for labeled claims; most consumer “red lights” are not cleared/registered. Clearance ≠ drug approval.

5. **Eye policy = informed choice.** Retinal overexposure cautions coexist with therapeutic eye/brain narratives — offer goggles, document benefits/risks/alternatives (Clearfield’s consent definition).

6. **Hard stops / notes:** pregnancy and pacemakers need clinician clearance; avoid uncontrolled HTN, careless use with compromised liver/alcohol load, and **lipodystrophy/HIV ART fat wasting**.

7. **Keep fat off the face (literally).** Face/neck panels at 12–18″ preserve facial fat; thyroid still gets exposure from the distance piece. Hashimoto’s protocols in literature often add **selenium + vitamin D**.

8. **Methylene blue + 660 nm** was flagged as an activation pairing; intranasal/IV routes discussed as clinician-side interest, not Red Light Method SOP.

9. **Lp(a) tip:** ~20% prevalence, ~90% hereditary, statins may worsen it; prioritize **ApoB**; niacin 1–3 g (flush), aspirin ≥150 mg, fibrates, carnitine, CoQ10 discussed as tools.

10. **Postmenopausal bleeding ≠ “aging backwards.”** Work it up; HRT confounders likely in mid-50s “period return” anecdotes.
