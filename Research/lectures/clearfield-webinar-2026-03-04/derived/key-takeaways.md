4. **PEMF’s dual claim:** break RBC Rouleau + restore membrane ion gradients so O₂/nutrients enter and waste exits.

5. **Frequency has directionality:** low Hz to open (vasodilation); high Hz to constrict (acute edema). Schumann **7.83 Hz** as Earth’s reference; evening programs often **≤3 Hz**.
