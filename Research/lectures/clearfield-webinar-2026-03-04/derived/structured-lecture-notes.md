## Segment 3 — Tip of the Week: MK-677 vs MK-777 (Ghrelin Agonists) (18:50–27:00)

**Speaker:** Dr. Franz (Speaker 4)

### ★ Core Framing
> MK-677 clearly raises GH/IGF-1. The clinical question is **which phenotype** benefits from the anabolic trade-off — and which patients you may accelerate metabolic risk in.

### MK-677 (Ibutamoren)
- Oral **non-peptide** ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a) agonist
- ↑ Pulsatile GH, IGF-1, IGFBP-3; half-life ~24h → once-daily dosing
- Preserves physiologic pulsatility better than exogenous HGH (still raises baseline tone)
- **NASA / Ann Intern Med 2008** (2-year RCT, healthy older adults): sustained youthful-range IGF-1; fat-free mass ↑ ~1.66 kg; **no** significant strength/functional gain; ↑ fasting glucose / impaired glucose tolerance
- Murphy et al.: robust GH/IGF-1 rise in adult GHD — axis works; metabolism pays a cost

### Side Effects (predictable physiology)
- Marked ↑ appetite, weight gain, peripheral edema, transient paresthesia
- ↑ Fasting glucose, ↓ insulin sensitivity; occasional mild BP rise
- Chronic GH/IGF-1 antagonizes insulin at hepatic + peripheral level

### MK-777
- Marketed as “cleaner cousin” with fewer appetite/edema effects
- **Limited clinical development** — no robust long-term human outcome trials, no aging data, minimal safety package
- Franz: more **marketing narrative** than evidence; common on “wild west” peptide sites
- Both agents: **research-only / not FDA-approved**

### Phenotype Caution
- Be especially cautious in prediabetes, metabolic syndrome, oncology patients, strong FHx of insulin resistance
- Cancer patients: Franz would not use (IGF concern / license caution); panel notes ongoing debate (Hertoghe-style high IGF quartile ↔ lower cancer graphs vs conventional caution)
- Historical context (Clearfield): older injectables (sermorelin, GHRP-2/6) — one ↑ appetite, one ↑ nausea; CJC-1295 era followed

### Clinical Pearl
> Just because you can amplify growth signaling doesn’t mean you should. Match anabolic tools to phenotype.
