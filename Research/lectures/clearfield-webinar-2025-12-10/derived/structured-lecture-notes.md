## Segment 2 — Tip of the Week: Holiday Weight-Loss Botanicals (14:40–23:52)

**Speaker:** Dr. Nario (Speaker 3)  
**Add-on:** Bill Clearfield — post–GLP-1 maintenance tip

### Framing
- Not green tea / L-carnitine / CLA; not GLP-1 agonists
- Goal: **modest metabolic edge** (≈1–3 kg over 8–12 weeks) during “six-week seasonal metabolic apocalypse”
- Patients often already take these for inflammation, cardiometabolic, or gut indications — reframe for holiday stacking

### Botanical Table (as presented)

| Agent | Typical dose cited | Evidence snapshot | Mechanism / pearl |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curcumin** | 1000–1500 mg/day (+ piperine or liposomal) | 2019 RCT: +2 kg loss vs lifestyle alone in 30 days | NF-κB suppression; ↑ adiponectin; fatty-acid oxidation. Best in inflammatory phenotypes (OA, ↑hsCRP, PCOS, fatty liver). Don’t underdose at 500 mg once daily. |
| **Capsaicin** | 2–10 mg/day capsaicinoids | 2022 meta: +50–70 kcal/day; ~0.9 kg over 8–12 weeks | TRPV1 → brown fat; mild appetite ↓ |
| **Berberine** | 500 mg 2–3×/day | 2020 meta: ~2.3 kg / 12 weeks + A1c & TG improvements | AMPK; insulin sensitivity; ↓ hepatic lipogenesis. “OG GLP-1 before GLP-1.” Uses: prediabetes, PCOS, dyslipidemia, SIBO |
| **Citrus aurantium / synephrine** | 10–15 mg/day | 2016 review: ~2.5–3.1 kg over 6–12 weeks (± caffeine) | Mild β3 agonism; ↑ energy expenditure. **Avoid** uncontrolled HTN / arrhythmias |
| **Garcinia cambogia** | 500–1000 mg 3×/day | 2011 meta: ~0.88 kg over 2–12 weeks | ATP-citrate lyase inhibition; mild appetite suppression. Modest but “psychologically satisfying” |

### Clearfield Clinic Pearl — Weight Maintenance After GLP-1
- High-dose **berberine** ~400–500 mg × **2 capsules twice daily**
- Plus **trans-resveratrol** ~100 mg/day
- Used after GLP-1 / mini-dose programs to blunt regain
- Emphasized: study-backed doses often exceed label suggestions — clinicians must know agents well enough to dose therapeutically
