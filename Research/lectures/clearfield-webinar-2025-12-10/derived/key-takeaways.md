# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-12-10

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Holiday botanicals are modest adjuncts (≈1–3 kg / 8–12 weeks), not GLP-1 replacements** — curcumin, capsaicin, berberine, synephrine, and garcinia stack with peptides, diet, and exercise during seasonal food stress.

2. **Dose to the evidence, not the timid label.** Curcumin 1000–1500 mg/day (± piperine/liposomal); berberine 500 mg 2–3×/day. Underdosing is why patients “tried it and nothing happened.”

3. **Post–GLP-1 maintenance pair (Clearfield clinic):** high-dose berberine (400–500 mg × 2 capsules BID) + trans-resveratrol ~100 mg/day to blunt regain.

4. **Synephrine safety filter:** avoid citrus aurantium protocols in uncontrolled hypertension or arrhythmias.

5. **Epigenetic terrain framing (Saleya):** toxins, nutrient depletion, EMF, and stress drive gene expression; up to ~98% of chronic disease attributed to epigenetic factors in this lecture’s model.

6. **Nano + adaptogen thesis:** particles <100 nm (~20 nm formulas) for bioavailability/mitochondrial delivery; adaptogens for stress resilience and homeostasis — **alongside** medical therapy, never as a quiet substitute.

7. **Oncology adjunct posture:** in the tongue-cancer case, continue chemo/meds, escalate antioxidants/nutrients aggressively, eliminate sugar/inflammatory foods, and negotiate a short observation window with oncology — document and coordinate.

8. **High-dose micronutrients appeared in the cancer protocol** — vitamin D ~50,000 IU and vitamin C ~1,000 mg daily cited; cross-check calcium/Coimbra-style cautions before copying doses.

9. **Start intensive packs with a cleanse runway** (detox tea + kidney/liver/glutathione support ~2 weeks) before piling on Elite-level stacks — reduces overwhelm and “opens” absorption (patient testimonials).

10. **Language and law matter:** reframe “nanotechnology” as “nano scale” for skeptical patients; cyclodextrin is a parallel bioavailability story; compounded semaglutide marketing can draw manufacturer cease-and-desist letters.
