## ⚡ Infographic 2: "Ketamine → Glutamate → mTOR"

**Title:** The Growth-Switch Pathway (Brain-Selective)

```
KETAMINE (IV / IM / IN)
  │
  ├── Blocks NMDA on inhibitory interneuron
  │
  ├── Glutamate SURGE (70–80% of brain signaling vs ~2% serotonin)
  │
  ├── Postsynaptic AMPA + NMDA → Ca²⁺ → kinases / BDNF
  │
  └── mTOR ON → new spines, synapses, rapid mood change
```

**Callouts:**
- Suicidality may drop in **4–5 hours**
- Neuroplasticity window ~**24–72 hours** after infusion
- Classic psychedelics hit mTOR via **5-HT2A**; ketamine does **not**

## 📊 Infographic 3: "Bioavailability Ladder"

**Title:** Route Matters

| Route | Bioavailability | Clinical note |
|---|---|---|
| **IV** | ~100% | Best outcomes focus; Level 1 CANMAT (single IV) |
| **IM** | ~90–95% | Needle-phobia / hard-stick niche |
| **Intranasal** | ~15–48% | Includes Spravato (S-isomer) |
| **Oral** | ~10% | Level 3–4 evidence; bladder risk ↑ with high dose |

**Footer:** Oral first-pass destroys ~90%. Don’t equate mg-for-mg across routes.

## 💉 Infographic 4: "Zen Protocol Snapshot"

**Title:** Induction → Boosters → Individualize

```
INDUCTION (6 IVs)
  2×/week × 3 weeks (non-consecutive)
  Start: 0.5 mg/kg over 40 min
  Sweet spot: 0.7–1.0 mg/kg over 40–60 min
  Ceiling outpatient psych: avoid ≥2 mg/kg

OPTIMIZATION
  Weekly boosters × 4  →  total ~10 treatments
  Cited response push: ~85–87%

THEN
  PRN / monthly / q2–3 months as needed
```

**Durability:** 1 infusion ~7–10 days → after 6 ~5–7 months → after 10 up to ~12 months (some patients)

## 🛡️ Infographic 6: "Safety Guardrails"

**Title:** Low Risk ≠ Zero Risk

**Screen / exclude:** pregnancy · psychosis/mania · aneurysm/ICH history · uncontrolled HTN · Child-Pugh C · unstable active SUD · severe prior ketamine allergy

**Monitor:** continuous VS (± EKG) · ≥30 min post-infusion · no same-day driving

**Prep window:** avoid opioids / benzos / muscle relaxants ~**12 hours** prior

**Network signal:** ~450 REMS clinics → 2 minor O₂ AEs cited; crash cart + ACLS readiness required

**Cost (Zen quote):** ~**$600** / IV out-of-pocket

## 🌿 Infographic 7: "Ibogaine — High-Risk Plasticity Window"

**Title:** What Clinicians Should Know (Not Prescribe Casually)

```
Tabernanthe iboga → ibogaine → noribogaine (long-acting)
Polypharmacy: NMDA + opioid receptors + 5-HT + BDNF/GDNF
Best evidence niche: OUD addiction interruption (observational)
Stanford veterans PTSD signal + Mg cardiac co-research (experimental)
```

**Red banner:** QT prolongation / arrhythmia risk → EKG, electrolytes, **supervised medical setting only**

**Footer:** Neuroplastic disruptor ≠ scalable longevity drug.
