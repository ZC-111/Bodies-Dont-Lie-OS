# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2026-02-11

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **~30% of patients remain TRD** after sequential antidepressants (STAR*D cascade: ~27.5% → 21% → 16% → <7%). FDA TRD = ≥2 failed adequate trials.

2. **Ketamine’s job is to reach brain mTOR** via a glutamate surge (AMPA/NMDA cascade) — synaptogenesis and rapid state change, not SSRI-style monoamine waiting.

3. **Single IV ketamine is CANMAT Level 1** for adult TRD; specialty response rates cluster around **~80%**. Spravato ~**65%** — complementary, not identical.

4. **Suicidality can lift in 4–5 hours**; single-infusion durability ~7–10 days. Completing **6 + 4 boosters (~10)** extends durability toward months and pushes response ~85–87%.

5. **Route is pharmacology:** IV ~100% bioavailability vs oral ~10%. Don’t equate oral troches mg-for-mg with IV; oral = Level 3–4 / bridge use; **avoid unsupervised at-home use**.

6. **Default third-line after ≥2 fails** — skip the line for active suicidality and selected first-responder/military contexts. Screen out pregnancy, psychosis/mania, aneurysm/ICH, uncontrolled HTN, Child-Pugh C.

7. **Cancer is not an automatic ketamine contraindication** — mTOR activation here is brain-selective via glutamate, not a systemic “cancer switch.”

8. **Ibogaine is a high-risk neuroplastic disruptor** (NMDA + opioid + neurotrophic signaling) with best signals in **OUD interruption** — cardiac QT risk demands EKG, electrolytes, and supervised settings only.

9. **VA access is two contracts:** CCN → Spravato; **VCA → IV ketamine**. Awareness gaps block veterans more than policy alone.

10. **Balance mTOR:** episodic brain activation for repair (ketamine/psychedelic-adjacent tools) vs peripheral inhibition for longevity — Goldilocks glutamate (exercise, meditation, sleep) belongs in the aftercare plan.

## Comparison: IV Racemic Ketamine vs Spravato (Esketamine)

| Dimension | IV racemic ketamine | Spravato (S-ketamine) |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence tier (CANMAT framing)** | Single IV = Level 1 | Non-IV tiers lower / distinct |
| **Typical response cited** | ~80% | ~65% |
| **FDA labels** | Off-label for mood (anesthetic on-label) | TRD + suicidality |
| **Flexible indications** | PTSD, anxiety, pain (evidence-guided) | Narrower labeled uses |
| **Insurance** | Often cash / VCA for veterans | More often covered; REMS |
| **Induction cadence** | ~6 over ~3 weeks | ~8 over ~4 weeks, then weekly ×4 |
| **Maintenance** | Individualized PRN | Weekly to q4 weeks |
| **Bioavailability** | ~100% | Intranasal ~15–48% |
| **Best for** | Speed, depth, complex comorbidity | Insured long-term sustainability |

**Consensus from talk:** Use IV to capture response quickly; bridge to Spravato when coverage and durability favor it.
