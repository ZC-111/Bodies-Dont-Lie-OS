# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-10-29

## Top 10 Practice Pearls

1. **Beachhead first:** One message, one market, one entry offer. Be known as the steakhouse — not the place that also serves Alfredo.

2. **~80/20 of revenue** usually comes from a minority of offerings. Stop rotating weekly "new shiny thing" promotions that confuse patients.

3. **Marketing's job is not leads** — it is customers who pay **premium prices**. Interruptive Facebook lead ads often fail the know/like/trust test.

4. **Ideal cash buyer is 55+** with disposable income; they often spend **4–8×** more than younger outliers and buy on reputation/referrals.

5. **Educate before the visit** with local YouTube/content + testimonials. Prospects research you for **4–6 months** — control that narrative within a **25–50 mile** radius.

6. **Sell signature packages, never à la carte** for core programs. Patients want to be told the full plan (bypass analogy), not piece-priced steps that feel like insurance medicine.

7. **Pricing rule:** all delivery costs ≤ **25%** of fee. Stop setting prices by polling underpaid colleagues at conferences.

8. **Raise prices 25%** as a near-term experiment — Gallogly claims conversions often *increase*; higher fees also raise compliance.

9. **Cash velocity:** collect when the patient says yes (not only day-of). Case: Ash Goyle waitlisted 60 days with prepayment; $10K → $125–135K/month cash.

10. **Outcomes over greed framing:** time with patients + coherent protocols drive results; profitable cash systems fund better care and protect against insurance clawbacks.
