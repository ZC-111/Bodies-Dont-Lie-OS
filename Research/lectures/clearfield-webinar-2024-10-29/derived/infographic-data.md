## 💳 Infographic 5: "Premium Pricing Rules"

**Title:** Stop Polling Broke Doctors at Conferences

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cost ceiling** | Delivery costs ≤ **25%** of price ($250 cost → ≥ $1,000 fee) |
| **Sell packages** | Never à la carte for signature programs |
| **No discount culture** | Discounts signal low confidence |
| **Immediate test** | Raise prices **25%** tomorrow — conversions often rise |
| **Compliance upside** | Higher price → higher adherence |

**Quote card:** "Your price tells your prospect everything they need to know about your skill set."
