## Segment 4 — The Stairway System: Beachhead (35:00–45:55)

**Speaker:** Matthew Gallogly

### ★ Core Framework
**Stairway System** (5 rungs; webinar covers first three deeply):
1. **Beachhead**
2. **Educate & Excite**
3. **Convert**
4. High-end offers (preview)
5. Monthly recurring revenue (preview)

### Overarching Priority
> First and foremost: **amazing patient outcomes**. More time with patients / learning → better care. Money and time-off are consequences of a system that supports outcomes — not the primary goal.

### Insurance Risk Context
- Client example: **$2.5M insurance clawback** threat → negotiated to **$750K**; cash system enabled same-day wire

### Beachhead Principle
- Military analogy (Normandy): concentrate force on a narrow toehold
- Business rule: **one message → one market → one offer**
- **Offer** = what gets them *into* the office (not necessarily everything you sell after)

### Problem: Too Many Offers
- Rotating weekly promotions (weight loss → red light → Alzheimer's device → new IV) confuses market
- **Mexican restaurant serving Fettuccine Alfredo** metaphor: if you claim to do everything, patients trust nothing
- Be **Ruth's Chris** (known for steak), not a place that does steak + seafood + everything poorly
- **80/20:** ~80% of revenue typically comes from ~20% of offerings

## Segment 5 — Educate & Excite: Know, Like, Trust (45:55–58:00)

**Speaker:** Matthew Gallogly

### Marketing Purpose Reframe
> Purpose of advertising is **not** to get a lead — it is to get a **customer who pays premium prices**.

### Ideal Cash Patient
- Core buyer: **age 55+** (upper-middle-class disposable income)
- Spend **4–8× more** than typical 40-year-olds
- Buy on reputation / referrals from friends & family (outdoor kitchen example: hired most expensive recommended contractor)

### Why Facebook Lead Ads Underperform
- Interruptive; "make you go away" form fills; weak know/like/trust
- Interruptive leads → price shopping, low premium conversion
- Gallogly's clients often **don't rely on traditional paid ads**

### Content / Video Strategy
- Prospects already search YouTube (#2 search engine) for: knee pain, menopause weight gain, fatigue, brain fog, etc.
- Active searchers are more willing to pay premium for solutions
- Use video for: education, in-room indoctrination, follow-up, **testimonials**
- Prospects typically **research / "stalk" you for 4–6 months** before calling — control that narrative
- Dominate **25–50 mile radius** around practice (not influencer vanity metrics)

### Case: Dr. Joshua Schachter (Wichita Falls, TX)
- Former Chief of Surgery; oversaw ~**16,000** joint replacements; left insurance Jan 1
- Now ~**$235K/month** cash; takes **2 weeks off every 6 weeks**
- Military town ~50K people; packages **$15–30K** (biologics, functional medicine, or combo)
- **>40% referral** business; 7-minute Indoctrination video before provider enters room
- Example sale: **$37K** package (1-year wellness + multi-joint BMAC/PRP; spouse also bought wellness)

## Segment 6 — Convert: Scripts, Signature Solutions, Premium Pricing (58:00–1:06:00)

**Speaker:** Matthew Gallogly

### Pricing Reality Check
- Most doctors set prices by polling other underpaid doctors at conferences ("PRP is ~$750")
- **Cost rule:** all procedure costs (kits, labs, staff, hormones, etc.) should be **≤25% of price**
  - Example: $250 cost → charge **≥ $1,000**

### Conversion Keys
1. **Scripts** — advise, guide, recommend (not "sales class" theater)
2. **Signature solution** — packaged protocol, not à la carte
3. **Premium price** — no discounting culture

### Why À La Carte Fails
- Patients cherry-pick ("just the blood test / just the cleanse") and disappear
- Feels like insurance medicine: MRI → PT → steroid → wait 18 months
- Quadruple-bypass analogy: you wouldn't accept "start with one artery" or "2 arteries full price / 2 at 50% off"
- **55+ patients want one-stop solutions** — give credit card once, get a complete plan

### Case: Dr. Amy Duffy (Clemson, SC — functional medicine)
- 2022: **$1.2M** revenue; top package **$5,500**
- After Gallogly's event advice: raise to **$10K** → two sales that next Monday
- 2023: **$2.4M** (doubled); no longer sees patients personally; packages + scripts only

### Compliance Effect
> The more patients pay, the more compliant they become — psychological / placebo stake in the outcome.
