## 4. 60-second video script

**Hook (0–5s):** “Stuck in chronic illness? Stop collecting random protocols — pick a pillar.”  
**Beat 1 (5–20s):** Halasa’s CDPM: Redox, Immune, Metabolic, Regenerative — lead with what’s primary.  
**Beat 2 (20–35s):** Measure oxidative stress with endothelial/ANS scores; don’t candy-shop detox labs.  
**Beat 3 (35–50s):** Immune: modulate Th1/Th2/Th17 — steroids aren’t the same as a map. Homocysteine needs the full quartet.  
**CTA (50–60s):** “Ask your team: which pillar first — and how will we measure it?”

## 6. Patient takeaway card

**Title:** A simpler map for complex care  

**Plain-language bullets (5):**
1. Big problems often need four lenses: stress/toxins, immune balance, metabolism/hormones, and repair.  
2. Ask which lens is *first* for you — not every therapy on day one.  
3. “Detox” should match *your* history — not a random full panel.  
4. If homocysteine is high, B12 alone is usually not enough.  
5. If you’re depleted or post-injury, starting low and going slow for months can matter.

**Ask your clinician:** “Which pillar are we leading with, and what are we measuring?”  
**Avoid / myth bust:** “More supplements = faster healing”; “Steroids fix immune imbalance long-term.”  
**Disclaimer:** use block above. Keep doses off patient cards unless clinician-directed.

## 7. Clinician pearl card

**Title:** CDPM intake — Halasa framework  

**Pearls (5, denser):**
1. Route by lead pillar; allow overlap (e.g. IN progesterone = metabolic + immune).  
2. Baseline endothelial/ANS/sudomotor; re-score interventions.  
3. Immune: lymphocyte + cytokine map before long-term suppression; LDN as Th17 tool framing.  
4. Redox: cyclodextrin for fat-soluble/spike/mycotoxin claims; charcoal/EDTA for water-soluble.  
5. Metabolic: HOMA goal &lt;1.9; Hcy quartet (methylB12 + folate/folinic + P5P + betaine); TBI starts at ¼–½ dose × 3–6 mo.

**Monitor:** pillar scores, Abs 8–12 wk if holding LDN, hormone ratios, Hcy.
