## Segment 2 — Hormone Testing Logistics & TBI Dosing Lessons (~16:00–~26:00)

**Speakers:** Bill Clearfield, Speaker 6 (audience), panel

### Hormone Testing
- **Menstruating patients:** serum ideally **days 19–21** (progesterone peak + secondary estradiol peak)
- **Irregular cycles / unclear timing:** 28-day saliva series (~11 samples) to map the curve vs. a normal menstrual graph
- Serum is a “snapshot”; experience still guides interpretation after ~25 years of practice
- Upcoming online women’s hormone faculty class (Dr. Chynn, Dottie, Tara Scott + junior faculty)

### TBI / Depleted Patients — Dosing Pearl
> Start at **half or quarter doses**. More than half of depleted/TBI patients do well without full replacement doses. Expect **3–6 months** before judging failure; long-term improvement cited ~**90–95%**.

- Mentor lesson (Joel case / Dr. Gordon training): do not try to “fix everything in two weeks”

## Segment 4 — Dr. Halasa: CDPM Four-Pillar Framework (~29:25–~50:00)

**Speakers:** Dr. Halasa (Speaker 7), Dr. Joseph James, Bill Clearfield

### ★ Core Thesis
> Approach **any** chronic disease through four overlapping pillars: **Redox · Immune · Metabolic · Regenerative**. Prioritize by which pillar is primary for *this* patient, then sweep the others.

### Context
- AMA-adjacent push toward multi-system “360 HP” history/physical for complex disease (COVID as exemplar of multi-system failure by siloed specialty care)
- Halasa’s CDPM framing aims to give integrative medicine a **shared language, protocols, and publishable structure**
- Open invitation to co-author / use AI to convert slide decks → NIH-style articles (World Health / nonprofit channel mentioned)

### Pillar Priority Logic (examples)
| Dominant presentation | Lead pillar | Then… |
|---|---|---|
| High sugar / lipids / sex-hormone imbalance | Metabolic | Redox → Immune → Regenerative |
| Anti-NMDA encephalitis, RA, allergy polarity | Immune | Metabolic deficits, redox sources |
| EMF brain fog, toxin-heavy OS | Redox | Immune modulation (e.g., LDN), hormones |
| Post-cleanup healing / cancer as “abnormal regen” | Regenerative (last) | After metabolic + immune + redox addressed |

**Overlap pearl:** Intranasal **progesterone** is “metabolic” replacement *and* an **immune modulator / anti-inflammatory** for brain inflammation — do not rigidly silo.

## Segment 5 — Redox Assessment, Toxins & Cyclodextrin (~50:00–1:08)

**Speaker:** Dr. Halasa

### Measuring Oxidative Stress
1. **Direct antioxidants:** GSH redox, NAD status (when available)
2. **Damage metabolites:** e.g., urinary 8-oxo-guanosine (oxidized nucleotides)
3. **Functional vascular readout:** finger PPG / endothelial function after brachial occlusion → compliance/elasticity score (mild / moderate / severe)
4. **ANS + sudomotor** as OS-sensitive unmyelinated-fiber assays (see Segment 6)

**Severe endothelial dysfunction ↔ insulin resistance risk** (receptor “rusting” by free radicals)

### Free-Radical Generators Filed Under Redox
- Chronic infection (Lyme, EBV, etc.) — toxins → radicals → later immune polarization
- Heavy metals / petrochemicals / plastics / organophosphates
- Mycotoxins; spike protein (infection or vaccine context)
- Poorly metabolized drugs (pharmacogenetics → toxic metabolites)

### Fat-Soluble vs Water-Soluble Detox
- Charcoal / EDTA-class binders: better for **water-soluble** toxins
- Sweating / sauna: partial for fat-soluble; limited alone
- **Cyclodextrin:** HDL-like cage (lipophilic core, hydrophilic shell) — “trash truck” for fat-soluble toxins → bile/feces
  - Also a **drug-delivery** vehicle (intranasal progesterone, oxytocin, IV/topical/ocular compounding)
  - Preferred adjunct during **GLP-1 / weight-loss** fat mobilization (toxin dump risk)
  - Claimed utility for **mycotoxins** and **spike protein** chelation/clearance (speaker-stated; literature referenced verbally)

### Practice Guardrail
> No “candy-shop” blanket detox. History + exam decide which toxin labs (mycotoxin, metals, spike, EMF) — prioritize complaints first.

## Segment 6 — Endothelial / ANS / Sudomotor as Clinic “Stethoscope” (~1:08–1:17)

**Speaker:** Dr. Halasa

### Why Every Integrative Clinic Should Own a System
- Endothelial cells = most OS/inflammation-sensitive tissue → early, scoreable, **subclinical** detection
- Use scores to **monitor** any intervention (ozone, exosomes, HRT, detox) over weeks–months
- Big Pharma already uses similar vascular/ANS endpoints for drug effect monitoring
- FDA-cleared class of devices referenced (brand not named — CME framing)

### Autonomic Neuropathy Signals
- Orthostatic brain fog / dizziness = failed sympathetic HR rise
- Sleep-onset failure = can’t switch sympathetic → parasympathetic
- Goal is **variable** HRV, not a fixed “always 75 bpm”

### Sudomotor Plate
- Sweat conductance → sympathetic cholinergic (ACh → NO → vasodilation) function in feet
- Dilute/non-conductive sweat → sympathetic neuropathy → implies unmyelinated C-fiber pain neuropathy risk
- Same platform often includes **ABI** + bioimpedance **fat/muscle ratio** (obesity as OS driver)

## Segment 7 — Immune Mapping & Modulation (Not Suppression) (~1:17–~2:00)

**Speakers:** Dr. Halasa, Dr. Joseph James, panel

### ★ Modulation vs Suppression
> Map polarization (**Th1 / Th2 / Th17** via lymphocyte map + cytokine profile), then **inhibit the overactive arm** — do not blanket-suppress (steroids ↑ cancer/infection risk; push Th17/Th2 in cancer).

### Quick CBC Differential Clues
| Pattern | Suggests |
|---|---|
| Neutrophilia | Th17 / bacterial / fungal flavor |
| Lymphocytosis | Th1 / viral / anti-tumor cytotoxic |
| Basophils / mast cells ↑ | Th2 / allergy / parasite |

**COVID/spike twist:** Spike–ACE2 / AT1 signaling can **fool** macrophages → Th17/Th2 instead of expected Th1 → eosinophilia misread as “bacterial.”

### Cytokine Compass
- ↑ IL-6 → Th17
- ↑ IFN-γ + IL-12 → Th1
- ↑ IL-4 → Th2

### Agents Discussed (speaker-stated mechanisms)
| Agent | Polarization note |
|---|---|
| **Low-dose naltrexone (LDN)** | Strong Th17 inhibitor; useful both Th1- and Th2-dominant disease *when Th17 co-drives* |
| **Ivermectin, methylene blue, ABA therapy** | Th17 inhibition cited |
| **Thymosin α-1** | Polarizes toward Th1 — **favored in cancer** |
| **Thymosin β-4 / β-2** | Toward Treg/Th2 — **avoid in cancer**; may help material/autoimmune contexts differently |
| **Antifungals / some tetracyclines** | Kill pathogen + Th17 down |
| **Hypericin** (yellow photosensitizer supplement) | CD8 / Th17-relevant + anti-cancer AMPK/mTOR notes |
| **Antihistamines** | Inhibit Th2 → may help shift toward Th1 in cancer framing |

**Cancer layers (regenerative pillar):** not keto *or* ivermectin *or* antifungals alone — multi-layer (anti-fibrotic, anti-platelet, PDT, checkpoint Abs, metabolism, Th1 polarization). Prefer **circulating tumor cells** over PET for treatment monitoring when available.

### Steroid Caveat (Joseph James)
Chronic high cortisol / saturated receptors → exogenous steroids may **fail or worsen** inflammation; prefer true modulation (peptides, mapped inhibitors).

## Segment 8 — Metabolic Pillar Deep-Dive: Hormones, Iodine, Genetics (~2:00–~2:10)

**Speaker:** Dr. Halasa

### Metabolic ≠ Only Metabolic Syndrome
Optimize **all endogenous chemistry**: vitamins, minerals, sex steroids, drug metabolism, nutrigenomic SNPs.

### Iodine (5–10 mg cited; not “only thyroid”)
- Halogen displacement (fluoride/bromide/pool exposure) → deficiency common
- Antioxidant enzyme expression; cell differentiation; **aromatase inhibition**
- Pair with **selenium** when starting iodine (thyroid protection; Stefan: front-load Se 200–300 µg × months if worried)
- Counteracts estrogen dominance / cystic breast context (speaker framing)

### Estrogen Dominance
- Report **E1 / E2 / E3 ratios**, not “an estradiol number”
- Estrone = inflammatory / proliferative; fuels prostate/endometrial risk and metabolic syndrome
- Testosterone without progesterone + aromatase control → aromatization in adipose → more estrone
- Tools: progesterone, iodine, lifestyle

### Oxidized LDL Framing
> Atherosclerosis driver emphasized as **oxidized LDL + high-glucose / carb load**, not “LDL a little high” alone → prefer low-carb control of oxidation over reflexive statin for mild LDL elevation *(speaker opinion this night)*.

### Regenerative Status
- Klotho, oxytocin, telomeres, polyphenols; **IL-11** as emerging fibrosis/aging cytokine target
- Cancer filed here as **abnormal regeneration** — treat after/alongside the other three pillars

### Halasa Preferential Plan
1. **Endothelial/ANS baseline on everyone** — objective OS/inflammation score
2. Normalize **sex hormones** first (may fix T4→T3 conversion via redox without immediate thyroid Rx)
3. Screen metals/toxins given occupational history; consider EMF
4. Methylation support for homocysteine (folinic preferred over folic if SNPs/receptor Abs)
5. Do **not** pile every therapy day one

### Clearfield’s Actual Stack (for contrast)

- NAD months; E2 patch 0.0375 2×/wk; progesterone 200 mg (not progestins); T 20 mg IM weekly

**Disagreement pearl:** Halasa would **withhold thyroid hormone** initially (OS + low sex hormones driving poor conversion / more autoimmunity if you push thyroid early). Clearfield treats Abs + HRT aggressively and holds LDN in reserve.
