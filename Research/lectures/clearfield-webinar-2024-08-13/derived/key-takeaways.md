# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-08-13

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Frame every chronic illness through four overlapping pillars:** redox, immune, metabolic, regenerative — prioritize by primary pathology, then address the rest.

2. **Endothelial + ANS/sudomotor testing is the integrative “stethoscope.”** Score subclinical oxidative stress and objectively track exosomes, HRT, detox, or ozone over time.

3. **Cyclodextrin is the standout fat-soluble detox / delivery tool** (mycotoxin & spike claims included). Pair during weight-loss toxin release; charcoal/EDTA still for water-soluble loads.

4. **Modulate, don’t suppress.** Lymphocyte map + cytokine profile → inhibit the overactive Th1/Th2/Th17 arm (LDN/ivermectin/methylene blue as Th17 tools). Steroids blanket-suppress and can worsen cancer polarity.

5. **Cancer needs multi-layer therapy + Th1/cytotoxic bias.** Thymosin α-1 yes; thymosin β toward Treg/Th2 — avoid. CTC monitoring preferred over PET when available.

6. **Intranasal progesterone (+ cyclodextrin) is dual-use:** metabolic replacement *and* brain-sparing immune modulation / GABA sleep / sympathetic downshift.

7. **Iodine 5–10 mg is more than thyroid support** — aromatase inhibition and estrogen-dominance counterbalance; front-load **selenium** if autoimmunity worry.

8. **HOMA-IR goal < 1.9** using `(glucose × insulin)/405`. Clearfield case: Synergy + Paleo drove HOMA 3.3 → 0.99 in ~1 year.

9. **Homocysteine needs the quartet:** methylcobalamin + folinic/folate + P5P + betaine — not B12 alone.

10. **Depleted/TBI patients: start quarter–half doses; give 3–6 months.** Heroic full replacement creates side effects; long-haul improvement cited ~90–95%.
