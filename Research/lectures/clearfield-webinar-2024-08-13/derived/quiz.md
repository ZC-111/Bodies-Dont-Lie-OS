## Section B — Redox, Detox & Functional Testing (7 questions)

### Q7. Why does Halasa classify chronic infections (e.g., Lyme, EBV) under the redox pillar first?
**Answer:** Pathogens act as **free-radical generators** via toxins; inflammation and immune polarization are downstream. Infections still require simultaneous immune empowerment and metabolic targeting of the organism.

### Q8. Which detox modality did Halasa call out as uniquely suited to **fat-soluble** toxins (vs charcoal/EDTA-class binders)?
- A) Activated charcoal alone
- B) **Cyclodextrin** ✓
- C) Oral EDTA only
- D) High-dose vitamin C only

> *HDL-like cage: lipophilic interior, hydrophilic exterior → bile/fecal clearance. Also used as a compounding delivery vehicle.*

### Q9. During GLP-1–assisted weight loss, why pair cyclodextrin (per Halasa)?
**Answer:** Fat mobilization releases stored **fat-soluble toxins** that can aggravate organs; cyclodextrin is proposed to capture them during lipolysis.

### Q10. What does severe endothelial dysfunction imply beyond “vascular stiffness”?
- A) Guaranteed need for CABG
- B) **High oxidative-stress burden and increased insulin-resistance risk** ✓
- C) Purely anxiety-driven HRV change
- D) Normal redox if CRP is normal

### Q11. Sudomotor foot-plate testing primarily assesses which fiber system, and what clinical neuropathy link is drawn?
**Answer:** **Sympathetic cholinergic** innervation of sweat glands (unmyelinated). Poor sweat conductance → sympathetic neuropathy → implies risk to unmyelinated **C-fiber** pain pathways (small-fiber / pain neuropathy).

### Q12. Pharmacogenetic testing was filed under redox because:
- A) Genes directly measure ApoB
- B) **Poorly metabolized drugs can generate free-radical metabolites and side effects** ✓
- C) It replaces endothelial testing
- D) It diagnoses mycotoxins

### Q13. True or False: A fixed heart rate of 75 bpm with no variability is considered a healthy autonomic pattern in this lecture.
**Answer:** **False.** Healthy HRV moves with posture and sleep-stage shifts (sympathetic ↔ parasympathetic). Fixed rate suggests autonomic neuropathy / OS injury to unmyelinated fibers.

## Section C — Immune Modulation & Hormones (7 questions)

### Q14. What is the key difference between immune **modulation** and immune **suppression** in Halasa’s teaching?
**Answer:** Modulation identifies which arm is overactive (Th1 / Th2 / Th17) and inhibits **that** component. Suppression (e.g., high-dose steroids) quashes the whole system → infection/cancer risk and can worsen cancer polarization.

### Q15. Which peptide polarity pairing is correct for cancer care in this talk?
- A) Thymosin β-4 encouraged; thymosin α-1 avoided
- B) **Thymosin α-1 encouraged (→ Th1); thymosin β-4/β-2 avoided (→ Treg/Th2)** ✓
- C) Both thymosins interchangeable
- D) Neither peptide ever used near cancer

### Q16. Low-dose naltrexone is highlighted primarily as an inhibitor of:
- A) Only Th2
- B) Only Treg
- C) **Th17** ✓
- D) Only NK cells

### Q17. How can a CBC with differential give a rough polarization hint?
**Answer:** Neutrophilia → Th17-leaning; lymphocytosis → Th1/cytotoxic; basophil/mast elevation → Th2. COVID/spike can falsely push Th17/eosinophilia despite viral infection.

### Q18. Iodine’s non-thyroid roles emphasized tonight include:
- A) Only raising TSH
- B) **Antioxidant enzyme expression, cell differentiation, and aromatase inhibition / estrogen-dominance counterbalance** ✓
- C) Replacing all HRT
- D) Chelating lead exclusively

### Q19. Speaker 9’s full homocysteine-lowering quartet includes which four components?
**Answer:** **Methylcobalamin + folate/folinic + pyridoxal-5-phosphate (P5P) + betaine (TMG/betaine HCl)** — not B12 monotherapy.

### Q20. In the aluminum-factory / hot-flash case, Halasa’s first objective test for “everyone” and his reason to delay thyroid hormone were:
**Answer:** **Endothelial (and ANS) function testing** as a universal redox/inflammation stethoscope. Delay thyroid Rx because low sex hormones + OS may impair **T4→T3 conversion**; forcing thyroid early may increase sympathetic drive and autoimmunity. Normalize sex hormones ± toxins/methylation first, then reassess.
