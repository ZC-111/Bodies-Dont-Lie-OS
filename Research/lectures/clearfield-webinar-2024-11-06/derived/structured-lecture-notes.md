## Segment 2 — Microbiome as Organ & Diversity Thesis (17:19–27:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner, NMD

### ★ Core Thesis
> Treat the microbiome as an **organ** (~4 lb ecosystem). Chronic Western disease consistently tracks with **low microbial diversity**. Optimize diversity and metabolites — not single “good/bad” bug names.

### Key Concepts
- Ecosystem includes bacteria (>90%), amoebas, protozoa, fungi, occasional parasites
- Gene estimates vary (100:1 → 10:1 → 1:1); Dobner leans ~**10:1 genes**, ~**1:1 microbe:human cell** (~32 trillion each)
- **Rob Knight (UCSD)** TED framing: person-to-person stool whole-genome profiles may share only ~**10%** named-microbe similarity → forensic uniqueness
- 2016 multi-disease survey: immune, metabolic, and cancer cohorts showed **reduced diversity** vs healthy patterns
- Shift from reductionist “name the pathogen” to **ecosystem restoration**

### Clinical Pearl
> Patients often feel “unique but not useful” about their microbiomes. The intervention is usually **add diversity**, not endless eradication cycles.

## Segment 4 — FMT Fundamentals, Mechanisms & Delivery (32:23–41:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner

### Definition & History
- Transfer of stool from healthy donor → GI tract of sick recipient
- Historical roots: 4th-century China (e.g., gastritis); modern clinical research ~early 2000s onward
- Research interest beyond C. diff: neuro, immune, oncology, GI (legal US use constrained — see Segment 7)

### Delivery Routes
| Route | Context | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Colonoscopy | Hospital gold standard | Prep + perforation risk |
| NG tube | Hospital option | — |
| Enema | Common DIY | Variable |
| **Frozen oral capsules** | Dobner preferred outpatient | Avoids procedure |

- **Kao et al., 2017:** colonoscopy vs frozen oral capsules — both **96.2%** effective for indicated use
- Capsule eligibility caveat: patient must be able to swallow capsules

### Proposed Mechanisms
1. **Microbiome modulation / competitive occupancy** — donor community “moves in”
2. **Quorum sensing disruption** — C. diff toxin production when community thinned; donor biomass reduces signaling
3. **Bile acid metabolism** — microbial conjugation; some bile acids reduce C. diff susceptibility (theory still evolving)
4. **Host immunity education** — healthy donor signaling may retrain immune tone (appealing for IBD; not legal indication without IND)

## Segment 5 — Donor Screening: “Cream of the Crop” (38:00–47:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner

### ★ Clinical Pearl
> The **donor is the most important part of FMT**. DIY “ask a buddy to poop” is inadequate.

### Screening Stack
- ~**6-page** questionnaire
- **7-day** diet diary + **7-day** stool tracker (prefer Bristol 4, regularity)
- Interview + physical exam
- Serum + stool labs; **whole-genome sequencing** for diversity scores
- Prefer: vaginal birth, breastfed, good sleep, normal body habitus, no disease history, minimal/no antibiotics, no medications
- Strong preference against C-section donors for *donor bank* (not a judgment that C-section people cannot become healthy)

### Supporting Evidence She Emphasizes
- **2021 antibiotic-class study:** ≥4–5 lifetime antibiotic *classes* → higher Type 2 diabetes incidence
- Fast-food / preservative / residual antibiotic exposure can wreck early post-FMT engraftment (McDonald’s Big Mac day-1 recurrence anecdote → now hard rule: **no fast food ≥1 month** post-FMT)
- Vaginal birth seeds infant with maternal stool; C-section ↑ asthma, allergies, celiac, GI illness, asthma risk signals
- **Maria Domínguez-Bello** work: rectal/stool seating of C-section infants ≈ vaginal-birth-like microbiota more than vaginal swab alone
- Infant stool slurry from older children (~8–9y) within first week also shifted C-section infants toward vaginal-birth-like outcomes
- Ideal: bank maternal stool in third trimester *before* emergency C-section + IV antibiotics
- **First 1,000 days** nutrition (preconception → age 2) for epithelial development

### Hard Finding Quote (colleague)
> Dr. Sabine Hazan (Bay Area): may not truly have “healthy stool” widely available in the US — underscores screening rigor

## Segment 6 — Clinical Cases (C. diff Focus) (28:00–1:35:00 interwoven)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner

### Case A — Late-60s Woman, Recurrent C. diff + Depression
- C. diff after clindamycin for MRSA; 15–20 BMs/day × ~4 months
- Failed **4** vancomycin courses (2× 10-day; 2× month-long pulse tapers)
- History: MDD hospitalizations ×3, SI/attempt; felt “dark cloud” worsen with each antibiotic
- Prep: biofilm disruptors during final vanco taper + **Saccharomyces boulardii**
- FMT: **30 capsules** — 10/day × 3 days, empty stomach after broth-lean prep
- Day-after call: “I don’t want to talk about my poop — **is this what it feels like to be happy?**”
- Teaching point: gut–brain shift can precede (or overshadow) stool metrics within ~24h

### Case B — Pregnant Patient (~22 weeks), Recurrent C. diff
- Recurrent C. diff ~7 months; 3 vanco rounds; still toxin-positive; antibiotics through pregnancy
- Prior IBS, microscopic colitis, food intolerances
- Risk discussion: untreated → infant could be seeded with toxin-laden stool at vaginal birth; miscarriage data sparse
- Protocol: **two** 30-cap FMT courses (multi-recurrence / rising virulence); S. boulardii + L-glutamine; minimized polypharmacy
- 2 weeks: toxin negative; diarrhea may persist (**post-infectious IBS** + high anxiety common)
- 6 weeks: first solid stool in >10 years of IBS pattern; delivered healthy boy at **42 weeks**; child well at ~4 years

### Case C — Late-50s Hospice Patient (pre-2022 stool-bank era)
- Recurrent C. diff; Flagyl ×2 (Dobner: **avoid Flagyl** — high recurrence), fidaxomicin partial, vancomycin ×3
- Comorbid: primary immunodeficiency, post-COVID restrictive lung disease (no breath sounds mid/lower lobes), babesiosis, recurrent UTIs; **120 days** continuous diarrhea; 16 lb weight loss; hospice
- Coordinated 30 capsules split **10/day × 3** (cost constraint)
- After **first 10 capsules**: coughed **softball-sized mucus plug** → mid/lower lobe breath sounds returned; O₂ sats/BP stabilized; “I don’t feel like I’m dying anymore”
- Outcome: discharged from hospice; alive/well ~4 years later
- Teaching: gut–lung axis unexplained but clinically striking

### Case D — Mid-30s Woman, Pan-colectomy / J-pouch, Crohn’s + C. diff
- College rectal bleeding → Crohn’s → pan-colectomy; pouchitis; Humira; chronic Cipro/Flagyl → C. diff
- OpenBiome capsules previously → C. diff–free ~8 years; Campylobacter Rx → C. diff return
- Failed **Vowst** (oral FMT drug, Mar 2023) — fecal incontinence, still toxin+
- Adaptation: thaw capsules slightly so inner gel layer opens earlier (no colon); dose **with small food** (potato) to slow transit; **5 caps/day × 6 days** (hold second bottle)
- Result: toxin negative; **PCR still positive** = carrier; higher recurrence risk; continue biofilm work + S. boulardii + nutrients
- Psych: antipsychotics framed as microbiome insults; discussion of alternatives / future FMT-for-depression evidence (not legal indication)

## Segment 7 — US Regulation, Access & Prep Protocol (52:00–1:07:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner

### Regulatory Timeline
- **2013:** FDA treats FMT as IND for non–C. diff uses
- **2016:** draft relative-donor guidance never finalized (pushback)
- **Nov 2022:** **Rebyota** (rectoally delivered FMT drug) approved → within days FDA guidance: **no stool banks / no third-party distribution**
- Legal outpatient indication: **C. diff not responding to standard therapy**
- Provider must personally identify, screen, curate, and deliver FMT (Dobner: weekends in “poop lab”)

### Commercial Oral Product
- **Vowst** (Mar 2023): 4 oral capsules; pharmaceutical-grade; Case D poor response

### Epidemiology Notes
- Hospital sheets after industrial laundry still carry **60–80%** C. diff spores (Medscape/Medline ~2018–19 citation)
- Recurrence definition: infection again within ~8 weeks; each recurrence ↑ future risk
- Post-FMT: toxin clearance often + PCR negative (organism genetics gone) — ideal

### Dobner Prep / Dosing Pearls
- Stop antibiotics **24h** before FMT
- Day before: liquid/blended diet; coffee OK day-before (decaf if concerned)
- Day of: clear liquids AM; **no coffee** (transit); 10–30 capsules in ~60 min; solids ~2h later (earlier if nausea — avoid vomiting capsules)
- Hold most meds **≥2h** post-FMT; **thyroid meds** exception (circadian dosing)
- Loperamide sometimes used to retain material in high-frequency diarrhea
- Multi-recurrence (≥3): prefer **two** 30-cap courses; classic win pattern **30 = 10+10+10** over 3 days if affordable
- Biofilm disruptors with **quorum-quenching** (e.g., **oral EDTA** in combo products) before/with antibiotic course

## Segment 8 — Post-FMT Support, Nutrition & Metabolites (1:07:00–1:35:00)

**Speaker:** Piper Dobner

### Feed the New Biome
- Fiber-rich plants; sauerkraut, kimchi, other ferments
- No fast food (especially first month)
- **S. boulardii** for recurrence prevention / hospital-acquired C. diff risk reduction (~20B CFU/day with antibiotics in cited study)
- Consortium probiotics OK — **rotate** bottles/strains; don’t stay on one product for months
- Hold multi-strain probiotics ~2–4 weeks after FMT (ecosystem already delivered); S. boulardii may continue
- Epithelial support: vitamins **A, C, E**; zinc, copper, selenium; postbiotics; omegas
- Sleep first — non-negotiable healing window
- Lavender tea as anxiolytic adjunct for post–C. diff anxiety

### Landmark Diet Signals
- **≥30 plant foods/week** vs <10 → higher diversity (teas & spices count)
- Fermented-food arm increased diversity and lowered inflammatory markers more impressively than fiber-only in cited trial (“feed more fibrous food” / FEFI-style framing in talk)

### Metabolite > Taxonomy
- Body-site swab study (>1,000 people): phyla names vary wildly by site, but **metabolic pathways converge**
- **Horizontal gene transfer** can pass butyrate (etc.) production between taxa
- Postbiotics (e.g., *S. cerevisiae* preparations) → antioxidant capacity ↑, T/NK cell function ↑
- Microbes produce B vitamins, K2, neurotransmitter precursors, EPS with immune/anti-oncogene signals
- Vitamin A upregulates **FoxP3** → Treg binding — underrated for gut repair (caution in pregnancy)

### Probiotic Philosophy
- Spores and non-spores both useful; rotate
- *L. rhamnosus* GG well-studied — still don’t fetishize single strains
- Ideal future: metabolite panels on consortia (push at SFI)
