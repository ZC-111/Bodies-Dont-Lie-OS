## Section A — Microbiome Fundamentals (6 questions)

### Q1. How does Dobner primarily frame the gut microbiome clinically?
- A) A collection of named pathogens to eradicate
- B) **An organ-level ecosystem (~4 lb) whose diversity and metabolites matter most** ✓
- C) A sterile tube that only needs enzymes
- D) Relevant only after colonoscopy

> *Bacteria dominate (>90%), but fungi, protozoa, and other organisms complete the ecosystem.*

### Q2. Approximate human cell : microbe cell ratio Dobner favors:
- A) 100:1 microbes to cells
- B) 10:1 microbes to cells
- C) **~1:1 (~32 trillion each)** ✓
- D) Microbes are negligible vs human cells

### Q3. The 2016 multi-disease observation Dobner cites most strongly associates chronic Western illness with:
- A) Excess Bifidobacterium
- B) **Low microbial diversity** ✓
- C) Universal parasite infection
- D) High alpha diversity only in cancer

### Q4. Alpha vs beta diversity — which pairing is correct?
- A) Alpha = between people; beta = within one sample
- B) **Alpha = richness within a sample; beta = comparison across samples/people** ✓
- C) Both mean the same thing
- D) Beta only applies to skin swabs

### Q5. Which medication classes does she explicitly flag as diversity depleters?
**Answer:** NSAIDs, metformin, PPIs, and antipsychotics/psychotropics (among others). She also emphasizes environmental pesticides/glyphosate.

### Q6. True or False: Matching a patient’s stool taxonomy names to a “healthy” list is Dobner’s primary optimization strategy.
**Answer:** **False.** She stresses metabolite/pathway convergence and horizontal gene transfer — **what bugs make** often matters more than **who is named**.

## Section C — Cases, Donors & Aftercare (7 questions)

### Q14. In the late-60s C. diff + depression case, the most striking early outcome after FMT was:

### Q15. Key donor disqualifiers / preferences she emphasizes include:
**Answer (any solid subset):** Prefer vaginal birth + breastfed; Bristol-4 regularity; whole-food/organic diet; no disease history; minimal/no antibiotics or meds; rigorous labs including MDRO screening after the 2019 ESBL E. coli FMT-related death; WGS diversity scores.

### Q16. Pregnant C. diff patient: why was untreated infection a fetal/neonatal microbiome concern?

**Answer:** Patient expelled a large mucus plug, regained mid/lower lobe breath sounds, stabilized sats/BP, left hospice — illustrating unpredictable but powerful gut–lung coupling.
