# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2024-11-06

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Treat the microbiome as an organ.** ~4 lb ecosystem; aim for diversity and metabolites, not endless named-bug whack-a-mole.

2. **Chronic Western disease tracks with low diversity.** Zoom out before diving into single-strain fetishism.

3. **Common drugs deplete biomes** — NSAIDs, metformin, PPIs, antipsychotics — and pesticides/glyphosate compound the loss.

4. **Donor quality is the transplant.** Six-page screen, diet/stool diaries, labs + MDRO + WGS; DIY buddy stool is not equivalent.

5. **Frozen oral FMT capsules ≈ colonoscopy efficacy (~96.2%)** when swallowing is possible — prefer less procedural risk.

6. **US legal outpatient lane = recurrent C. diff failing standard therapy.** Post–Nov 2022: no stool-bank shipping; provider must curate end-to-end.

7. **Avoid Flagyl for C. diff;** prefer vancomycin, then escalate to FMT early — each recurrence raises the next risk. Dobner often moves after one clear failure.

8. **Gut–brain and gut–lung shifts can be dramatic within 24 hours** (depression lift; hospice mucus-plug / lung recovery cases).

9. **Post-FMT: feed diversity** — ≥30 plants/week, fermented foods, no fast food ≥1 month; S. boulardii yes; rotate probiotics; don’t smother a new ecosystem with 12-strain products on day one.

10. **Biofilms + quorum sensing drive recurrence.** Oral EDTA-containing disruptors (not IV) before/with antibiotics; then FMT while the niche is open.

## Debate / Nuance Summary: Capsules vs Colonoscopy vs Drugs

| Dimension | Colonoscopy FMT | Frozen oral capsules | Rebyota / Vowst |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Efficacy (C. diff lane)** | Gold-standard reputation; ~96% in Kao head-to-head | ~96% in same comparison | Pharmaceutical options; case-variable (Vowst fail in J-pouch case) |
| **Burden** | Prep + procedure risk | Swallow burden (10–30 caps) | Vowst = 4 caps; Rebyota rectal |
| **Access post-2022** | Hospital pathways | Provider-curated only (no bank ship) | Prescribable products |
| **Special populations** | Full colon ideal | Adapt thaw/food dosing for J-pouch | May still fail / need customized care |
| **Best for** | Inpatient / can’t swallow | Outpatient recurrent C. diff | When compounding/bank route unavailable |

**Consensus from talk:** Capsules are clinically compelling; regulation and donor logistics — not biology — are the bottleneck.
