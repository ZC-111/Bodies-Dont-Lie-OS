## 🎯 Infographic 3: "FMT Delivery Showdown"

**Title:** Same Cure Rate — Less Procedure Risk

| Route | Typical Setting | Headline |
|---|---|---|
| Colonoscopy | Hospital | Traditional “gold standard” |
| Frozen oral capsules | Outpatient | Dobner preferred |
| Enema | DIY / clinic | Variable technique |
| NG tube | Hospital | Alternative access |

**Evidence callout (Kao 2017):** Colonoscopy ≈ capsules ≈ **96.2%** effective for indicated C. diff use.

**Rule:** If the patient can swallow capsules, avoid unnecessary prep + perforation risk.

## 📋 Infographic 4: "Donor = The Transplant Organ"

**Title:** Six-Page Screen or Don’t Dose

```
MUST-HAVES (aspirational bank)
  ✓ Vaginal birth + breastfed preferred
  ✓ Bristol 4, regular stool tracker (7 days)
  ✓ Diet diary (7 days) — whole / organic / biodynamic lean
  ✓ Sleep, mood, body habitus “healthy human”
  ✓ Minimal–no antibiotics or chronic meds
  ✓ Interview + exam + serum/stool + WGS diversity
  ✓ MDRO screen (post-2019 ESBL death guidance)
```

**Footer:** DIY buddy stool ≠ screened organ. Hazan: truly “healthy US stool” may be rare.

## ⚖️ Infographic 5: "US Access Timeline"

**Title:** Why Your Patient Can’t Just Order Capsules

```
2013  → FDA: FMT = IND outside C. diff pathway
2016  → Relative-donor draft (never finalized)
Nov 2022 → Rebyota approved
         → Days later: no stool banks / no middlemen
Mar 2023 → Vowst oral FMT drug arrives
NOW     → Provider must screen + curate + deliver
         → Legal outpatient target: recurrent C. diff
```

**Callout:** Cash-pay does **not** automatically authorize non–C. diff indications.

## 🥗 Infographic 7: "Feed the New Tenants"

**Title:** Post-FMT Non-Negotiables

```
DO
  • ≥30 plant foods / week (tea + spices count)
  • Fermented foods daily if tolerated
  • S. boulardii (esp. with antibiotics / recurrence risk)
  • A–C–E, Zn, Cu, Se for epithelium
  • Sleep as therapy #1
  • Lavender tea for C. diff anxiety

DON’T
  • Fast food (hard ban month 1)
  • Same probiotic bottle for months
  • Pile oregano / SIBO stacks on day 0
  • Months of daily coffee enemas
```

**Evidence vibe:** Fermented-food diets ↑ diversity + ↓ inflammatory markers more than fiber-only in cited trial.
