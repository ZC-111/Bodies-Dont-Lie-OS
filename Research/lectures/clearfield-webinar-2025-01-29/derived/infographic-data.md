## 📏 Infographic 2: "ALA-Equivalent Dose Ladder"

**Title:** Volume → Approximate ALA Equivalency

| Poly-MVA | ≈ ALA equivalency | Typical use tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 mL | ~40 mg | Ultra-sensitive / drops titration |
| 5 mL | ~200 mg | ★ Common test / start |
| 10 mL | ~400 mg | Autoimmune ceiling often enough |
