## Segment 4 — LAMC Mechanism, Evidence & Dosing Ladder (~25:00–1:11:00)

**Speaker:** Al Sanchez

### ★ What Is the Complex?
- **Lipoic acid mineral complex (LAMC)** = Poly-MVA core
- Bonds **alpha-lipoic acid (ALA)** + **thiamine (B1)** via **palladium** bridge → unique redox / electron-shuttle molecule
- Also carries B vitamins, folate, methionine — multi-ingredient cocktail
- Bound complex is **not light-sensitive** (unlike free ALA in a bag)
- Dark “Coca-Cola” color primarily from combined B vitamins — not a dirty solution

### Mechanism Highlights
- Drives mitochondrial cascade / electron shuttling → ↑ intracellular oxidation via H₂O₂ component **without requiring O₂** the way ozone or high-dose C do
- Stabilizes metabolics so cells exit “panic / low battery” mode
- Best as **synergist / driver**, not standalone silver bullet
- Set the table first: hydration, membrane lipids, K⁺, Mg²⁺, then drive with LAMC
- Works with chord / sulfate IV protocols; **neutral pH, low osmolarity**

### ALA-Equivalent Ladder (transcript)
| Poly-MVA volume | Approx. ALA equivalency |
|---|---|
| 1 mL | ~40 mg |
| 5 mL | ~200 mg |
| 10 mL | ~400 mg |
| 20 mL | ~800 mg |
| 40 mL | ~1600 mg |

### Evidence / Use Cases Cited
- **MS fatigue** (Dr. Krupp)
- **HIV fatigue** (Dr. Blick)
- Palliative care QOL / sleep / neurologic energy demand
- **ALS-associated fatigue**
- Fluoroquinolone-injury support (with nutritional IVs, glutathione, NAD)
- **Radiation studies** at 10–20 mL: preserved antioxidant activity, DNA/chromosome protection, spleen colony formation, less weight loss, **enhanced** radiotherapy kill of target cells; inflammatory markers still reduced **18 weeks** post-exposure at 10 mL
- Athletic recovery (not peak speed — faster mitochondrial restore)
- Veterinary origins for IM / topical / ocular routes (easier than finding veins)

### Dosing Philosophy
- Typical start / test: **5 mL**; sensitive patients may need **1 mL** or drops
- Oncology patients often tolerate middle–high doses; autoimmune / PANDAS / mold-sensitive → **low and slow**
- Prefer **consistent divided dosing** over one mega dose (e.g., don’t chase 50–60 mL once)
- Extremes cited: one patient needed ≥4 oz/day; another needed months to titrate to 40 mL from half-teaspoon
- Children: Clark’s rule; clinics may use lower daily IV doses 5×/week behind other therapies

### Stacking Protocols (Anderson-style example)
| Day | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Mon | Nutrient protocol → LAMC |
| Tue | IPT + glutathione (AM/PM mitochondrial support still oral) |
| Wed | Oxidative day + oral LAMC |
| Thu | IPT + glutathione → nutrient |

- Oxidative strategies (artemisinin, high-dose C, ozone, peroxide): often **LAMC before** or sandwich oral pre/post
- Light therapies: LAMC is a **photosensitizer** in yellow / green / blue wavelengths (“Coca-Cola protocol” with MAH / Weber-type IV light)
- PRP / prolotherapy locals: B-vitamin base + LAMC advances the cocktail

## Segment 5 — Extended Q&A: Safety, Routes, Compatibility (1:11:00–1:45:00)

**Speakers:** Al Sanchez, Dr. Nario, panel

### Palladium
- Bridge mineral only — **not free**, body does not use Pd as nutrient
- Analogized to cobalt in cobalamin
- **Heavy-metal testing BEFORE starting** — Pd will spike labs and alarm patients
- Not nephro/hepatotoxic per presented IND/safety narrative; still useful to track LFTs/kidneys as **response** markers

### Monitoring
- Metabolites / organic acids, fatigue/QOL scales
- Inflammatory markers (CRP, homocysteine)
- MTHFR status not a barrier; B-vitamin improvements reported

### Oral IPT Dosing
- Minimum **10 mL BID**; maximum **~20 mL**
- Food not required; **water** improves gut transit/absorption; complex designed not to break down in gut

### Radiation Patients
- Give **before and after** RT; nutritional/IV support if oncologist allows
- Oral larger pre/post doses when same-day IV stacking impractical
- Guideline mention: separate LAMC from some IVs by **2–6 hours** — oral bridge if schedule tight; even 3–10 mL push better than nothing

### EBO / Ozone Stacking
- Prefer LAMC **in blood before** oxygenation
- Ozone bubbled through Poly does not destroy the molecule; blood darkens slightly, not black

### Carriers & Mixing Rules
| Compatible carriers | Do **not** co-bag |
|---|---|
| Normal saline, D5W, lactated Ringers | Glutathione, methylene blue (stack sequentially, not same bag) |

### IV Access
- Compounding still available in some states: e.g., **Drug Crafters (Frisco, TX)**, **Harbor Health (WA)** — Sanchez to circulate updated list
- Office filtration: **0.2 µm** disc filter; document SOPs/state rules

### Other Routes
- IM starter (esp. if clinic already gives B12 shots)
- Nebulized (COVID-era experience)
- Topical / SQ regenerative
- **Eye drops** (compounded): veterinary equine ocular cancer origin; human dry-eye anecdotes

### Beta-Oxidation & Cachexia (Dr. Bennett)
- Cachexia = cells cannibalize tissue when fuel/energy fails — hard landslide
- Goal: **normalize mitochondrial pathways** so tissue isn’t used as fuel; more calories alone insufficient
- Unchecked β-oxidation can strip membranes → muscle/tissue breakdown; LAMC hoped to supply alternate energetic support

### Contraindications / Cautions
- Avoid stacking **extra free ALA**
- Separate **heavy oxidative days** by **~24–48 hours** when both used aggressively
- Free high-dose oral ALA (~900 mg chronic) linked to thyroid suppression — LAMC argued safer due to B-cofactor balance
- Hypersensitivity rare (~2 severe cases in Sanchez’s career); optional **skin or sublingual** test for highly sensitive patients
- Hashimoto’s / EBV / Lyme / mold: yes — usually **≤10 mL** needed; titrate; Nario often uses 10–40 mL in mold when stacking

### Greek / In-Vitro Chemosensitivity Tests
- Poor model for vitamin C, glutathione, Poly — need whole-organism context
- Better for chemo selection; ~90% of in-vitro nutraceutical signals fail clinically
- Clinical judgment over petri-dish “no”
