# Key Takeaways — Clearfield Webinar 2025-01-29

## Top 10 Clinical Pearls

1. **Poly-MVA is a lipoic acid mineral complex (LAMC)** — ALA + thiamine bridged by palladium — built as a mitochondrial **driver/synergist**, not a standalone cure.

2. **Start ~5 mL and titrate.** Sensitive (mold/autoimmune) patients may need 1 mL or drops; many autoimmune cases never need >10 mL. Consistency beats mega single doses.

3. **ALA-equivalent ladder:** ~40 mg/mL → 5 mL≈200, 10≈400, 20≈800, 40≈1600 mg. Useful for clinicians who already think in ALA terms.

4. **Stack, don’t solo.** Best outcomes framed with IPT, nutrients, glutathione, oxidative therapies, light, and RT support — “DJ after the table is set.”

5. **Radiation adjunct:** dose before and after RT; cited protection of DNA/mitochondria without blocking tumor-kill; inflammatory benefit persists weeks later.

6. **Mixing rules:** NS / D5W / LR OK; **never co-bag** glutathione or methylene blue; separate heavy oxidative days ~24–48h; avoid extra free ALA.

7. **Palladium will spike heavy-metal labs** — test before starting; explain cobalamin/cobalt analogy so patients don’t panic.

8. **Mold brain fog** was an early real-world win in this webinar (Eric); Nario stacks higher volumes (10–40 mL) when clinically appropriate.

9. **Hashimoto’s tip:** EBV + low vitamin D → antibody flares; prioritize keeping vitamin D from dipping; Poly can support mitochondrial side of autoimmune fatigue.

10. **Cachexia logic:** stop tissue cannibalization by restoring mitochondrial energy pathways — calories alone don’t reverse the landslide.

## Comparison: Free ALA vs LAMC (Poly-MVA)

| Dimension | Free ALA | LAMC / Poly-MVA |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Single molecule | ALA–thiamine–Pd complex + B cofactors |
| **Light stability** | Degrades in light | Stable in bottle / IV light use |
| **Role in protocol** | Antioxidant nutrient | Metabolic driver / redox shuttle |
| **Standalone use** | Common | Discouraged as sole therapy |
| **Thyroid caution** | High chronic oral ALA (~900 mg) flagged | Framed as cofactor-balanced, lower runaway risk |
| **Color** | Typical yellow | Dark “Coca-Cola” (B vitamins) |
| **Monitoring quirk** | Standard | Pre-test metals (palladium spike) |

**Consensus from lecture:** Prefer LAMC as stacked mitochondrial support; don’t treat it like bottled ALA swaps.
