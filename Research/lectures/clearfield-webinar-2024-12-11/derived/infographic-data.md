## 🎯 Infographic 1: "The Soy Serving Ladder"

**Title:** How Much Soy Is Enough?

| Intake | Isoflavones (approx.) | Soy Protein (approx.) | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 traditional serving** | ~25 mg | ~7–8 g | Daily baseline (cup soy milk or 3–4 oz tofu) |
| **★ 2 servings/day** | ~50 mg | ~15–25 g | Efficacy threshold in most epi/clinical benefit studies |
| **25 g soy protein** | Variable | **25 g** | FDA heart-health claim dose |
| **~4 servings/day** | ~100 mg | ~30–40 g | Practical upper limit (diversity, not proven toxicity) |
| **Extreme case reports** | ~360 mg | Very high | Feminization anecdotes — not normal intake |

**Footer:** Rule of thumb — **3–4 mg isoflavones per gram soy protein**.

## ❤️ Infographic 3: "Soy & Heart — Dual Mechanisms"

**Title:** Protein + Oil, Two Different Levers

```
SOY PROTEIN (intrinsic)
  └── Direct LDL ↓ ~3–4%
  └── FDA claim (1999): 25 g/day

SOY FOODS displacing SFA meats (extrinsic)
  └── Additional LDL ↓ → combined ~6–8% (Messina)
      (Jenkins estimate was 8–10%)

SOYBEAN OIL
  └── ~62% PUFA; both linoleic + ALA
  └── FDA heart claim (2017)
  └── >7% of US calories; >40% of US essential FA intake
```

**Footer:** Each 1% LDL reduction ≈ 1–2% CHD risk reduction over time.

## 🎗️ Infographic 4: "Breast Cancer — Fear vs Data Arc"

**Title:** From Mouse Scare to Survivor Guidance

```
1990s–2000s     Athymic mouse models → fear for ER+ survivors
Clinical RCTs   No ↑ breast density or proliferation (even 100–235 mg)
2009 Shanghai   Post-diagnosis soy → ↓ death & recurrence (n≈5,000)
Meta-analyses   ~26% ↓ recurrence (esp. postmenopausal, ER+)
ACS 2022        Post-diagnosis isoflavones ↔ lower recurrence
AICR            Soy = one of 5 factors that may ↓ BC mortality
```

**Messina pearl:** Maximal *prevention* benefit likely needs **childhood/adolescence** soy intake.

**Contrast:** Combined HT ↑ cell proliferation 4–10× in 12 weeks — soy does not.

## 🧬 Infographic 5: "Isoflavones ≠ Estrogen"

**Title:** Phytoestrogen Label Does Not Equal Hormone Action

| Endpoint (postmenopausal RCTs) | Hormone estrogen | Soy isoflavones |
|---|---|---|
| Endometrial thickness | ↑ | No effect |
| Vaginal maturation index | ↑ | No effect |
| FSH | ↓ | No effect |
| Circulating estrogen | ↑ | No effect |

**Receptor note:** Preferential **ERβ** binding (anti-proliferative) vs ERα.

**Hot-flash tip:** Native ratio ≈ **50% genistein / 40% daidzein / 10% glycitein**. Glycitein-heavy 1990s pills failed; high-genistein supplements worked (2012 meta).

## 👨 Infographic 6: "Will Soy Feminize Men?"

**Title:** Clinical Trials vs Internet Myths

| Evidence type | Finding |
|---|---|
| **41 RCTs, ~1,700 men** | No change in total T, free T, E2, estrone |
| **3 semen RCTs (healthy men)** | No adverse sperm/semen effects |
| **2 oligospermia case reports** | 80 mg/day isoflavones × 3 mo → count normalized |
| **Gynecomastia cases** | ~360 mg/day (~9× Japanese male intake) |

**Footer:** Typical vegan intake ~**20 mg/day** — nowhere near problem doses.

## 🛢️ Infographic 7: "Seed Oil Claims — Scorecard"

**Title:** The Hateful Eight on Trial

| Accusation | Verdict (per Messina) |
|---|---|
| Modern / processed | True (acknowledge) |
| High n-6:n-3 ratio harmful | **Irrelevant metric** |
| Causes inflammation | **Refuted** (RCT metas 2012/2017/2021) |
| Causes oxidative stress *in vivo* | **Not supported**; store oils properly |
| Ecological “oils up = disease up” | Confounding (like TV stations ↔ cancer) |
| Replace SFA with n-6 PUFA | **Improves lipids, CVD risk** (Nordic review 2024) |

**AHA linoleic target:** 5–10% of calories — US already mid-range (~7.5–8% PUFA).

## 📦 Infographic 8: "Nova Trap — Why Tofu ≠ Twinkie"

**Title:** Ultra-Processed Is Not a Nutrient Score

```
Fruit + milk smoothie          → Nova Group 1
Same smoothie + protein scoop  → Nova Group 4 (UPF)

Cow’s milk                     → Group 1
Soy milk                       → Group 4

Yet soy milk vs cow’s milk (17 RCTs):
  LDL −6% | HDL +4% | SBP −8 | DBP −5 | CRP −22%
```

**Action:** Judge soy foods by **clinical outcomes**, not processing labels.
