### Q5. What labs does Nario recommend monitoring when patients use LL-37 peptides?
**Answer:** CMP and CBC (hepatic/renal handling), plus inflammatory and infection markers (WBC, CRP/ESR). No routine commercial LL-37 blood assay was offered.
