# Nutrition Research

## Purpose

Hold source material for food, diet, fasting, and **nutritional supplements** (vitamins, minerals, probiotics, nutraceuticals).

## What Belongs Here

- Food / dietary / fasting PDFs and papers
- Protocol notes (intermittent fasting, elimination, therapeutic diets)
- **Vitamins, minerals, multivitamins, absorption research**
- **Supplements & nutraceuticals** (probiotics, peptides-as-supplements, coral calcium, Emergen-C–class products)
- Clinical nutrition / MNT coding references when research-relevant

## Does Not Belong Here

- Herbals, essential oils, flower essences, tinctures, homeopathy → [materia-medica/](../materia-medica/README.md)
- Mold / mycotoxins / autoimmune → [immune/](../immune/README.md)
- Patient charts, NuSkin signup contracts, receipts → stay out of Research (practice ops)

## Branch Rules

- Name the branch **nutrition**, not “diet”, “fasting”, or “vitamins” — those are topics under it
- Link to [immune/](../immune/README.md) when nutrition modulates immune/autoimmune outcomes
- Link to [materia-medica/](../materia-medica/README.md) when a protocol mixes herbs + vitamins
