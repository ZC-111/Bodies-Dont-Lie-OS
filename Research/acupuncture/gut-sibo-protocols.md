# Gut & SIBO — Acupuncture Protocols (Adjunct)

Clinical quick reference for digestive terrain, bloating, and relapse-prone small-bowel patterns. Drafted as a **TCM adjunct** to biomed SIBO care (e.g. Clearfield / Hill three-phase pathway) — not a replacement for antimicrobials, labs, or red-flag workup.

## When This Pack Applies

| Biomed context | AP role |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 antimicrobials | Support SP/ST; calm shen; mild move-qi — avoid heroic draining in Xu patients |
| Phase 2 secretions / motility | **Primary AP window** — tonify SP, move fu, restore “clear ascent / turbid descent” |
| Phase 3 nutrient rebuild | Tonify qi/blood; support assimilation |
| Relapse after kill-only care | Pattern discrimination + Phase 2-style points |
| Stress-triggered bloating | LV invading SP protocols |

**Red flags (AP does not lead):** acute abdomen, GI bleed, obstruction, unexplained weight loss, febrile colitis — escalate biomed.

## Combinations by Pattern

### Spleen Qi Deficiency (tired gut / loose stools)

**Points:** ST-36, SP-6, REN-12, BL-20, BL-21  
**Intent:** tonify SP qi, strengthen transformation/transportation  
**Biomed echo:** hypochlorhydria / enzyme insufficiency terrain; Phase 2–3

### Dampness Obstructing the Middle

**Points:** SP-9, REN-12, ST-36, SP-6, ST-40  
**Intent:** dry/resolve Damp, restore middle jiao clarity  
**Biomed echo:** bloating, sticky stools, foggy head with meals

### Damp-Heat in the Intestines

**Points:** ST-25, ST-37, LI-11, SP-9, ST-44  
**Intent:** clear Heat, resolve Damp, move the fu  
**Biomed echo:** urgent, foul, burning stools — still rule out IBD red flags

### Liver Invading Spleen (stress relapse)

**Points:** LV-3, LV-13, ST-36, SP-6, REN-12  
**Optional:** LI-4, GB-34  
**Intent:** spread LV qi, stop insult to SP, reduce stress bloating  
**Biomed echo:** relapse with deadlines / sympathetic tone; “stop grazing” counseling

### Food Stagnation / Epigastric Oppression

**Points:** REN-12, ST-36, ST-21, P-6, LI-4  
**Intent:** promote digestion, descend ST qi, ease nausea/fullness  
**Biomed echo:** early satiety, post-prandial pressure; enzyme/bile Phase 2 context

### Cold in the Middle (cramping better with warmth)

**Points:** REN-12, ST-36, REN-6, SP-4 (moxa-friendly where trained)  
**Intent:** warm the middle, stop cold pain/diarrhea  
**Caution:** avoid heavy warming in Damp-Heat presentations

### Motility / “Move the fu” (constipation-leaning or stuck gas)

**Points:** ST-25, ST-37, TB-6, ST-36, LV-3  
**Intent:** move qi in the intestines, reduce distension  
**Biomed echo:** MMC support adjunct — not a laxative protocol alone

### Shen–Gut (anxiety grazing / insomnia with IBS-overlap)

**Points:** HE-7, Yintang (M-HN-3), ST-36, SP-6, P-6  
**Intent:** settle spirit, support SP, reduce all-day snacking drive  
**Cross-link:** [sleep-spirit-protocols.md](sleep-spirit-protocols.md)

## Pairing with Hill Three-Phase Care

| Phase | Emphasize |
|---|---|
| 1 Antimicrobial (~30d) | ST-36, SP-6, REN-12; light LV-3 if stress; avoid aggressive jing-well draining if Xu |
| 2 Motility + secretions | Full SP/ST + move-fu sets; LV→SP if stress; P-6 if nausea |
| 3 Nutrients ≥6 mo | ST-36, SP-6, BL-20/21, REN-12; Blood tonification if pale/fatigue |
| Relapse | Re-pattern; don’t only “re-kill” |
