## What Belongs Here

- Source citations and references
- Study summaries (one finding per file when possible)
- Open questions and hypotheses
- Catalogs of external-drive archives (before selective import)

## Naming Rules

- `YYYY-MM-DD-Short-Title.md` for dated findings
- `topic/Question-Being-Investigated.md` for ongoing inquiry
- Always link evidence level: observation, derived, interpreted, speculative
