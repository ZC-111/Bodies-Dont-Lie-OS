# Materia Medica Research

## Purpose

Hold source material for plant and remedy systems — herbals, essential oils, flower essences, tinctures, and homeopathy.

## What Belongs Here

- Herbal monographs, formulas, and protocols
- Essential oils / aromatherapy
- Flower essences (e.g. Bach)
- Tinctures and botanical extracts
- Homeopathy materia medica and repertory notes
- Research papers on phytotherapy

## Branch Rules

- **Herbals, oils, flowers, tinctures, homeopathy** live here — not under nutrition
- **Vitamins, minerals, probiotics, peptides-as-supplements** go to [nutrition/](../nutrition/README.md)
- If a file is both (e.g. herbal adaptogen + metabolic diet), primary branch = dominant modality; cross-link the other
- Link to [immune/](../immune/README.md) when used for mold/autoimmune protocols
