## 3. Answer thesis (2–3 sentences)

SIBO often returns because antimicrobial courses suppress overgrowth without restoring the terrain that keeps the small bowel clear. Hill’s teaching frames a three-phase model: suppress, restore secretions/motility, then rebuild nutrients for months. Acupuncture can support middle-jiao patterns and stress-gut drivers as an adjunct — not a replacement for biomed care or escalation.

## 4. Body beats (bullets)

1. Open with relapse reality (~44% / 9 mo framing from webinar synthesis) — normalize without fear-mongering.  
2. Contrast kill-only cycles vs staged care; name Phase 1–3 goals and rough durations.  
3. Explain Phase 2 drivers in plain language: HCl/enzymes/bile, MMC, stop all-day grazing, stress/sympathetic tone.  
4. Note Phase 3 nutrient collateral (≥6 months): B12, Fe, fat-soluble vitamins, etc.  
5. Integrative insert: TCM pattern (SP qi xu, Damp middle, LV→SP) maps to “how hard to push” during rebuild; show classic middle-jiao duo as teaching visual.  
6. Close with clinician ask + red-flag reminder.
