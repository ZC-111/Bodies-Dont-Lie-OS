## Disclaimer (spoken or on-screen end card)

Educational content — not personal medical advice. Verify with a qualified clinician.

## 1. Hook (0–5s)

Half of SIBO cases relapse by nine months.

## 2. Beat sheet

| Time | Beat | Spoken / action | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Hook | “If your SIBO came back — you’re not alone. About half relapse by nine months.” | `~½ relapse @ 9 mo` |
| 5–25s | Beat 1 | Kill-only care fails when secretions, motility, and nutrients never get fixed. | `Kill ≠ finish line` |
| 25–50s | Beat 2 | Walk three phases: ~30-day antimicrobials (± *H. pylori* when both suspected) → motility/HCl/bile → ≥6 months nutrient rebuild. | `1 Kill → 2 Stage → 3 Rebuild` |
| 50–70s | Beat 3 | Optional integrative beat: acupuncture supports middle-jiao / stress-gut patterns while biomed fixes the stage — pattern tells how hard to push. | Show middle-jiao SVG |
| 70–90s | CTA | “Ask: what’s my Phase 2 and Phase 3 — not just my next antimicrobial.” | `Phase 2 + 3?` |
