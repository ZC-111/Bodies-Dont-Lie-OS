## 3. Answer thesis (2–3 sentences)

Breath tests are useful tools with meaningful miss rates — especially for distal small-bowel disease. Hill’s teaching prioritizes the clinical picture and warns against treating a negative test as a complete clearance. Readers should understand limits and ask better interpretation questions, not self-diagnose or skip alarm-feature evaluation.

## 4. Body beats (bullets)

1. Hook: negative test, persistent bloating — common confusion after educational videos.  
2. Explain sensitivity numbers with claim tags; avoid overstating “accuracy.”  
3. Anatomy metaphor: sampling feet of a ~19-foot small bowel.  
4. Supportive labs mentioned in pathway (B12/Fe, fat-soluble vitamins, etc.) as context — not a home panel.  
5. Pearl: B12 deficiency → classic SIBO framing; symptomatic without B12 deficit → consider IMO (name as teaching pearl; don’t invent IMO protocol).  
6. CTA + red flags (bleed, obstruction, unexplained weight loss).
