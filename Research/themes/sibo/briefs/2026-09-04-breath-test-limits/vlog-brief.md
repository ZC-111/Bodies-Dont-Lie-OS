## Disclaimer (spoken or on-screen end card)

Educational content — not personal medical advice. Verify with a qualified clinician.

## 1. Hook (0–5s)

A negative breath test doesn’t always mean you’re fine.

| Time | Beat | Spoken / action | On-screen text |

| 25–50s | Beat 2 | The 19-foot problem: tests sample limited segments of a long small bowel. | `~19 ft SI` |

- Don’t give antimicrobial self-start instructions.  
- Don’t minimize alarm symptoms.
