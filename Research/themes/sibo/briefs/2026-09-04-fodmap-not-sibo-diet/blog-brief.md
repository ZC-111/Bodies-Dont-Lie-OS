## 3. Answer thesis (2–3 sentences)

Low-FODMAP can reduce fermentable load and IBS-like symptoms, but Hill’s teaching rejects treating it as the default long-term SIBO diet. Relapse prevention depends more on secretions, motility, and nutrient rebuild than on indefinite restriction. Public content must label this as a speaker stance and avoid nutrition-prescription LARPing.

## 4. Body beats (bullets)

1. Hook: FODMAP ≠ stamp from content kit.  
2. Clarify what low-FODMAP is good at (symptom modulation in IBS frameworks).  
3. Contrast with three-phase SIBO goals — diet is not Phase 2.  
4. Name risks of chronic ultra-restriction (malnutrition, fear of food) without moralizing.  
5. Point readers to better questions: Phase 2 secretions/motility, Phase 3 nutrients, planned re-expansion.  
6. Light AP note optional: Damp/food stagnation patterns ≠ license for extreme diets.
