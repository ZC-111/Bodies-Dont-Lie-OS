## Disclaimer (spoken or on-screen end card)

Educational content — not personal medical advice. Verify with a qualified clinician. This is one clinician teaching stance, not a universal diet rule.

## 1. Hook (0–5s)

FODMAP is not a SIBO diet.

## 2. Beat sheet

| Time | Beat | Spoken / action | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Hook | Big ≠ stamp: low-FODMAP ≠ default SIBO protocol. | `FODMAP ≠ SIBO diet` |
| 5–25s | Beat 1 | Low-FODMAP can calm IBS-like symptoms — that doesn’t mean endless restriction fixes relapse. | `Symptom tool` |
| 25–50s | Beat 2 | Relapse work is Phase 2–3: secretions, motility, nutrients — not forever food fear. | `Phase 2 + 3` |
| 50–70s | Beat 3 | Speaker stance, not dogma: short bridges under care can help; chronic ultra-restriction is the trap to question. | `Stance ≠ dogma` |
| 70–90s | CTA | Ask when foods re-expand and what your Phase 2 plan is. | `Re-expand when?` |

## 4. Do-not-say (on camera)

- Don’t order viewers off FODMAP cold.  
- Don’t list “safe foods” as medical advice.  
- Don’t mock people using FODMAP under supervision.

## 5. CTA

**Ask / next step:** “When do we re-expand — and what’s my Phase 2 plan?”
