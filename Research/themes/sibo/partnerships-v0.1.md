# SIBO Theme — Partnerships v0.1

Educational affiliate / partner map for SIBO blogs & vlogs.  
**Not** clinical endorsements. Keep claim tags (`speaker_claim` / `product_pitch` / `cited_study`). Disclose paid relationships.

Theme hub: [README.md](README.md)

## Doctrine

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Map to content | Partner by brief + phase — not random gut brands |
| Timing | Probiotics / ferments **after** antimicrobial month in Hill synthesis (~day 31) |
| CTA style | “Ask your clinician about this *category*” — no DIY dosing protocols |
| Max stack | One primary lab lane + one primary product lane per piece |
| Disclose | Affiliate / speaker ties / samples |

## Product / category lanes (opinionated)

### A. Aligns with existing Hill / Clearfield assets (`product_pitch` already in pathway)

| Category | Examples / notes | Content window |
|---|---|---|
| Botanical Phase 1 kits | Biotics Research SBO / ADP lineage (speaker-linked — **disclose**) | Relapse / three-phase structure only; no consumer DIY stack |
| Phase 2 secretions / motility | Hydrozyme / Beta Plus / Motilityzyme (named in lecture) | “What’s my Phase 2?” CTA |
| Targeted probiotics | ***S. boulardii***, ***L. plantarum*** | After ~day 31; strain-specific, not megadose blends |

### B. Consumer interest — park in follow-up series (not lead SIBO v1)

| Category | Examples | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Akkermansia | Pendulum (live capsule; anaerobic — **not** home yogurt) | Separate metabolic/lining lane; soft fit for relapse posts; watch inulin flares |
| *L. gasseri* DIY yogurt | Cutting Edge / Cultured Food Life starters; **Luvele** or sous vide holding **~97–100°F / 36h** | DIY Phase-3 lifestyle content; verify thermometer; prebiotic fiber risk |
| Fermented foods | Kimchi, kraut, kefir (unpasteurized, raw) | Re-expansion story; pathway often **holds ferments during active Rx** |
| “Kimchi pills” | Capsule extracts | Usually weak (strain/dose clarity) |
| Postbiotic | Butyrate (mentioned in pathway adjuncts) | Cleaner supplement angle than random ferment pills |

### C. Labs

| Lane | Fit | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Breath testing via clinicians | Breath-test limits brief | Don’t sell self-diagnosis |
| Phase 3 nutrient panels | B12, iron studies, fat-solubles | Support rebuild narrative, not SIBO proof |

## Do-not-partner (for this theme)

- Detox / parasite stack spam  
- Rifaximin coupon farms  
- Shotgun megadose probiotics that contradict the content kit  
- Invented IMO protocols (still a coverage `gap`)  
- Fake Costco “product affiliate” URLs  

## Suggested outreach order

1. Join **Amazon Associates** → Florastor link for post–Phase 1 probiotic mention.  
2. Join **iHerb** (or Impact publisher) → Phase 3 nutrient + named-strain catalog.  
3. Soft outreach to **Biotics** (pathway continuity) + **Florastor/Biocodex** (creator education).  
4. Optional: Costco **membership** affiliate if audience asks “where to buy cheap.”  
5. Defer Akkermansia / gasseri-yogurt / kimchi-pill deals until a dedicated “after the kill month” series.
