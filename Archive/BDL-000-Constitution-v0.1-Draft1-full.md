Preamble

Bodies Don't Lie OS is an open framework for discovering, organizing, and applying knowledge about resilient systems.

We believe that every complex system—whether biological, ecological, engineered, social, or digital—produces observable evidence about its condition.

Our responsibility is not to force reality to fit our assumptions, but to continually refine our understanding so that our actions align more closely with reality.

We commit to curiosity before certainty, evidence before opinion, stewardship before ownership, and learning before conclusions.

Article I
Purpose

The purpose of Bodies Don't Lie OS is:

To help people observe more clearly.
To improve decision making through evidence.
To cultivate resilient systems.
To preserve knowledge for future generations.
To build tools that amplify human understanding rather than replace it.
Article II

No single discipline owns truth.

Every discipline contributes to understanding.

Article III
First Principles
Principle One

Reality leaves evidence.

Principle Two

Healthy systems continuously communicate their condition through observable signals.

Principle Three

Observation precedes understanding.

Principle Four

Understanding precedes intervention.

Principle Five

Resilience is demonstrated through adaptation.

Principle Six

Knowledge grows through testing, refinement, and sharing.

Principle Seven

Long-term stewardship is more valuable than short-term optimization.

Article V
Evidence

Claims should be proportional to the available evidence.

When uncertainty exists:

Document uncertainty.

Do not hide it.

New evidence is welcomed.

Changing one's mind in response to evidence is considered a strength.

Article VI
Contributors

Every contributor is expected to:

Observe honestly.

Credit others.

Document assumptions.

Share improvements.

Remain teachable.

Protect future contributors by leaving clear documentation.

Article VII
Technology

Technology exists to express the principles.

Technology is not the principles.

Programming languages change.

AI models change.

Frameworks change.

The Constitution remains.

Article VIII
Stewardship

Bodies Don't Lie OS is intended to grow beyond any individual.

The goal is not simply to build software.

The goal is to cultivate a living body of knowledge that future generations can improve.

Closing Statement

We recognize that understanding is never complete.

Therefore, this Constitution is not a declaration of certainty.

It is a commitment to continual observation, disciplined learning, and responsible stewardship.
